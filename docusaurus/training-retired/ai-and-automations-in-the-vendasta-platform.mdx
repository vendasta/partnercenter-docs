<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🤖</span>Your AI Receptionist & Automated Workflows</div>

The AI Receptionist is your always-on, first-touch handler. Whether a lead comes in through chat, voice, or SMS, it captures and qualifies them instantly — no manual effort required. But the first touch is just the beginning.

### Where automations take over

When your AI Receptionist captures a lead, automations can kick in immediately: send a follow-up message, add the lead to a pipeline, notify the business owner, or trigger a nurture sequence. The AI handles the first touch; automations handle the rest of the journey.

### Why automated workflows matter

Automated workflows save time and ensure that no critical tasks fall through the cracks. For small businesses, this operational consistency is the difference between a lead that converts and one that goes cold.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🎯</span>Customizing Automation Triggers</div>

Building effective automations requires more customization than configuring the AI Receptionist on its own. Tailoring triggers to fit your business needs enhances the relevance of your communications and keeps customers engaged.

### Behavior-based triggers

Set triggers based on customer actions, such as:

- Website visits
- Missed appointments
- Chat or form submissions
- Product activations

### Time-based triggers

Automate messages based on specific dates or milestones, such as:

- Anniversaries (business anniversary, customer anniversary)
- Follow-up reminders (e.g., 3 days after a quote)
- Re-engagement (e.g., 30 days of inactivity)

By building your AI Employee and exploring the full value of automation, you create a more personalized experience for local businesses.
