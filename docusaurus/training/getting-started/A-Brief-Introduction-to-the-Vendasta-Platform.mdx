Welcome to the Vendasta platform at a glance. In this course, you will explore the Vendasta platform and learn how and why we partner with experts such as yourself to unify your tools, teams, and clients, so you can achieve more in less time.

Small to medium business owners face a myriad of problems at every stage along the journey of a potential customer. These solutions are often siloed and complex. The Vendasta platform helps you break down these silos and simplify the solutions, while still serving more customers at scale.

:::tip What you'll cover
- How the Vendasta platform helps you serve SMB clients at scale
- The two main environments: Partner Center and Business App
- Key user roles and what each one can do
- The prospecting-to-performance workflow: Snapshot, Business App, and Executive Report
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🏷️</span>Vendasta: Your White Label Provider</div>

As a white label provider, we provide you with a platform, products, and services that you can brand with your own brand. White-labeling with Vendasta allows you to expand your product offering and provide a central digital hub to your clients—all under your brand.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🖥️</span>Your Dashboard & Your Client Dashboard</div>

The Vendasta platform helps you streamline your data and communication processes, all under your own brand. Find what you need in **Partner Center** and deliver it to your clients in your own branded **Business App**.

### Two main platform environments

| Environment | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Business App** | Gives your clients (SMBs) a single place to find products you sell, manage their digital strategy, and grow their business |
| **Partner Center** | Enables you to manage your business, pipeline, bill, and fulfill all from one single source of truth |

You will operate mostly out of your command center—**Partner Center**—by navigating to partners.vendasta.com.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">👥</span>Key Users of the Vendasta Platform</div>

| User | Environment | Purpose |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Partners (you)** | Partner Center | Market, sell, bill, fulfill, deliver. Access to Vendasta Marketplace |
| **Vendors** | Vendor Center | Integrate products and services for your Storefront. You can also create your own products here |
| **Salespeople** | CRM | Pipeline management, sales proposals, ordering systems, integrated training |
| **Clients (SMBs)** | Business App | Centralized reporting, communication tools, CRM, service tracking, digital products—single login |

As a Partner, you have access to them all.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📊</span>From Prospecting to Proof of Performance</div>

Starting the conversation with prospects is only the first step. Integrating them into your pipeline, knowing which leads have the greatest needs, and demonstrating proof of performance is what gets you from conversations to clients—from prospecting to profitable.

### The powerhouse trio

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Snapshot Report** | Prospecting tool. Start the conversation, get prospects into your pipeline, position yourself as the expert. Follows the customer journey |
| **Business App** | Access point for your clients. Once they move from prospect to client, direct them here |
| **Executive Report** | Proof-of-performance tool. Clients see how you've impacted their presence online |

This powerhouse trio works together to generate leads, customize your client experience, and demonstrate proof of performance.
