## What white label covers

**White-label products and services** are re-brandable, re-sellable items produced by one company to be re-branded and re-sold by another. That is the model the whole platform runs on: the workspaces, the products you resell, and the services that fulfill them carry your brand, so your clients see your name throughout.

Branding is quick work with wide reach, and it is worth doing before anything client-facing goes out. What you upload once appears in Business App, in outgoing email, on login pages, on invoices, and everywhere else the platform represents you.

<LessonSection>

## Brand both workspaces at once

Partner branding is one page, and it controls how your business is represented everywhere. Six settings do the work, in this order. If you run multiple markets, note the `All Markets` selector at the top right: branding can differ per market.

<LabCallout title="upload your brand">
Go to `Administration` → `Partner branding`.

<LabChecklist
  storageKey="brand-it-lab-1"
  steps={[
    <>In <strong>Company Name</strong>, enter your business name exactly as you want clients to see it. This is what appears in email headers, login pages, and notifications, so use the name they already know you by.</>,
    <>Under <strong>Theme</strong>, choose Light or Dark for your own team's Partner Center. Set <strong>Business App Theme</strong> separately, and User System Default is the safe choice because it respects each client's own device setting.</>,
    <>Upload your <strong>Logo</strong>. Recommended is 360 by 58 pixels with a transparent background. Turn on <code>Use a different logo for dark theme</code> if you have a second version.</>,
    <>Upload a square <strong>Company avatar / shortcut icon</strong>, 512 by 512 pixels. This is what a client sees when they save Business App to their phone's home screen, and it represents you in Conversations chat.</>,
    <>Upload a <strong>Favicon</strong> for the browser tab, at 16 by 16 pixels. Browsers cache favicons hard, so give yours time to appear.</>,
    <>Enter your <strong>Primary Color</strong> as a hex code, or pick it, and save.</>,
  ]}
  confirm={<>Refresh the page: Partner Center is wearing your brand, and so is every client's Business App.</>}
/>
</LabCallout>

For every setting on the page, [this guide walks through branding and customization](/getting-started/getting-started-guides/getting-started-with-branding-and-customization).

</LessonSection>

## Make Business App yours

Business App is where your clients log in. It is called "Business App" until you rename it, and the name you choose appears in its navigation and in the emails it sends.

<LabCallout title="rename it, and turn on the backlink">
Go to `Accounts` → `Manage Business App` → `Customize Business App` → `Branding`.

<LabChecklist
  storageKey="brand-it-lab-2"
  steps={[
    <>Find <strong>Rename Business App</strong> and enter your preferred name. It shows in the Business App navigation and in the emails it sends. Two or three words carries best across device sizes, and plain words beat special characters.</>,
    <>Scroll to <strong>Conversations Web Chat</strong> and decide on the footer. The checkbox reads <code>Show "Chat Powered By [your company name]" on all Web Chat widgets</code>, and it pulls that name from Partner branding.</>,
    <>Save.</>,
  ]}
  confirm={<>Your clients' portal now carries a name you chose rather than a generic one.</>}
/>
</LabCallout>

The footer pulls your agency name and website from your Company Profile, so fill that in first if the fields look empty. That backlink is worth switching on deliberately: it earns SEO value from every client site running your widget.

## Decide what clients see

You choose which pages appear when a client logs in. Hiding a page removes it from their navigation without disabling anything underneath it, so this is about focus rather than restriction.

<LabCallout title="set your page visibility">
Go to `Customize Business App` → `Pages`.

<LabChecklist
  storageKey="brand-it-lab-3"
  steps={[
    <>Select a page from the left sidebar.</>,
    <>Tick or clear its <code>Show this page</code> checkbox, and save.</>,
    <>Work down the list with one question in mind: does the client do this themselves, or do you do it for them?</>,
  ]}
  confirm={<>A client logging in sees the pages that match how you actually work with them.</>}
/>
</LabCallout>

A useful starting point: **Store** and **My Products** make sense when clients self-serve, and come off when every purchase goes through your team. **Invoices** comes off when you bill outside the platform. **Projects** comes off if you do not run fulfillment workflows. Page visibility applies to every client at once, or per market if you use them, so this is a decision about how you work rather than about one client.

</LessonSection>

## What your plan carries

Two pieces of the brand story are decided by your subscription rather than by these screens, and both are worth knowing before you promise anything to a client.

:::info What your subscription gates
**White label** depends on your plan. On plans that include it, the platform carries your brand with no mention of Vendasta, in Partner Center and in every client's Business App. Free tiers do not include it, and some white-label options vary by plan. **Custom domains** are available on select plans as well. [Plan details are on the pricing page](https://www.vendasta.com/pricing).
:::

A **custom domain** is the piece most often mistaken for a first-day task. It lets clients and salespeople reach the platform through a link that carries your brand, such as app.youragency.com, instead of a default URL. Treat it as an upgrade to plan for rather than something to chase now, and set the expectation with clients accordingly.

:::tip Try it now
Open Business App the way a client would and look at it end to end: the name in the navigation, your logo, the color, the tab icon. That is the first thing every client you bring on will see.
:::
