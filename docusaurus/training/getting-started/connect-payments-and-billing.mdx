## Two prices on every product

Getting paid runs on one relationship: you buy products from Vendasta at a wholesale price, and you sell them to your clients at the retail price you set. Every product has two prices, and the difference between them is your margin. Flip each one:

  <IconFlipCard

Each active product creates two matching subscriptions: a wholesale subscription from you to Vendasta, and a retail subscription from your client to you. You manage both from Partner Center.

## Choose how you collect

**Vendasta Payments** lets a client pay an order or invoice with a card inside the platform, and pays that revenue out to your bank account. It comes with a paid subscription; free and trial tiers do not carry it. It runs on Stripe, and there are two ways to set it up:

- **`Connect an existing account`.** The Stripe account belongs to you, and everything on it stays yours wherever your business goes: your transaction history, your reporting, and the rates you have already negotiated with Stripe. Your Stripe rates are unchanged, and the platform adds a **0.75 percent platform fee** on each transaction.
- **`Set up Stripe account`.** A new account created through the platform rather than under your own Stripe login. There is no platform fee, and processing runs at the standard rate: 2.9 percent plus 30 cents per transaction in USD, CAD, AUD, and NZD. Approval typically takes one to two business days. The account lives inside the platform, which means the account and the payment history on it stay with the platform.

Both collect the same payments; the difference is who holds the account. **Connecting your own account is the recommended route.** The 0.75 percent buys you ownership: the history, the reporting, and the Stripe relationship stay with your business wherever it goes. If you do not have a Stripe account yet, opening one at stripe.com takes a few minutes and leaves you with an account of your own to connect.

Decide before your first transaction, because connecting your own Stripe account stays available only until you process a payment through a platform-created one. You can also skip in-platform collection entirely and bill clients through whatever system you already use.

One check before you apply: the platform reads your details from the **Billing contact** card at `Administration` → `My billing`, and an incomplete address there is what produces an "Unsupported in Your Area" message. It is usually filled in when your account is created, so give it a look before you start.

The application itself is a Stripe identity check, and it goes fastest with everything in front of you:

- Your **legal business name**, exactly as it appears on your tax ID
- Your **business number**: EIN in the United States, CRA business number in Canada
- Your **operating name**, if you trade under a different one
- Your **registered business address**. A home address works if you do not have one
- The **account representative's** legal name, home address, and government ID
- The **bank account** your payouts land in: country, currency, transit number, institution number, and account number

Two things on that page are yours to set rather than Stripe's, and both are easy to miss. The **`Account statement description`** is how charges appear on your customers' statements: replace the default with your own business name. And **`Receiving bank account`** is an in-platform form rather than a Stripe screen, which is where those bank details go.

This part runs across several Stripe screens, it asks for government ID, and approval typically takes one to two business days. Work through it with [the Vendasta Payments guide](/administration/commerce/vendasta-payments/) open beside you, which covers every field in order.

![The first screen of the Vendasta Payments application, a Stripe form asking for the type of business](./img/vendasta-payments-application.jpg)

Once you are approved, payments your clients make appear under `Commerce` → `Payments`. Stripe holds your first payments while it verifies the bank account you added, which takes up to a week, and pays out on a seven-day rolling basis after that, into the account you just added. The full field-by-field detail is in [the Vendasta Payments guide](/administration/commerce/vendasta-payments/).

:::tip Try it now
Get that first hold out of the way before a client is waiting on it. Put a one-dollar retail price on a product, activate it on an account of your own, and pay the invoice. Stripe runs its bank verification on that transaction, so the wait is behind you by the time real revenue arrives.
:::

## Set billing to collect on its own

Default billing settings decide what happens on every renewal, and they apply automatically to each new account you create. Set this once and collection is handled from then on.

<LabCallout title="turn on default billing automation">
Go to `Administration` → `Default billing settings`.

<LabChecklist
  storageKey="connect-payments-lab-3"
  steps={[
    <>Select your <code>Market</code>. The rest of the page appears only once you do.</>,
    <>Turn the <code>Subscriptions</code> toggle on. It is off by default, and it is what tracks and collects payment on each renewal.</>,
    <>Set <code>Subscription scheduling</code>: renew on each subscription's own start date, or put every new subscription on one shared day of the month.</>,
    <>Set <code>Payment collection</code>: <code>Automatic</code> or <code>Manual</code>.</>,
    <>Set <code>Subscription renewal</code>: <code>Email payable invoice to billing recipient</code> raises an invoice you send, with a required <code>Invoice due date</code>; <code>Automatically charge payment method on file</code> collects without you touching it.</>,
    <>Read the <strong>Billing summary</strong> panel on the right, then select <code>Save</code>.</>,
  ]}
  confirm={<>Every account you create from now on starts with collection handled, without you configuring it each time.</>}
/>
</LabCallout>

![The Default billing settings page, with a Market selected, the Subscriptions toggle on, Subscription scheduling set to use the subscription start date, Payment collection set to Automatic, and Subscription renewal set to email a payable invoice with a 7-day due date](./img/default-billing-automation.png)

The page carries a `Memo` field as well, which prints at the bottom of every invoice subscription billing creates. And one limit worth knowing, which the page states in a banner of its own: this applies to accounts created after you save. Accounts that already exist keep their current settings, and any single account can be switched from its own billing settings under `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`. That is how a client who needs net terms, or who should not be charged the moment an order lands, gets treatment different from your default. [This guide covers the options](/administration/commerce/default-billing-settings/).

## Where your retail prices live

Pricing is yours to control, and automatic collection depends on it: without a retail price on an edition, there is no amount for it to calculate. Products arrive carrying the vendor's suggested retail price, so a number is already there and nothing is blocking your first sale.

Your prices live at `Marketplace` → `Products`, on a product's `Product info` tab, in the **Product settings** section, where **Retail price** lists each edition separately. Your **wholesale cost** lives elsewhere: `Marketplace` → `Discover Products` carries `Your cost` columns beside the vendor's suggested retail, which is where you check margin before you set a number.

The natural moment to set your own prices is when you build the packages you actually sell, not product by product on day one. One fact keeps the numbers clean whenever you get to it: a catalog price change applies to new subscriptions from then on. Existing clients keep the price they signed up at unless you edit their subscription directly, and an edit takes effect on the next billing cycle. [This guide covers subscription management](/commerce/subscriptions/subscription-management).

## What this month is tracking toward

One tab answers "where is my Vendasta bill sitting right now?" Go to `Administration` → `My billing`, then the `Estimated Usage` tab. It breaks down what you will pay at the end of the month based on the products currently active: `Products`, `Units`, `Gross Total`, `Discounts`, and `Net Total`, product by product rather than as one figure. It pages, so work down it rather than looking for a total at the bottom.

The `Discounts` column shows what is coming off your gross. `Active subscriptions`, three tabs along, names which products are in the current cycle and when any of them expire. [This guide covers every tab on the billing page](/administration/my-account/my-billing/).
