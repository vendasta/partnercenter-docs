## Help built into the platform

The fastest answer usually comes from the AI assistant behind the sparkle icon at the top right of Partner Center. It draws on Vendasta's documentation, answers in seconds, and links the articles it drew from, which makes it the natural first stop for any how-do-I question. The help menu beside it opens the documentation directly whenever you would rather read than ask.

People are part of the platform too. Every partner with a paid subscription is entitled to a free onboarding session with a Customer Success Manager, who supports as many training sessions as you need: CRM, Snapshot Reports, billing, marketing, and more. Reach your assigned Client Success team from the bottom left corner of Partner Center. [This guide covers what onboarding includes](/getting-started/partner-onboarding).

## Two documentation sites, two audiences

The written documentation splits cleanly along the same line as the two workspaces:

- **This site** covers your side: Partner Center, billing, the Marketplace, and everything you run as a partner.
- **[docs.businessapp.io](https://docs.businessapp.io)** covers Business App and the products your clients use. It carries nothing from your side (no Partner Center, no wholesale pricing), so you can share the whole site or a single article with a client without breaking your white label. Its built-in AI chat answers their product questions too.

When a client asks how a product works, a link to the matching docs.businessapp.io article is often the fastest, cleanest answer you can give.

:::tip Try it now
Open the AI assistant from the sparkle icon at the top right of Partner Center and ask it something you genuinely wondered this week. Follow one of the article links in its answer to see where the knowledge lives.
:::

## Live webinars

Vendasta hosts live training webinars for partners, with a range of session times through the [webinar calendar](https://www.vendasta.com/webinar). The Vendasta Services sessions are a strong example: they cover fulfillment from pre-sale to delivery, [with topics listed here](/vendasta-services/working-with-our-team/vendasta-services-training-webinars-for-partners).
