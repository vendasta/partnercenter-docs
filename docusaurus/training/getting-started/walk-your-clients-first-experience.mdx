## Work is done before your client logs in

The first email a new client gets from you is a list of finished work. While their account was being set up, AI read their website, prefilled their business profile, named their AI Chat Receptionist, scanned more than 40 directories for their listings, and gathered their reviews. When Vibe is active on the account, it built them a starter website as well. The email lists each item with a check mark and closes on one button, **See what's ready for you**, which opens their Business App. Setting a password happens on the way in, when they need one.

Every line is generated from the business's own data and from the products active on the account, so two clients rarely receive the same list. A line about reviews appears when Reputation is active. A line about directories appears when Local SEO is active. The website and business profile lines appear for every business with a website on file. In the first 24 hours after the account is created, the review and visibility lines read as scanning, with results in about 24 hours, then switch to the finished wording once the results are in.

:::info Why the email takes a few minutes
The onboarding email holds its send until the products on the account have finished activating, and when Vibe is active, until the website has been built. Allow 5 to 15 minutes for it to arrive. If a build runs long, the email still goes, without the website line.
:::

## Where each link takes your client

Every line on the work card is a link, and each one opens the screen where that work continues. The directory line opens `Local SEO` → `Listing Sync`, where the scan's findings are waiting: the directories already carrying the business, and the ones with no listing yet. Connecting Google and Facebook is the first move there, and each source shows its own listing score once it is connected. The business profile line opens `Business Profile` → `Descriptions`, where a short description and a long one are already drafted and waiting for a `Save`.

Name that on your handoff call: the scan found where the client is missing, and connecting those sources is the work it found.

:::tip Try it now
On your test account's `Home`, select `view your results` on the directory line and count the sources reading **Listing Not found**. That count is what your next client opens on, and it is the first thing worth explaining to them.
:::

## Three settings decide what they see

Everything on that first day traces back to three decisions on your side, and two of them are settings you make once.

**What is active on the account when you invite.** Lines in the email and on `Home` are product-gated, so the products you activate before you invite decide what the client reads about. Vibe is the biggest single lever: when Vibe is active and the account has no site yet, sending the onboarding email builds the business a free starter website first, one per account, and the email leads with it. Activate Vibe before you invite, and a client without a website opens their first email to one that exists. If you build websites yourself, or theirs is already live, leave Vibe off the account and the email leads with the rest of the work.

**The upgrade path on each product.** Each Recommended card follows the product's `Upgrade` setting in your Marketplace. `Self-serve upgrade` puts **Get started** on the card and opens checkout: with Vendasta Payments connected, the client pays by card inside Business App; without it, the purchase arrives as an order for you to process. `Contact a salesperson` puts **Contact us** on the card instead. A product you have never configured is treated as upgradeable and shows **Get started**. Turning `Upgrade` off removes that product's card from Home entirely.

**The Home cards themselves.** Both the work card and the Recommended cards are on by default for every client. `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Customize Business App` → `Pages` → `Home` carries one checkbox for each under **Page Features**: **"AI has done the work" card** and **"Recommended for your business" cards**. Clearing one hides that section for all of your clients. The setting controls display only, so a Vibe site still builds and an upgrade path still works underneath it.

## Send the email to yourself

The preview beside the invite panel is a static sample: your branding, the layout, and the button, with placeholder work. The real email is generated for the business. The one way to read exactly what a client receives is to receive it. Use a recently created account for this: the work card carries roughly the last 30 days of work, so an older account shows a shorter list.

:::tip Try it now
Open your test client account under `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`, select `Invite users`, add your own email address, and select `Invite 1 user`. Allow 5 to 15 minutes, then read the email the way a client would and follow **See what's ready for you** through to Home. To send it again later, the `⋮` menu on your row carries `Send Onboarding Email`.
:::
