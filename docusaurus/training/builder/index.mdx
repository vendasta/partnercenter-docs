In most businesses, a person is the glue: someone takes the call, types the details into the CRM, checks the calendar, sends the confirmation. This track hands that glue work to your AI Employees, so they take the actions people usually do, communicate, book the appointment, add details to an account, send the email, and pass the work to each other through automated handoffs instead of a person carrying it between systems.
