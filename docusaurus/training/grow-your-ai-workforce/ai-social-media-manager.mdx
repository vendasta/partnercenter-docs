The content is the business's own: posts draw on its reviews, photos, services, and seasonal offers, so the pages read like the business wrote them. You can also work with it conversationally at any time: ask for a post, adjust a draft, or pull performance insights from chat.

## What it does on day one

A configured Social Media Manager already:

- Generates the social calendar on the schedule you set, with the content mix, post length, and networks you chose
- Writes on-brand posts and sources or generates the matching images
- Publishes automatically, or holds every post as a draft for approval, whichever you set
- Answers in chat: write a post, update a draft, delete one, or report on performance

The publish setting is the one decision to make deliberately. A business that wants a hands-off program sets auto-publish; one that wants eyes on every post holds drafts for approval. Both run on the same calendar.

</LessonSection>

## Activate the AI Social Media Manager for your clients

The manager is not a separate purchase; **Social Marketing (Social AI)** carries it. You activate Social Marketing on the client's account, from the account's `Products` tab in `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`. From that moment the client sees Social Marketing inside their Business App, under your brand: the calendar, the drafts waiting for approval, and every connected network in one place.

:::info Which edition carries the manager
The AI Social Media Manager unlocks on the **Premium** edition of Social AI, together with the AI Blogger. The two arrive as a pair: a client who gets one gets both.
:::

When a client is ready, [the Social Media Manager setup guide](/ai/ai-workforce/social-media-manager) walks the full configuration end to end: the calendar, post instructions, images, and publish settings.
