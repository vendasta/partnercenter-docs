## Beyond the core five

Three more employees are available when a business wants to go deeper: the AI Data Analyst, the AI Support Employee, and the Inside Sales Representative. The sales materials use their selling names for two of them, the AI Support Agent and the AI Inside Salesperson: same employees, same builds. These are custom-built rather than pre-configured: you create each one as a Custom AI Employee, defining its purpose, capabilities, and knowledge, and the setup guides below hand you the build. Lead with the core five, and bring these in when the business asks for more.

The build itself uses the same skills as any custom employee; [the lab in Hire your first AI Employee](/learn/ai-workforce/custom-employee-lab) walks through one from scratch.

<LessonSection>

## AI Data Analyst

The AI Data Analyst turns scattered business data into a roadmap for growth. It reads CRM records, reviews, and social engagement, and turns them into actionable insights using a structured analysis framework, so the answer to "what should we do next?" comes from the data rather than a hunch.

## AI Support Employee

The AI Support Employee resolves customer inquiries in chat with a structured troubleshooting approach, answering from the knowledge base and staying empathetic under pressure. When a question falls outside what it knows, it captures the details and escalates to a person instead of guessing.

## Inside Sales Representative

The Inside Sales Representative qualifies inbound leads before they reach a calendar. It asks the qualifying questions, captures contact information, books appointments for the leads that fit, and holds the line on the ones that do not, so the sales team's time goes to conversations worth having.
