This is the employee whose first business to serve is usually yours. Your CRM in Partner Center is where your own pipeline lives, and the Sales Assistant is what keeps it clean while you sell: meetings recorded, records updated, follow-ups created, coaching insights on every call.

<LessonSection>

## What it does on day one

A configured Sales Assistant already:

- Keeps CRM records up to date and creates follow-up activities so the next step is never lost
- Answers pipeline questions in chat, from "which deals stalled this month?" to "who have I not followed up with?"

On the **Pro** edition, conversation intelligence adds the capture:

- Auto-records and transcribes customer calls and meetings, once recording is enabled, with 750 minutes included each month
- Updates CRM records with lead and job details straight from those conversations
- Surfaces sales coaching insights from real calls

</LessonSection>

## Activate the AI Sales Assistant for your clients

The assistant is not a separate purchase; **CRM AI** carries it. You activate CRM AI on the client's account, from the `Products` card on the account Overview in `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`. From that moment the assistant works inside the client's CRM in their Business App, under your brand: meetings recorded, records updated, and the chat ready for pipeline questions.

:::info Which edition carries the assistant
CRM AI **Standard** or **Pro** unlocks the Sales Assistant. A basic CRM is free with every Business App account, and your own CRM comes with Partner Center, but the free CRM does not include this employee: the CRM AI subscription is what adds it. Conversation intelligence, the recording and coaching layer, is **Pro** only.
:::

:::tip Try it now
Open your `CRM` in Partner Center and pick any company record. You never have to type those fields in by hand again: the Sales Assistant fills them from the conversation itself.
:::

When a client is ready, [the AI Sales Assistant setup guide](/ai/ai-workforce/ai-sales-assistant) walks the full configuration end to end: recording, CRM updates, and the chat interface.
