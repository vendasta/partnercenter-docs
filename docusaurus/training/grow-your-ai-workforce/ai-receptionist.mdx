## What it does on day one

Before any tuning, a freshly configured receptionist already:

- Answers calls, texts, and web chats around the clock
- Sends the missed-call text-back automatically, so a caller who gave up still hears back in seconds
- Qualifies leads with the questions you set, and books estimates and appointments on the calendar
- Detects the language a visitor writes in and answers in it, from more than 40
- Hands the conversation to a person the moment it runs out of answers, with the details captured

Everything it says comes from its knowledge sources, so the answers are the business's own. If you have not set up your own Chat or Voice Receptionist yet, [Hire your first AI Employee](/learn/ai-workforce/) is the full walkthrough: putting one to work, teaching it to book, and training it to answer accurately.

</LessonSection>

## Activate the AI Receptionist for your clients

The receptionist is not a separate purchase; a product carries it. For a client, you activate **Conversations AI** on their account, from the `Products` card on the account Overview in `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`, choosing an edition as you do. From that moment the client sees Conversations AI inside their Business App, under your brand, carrying their conversations from web chat, text, and the social channels into one inbox. [This guide covers activating products on an account](/accounts/manage-accounts/product-management).

:::info Which edition answers which channel
Every edition of Conversations AI includes chat. **Pro** adds the missed-call SMS text-back. **Premium** adds voice, the receptionist that answers the phone. A client who wants their phone answered needs Premium.
:::

For your own business, your Vendasta subscription decides instead: the Starter plan includes the Chat Receptionist, and Professional and above adds the Voice Receptionist and the social channels. [This guide breaks down the features by plan](/ai/ai-workforce/ai-receptionist-features-by-plan).

:::tip Try it now
Click `Chat` in `AI` → `Workforce` to open a conversation with your own receptionist, private to you and needing no widget install. Use an incognito window so the session starts clean, ask it your business hours, then decline to give your phone number and watch how it handles that.
:::

When a client is ready, the setup guides walk the full configuration end to end: [the AI Chat Receptionist guide](/ai/ai-workforce/ai-chat-receptionist) and [the AI Voice Receptionist guide](/ai/ai-workforce/ai-voice-receptionist) cover the role, channels, capabilities, knowledge, and testing.
