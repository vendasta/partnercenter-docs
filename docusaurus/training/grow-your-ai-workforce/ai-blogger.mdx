Fresh, local, service-specific posts are what search engines reward, so the program supports local SEO with every edition of the calendar. Like the Social Media Manager, it also works conversationally: draft a new post, refine one, reschedule, or delete, all from chat.

<LessonSection>

## What it does on day one

A configured AI Blogger already:

- Generates the blog calendar on the schedule you define
- Writes long-form posts with topics pulled from the business's services and jobs
- Sources or generates a featured image for every post
- Publishes to WordPress automatically, or holds drafts for approval, whichever you set

</LessonSection>

## Activate the AI Blogger for your clients

The blogger is not a separate purchase; **Social AI** carries it, the same product that carries the AI Social Media Manager. You activate Social AI on the client's account, from the `Products` card on the account Overview in `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`. From that moment the client sees the blog program inside their Business App, under your brand: the calendar, the drafts, and the WordPress connection in one place. One requirement to check first: the AI Blogger publishes to WordPress only, so the client needs a WordPress site for it to write to.

:::info The Premium pair
The AI Blogger unlocks on the **Premium** edition of Social AI, together with the AI Social Media Manager: one edition keeps the social pages and the website fresh at once, and a client who gets one gets both.
:::

When a client is ready, [the AI Blogger setup guide](https://docs.businessapp.io/business-app/ai/ai-workforce/ai-blogger) walks the full configuration end to end: the blog calendar, post instructions, and the WordPress connection.
