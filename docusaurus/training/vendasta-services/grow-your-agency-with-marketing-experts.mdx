<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📍</span>Listings Management</div>

Our team of experts can claim online listings in record time and ensure all your clients' business information is always accurate and consistent.

### Services

- **Google Business Profile Verification & Claim:** Claim a GBP listing and give full ownership to the business while ensuring information is accurate. Clients can see how consumers engage with their listing.
- **Google Business Profile Posts:** We craft and publish posts to promote products and services.
- **Listing Claim:** Claim listings on Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, WebMD, Doctors.com, RateMDs, and more.

### Benefits

| For You | For Your Clients |
|---------|------------------|
| Save time and focus on selling | Get found online by more people |
| Get a team of experts in listings | Ensure business information is accurate |
| Stop creating reports manually | Attract more local customers |

### How we claim listings

**Credentials** → For non-GBP listings, we need credentials for a Gmail account associated with the business. **Claiming** → Steps vary by site (postcard, automated phone call). **Editing** → Once claimed, we edit business information to match the order form.

:::tip Required Products
Listing Builder and Task Manager are required for any listing service. For Google Business Profile Posts, we also require Social Marketing.
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📱</span>Social Media Content</div>

Our team writes and publishes engaging posts on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Google Business Profile, and Pinterest. We also create custom images and build social media pages upon request.

### Services

- **Social Posts: 1x Week:** 1 post/week on your choice of platform. Extended Reach add-on broadcasts to other connected accounts.
- **Social Page Build:** Creation of one social page (included with 1x Week) or purchased separately.
- **Custom Images:** We use client photos when possible. Stock images at no extra cost. Add-on for custom images (logo, text, shapes, color filters).
- **Facebook Post Boosting:** Add-on to allocate monthly budget and target specific audiences.

### Benefits

| For You | For Your Clients |
|---------|------------------|
| Grow by selling white-label social services | Increase social media engagement |
| Get a team of experts who do the heavy lifting | Expand audience on social media |
| Use white-label platform for proof of performance | Promote products and services |

### Process

Onboarding → Connecting accounts in Social Marketing → Writing & scheduling → Revision (5-business day window) → Reporting → Strategy call (if purchased).

:::tip Required Products
Task Manager and Social Marketing. Timeline: SMB hears from us within first week of month; posts prepared within 10 business days; approval by 15th.
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⭐</span>Reviews Management</div>

Our Marketing Experts are trained to respond to reviews—positive or negative—with unique responses that address the customer's comments. We can also help businesses get more reviews from existing customers.

### Services

- **Review Responses:** We respond to both positive and negative reviews on behalf of the business. Positive reviews: respond immediately. Negative reviews: we send responses for approval via email.
- **Review Requests:** Provide a list of customer contact info; we send email or SMS to request reviews.

### Benefits

| For You | For Your Clients |
|---------|------------------|
| Focus on selling while we respond | Build customer loyalty and humanize your brand |
| Trust proven processes for positive and negative | Turn happy customers into brand advocates |
| Full integration with Vendasta platform | Turn negative reviews into opportunities |

### Process

Onboarding → Connecting review sites → Strategy (language, response style) → Monitoring via Reputation Management → Responses → Reporting (Reputation Management, Executive Report).

:::tip Required Products
Reputation Management for Review Responses. Customer Voice for Review Requests. Task Manager. Sites: Facebook, Google, Bing, Yelp, Booking.com, TripAdvisor (not Glassdoor). Options: Up to 10 or Unlimited.
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📢</span>Digital Advertising</div>

Vendasta's advertising experts build, run, and manage campaigns on Google, Bing, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Display. They can also set up call tracking, build landing pages, and create banners.

### Services

- **Search Campaign:** Serve ads when customers search for services or products.
- **Social Ads:** Strengthen social presence, find customers, drive conversions.
- **Display Campaign:** Visually appealing banners with client brand.
- **Dynamic Facebook Auto Campaign:** Display dealership inventory to intent-based audiences.
- **YouTube Campaign:** Target customers seeking information or willing to interact with the brand.

### Benefits

| For You | For Your Clients |
|---------|------------------|
| Grow by selling advertising services | Reach potential customers with targeted campaigns |
| Get a team of experts in digital advertising | Increase brand awareness and drive sales |
| Complement your portfolio | Get more phone calls |

### Process

Purchase (order form: budget, audience, goals) → Setup & Launch (keyword research, tracking, audit) → Optimization → Reporting (Advertising Intelligence, Task Manager).

:::tip Required Products
Task Manager only. Reporting: Advertising Intelligence in Business App. Recommend 3+ months for optimized results.
:::
