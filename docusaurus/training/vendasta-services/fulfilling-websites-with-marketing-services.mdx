## Step 1: Understand scoping and fulfillment (4 min)

Effective website project scoping aligns client goals with deliverables. Understanding fulfillment processes helps you set expectations and deliver results.

### What effective scoping means

Scoping is the process of defining project scope, deliverables, timelines, and expectations before work begins. It ensures alignment with client goals and prevents misunderstandings. Good scoping leads to smoother fulfillment and happier clients.

### Fulfillment processes

Fulfillment is how Marketing Services delivers website projects. Learn the workflows—from onboarding calls to content creation forms to project tracking. These processes apply to websites and beyond; the insights empower you to craft actionable plans for any website success.

:::tip In Practice
A partner receives a website request from a client. Before ordering, they use the Website Content & Creation Form to gather requirements and the Website Quote request to get pricing. On the project onboard call, they share the gathered details. The project runs smoothly because scoping was done upfront.
:::

## Step 4: Know your contacts (5 min)

Know who to contact for what. Different teams handle different needs.

### Contact Marketing Services

For questions about Marketing Services products, fulfillment, active projects, etc.

- **Email:** marketingservices@yourdigitalagents.com
- **Phone:** 1-866-378-8031

### Contact Vendasta sales (partners only)

For sales-related questions: subscription inquiries, product recommendations, discussing prospects and opportunities.

- **How to find:** Contact your assigned Vendasta sales representative
- **For agencies on Customer Success team:** customersuccess@vendasta.com
- **Office hours:** Daily, Mondays–Fridays

### Contact onboarding

For onboarding assistance, reach your assigned Vendasta Onboarding Specialist.

- **Email:** onboarding@vendasta.com

### Contact support on demand (partners only, NOT white-labeled)

For platform-related questions: Partner Center, Business App, Social Marketing, Customer Voice (legacy feature, being phased out), etc.

- **Email:** support@vendasta.com
- **Or:** Partner Center → click the question mark icon (top right)

:::tip In Practice
A partner has a presale question about a complex eCommerce website. They email marketingservices@yourdigitalagents.com and CC their Vendasta Sales Rep. Marketing Services coordinates with the best-suited team members to assist. The partner gets a clear answer before committing to the project.
:::
