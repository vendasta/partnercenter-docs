<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📱</span>Digital Advertising</div>

Vendasta Services runs managed digital advertising through **MatchCraft**, the advertising technology Vendasta acquired in 2022. Our advertising experts can build, run, and manage campaigns across search, social, display, and video—and set up call tracking, build landing pages, and create banners for your clients.

### Two services to choose from

|  | MatchCraft Express Ads | MatchCraft Managed Ads Campaign |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Awareness, promotions, getting a name online | Growth, conversions, scaling across channels |
| **Setup style** | Do-it-for-me, via the AI Instant Launch tool | Do-it-with-me, specialist-built with an onboarding call |
| **Platforms** | Search only | Search, social, display, and video, by spend tier |
| **Typical budget** | $100–$500+/month | $500–$3,000+/month |

:::tip Which one to recommend
Lead with Express Ads for a client testing the water on a small budget who mainly wants to be seen—it launches in minutes and is not conversion-focused. Move to Managed Ads Campaign when the client wants measurable results across more than one channel.
:::

### Campaign types

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Search — Google and Bing** | Text ads served when people actively search for the client's services (pay-per-click) |
| **Display — Google** | Image banners across websites to build awareness and retarget (pay-per-impression) |
| **Performance Max — Google** | Goal-based campaigns running across Google's full inventory: Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Maps |
| **Meta — Facebook and Instagram** | Social ads for demand creation with precise audience targeting |
| **YouTube** | Video ads before, during, or after videos, for reach and awareness |
| **TikTok** | Short-form vertical video in the For You feed and other placements, for reach and awareness |

:::info Catalog and inventory ads
For clients with product inventory, such as auto dealerships, Meta delivery runs through a product catalog in Commerce Manager using an Advantage+ Catalog or Dynamic Ads Sales campaign. With Marketplace enabled as a sales channel, products are delivered across eligible placements including Facebook Marketplace, Facebook Feed, and Instagram Feed.
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📋</span>Setting Expectations</div>

When reselling Digital Advertising, set expectations with clients. Here's what you should know:

### Campaign launch

Timelines differ by service.

**Express Ads** launches in minutes through the AI Instant Launch tool, which builds a shell search campaign from the business name, address, and website. The client can edit and approve every detail before it goes live. Our team then customizes keywords, ad copy, and engagement features within **5 business days**, confirming by email what was changed.

**Managed Ads Campaign** is a staged process, not a single turnaround window. Each step depends on the client returning information or approving work.

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| Order review and confirmation email | Within 2 business days of the fulfillment form |
| Onboarding call | Bookable in as little as 1 business day |
| Campaign build for review | Within 5 business days of the onboarding call |
| Set live after approval | Within 5 days |

:::warning Set the expectation carefully
Quoting a single flat number for Managed Ads oversells it. The overall target is to launch within 30 days wherever possible, and campaigns are never set live without client approval. Missing collateral or a delayed approval pushes every downstream step. Reach out to the team if you have questions about the order form.
:::

### Billing

You'll be billed both the **setup fee** and **first month fee** upon activation of a new campaign. The anniversary date for following months is set one month from the campaign launch date. View purchases in the Billing page in Partner Center.

### Campaign performance

We constantly measure results and make optimizations. Typically, the first month is not the best—we're still learning about the audience and analyzing early results.

### Communications

Your assigned **Marketing Strategist** is your primary point of contact for anything related to your client's campaign. They'll direct you to advertising specialists if needed.
