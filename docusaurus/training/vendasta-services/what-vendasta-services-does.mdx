## The team behind the work

Vendasta Services is a team of AI and automation experts who help you choose, configure, and run the right tools, so day-to-day work gets easier. The same team delivers hands-on work on websites, social media, digital advertising, reviews, and listings, with clear processes and realistic timelines.

What that gives you is capacity you did not have to hire: practical AI setup, automations that save time, and professional services your clients can rely on. You keep the relationship and the retail price; the team does the work behind your brand.

## What we can do for you

Six service lines cover what we deliver today:

| Service line | What we deliver |
|---|---|
| **AI Workforce** | Configures and launches an AI employee, then keeps it tuned: eight roles, from AI Receptionist to AI Blogger |
| **Websites** | Express, Templated, and Accelerated Templated builds, plus hosting, imports, and Website Support+ |
| **Social Media Management** | Standard and Plus tiers: monthly posts and stories, custom images, Facebook post boosting, and SEO blogs |
| **Digital Advertising** | Express Ads and Managed Ads Campaign on MatchCraft, with creatives, tracking, and reporting |
| **Listings, claims, and optimization** | Listing Claim, Google Business Profile Optimization, and Local Listings Management across Google, Apple, and Bing |
| **Blog Posts** | Long-form SEO blogs, one time or monthly, published to WordPress Hosting or Accelerated Templated sites |

Each line has its own scope, requirements, and timelines. The [Vendasta Services documentation](/vendasta-services) holds the detail for every one of them, and you will pull from it as you scope real work.

:::tip Try it now
Open the [grey-labeled guides at servicesdocs.io](https://servicesdocs.io/). They carry the same service information with no brand or platform names anywhere in them, which makes them safe to send straight to a client who wants to know what the work involves.
:::

## Two brands, one conversation

Every service runs white label, so your client sees your brand on the platform, the products, and the results. The team fulfilling the work sits behind that, and you control how visible they are.

- **The default contact is unbranded.** The team reaches out from marketingservices@yourdigitalagents.com and 1-866-378-8031. Neither carries the Vendasta name, so both are safe in front of a client.
- **You can keep the team away from your clients entirely.** Put your own contact information on the order and fulfillment forms, and the team communicates only with you.
- **Digital Ads is partner-only by design.** Because campaign conversations cover budgets, wholesale cost, and your retail price, the team keeps that communication with you and never with the client. Every other service line can go direct to the customer if you choose it.

There is one exception worth preparing your client for. Several services need access granted under the name **Digital Agency**: Google Business Profile claims, Facebook access for review responses and social posting, and delegate access at a domain registrar. Those requests arrive from an @yourdigitalagents.com address or from an account named Digital Agency, and that name cannot be changed even when the rest of your communications are white labeled. Telling a client to expect it before you order keeps the handover smooth.
