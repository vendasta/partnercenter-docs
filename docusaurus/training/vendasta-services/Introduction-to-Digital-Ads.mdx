In this course, you'll learn about the benefits of digital advertising, types of campaigns, and how to pair the ad medium with the campaign objective. Vendasta's advertising experts can build, run, and manage campaigns on multiple platforms—and set up call tracking, landing pages, and banners for your clients.

:::tip Learning Goals
- Understand digital advertising and the types of campaigns available
- Know the key benefits of digital advertising for SMB clients
- Pair the right ad medium with campaign objectives: awareness, engagement, or conversion
- Set correct expectations for launch timelines, billing, and campaign performance
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📱</span>Digital Advertising</div>

Vendasta's advertising experts can build, run, and manage campaigns on multiple platforms including Google, Bing, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Display. They can also set up call tracking, build landing pages, and create banners for your clients.

### Campaign types

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Search Campaign** | Serve ads to potential customers when they're searching for services or products |
| **Social Ads** | Strengthen social presence, find more customers, drive conversions |
| **Display Campaign** | Visually appealing banners with client's brand in front of online customers |
| **Dynamic Facebook Auto** | Display dealership inventory to customers who've shown intent to buy |
| **YouTube Campaign** | Target customers seeking information or willing to interact with the brand |

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">✨</span>Benefits of Digital Ads</div>

- **Scale:** Digital advertising allows you to scale
- **Target:** Reach your ideal audience at the right time
- **Measure:** It's easy to track and measure ROI
- **Insights:** Gain insights on your audience

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🎯</span>Pairing Ad Medium With Objective</div>

1. **Identify the advertiser's objective**
2. **Common campaign objectives:** Awareness, Engagement, Conversion
3. **Pair the ad medium with the objective**

Different ad types suit different objectives. Search works well for conversion. Display and YouTube can drive awareness. Social supports engagement and conversion.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📋</span>Setting Expectations</div>

When reselling Digital Advertising, set expectations with clients. Here's what you should know:

### Campaign launch

**Turnaround time:** 10 business days to launch after we receive the completed order form, required access, and collateral. Reach out to our team if you have questions about the order form.

### Billing

You'll be billed both the **setup fee** and **first month fee** upon activation of a new campaign. The anniversary date for following months is set one month from the campaign launch date. View purchases in the Billing page in Partner Center.

### Campaign performance

We constantly measure results and make optimizations. Typically, the first month is not the best—we're still learning about the audience and analyzing early results.

### Communications

Your assigned **Marketing Strategist** is your primary point of contact for anything related to your client's campaign. They'll direct you to advertising specialists if needed.
