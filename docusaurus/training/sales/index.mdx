## Managing a team as well?

One more series episode is aimed at sales leaders rather than individual sellers, so it sits outside the five core steps: **[Become a more effective sales manager](/learn/sales/become-an-effective-sales-manager)** covers thirteen tactics on coaching, pipeline definition, and measuring a team honestly. Take it after the skill check, or skip it if you do not manage anyone yet.
