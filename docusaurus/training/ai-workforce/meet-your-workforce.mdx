## Meet the AI Workforce

The AI Workforce is a package of AI-powered agents that answer every lead, keep your reputation current, and keep your business visible online, automatically and around the clock. Think of it as a full-time office team running in the background while you are with clients.

You get five AI agents, local visibility tools, and automated marketing campaigns, all connected to a single inbox you can manage from your phone. And the team you hire here is the same one your clients get later, under your brand: everything it does for your business, it can do for theirs.

## The roster

In Partner Center, the team shows up as **AI Employees**: digital team members you configure to answer questions, capture leads, and do real work. Four come pre-built, ready to work out of the box and tailored to the business they serve:

| AI Employee | The job |
|---|---|
| **Chat Receptionist** | Greets visitors, answers questions from its knowledge, and captures every conversation as a lead, around the clock |
| **Voice Receptionist** | Answers the phone: covers after hours, rescues missed calls, and books while the line would have rung out |
| **Reputation Specialist** | Responds to reviews using your rules and knowledge, and answers questions about reviews and NPS in plain conversation |
| **Sales Assistant** | Keeps the CRM up to date from meetings and records, creates activities, and answers questions about the pipeline |

The AI Receptionist you just met arrives as these two receptionist employees, one for chat and one for voice. The Social Media Manager and AI Blogger, the two agents keeping your pages and website active, arrive with Social AI.

Beyond the pre-built, you can build **Custom AI Employees** from a blank slate: you define the purpose, capabilities, knowledge, and channels. Partners have built inside sales representatives, data analysts, support employees, and HR coordinators this way; the [custom builds are covered in the lab](/learn/ai-workforce/custom-employee-lab) later in this path.

</LessonSection>

## Where each one lives

The rule from the ecosystem carries straight over: **an AI Employee lives in the workspace of the business it serves.** Your receptionist, answering for your business, lives in your Partner Center. A client's receptionist lives in their Business App, powered by their business knowledge. Pick the workspace first, and channels, knowledge, and captured leads all land where you expect.

:::tip Try it now
Open **AI** in Partner Center. The employees you see there are yours, serving your business. Every client gets their own roster inside their Business App.
:::

## What the plans include

Two gates are worth knowing before you plan a rollout. The **Starter** plan includes the Chat Receptionist; the **Professional** plan and above unlock the Voice Receptionist and social channels (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) as well. The **Sales Assistant** is part of CRM AI and comes with a CRM AI Standard or Pro subscription. When you are scoping a client, [this guide breaks down what each plan includes](/ai/ai-workforce/ai-receptionist-features-by-plan).
