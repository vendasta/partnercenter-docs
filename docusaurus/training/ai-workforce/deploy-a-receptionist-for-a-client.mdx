## Open their workspace from yours

The receptionist you built belongs to your business. Your client's receptionist is a separate employee, living on their account, answering with their knowledge and their number. You configure it from their workspace.

Go to `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`, open the account, then select `Open in` → `Business App`. Inside their workspace, `AI` → `Workforce` lists the employees their editions cover.

The Configure page there is the page you have been working on for four steps. On the left: the photo, the `Name` field, the `Autonomy` level, and a `View prompt` button. On the right, in order: **Triggers**, **Profile** holding the **Role**, **Capabilities**, **Knowledge sources**, and **Automations**.

<LabCallout title="reach the client's receptionist">
Go to `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Manage Accounts`.

An employee the client's editions do not cover shows a single `Upgrade to gain access` button in place of `Chat` and `Configure`. That is the edition gate, and it is the fastest way to see what they bought.

## Make it theirs

Three things change between your receptionist and your client's. Everything else follows the pattern you already know.

- **The knowledge sources.** Their website, their price list, their policies. The **Business Profile** source is already attached and answers from their business details.
- **The phone number.** Theirs, on their account.
- **The website the widget goes on.** Theirs, which means a second embed on a site you may not control.

The **Role** carries your craft, not your content. Write theirs in their words, about their business, with the same shape you used for your own.

<LabCallout title="give the receptionist the client's knowledge">
Stay on the client's Configure page.

<LabChecklist
  storageKey="deploy-for-client-lab-2"
  steps={[

    <>Add their price list, so the answers on price come from a source you can correct.</>,

/>
</LabCallout>

Then run the ten-question audit on their business, in `Chat`, the way you ran it on yours. The questions change because their customers ask different things.

</LessonSection>

## Register their texting number

For a business in the United States to send SMS, carriers require registration first, and that registration is called A2P 10DLC. Calls and web chat work right away, and SMS begins only once registration is approved, so start it during onboarding. A client texting only within Canada has nothing to register.

<LabCallout title="start a client's SMS registration">
Go to that client's `Business App` → `Administration` → `Conversation`.

<LabChecklist
  storageKey="put-a-receptionist-lab-1"
  steps={[
    <>Find the SMS registration card. It shows a <strong>Not submitted</strong> status until the business completes it.</>,
    <>Select <code>Register now</code> to open the form.</>,
    <>Save your progress and stop there if you are missing any of the business's legal details. Drafts hold, so nothing you have entered is lost.</>,
  ]}
  confirm={<>The registration is open with a draft saved, and you know what you still need from the client to submit it.</>}
/>
</LabCallout>

The form asks for the legal business details the carriers require. Fill it in one sitting once you have them. [This guide covers every field in US SMS registration](/business-app/conversations/sms/us-businesses-sms-registration).

## Prove it works on their account

Configured is not the same as working. Three checks prove the receptionist before the client meets it, and each one depends on what they bought.

:::info What their edition covers
Voice needs Conversations AI Premium, and it is available in the United States and Canada. Missed-call text-back needs Conversations AI Pro, and it needs call forwarding set on the client's real line. Web chat works on every edition.
:::

<LabCallout title="run the go-live test">
Go to the client's account, with a mobile phone in your hand.

## Hand it over

Thirty minutes with the client, and they leave knowing where to look. Open `Conversations` and show them where every lead lands. Play back the test call so they hear how their receptionist sounds. Confirm who gets notified, and how. Then take questions.

[Walk your client's first experience](/learn/getting-started/walk-your-clients-first-experience) covers what they saw the day their account opened.
