**Most partners start here:** new to Vendasta, begin with [The Vendasta story](/learn/getting-started/the-vendasta-story). Already established, jump to [Meet your AI Workforce](/learn/ai-workforce/meet-your-workforce).

## How these paths work

Paths are sequenced - each lesson builds on the one before and hands off to the next. Libraries are drop-in references you can visit in any order. Lessons link to [Documentation](/) for step-by-step instructions, so you always learn the why here and click through for the how.
