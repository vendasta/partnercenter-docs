Welcome to Vendasta Learn.

Learn takes you from a new Partner Center account to an AI Workforce you run for your own business and sell to your clients. It is one journey in six paths, and each path leaves work in place: a platform that carries your brand and collects payment, an AI Employee answering for a real business, a pitch you have rehearsed against a live objection, an automation that runs when nobody is in the conversation.

Learn is not the documentation. The Documentation tab is where you go with a task in hand: how a setting works, what a field means, which edition carries a feature. Learn is where you go when you want to know what to do next and in what order. Every step here is a sequence of actions in your own Partner Center, with the documentation linked at the moment a task needs it.

The order matters, and it is built on one idea: you run it before you sell it. You set up the platform for your own business before you bring in a client. You hire an AI Receptionist for your own front desk before you deploy one for a client. You meet the whole roster before you pitch it. By the time you are in a sales conversation, every claim you make is something you have already watched work.

## How to use Learn

Every path opens with an overview that lists its steps, the time it takes, and what you will be able to do at the end. Steps carry **Try it now** actions: short tasks in your own Partner Center that leave the result in place. Each path ends with a skill check of scenario questions drawn from every step. Take it as many times as you like; a wrong answer points you at the step to revisit. The last step of each path hands off to the next one.

Take the six paths in order the first time through. After that, come back to any step as a reference.

**Before you begin.** Get set up assumes three things, and every path after it builds on that setup:

- **Partner Center admin access.** You will change settings in `Administration` that only an admin can reach.
- **A paid subscription.** Several steps activate products on your own account or on a client's.
- **Access to your domain's DNS settings.** Connecting your email domain adds records at your DNS host.

Partner Center, at [partners.vendasta.com](https://partners.vendasta.com), is your administration workspace and where most steps happen. Business App is what your clients use; some steps open a client's Business App from Partner Center so you can configure what they will see.

## Connect your own systems

One build, end to end: an AI Employee for a home-services client that books jobs on a real calendar, saves every lead to the CRM, and checks the weather before it confirms outdoor work. Three steps need no code at all: the first, the automation handoff, and autopilot. The rest reach outside the platform with a custom tool, a webhook, a scoped API token, and a booking into a system your client already runs.

<p className="learn-journey__meta">10 steps · Intermediate to Advanced · About 2 hours 10 minutes</p>

1. [Start with what the platform already does](/learn/builder/start-with-what-exists): what makes a system connectable, the ladder from a setting to custom code, and the one job left over.
2. [Build the doorway](/learn/builder/build-the-doorway): a working call becomes a custom tool, and a prompt gives it judgment.
3. [Watch it work](/learn/builder/watch-it-work): test where it runs, read what it did, and isolate a failing call.
4. [Hand off to the automation](/learn/builder/hand-off-to-the-automation): trigger scope, guarding a trigger, and the follow-through worker.
5. [Put your workforce on autopilot](/learn/builder/autopilot): automations that run when nobody is in the conversation.
6. [Wire it to your own systems](/learn/builder/wire-it-to-your-systems): send data out with a webhook and start an automation from outside.
7. [Call the API yourself](/learn/builder/call-the-api-yourself): a real call against your own data, in the browser, with no code.
8. [Book into an outside system](/learn/builder/book-into-an-outside-system): a booking that lands in the scheduling system your client already runs.
9. [Where the platform ends](/learn/builder/where-the-platform-ends): the custom-code line, handing it off well, and productizing what you built.
10. [Connect your own systems skill check](/learn/builder/wire-to-act-skill-check): twelve scenario questions covering the whole path.

## Three ways to get the work done

Once a client is live, the work of running their website, social calendar, ads, listings, and AI Employees falls into three buckets. You can run it yourself with AI Employees and automations, which is what these paths teach. You can bring in Vendasta Services to set up an AI Employee or a campaign with you. Or you can hand a service line over entirely and have Vendasta Services run it behind your brand. [The Vendasta Services documentation](/vendasta-services) covers each service line, what to have ready, and how the work runs once it is underway.
