Welcome to Vendasta Learn. Six paths take you from a new Partner Center account to an AI Workforce you run for your own business and sell to your clients. You set up the platform, set up your first AI Employee, meet the rest of the roster, learn how to sell it, and connect it to the systems you and your clients already run.

Learn is not the documentation. The Documentation tab is where you go with a task in hand: how a setting works, what a field means, which edition carries a feature. Learn is where you go for what to do next and in what order. The order rests on one idea: you run it before you sell it. You hire an AI Receptionist for your own front desk before you deploy one for a client, so every claim you make in a sales conversation is something you have already watched work.

## How to use Learn

Take the six paths in order the first time through, then come back to any step as a reference. Every path opens with an overview, every step carries a **Try it now** action in your own Partner Center, and every path ends with a skill check of scenario questions you can retake freely. The last step of each path hands off to the next one.

:::info Before you begin
Get set up assumes **Partner Center admin access**, **a paid subscription**, and **access to your domain's DNS settings**. Partner Center, at [partners.vendasta.com](https://partners.vendasta.com), is where most steps happen; some steps open a client's Business App from there so you can configure what they will see.
:::

## Three ways to get the work done

Once a client is live, you can run their website, social calendar, ads, listings, and AI Employees yourself with AI Employees and automations, which is what these paths teach. You can bring in Vendasta Services to set up an AI Employee or a campaign with you. Or you can hand a service line over entirely and have Vendasta Services run it behind your brand. [The Vendasta Services documentation](/vendasta-services) covers each service line and how the work runs.

## Where to get help

The AI assistant behind the sparkle icon in Partner Center answers how-do-I questions from this documentation. The chat in the bottom left corner reaches a person on your account team. [Where to get help](/learn/getting-started/where-to-get-help) in Get set up walks through every channel and when to use each.
