Vendasta Learn is a set of learning paths that take you from a new Partner Center account to selling and running an AI Workforce for your clients. Each path is a sequence of short steps with something to do in the platform, and you finish each one with work in place: a configured platform, an AI Employee answering for a real business, a pitch you have rehearsed, an automation that runs without you.

## The journey, in order

The paths build on each other. Take them top to bottom the first time through, then return to any step as a reference.

## Before you begin

Get set up assumes three things, and every path after it builds on that setup:

- **Partner Center admin access.** You will change settings in `Administration` that only an admin can reach.
- **A paid subscription.** Several steps activate products on your own account or on a client's.
- **Access to your domain's DNS settings.** Connecting your email domain adds records at your DNS host.

:::info Where you will be working
Partner Center, at [partners.vendasta.com](https://partners.vendasta.com), is your administration workspace and where most steps happen. Business App is what your clients use; some steps open a client's Business App from Partner Center so you can configure what they will see.
:::

## How the paths work

Every path opens with an overview that lists its steps, the time it takes, and what you will be able to do at the end. Steps carry **Try it now** actions: short tasks in your own Partner Center that leave the result in place. Each path ends with a skill check of scenario questions drawn from every step. Take it as many times as you like; a wrong answer points you at the step to revisit.

The last step of each path hands off to the next one, so once you start you can keep going without coming back here.

## Three ways to get the work done

Once a client is live, the work of running their website, social calendar, ads, listings, and AI Employees falls into three buckets. You can run it yourself with AI Employees and automations, which is what these paths teach. You can bring in Vendasta Services to set up an AI Employee or a campaign with you. Or you can hand a service line over entirely and have Vendasta Services run it behind your brand. [The Vendasta Services documentation](/vendasta-services) covers each service line, what to have ready, and how the work runs once it is underway.

## Where to get help

The AI assistant behind the sparkle icon in Partner Center answers how-do-I questions from this documentation. The chat in the bottom left corner reaches a person on your account team. [Where to get help](/learn/getting-started/where-to-get-help) in Get set up walks through every channel and when to use each.
