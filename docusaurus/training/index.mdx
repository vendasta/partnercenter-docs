Explore learning paths to get started in Partner Center, establish your AI workforce, and sharpen your sales craft.

:::info Your learning guides
In each of these learning paths, you will find a difficulty tag (**Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced**) that will let you know what level you are learning at, a **Lab** tag when you will go into the platform and build something yourself, and a **Video** tag when a step includes multimedia. You will also see the estimated time, anything required before you start, and the outcomes you will leave with.
:::

## How these paths work

Learning paths are sequenced: each step builds on the one before and hands off to the next. Libraries are drop-in references you can visit in any order.
