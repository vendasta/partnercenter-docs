## Your asset library, ready to rebrand

Speed to market is an asset problem before it is a skill problem, and every kit is already built: pitch decks, solution one-pagers, and a library of images for your own marketing materials. All of it is unbranded and editable, so it takes your logo, your proof point, and your price.

The order of use matters: **lead with the package to open the conversation, then pull the individual employee kit when your prospect wants to go deeper.** The package frames the outcome; the employee kit answers the "how does that part work" questions without turning the meeting into a product tour.

## The proposal that closes

A proposal is a structure, not a document, and the most common reason proposals do not close is leading with your solution. The proposal video teaches the Three Baskets framework: quantify the cost of inaction so the price feels small before you ever mention it, then present entry versus full-build pricing, with the one ROI line that reframes every pricing conversation. It works for any niche, at any price point.

## Ask for the decision

They have seen the demo, heard their own numbers, and read the proposal. The close is a question, and it has three moments. Flip each one:

<IconFlipCardGrid>
  <IconFlipCard
    gradient="blue"
    front="Direct close"
    back={'"Based on what you have seen, does this make sense to get started? I can have this running for you within a week." Ask it plainly, then stop talking.'}
  />
  <IconFlipCard
    gradient="green"
    front="They want time"
    back={'"I will send you the one-pager and the ROI numbers we ran today. Can we get 15 minutes on the calendar for a specific day to make a decision?" Always leave with a next step booked.'}
  />
  <IconFlipCard
    gradient="purple"
    front="After the yes"
    back="Confirm the plan, set expectations on setup time, and send your intake form the same day. The deployment clock starts now, and the next step in this path takes it from here."
  />
</IconFlipCardGrid>

A meeting that ends without a booked next step is a deal cooling on its own schedule. The specific day on the calendar is what keeps it yours.
