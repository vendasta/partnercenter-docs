Your client gets five AI agents, local visibility tools, and automated marketing campaigns, all connected to a single inbox they can manage from their phone.

## Why you sell the package, not the pieces

Your prospects do not buy AI. They buy more leads captured, faster response times, and lower payroll, and the offer that delivers those outcomes whole is the package. The numbers say the package is also how you keep the client:

## Where the packages live

*You are here: Partner Center. Everything in this section happens in your workspace.*

The vertical packages are built and waiting in the [Marketplace](/marketplace), under `Packages` → `Recommended Packages`: each one bundles the Conversations, Reputation, Social, CRM, and Local SEO products its vertical needs, with recommended retail pricing and a setup fee preset. Open one to review its contents and wholesale cost, then click `Add To Store` to make it yours. Adjust the retail price to your market and the offer is ready to sell.

Those bundled products are also what unlock the AI agents your pitch promises. There is no agent for sale on its own: the product carries it, and the edition determines which agent your client actually gets. Quote the edition that carries the agent you promised.

| AI Employee | Product that unlocks it | Edition that carries it |
|---|---|---|
| AI Receptionist, chat | Conversations AI | Every edition |
| AI Receptionist, voice | Conversations AI | Premium; SMS text-back arrives on Pro |
| Reputation Specialist | Reputation Management | Premium |
| Social Media Manager | Social Marketing (Social AI) | Premium, together with AI Blogger |
| AI Blogger | Social Marketing (Social AI) | Premium, together with Social Media Manager |
| AI Sales Assistant | CRM AI | Standard or Pro; the free CRM does not include it |
| AI Search Specialist | Local SEO / Listing Builder | Early access |

The AI Search Specialist is in early access. Mention it as what is coming, not as something you can turn on today.

When a client later wants another employee, [this guide covers the upgrade path](/ai/ai-workforce/upgrading-ai-employees).

## One opening line, tuned to the business

Every strong opener is the same move: name the outcome the owner already wants, phrase it as a question they answer yes to, and keep the technology out of it. The yes comes first; AI comes after.

The line below fits any local business, because the problems underneath it are universal: missed leads, slow response, an online presence that goes quiet after hours. The industry versions sharpen the same line when your prospect maps cleanly to one. Click through to find your client's lane:

<div className="learn-tabs">
<Tabs>
<TabItem value="general" label="Any business">

After 5pm, most businesses go dark, and that is when leads slip away. **Keep capturing after hours.**

The default line. If your prospect does not map cleanly to an industry below, this is the one, and the conversation it opens is identical.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="home" label="Home services">

Contractors, HVAC, plumbers, electricians.

Homeowners book the first business that picks up. **Be that business, every time.**

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="pet" label="Pet services">

Groomers, boarding, veterinarians.

Pet parents do not leave messages. **Be the first one they reach.**

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="auto" label="Automotive">

Repair shops, dealerships, tire and oil centers.

Missed calls peak during the busiest hours. **Capture every inquiry even when the team is slammed.**

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="personal" label="Personal services">

Salons, spas, massage and wellness clinics.

While they are with a client, the next caller books with whoever answers. **Fill the book without missing a call.**

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="broker" label="Agents and brokers">

Real estate, insurance, mortgage.

Nearly half of real estate leads go to whoever responds first. **Never let an inquiry go unanswered.**

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="health" label="Health and medical">

Dental, chiropractic, physical therapy.

Patients call whoever picks up first. **Be the office that always answers.**

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
</div>
