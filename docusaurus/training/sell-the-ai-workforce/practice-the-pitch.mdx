## The rehearsal line

**Vendasta Pitch Partner: 1 (239) 695-6807.** Call it, pick your prospect's vertical, and pitch a voice AI playing a busy, skeptical small-business owner. It pushes back the way real owners do. You handle two objections, ask for the close, and the moment you hang up, you get a spoken coaching debrief on what landed and what to sharpen.

A rehearsal costs a phone call. A real prospect costs a relationship. Spend the phone call.

## Run it as a rep, not a chat

The call rewards preparation the same way a real one does. Before you dial:

1. **Pick the vertical** you chose for your opening line, and have that line ready to say first.
2. **Bring a number.** Run the ROI calculator for an imaginary business in that vertical, so when the owner pushes on price you have a figure to anchor to.
3. **Decide your close** before the call starts: the specific next step you will ask for, in one sentence.

During the call, sell the outcome, not the technology; when the objections come, treat them as the live version of the ones you already know. After the debrief, write down the single sharpest piece of feedback and make it the one thing you fix on the next dial.

:::tip Try it now
Dial 1 (239) 695-6807 right now, before the opening line goes cold. One full pitch, two objections, ask for the close, listen to the debrief. Then call again and fix the one thing it flagged.
:::

## Make it a habit, not a step

The line does not expire when this path ends. One rehearsal call before every real meeting is the habit worth installing: same vertical as the meeting, same numbers, same close. Three minutes of practice while the coffee brews, and you walk into the real call already warm.
