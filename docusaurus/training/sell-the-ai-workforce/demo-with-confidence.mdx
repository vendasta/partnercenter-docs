## The five to lead with, and the three to grow into

An AI Employee is easiest to sell when you describe it by who it serves and what it captures, never by its feature list. Five come pre-configured, and together they cover the front desk, the reputation, the content, and the follow-up a local business is already paying someone to do, or losing revenue for not doing:

## The live demo: one URL, zero prep

The strongest demo is not a slide, it is the prospect's own business answering its own phone. The demo play: build a live AI demo on the prospect's actual website, in the meeting, in under five minutes. Watch it done on a real business the presenter had never worked with before:

The video covers the exact live interaction that moves a prospect from "I need to think about it" to "let's do it." It also covers the moment a demo exposes an outdated website. That is not a dead end, it is a second line on the proposal: the website rebuild becomes part of the package.

:::tip Try it now
Run the play on yourself first: build the demo on your own website and have the conversation you would want a prospect to have. When it impresses you, it is ready for a meeting.
:::
