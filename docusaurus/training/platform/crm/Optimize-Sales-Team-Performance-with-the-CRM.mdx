Optimize your sales team's performance with Vendasta's CRM. This course focuses on setting up sales forecasting, monitoring activities, and using advanced reporting tools to ensure your team meets their goals and drives revenue growth.

:::tip Learning Goals
- Set up sales goals and forecast future revenue
- Monitor activities against goals and interpret premium reports
- Access pipeline trends and quality data using advanced reporting tools
:::

Accurate sales forecasts help you budget effectively and allocate resources across departments like sales, marketing, and administration. Use Vendasta's CRM to forecast future revenue and plan with confidence.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🎯</span>Set Individual and Sales Team Goals</div>

Forecasting sales can be daunting. With Goals, you can confidently:

- **Set clear sales targets** — Define measurable revenue objectives for teams and individual sales members
- **Monitor trajectory** — Track the trajectory of these targets over time
- **Foster a data-driven culture** — Set measurable revenue objectives both for teams and individual sales members
- **Optimize strategies** — Adjust sales strategies based on tracked performance

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📋</span>Monitor Sales Activity to Close More Leads</div>

### Activity feed

Monitor sales activity to ensure your sales team is taking all the right steps to close those top quality leads with the centralized activity feed. Get an overview of what your team is up to with a stream of all sales activities, updated in real time.

### Customize your feed

View, search, and filter this master list to focus in on activities by type, salesperson, Company, and more.

For instance, create a view to quickly check in on all the outbound calls your sales team has made by adding filters for activity type and call direction, selecting **Create view** from the view dropdown, and naming your view outbound calls.

:::tip Automatic Tracking
Vendasta makes it simple to monitor progress towards goals by automatically tracking the activities that contribute to these goals as a part of your daily work.
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📊</span>Track Your Goals Using Premium Reports</div>

### Salesperson & sales team activity target tracker

Showcases the revenue salespeople have closed in the month in comparison to their monthly targets, along with a pipeline analysis to show how to achieve revenue goals for the current month and the next.

### Sales manager metrics report

Ensure you and your team are taking the actions required to move opportunities through your pipeline. Monitor things like how many emails your team is sending or how many meetings you are having compared to the activity targets you set in goals.
