Uncover the marketing capabilities of Vendasta's CRM to generate and nurture leads effectively. This course covers everything from creating forms and setting up the webchat to running targeted email campaigns, ensuring you can attract and engage prospects seamlessly.

:::tip Learning Goals
- Create, style, and embed forms to capture lead information
- Set up the AI-assisted webchat widget for real-time engagement
- Develop and send email campaigns, monitor lead scores, and understand email deliverability and sender reputation
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📝</span>Create a Form in the Vendasta CRM</div>

Create, style, and embed forms in Vendasta's CRM to capture lead information efficiently. The form is built to integrate easily with reCaptcha, which helps protect your form from spam and ensure you're only dealing with genuine leads, maintaining the quality of your marketing data.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⚡</span>Automate Your Lead Generation</div>

### Send a follow-up email when a form is submitted

This automation triggers a campaign to be sent to a contact every time a new contact is created from a form submission.

To use this automation, you'll need to have a published email campaign that has been activated. Check out our recommended campaigns to find the one that will work best for you or build your own.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">💬</span>Set Up the Webchat</div>

### Automatically capture lead information

The AI will automatically work to capture contact information and add it to the CRM as a new contact. If you want to use the AI assistant as a help-bot and not as a lead capture tool, you can uncheck the **capture lead information** box to disable this feature.

In Partner Center, messages exchanged with customers can be reviewed in the **Inbox Messages** section.

### Add additional knowledge for the AI assistant

You can give your AI assistant additional knowledge from any source. Click **Add Knowledge** and choose between adding your own custom text knowledge (such as frequently asked questions) or enter a website address to scrape the website for business information.

:::tip Full Walkthrough
For a complete walkthrough of the webchat setup, visit the Vendasta Support center.
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📧</span>Send a Campaign</div>

### Authenticate your custom email domain

To update the sender address to use your custom domain instead of smblogin.com, you'll first need to update the SPF record in your Domain Name System (DNS). Log in to your DNS provider (e.g., GoDaddy if you bought your domain there) to authenticate your domain.

It's important to update your DNS before adding your custom email address. Until you update your SPF records, there's a high chance your emails will land in the spam folder. This applies even if you sign up for Vendasta with a custom-domain email address. Grey-label your sender address until you own a custom domain and update the SPF records.

You can only update the sender address with a custom email. Free email addresses (gmail.com, outlook.com, yahoo.com) will not save.

Authenticating your custom domain allows you to build a reputation under your brand's domain, minimizing any negative impacts from other senders on the shared domain.

### Personalize with dynamic content

Personalizing emails with dynamic content allows you to tailor your messages, making them more engaging and informative.

### Improve your email deliverability

| Best Practice | Why It Matters |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Write trustworthy subject lines** | Subject lines should convey real value. Aggressive statements like "Urgent" or "Act now" are associated with spam and can affect whether your email lands in the inbox at all. |
| **Keep your email list clean** | Regularly remove inactive contacts to maintain list health and improve deliverability. |
| **Avoid spam traps** | Spam traps are addresses created by providers to catch unwanted senders. Maintain a list of active users and never purchase an email list. |
| **Send emails your contacts love** | High open and click rates show valuable content. Monitor performance to discover what appeals and who to focus on in future campaigns. |
