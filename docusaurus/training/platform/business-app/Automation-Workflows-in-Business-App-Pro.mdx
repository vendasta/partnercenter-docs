<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⚙️</span>Automation for Local Businesses</div>

Automation helps local businesses streamline operations, enhance customer relationships, and drive sales without constant manual effort. Workflows can connect disparate tools, trigger actions based on events, and keep customers engaged at the right moments.

### Why automate?

Reduce repetitive tasks, ensure consistent follow-up, and scale customer engagement. Automation lets you focus on high-value work while the system handles routine touchpoints.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🔌</span>Zapier and Business App Pro</div>

Zapier is a third-party tool that connects external systems with Business App Pro. It enables workflows that would otherwise require manual data entry or custom development.

### How Zapier works

Zapier sends data—such as contact details—from your client's other software tools into the Business App Pro CRM. Instead of manually inputting customer details, data flows automatically when events occur in connected apps (e.g., a new booking, form submission, or sale).

### Use cases

Sync leads from booking systems, forms, or e-commerce into Business App Pro. Trigger follow-up messages, add contacts to pipelines, or kick off review request sequences—all from a single automation.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⭐</span>Reputation Management Premium and Review Requests</div>

To automate review requests in Business App Pro, Reputation Management Premium must be activated on the account.

### "send a review request" action

The "Send a review request" action in Business App Pro automations only works when Reputation Management Premium is active. Without it, the action will not function. This ensures clients receive the full value of automated review solicitation—getting the right requests to the right customers at the right time.

### Enhancing client engagement

Automated review solicitation keeps your clients' reputation efforts consistent. Trigger review requests after completed jobs, purchases, or positive interactions—without manual follow-up.
