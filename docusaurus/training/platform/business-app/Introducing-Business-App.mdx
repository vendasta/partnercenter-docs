Business App is the one-stop dashboard for your local business clients. Your white-labeled, customizable, client-facing app lets you communicate, provide one-click solutions, and deliver instant proof-of-performance all in one place. Learn core features and how to enhance customer relationships with ease.

:::tip Who it's for
Business App is **for your clients**. You configure it via Partner Center and Sales & Success Center, but the app itself serves your clients.
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📱</span>What Business App Provides</div>

Business App serves your clients the way they want to be served—allowing them to try a variety of freemium solutions. This product-led process works for you because they discover instant value, and you position a clear path from freemium to paying clients.

### Key Values (Free Inside Business App)

Single-sign-on portal. Products, recommendations, learning tools, proof-of-performance reporting, digital marketing tools, customer communication, schedule appointments/meetings, and more. Your app positions you as the **trusted expert**.

### Connect Google and Facebook First

Upon connection of **Google and Facebook**, your clients immediately unlock many key features: digital solutions and primary data reporting. Exploring all account connection opportunities is good practice, but Google My Business and Facebook are vital upon entry.

### Out of the Box

A solution set of freemium products that follow the customer journey and showcase a path to upsell. Single sign-on app branded with your logo. Access to your Store and all products they can enable and purchase. Free SMS number for customer communication. Reporting analytics via the Executive Report. All unlocked by connecting clients to their customized Business App.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🛒</span>The Value of Business App for You</div>

### Position Your Brand

Positioning your brand, products, and platform is easy with the Business App dashboard. You offer a full set of solutions (freemium to premium) and an all-encompassing experience—position it as such. Consider the whole client experience: their needs paired with everything they need in one place.

### Enable Ecommerce

Easily enable clients to sell products and services online through an easy-to-build ecommerce storefront—for free. An interactive customer journey map lets clients click through each stage and find solutions they might not be looking for.

### Drive Sales with Product-Led Growth

Business App operates on **product-led growth**. Show value first (freemium product to try), demonstrate the solution, then offer the premium version. Design your own customer upgrade path.

### Everything Under Your Brand

Everything is branded as your business. No mention of Vendasta. Showcase yourself as the trusted expert for all your clients' needs. Brand fulfillment of solutions plus your own—all in one place.

### Retain with Proof-of-Performance

Prove value month after month with automatic, detailed reporting. Send the Executive Report weekly or monthly—telling the story of your hard work, all automated within the platform.
