<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📍</span>Overview</div>

Local Listings Management tools ensure your clients' business information is accurate and consistent wherever customers search.

### The digital ecosystem

Business information appears across online sites, mobile apps, voice search, and GPS navigation systems. There is often a misconception among business owners that their information is accurate across listing sources—it usually isn't without management.

### The value proposition

Local Listings Management simplifies the complex world of search for your clients while providing them immediate value. Set up and manage your listings all from one place.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🖥️</span>Dashboard & Setup</div>

The Local SEO dashboard gives you a single place to set up and manage listings. Click on the dashboard features to explore access points for editing and updating important listings information.

### Hours tab

The hours tab allows you to set both regular and special hours. You can configure your regular operating schedule and add special hours for holidays, events, or seasonal changes.

### Where to make updates

When you or your client need to update business information in your platform, go to **Business Profile**. This is where you make updates that sync across sources. (Not Overview page, My Listing, or Listing Sync for core edits.)

### Dashboard features

The dashboard provides access points for editing and updating important listings information—all from one user-friendly interface.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📊</span>Customer Activity Tracking</div>

With Local SEO you can provide clients with a glimpse into their customer activity by tracking key metrics—all on one dashboard.

### What you can track

- **How customers find their listings** — Which channels and sources drive discovery
- **Where customers find them on Google** — Visibility across Google surfaces
- **What customers do once they find their listing** — Clicks, calls, direction requests, website visits

All of this data is available on one user-friendly dashboard, giving clients actionable insights into their local presence.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📦</span>Product Tiers</div>

A breakdown of features across the three listings products: Local SEO, Listing Distribution, and Listing Sync Pro.

### Features all tiers include

| Feature | Local SEO | Listing Distribution | Listing Sync Pro |
|--------|:---------:|:--------------------:|:-----------------:|
| Free sync to four listing sources (Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter) | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| My Listing (Free landing page) | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Google Business Profile insights | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Update and edit business information to sync across sources from one platform | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Sync status reporting | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |

### Listing Distribution adds

**Business data sync to the three primary data aggregators** — Data Axle, Neustar Localeze, and Foursquare. Listing Distribution sends business data directly to these aggregators, which feed thousands of downstream directories and apps.

### Listing Sync Pro adds

- **Protection from third party changes** — Safeguard your clients' data from unauthorized edits
- **Publish business data almost instantaneously** to a list of high traffic sources
- **Control of how you appear online** — More control over presentation
- **Ability to manage duplicate suppression** — Reduce duplicate or incorrect listings

### Free sources (local SEO)

The freemium version of Local SEO includes only **Google Business Profile** and **Facebook**. The other sources (Instagram, X/Twitter, etc.) are available on the Local SEO Pro edition.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">💰</span>Sales Strategy</div>

Hook clients on the immediate value-added features included with Local SEO, and upsell them on the rest.

### Immediate value

Start with Local SEO: one-place management, Google Business Profile sync, Facebook, insights, and My Listing. These deliver visible results quickly—clients see how customers find them and what they do next.

### Upsell path

Once clients see the value, introduce Listing Distribution (aggregator sync to Data Axle, Localeze, Foursquare) and Listing Sync Pro (protection, instant publishing, duplicate suppression, control). Each tier solves a bigger problem as the client grows.
