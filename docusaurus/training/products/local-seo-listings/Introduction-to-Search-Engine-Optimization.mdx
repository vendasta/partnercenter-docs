<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📈</span>Why is SEO Important?</div>

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) boils down to **trust**. Consumers trust the brands they find near the top of their search results.

### The challenge with SEO

In our fast-paced and increasingly digital world, consumers are interacting with brands and online content quicker and easier than before. Understanding and utilizing SEO is more important than ever.

SEO is about understanding what people are searching for online, the answers they are seeking, the words they're using, and the type of content they wish to consume. Knowing the answers to these questions will allow you to connect to the people who are searching online for the solutions you and your brands offer.

### The numbers

- **81% of consumers** search online before buying
- **84% of clicks** are organic rather than paid ads

Prospective buyers search online for helpful product information, price comparisons, and trustworthy reviews. Searchers often skip advertisements and click on organic search results to find what they're looking for. These are the **trusted** positions brands want to be in.

### The solution: search engine optimization

The most basic way to describe SEO: **It's the work that goes into a website to get it on the first page of Google.** It's about making sure that search engines like Google place your client's website on the first page of results when potential customers are searching for their product or service.

Most businesses don't have the time for this extra work and need outside help. SEO is complicated and most people don't have the knowledge or the time to do it effectively on their own. This is where you come in.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📚</span>SEO Terminology</div>

Your SEO strategy comes down to one thing: **a website that ranks.** Here are the key terms:

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **SEO** | Search Engine Optimization |
| **Backlink** | When one webpage hyperlinks to another website (incoming or inbound links) |
| **White Hat SEO** | Following search engine guidelines, focusing on a human audience, no spamming |
| **Black Hat SEO** | Practices banned by search engines (duplicate content, buying links, linked comments on blog posts) |
| **Onsite SEO** | Optimizing your site (content, title, structure) for keywords |
| **Offsite SEO** | Inbound links from credible external sites (external signals that drive traffic) |
| **Rank** | Where a site appears in search engines (low to high) |
| **PPC** | Pay-per-click, buying visits through ads (not organic site traffic) |
| **Keywords** | Content pages are tagged and coded with relevant keywords optimized to appear in targeted search |

### Keywords

When designing keywords, the goal is to **mirror the phrase a user inputs into search on Google**. Targeted and industry-specific keywords lead to better performance in search results, higher conversion rates, and happier clients. Clients know their industry best—they may feel empowered to provide a list. Avoid overly generalized or single-word keywords as a general rule.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🔗</span>Introduction to SEO Network</div>

Dynamic SEO strategies are some of the most important things to consider for ranking longevity and increasing high-quality website visitors. SEO Network provides just that.

### How does SEO Network work?

1. **Content pages:** The network code generates and integrates vital missing content pages. Relevant keyword-related content is added with new pages tagged and coded.
2. **Interlinking:** Interlinking with industry-related businesses begins. The Live Link Data Report shows links being generated, corresponding keywords, where the link appears, and relevancy score.
3. **Monitoring:** The system actively watches for new relevant link partners daily and checks to ensure links stay live over time.

### What makes SEO Network different?

SEO Network provides you and your clients with a dashboard where everything is clearly recorded—every link that's been built, every piece of content written, and the corresponding rankings on Google, Yahoo, and Bing.
