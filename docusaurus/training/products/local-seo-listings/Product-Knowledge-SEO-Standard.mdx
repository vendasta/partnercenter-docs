### Order and Assignment

- An order is received through Partner Center, generating a Task Manager project
- The Client Engagement team processes the order
- The SEO Team Lead assigns the project to an SEO Specialist

### Consultation and Follow-Up

- The Client Engagement team sends a confirmation email using a templated macro in Zendesk
- A consultation call is scheduled **5–6 business days** from the order date
- The consultation call involves the Client Solutions Specialist and SEO Specialist discussing project details

### Post-Consultation Steps

- SEO Specialists draft a post-consultation email
- The Task Manager is updated by the Client Engagement and SEO teams

### Performance Reporting

- After **30 days**, the SEO team generates a report
- The report is uploaded to the task manager and sent to Client Engagement for distribution
- Monthly performance reports include: Audit Fulfillment Form, Competitive Analysis, Keyword Re-evaluation, Backlink creation

### Ongoing Tasks

- Optimize on-page content and technical SEO elements
- Write and perform quality assurance checks on monthly blogs
- Quarterly SEO review calls occur in months 4, 7, and 10
