In this course, you will learn how Advertising Intelligence helps your clients manage and understand their digital advertising efforts. Digital ads put businesses in front of their target audience on a multitude of platforms—but understanding impact is complex and time-consuming. Advertising Intelligence brings it all under one roof.

:::tip Learning Goals
- Understand why digital ads matter and what constitutes a digital ad
- Know why digital ad reporting is essential
- Learn what Advertising Intelligence is and its key benefits
- Explore main features
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📱</span>Digital Ads: A Vital Component</div>

Digital advertising puts businesses on the map, in front of their target audience, on a multitude of platforms. Digital ads allow potential customers to see your business first when they search for products and services.

**Types of digital ads** include: Search ads, Display ads, Social ads (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Messenger), and PMAX (Performance Max). If your clients are just getting started, make sure they're not sent upstream without a paddle—or they might jump ship.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📊</span>Why Digital Ad Reporting?</div>

Understanding the impact of digital ads is incredibly complex and time-consuming. Clients need to organize, analyze, and consume data in digestible chunks to make informed spending decisions. They need to know if their ad dollars are paying off—but manually pulling data from multiple sources is not scalable and eats away at margins.

If clients aren't getting accurate, meaningful, and timely data, they have no way of knowing whether their ads are successful. Advertising Intelligence brings all ad campaigns under one roof so clients can easily see what's working and what isn't. Data is pulled in automatically—you become the expert without lifting a finger.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🏪</span>What is Advertising Intelligence?</div>

Advertising Intelligence is a **freemium** digital ad **reporting** tool that brings Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and many more into one dashboard. You can show your clients exactly how their ad spend is paying off.

**Important:** Advertising Intelligence specializes in customized, digital ad reporting. You **cannot run** digital ads through this product—it is reporting only.

### Connected platforms

Advertising Intelligence connects with: Facebook Ads, YouTube Ads, Google Search Ads, Instagram Ads, Messenger Ads, LinkedIn Ads, PMAX (Limited Visibility), and more. Visit Vendasta Support for the full list.
