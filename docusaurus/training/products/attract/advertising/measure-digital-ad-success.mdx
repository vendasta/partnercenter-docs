Position your agency as an expert in digital ad reporting and provide clients with software to compare campaigns and analyze meaningful metrics. Advertising Intelligence consolidates reporting from Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and more—all under your company's logo. It directly impacts the first stage of the customer journey: Awareness.

:::tip Learning Goals
- Connect Google Ads and Facebook Ads accounts
- Explore the dashboard and metrics
- Configure Settings
- Understand Advanced Reporting and multi-location
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🔗</span>Connect Google Ads and Facebook Ads</div>

The first time you launch Advertising Intelligence, you will be prompted to **connect your ad accounts** (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, etc.). Once connected, click **Get started** to be taken to the Overview page and view data from the connected network.

:::info
Explore Advertising Intelligence inside the Vendasta Marketplace.
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📊</span>Explore the Dashboard</div>

On the **Overview** page, you'll see one card per ad network connected (e.g., Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads) with important metrics for tracking ad campaigns.

**Free version:** The date range in the top right corner is locked at 30 days.

**Automation:** Consolidation of reporting from Facebook, YouTube, Google Display, and more is all automated—leave time-consuming, margin-eating manual reporting in the past. Advertising Intelligence is freemium: free with a clear upgrade path to Advanced Reporting.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⚙️</span>Configuring the Settings Tab</div>

Open the **Settings** page (top right corner). From there you can:

1. **Manage your connected accounts**
2. **Customize which metrics your clients will see** (e.g., toggle ROI on or off)

If you don't want clients to see ROI in their dashboard, use the toggle switch in the Settings page.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📈</span>Advanced Reporting</div>

**Advanced Reporting** is the upgrade for clients who want more detailed metrics or multi-location functionality.

### Advanced reporting overview

- **Custom date range:** 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, all time, or **custom**—so clients can spot trends and better time ads or allocate budget
- **Individual campaigns:** Detailed metrics for each campaign under Google Ads and Facebook. Choose which specific campaigns to upload
- **Campaign drill-down:** Click any campaign (highlighted in blue) to see campaign objective, individual ads, keywords, campaign funnel, and campaign status

### Settings and multi-location

Advanced Reporting has additional options for customizing metrics. The Executive Report has **multi-location capabilities** for larger clients.

With Advanced Reporting, clients can keep track of trends, compare individual campaigns, and utilize multi-location.
