<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📊</span>Executive Report and Business App</div>

The Executive Report shows immediate and incremental improvements—therefore ongoing value—over time. It is an **automatically generated proof-of-performance report** emailed weekly or monthly to your clients—making you the hero!

It shows changes over time from data sources that have been connected, including:

- Google Business Profile
- SEO
- Reviews
- Listings accuracy
- Website analytics
- Advertising
- Social media activity
- Vendor apps activated on the account

You can also provide project updates about work being done via Task Manager.

:::tip Snapshot Connection
We recommend the **Become a Snapshot Ninja** course to understand the powerful connection between the Snapshot and the Executive Report.
:::

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">✨</span>Key Features of the Executive Report</div>

The Executive Report assembles metrics from products throughout the Vendasta platform, making it your client's **single source of truth** for the state of their online presence.

| Feature | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| **More products and KPIs** | Advanced reporting from Reviews, Listings, Social, Website, SEO, and Advertising. Most active apps push data to the report |
| **Short-term and long-term trends** | Compare week-over-week or month-over-month. Use long-term trends to contextualize—e.g., "Fewer visits from Google Maps this week, but traffic has more than tripled in 6 months" |
| **Automated emails with highlights** | Delivered at the beginning of every week, month, or both. No sign-in required. Mobile-responsive |
| **Immediate value on day one** | After running a Snapshot or authenticating GBP, useful data appears in Business App within 5 minutes |
| **White-label** | Fully white-labeled with your logo and branding in both email and Business App |

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⚙️</span>Curate Your Client's Executive Report Experience</div>

### Keep your clients informed

**Partner Center → Administration → Customize → Business App Settings → Executive Report**

Customize how often you'd like to notify your clients (weekly or monthly) and what types of activities you'd like to notify them about.

### Two ways to deliver

1. **Set a weekly or monthly schedule** — Report sent via email to your clients
2. **Enable the Executive Report tab** — Clients can view the report at any time in Business App

### Enable the tab

**Partner Center → Administration → Customize Business App → Settings**

Under **Tab Settings**, check **Display Executive Report tab**. This allows clients to view the Executive Report at any time in **Business App → Executive Report**. Click **Apply Changes**.

### Enable Executive Report emails

**Partner Center → Administration → Customize → Business App Settings**

Under **Executive Report**, select **Business App** from the **Report Type** dropdown. Select **Weekly** or **Monthly** from the **Default Frequency** dropdown. Click **Apply Changes**.

By sending email notifications, you prompt clients to visit and engage with Business App. They'll understand where they're performing well and where to improve—and can take action, such as upgrading to a paid product or service.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📥</span>How Do I Get New Data Into the Report?</div>

New data appears **automatically** when supporting products are activated on an account and some time has passed for data ingestion. The time needed varies by product.

We support **custom data integrations via API**. If you have a database you'd like to connect to the Executive Report, work with your Account Manager for a custom integration via our Marketplace APIs.
