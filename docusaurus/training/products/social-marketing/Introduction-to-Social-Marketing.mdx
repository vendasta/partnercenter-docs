In our fast-paced digital world, businesses must engage with consumers on social media to stay competitive. If you're not present online, your competitors are. Social Marketing gives SMBs an effective and efficient way to connect with their audience—from one location, in a timely manner.

:::tip Learning Goals
- Stay connected with your audience using Social Marketing, a powerful white-labeled social media management tool.
- Schedule content for all networks at once, generate leads, and interact with your client base—all in the same innovative tool.
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📢</span>Social Marketing and the Customer Journey</div>

A strong social presence helps SMBs at many stages of the customer journey: **Awareness**, **Reputation**, and **Advocacy**. Businesses should have the ability to build fans, find customers, and engage with their community—from one location, in a timely manner.

### What Social Marketing is used for

Building fans, finding customers, and engaging with the community. All three—not friction-free payments, which is a separate capability.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">✍️</span>Top Features of Social Marketing</div>

### AI-powered composer and calendar

All-in-one composer and scheduling calendar. Schedule social media posts to Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, and Google Business Profile—all in one place. Your agency and clients can efficiently plan and publish across networks.

### Social Marketing pro: single AI prompt

Manage social media for more clients in less time. A single AI prompt in Social Marketing Pro generates content, so you scale without scaling headcount.

### Industry-related content stream

A built-in stream of industry-related content for effortless discovery of great material to share on social networks. Never run out of relevant posts.

### Intelligent lead generation

Set up smart lead searches based on chosen keywords. Find customers looking exactly for the products and services your clients offer.

### Social reporting and analytics

Post breakdown, audience reporting, and KPIs that identify the success of your social media strategy. See how the business is performing across platforms.

### Clickable.bio

Drive eCommerce through Instagram. Transform Instagram posts into a shoppable gallery of images—turn social fans into paying customers. Available free on Social Marketing Pro.
