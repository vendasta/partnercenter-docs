## Step 1: Understand the Go-to-Market playbook (3 min)

This lab delves into Vendasta's Go-to-Market playbook, providing essential training and resources to boost your sales performance. Explore proven strategies for new customer acquisition and discover how to leverage psychological principles for client commitments.

The **Go-to-Market playbook** is available in the Vendasta content library and includes:

- **Sales script for the initial sale** — use this on first calls with prospects (make a copy to customize)
- **Sales presentation for the upsell call** — used when introducing the full Business App package (make a copy)

These assets are designed to work together. The initial script focuses on a small, low-risk first sale. The upsell deck is used after onboarding, when trust is established.


## Step 2: Learn the Foot-in-the-Door strategy (4 min)

The **Foot-in-the-Door** strategy uses psychological principles to initiate minor agreements and upsell clients to larger packages—tailored for SMBs.

### Purpose of the initial call

The purpose of the initial call is **not** to finalize the largest sale possible or provide comprehensive training. It's to make a **small initial sale** that paves the way for onboarding and a long-term relationship—leading to upselling the larger Business App package later.

Start small (e.g., Local SEO, Listing Sync), onboard successfully, build trust, then introduce the full Business App package.

:::tip In Practice
You're on an initial call. Instead of a linear walkthrough, you open the local search grid and say: "This shows how you rank vs competitors across your service area—see where you're missing opportunities?" They lean in. You show the AI webchat capturing a lead. They ask: "How do we set that up?" You've triggered questions. You close with a small Local SEO + Listing Sync sale. On the onboarding call, you introduce the full Business App package. Foot-in-the-Door in action.
:::


## Step 3: Master demo techniques (3 min)

| Tip | What It Means |
|-----|---------------|
| **Start with the END in mind** | Show the best screen first. Don't start with setup—show the outcome. Naturally we want to showcase setup from the beginning, but prospects respond to results. |
| **Don't perform a linear demo** | Don't tell a story from start to finish. Prospects get disoriented and forget why they're on the call. |
| **Focus on your "aha" moments** | Highlight 2–3 really compelling functions without diving deep. If you do this correctly, the prospect will start asking questions—which is exactly what you want. You lose when they're silent or say they "have to think about it." |
| **Do not narrate your on-screen actions** | Don't say "Now I'm gonna click here and type in this field…" Prospects can see that. Instead, speak about the **value** the feature is delivering. |


## Step 4: Apply what you've learned (2 min)

### 1. Explore and experiment

- Order and activate Business App. Get comfortable with its interface and features.
- Use Business App for your own business first to see its impact.

### 2. Implement with test clients

- **Start Small:** Begin with one feature—AI-assisted web chat or Local SEO.
- **Expand Gradually:** Introduce more features as you and your clients get more comfortable.
