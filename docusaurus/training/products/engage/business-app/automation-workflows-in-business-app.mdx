:::note
This lab covers how to automate review requests and client workflows in Business App—using Zapier for integrations and Reputation AI for automated review solicitation—so your clients get consistent engagement without manual effort.
:::


## Step 1: Understand automation for local businesses (3 min)

**Read**

Automation helps local businesses streamline operations, enhance customer relationships, and drive sales without constant manual effort. Workflows can connect disparate tools, trigger actions based on events, and keep customers engaged at the right moments.

### Why automate?

Reduce repetitive tasks, ensure consistent follow-up, and scale customer engagement. Automation lets you focus on high-value work while the system handles routine touchpoints.


## Step 2: Connect Zapier to Business App (4 min)

**Read**

Zapier is a third-party tool that connects external systems with Business App. It enables workflows that would otherwise require manual data entry or custom development.

### How Zapier works

Zapier sends data—such as contact details—from your client's other software tools into the Business App CRM. Instead of manually inputting customer details, data flows automatically when events occur in connected apps (e.g., a new booking, form submission, or sale).

### Use cases

Sync leads from booking systems, forms, or e-commerce into Business App. Trigger follow-up messages, add contacts to pipelines, or kick off review request sequences—all from a single automation.


## Step 3: Set up automated review requests with Reputation AI (3 min)

**Read**

To automate review requests in Business App, Reputation AI must be activated on the account.

### "Send a review request" action

The "Send a review request" action in Business App automations only works when Reputation AI is active. Without it, the action will not function. This ensures clients receive the full value of automated review solicitation—getting the right requests to the right customers at the right time.

### Enhancing client engagement

Automated review solicitation keeps your clients' reputation efforts consistent. Trigger review requests after completed jobs, purchases, or positive interactions—without manual follow-up.

:::tip In Practice
A contractor uses Zapier to sync new job completions from their booking tool into Business App. An automation triggers 24 hours after a job is marked complete: "Send a review request" fires because Reputation AI is active. The customer receives a timely request and leaves a 5-star review. The contractor gets more reviews without lifting a finger.
:::


## Step 4: Get started with automations (2 min)

**Do**

### Explore the Automations tab

Dive into the Automations tab in Business App. Experiment with different triggers and actions. Customize workflows to fit the unique needs of each client—from review requests to lead nurture to appointment reminders.

### Discover integrations

Beyond Zapier, explore other integrations and software tools available in the platform. Connect various platforms to work seamlessly together and build end-to-end workflows.

### Share and iterate

Share automation ideas with other partners. Discuss what works, refine triggers, and iterate on workflows to maximize client engagement.
