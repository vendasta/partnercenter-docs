:::note
This lab explores the three core customer lifecycle stages Business App addresses—attracting leads through local SEO, converting them with AI webchat, and engaging them through AI-driven social content creation.
:::


## Step 1: Attract more customers with local SEO (3 min)

Business App helps local businesses attract new leads through local SEO and search visibility. The **local search grid** is a crucial feature—it offers insights into search engine performance across different locations, helps identify areas for SEO improvement, and tracks keyword performance over time. This enables businesses to optimize their search rankings and strategy effectively.

The local search grid provides invaluable visibility for clients' keyword rankings.


## Step 2: Capture more leads with AI webchat (3 min)

The **AI-powered webchat** in Business App captures leads by providing instant answers and collecting contact details (first name, last name, email, phone, and address) for follow-up. The AI Assistant's primary goal is to capture a lead while being helpful and answering questions. It's designed to be a knowledgeable, friendly representative of a business and deliver a great first impression.


## Step 3: Engage customers with AI social content (3 min)

Business App supports small and medium-sized businesses in managing their social media marketing strategy by offering **AI-driven content creation** that adjusts tone, length, and style—making post crafting more efficient. It provides a comprehensive set of AI-enhanced social media marketing tools, streamlining content creation with smart features. It emphasizes AI transparency, enabling users to refine both generated content and their inputs for better outcomes.

:::tip In Practice
A plumber uses Business App. The local search grid shows they rank #3 for "plumber near me" in their city—they identify keywords to improve. The AI webchat answers "Do you do emergency calls?" and captures the visitor's name and phone. A specialist follows up and books the job. Social AI's AI drafts a post about their latest project; they adjust the tone and publish. Attract → Convert → Engage in action.
:::
