Welcome to the Vendasta Academy's Business App Masterclass! During this course, you will get a comprehensive understanding of Business App and its optimization capabilities, gain practical skills in software setup and feature integration for customer engagement, and learn techniques for effective marketing and sales, local SEO, and online reputation management.

:::tip What You'll Learn
- How Business App helps local businesses attract, convert, and engage customers
- The recommended product package and what each product does
- How to perform impactful demos and follow Vendasta's sales playbook
:::

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🎯</span>Attract</div>

Business App helps local businesses **attract** new customers through paid and organic search, ensuring a strong online reputation. This includes ads, listings, reviews, and SEO—the foundational elements that make a business findable and trustworthy when prospects search.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🔄</span>Convert</div>

Business App helps local businesses **convert** leads into customers efficiently with automated processes and human-centric solutions. The AI-assisted web chat engages visitors in real time, turning website traffic into qualified leads and customers.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">💬</span>Engage</div>

Business App helps local businesses **engage** customers and build loyalty through seamless communication channels. Email marketing, social media, and review requests keep customers connected and encourage repeat business.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📦</span>Business App Recommended Package</div>

The recommended package includes:

| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Conversations AI** | Centralized messaging and communication |
| **Email & SMS Marketing** | Email and marketing campaigns |
| **Local SEO** | Organic search visibility |
| **Reputation AI** | Reviews and online reputation |
| **Social Marketing** | Social media presence and engagement |

Business App streamlines these essential elements into one platform for exceptional customer experiences.
