<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📍</span>Introduction to Conversations AI</div>

Conversations AI surpasses traditional communication by consolidating all communication into one convenient location and SMS number. It acts as a centralized platform for your clients to capture leads and facilitate efficient communication.

### Where conversations live

All conversations are easily accessible from a single location in Business App—whether from Google Business Messages, SMS, or webchat. No more switching between channels.

### Value for local businesses

Facilitate quick lead management from multiple sources. The shared team inbox ensures seamless collaboration and improved customer experience. Enable businesses to respond quickly—reducing the risk of losing customers due to slow response times. Optimize marketing investments and improve lead conversion by consolidating channels. Reduce acquisition costs and shorten time-to-convert.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📬</span>The Value for You</div>

### Partner benefits

Enhance customer experience. Increase brand loyalty and client retention. Build confidence for great client relationships. By consolidating messages into one place, Conversations AI keeps conversations top of mind.

### Client engagement

Clients who use Inbox are **four times more engaged** with your solutions than those who do not. It's available right in Business App—positioning you as the trusted expert. 24/7 open communication lets you check in, give project updates, or make timely product suggestions.

### Inbox in Business App

Inbox allows your clients to communicate with you and your team—and is included with every account at no additional cost. You control whether clients can message you via Business App customization settings.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🤖</span>The AI Assistant</div>

### Primary goals

**Answer questions about the business** — The AI responds with information from the business profile and trained knowledge.

**Capture lead contact info** — Collects visitor contact (mobile or email) for ongoing conversation. Business App notifies the business of new leads via email.

**Respond in the visitor's language** — The AI can reply in over 50 languages, including English, French, Spanish, and more.
{/* Language count source: https://docs.vendasta.com/ai/ai-workforce/ai-chat-receptionist/#supported-languages — verified 2026-07-31 */}

### Setup: no SMS registration required

US and Canada-based businesses can utilize SMS messaging. When Conversations AI is activated, a local SMS number is **automatically assigned**—you do not need to register an SMS number first.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⚙️</span>Get the Most Out of Your AI Assistant</div>

### Demo the AI web chat widget

Local SEO &gt; My Listing tab &gt; View My Listing. A fully working AI-assisted web chat is available to test for any account and demo to clients. Open any account's Local SEO and locate the Web Chat Lead Capture Widget via their My Listing page.

### Use the web chat widget on your website

Partner Center &gt; Inbox &gt; Inbox Settings. Configure the color, ensure business profile information is correct, and add the shortcode to your website header. The web chat widget appears on your site, ready to engage visitors and bring their information into Inbox.

### Fuel your AI assistant with knowledge

Business App &gt; Settings &gt; Inbox Settings &gt; Web Chat Settings &gt; AI Assistant. Click any title to see the knowledge the AI has access to. Click **Manage Knowledge** to add new knowledge. Write or paste text into a new knowledge source and select it to make it accessible to your AI assistant.
