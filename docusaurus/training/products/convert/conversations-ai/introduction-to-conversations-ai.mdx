<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">💬</span>Introduction to Conversations AI in the Platform</div>

Conversations AI helps you streamline communication with your client accounts and product and service vendors.

### Where Conversations AI lives

| Environment | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **Partner Center** | Build relationships with accounts, vendors, and Vendasta. The open inbox helps your team collaborate and communicate |
| **Vendor Center** | Conversations AI in Vendor Center enables direct communication for both pre and post-sales communication |
| **Business App** | Let your clients communicate with you and your team at no additional cost |

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">✨</span>Top Features of Conversations AI</div>

Conversations AI helps you message your clients, ask questions to vendors, and connect with your Vendasta account rep—all for free. It's conveniently located in a side panel that stays with you wherever you are in the platform.

### Upgrade to Conversations AI Pro or Premium

While Conversations AI in the platform helps your clients connect with you, your clients can capture new leads and communicate with their customers via SMS, Google Business Message, Web Chat, and more by upgrading to **Conversations AI Pro or Premium**.

Learn more about Conversations AI Pro and Premium in the Academy course.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📤</span>Send a Message to Users in the Platform</div>

### Two ways to start a new conversation with a client

1. **Click the blue [+] New Message button** and choose an account
2. **Send a message from an account's details page** under Manage Accounts

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">⚙️</span>Enable Conversations AI</div>

### Enable Conversations AI for your clients

Go to **Partner Center → Administration → Customize Business App → Conversations AI Messages** and check **Show this page**

### Set up the web chat

Add the AI-assisted Web Chat widget to connect with your website visitors.

1. Head to **Partner Center → Conversations AI → Conversations AI Settings**
2. Configure the color and ensure the business profile information is correct
3. Add the shortcode to your website header

The web chat widget will appear on the site, ready to engage with website visitors and bring their information right into Conversations AI.
