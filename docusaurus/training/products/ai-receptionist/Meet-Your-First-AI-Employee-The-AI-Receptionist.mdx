Running a small business means every call, message, or customer question matters—yet it's impossible to be everywhere at once. An AI Employee is designed to step in where things often get missed. This course walks through how the AI Receptionist works, how to train it like a teammate, and how to let it handle the busy work so you can focus on growth.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">👤</span>What is an AI Employee?</div>

An AI employee is a digital team member who:

- Welcomes and responds to customers instantly
- Captures names, emails, and phone numbers
- Schedules appointments directly in your calendar
- Provides accurate answers using your business profile or website
- Works across chat, text, and phone

Unlike basic chatbots, AI employees are not limited to a script. They can adapt, improve, and be guided—just like a real team member you would train.

| Chatbot | AI Employee |
|---------|-------------|
| Scripted replies | Learns and adapts based on trained knowledge |
| One channel (usually chat) | Works across chat, SMS, and other channels |
| Limited to FAQs | Captures leads, books appointments, follows instructions |
| Static | Improves with training |

### Why this matters now

Customers expect quick answers, 24/7 availability, and a smooth experience. Common challenges:

- **Missed calls mean missed business**—every unanswered ring can send a customer elsewhere
- **Customers want instant replies**—whether on website, text, or phone
- **Your day has limits**—you can't answer and book around the clock
- **Hiring staff is expensive**—adding people isn't always realistic

An AI Employee steps in where these pain points hit hardest, making enterprise-level customer service affordable for small businesses.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🤖</span>Meet the AI Receptionist</div>

The AI Receptionist is your first point of contact—at the desk, online, or on the phone.

### What the AI Receptionist does

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Greets customers instantly | Every caller or visitor feels acknowledged |
| Captures names, emails, phone numbers | No lead slips through the cracks |
| Books appointments into your calendar | Customers schedule without waiting |
| Provides conversation summaries and transcripts | You stay informed without listening to every call |
| Works 24/7 | Your business is always open |

### Misconception vs reality

| Misconception | Reality |
|---------------|---------|
| "It's robotic." | You choose the tone and style so it sounds like your business |
| "It's hard to change." | Update instructions anytime, like coaching a new hire |
| "It's risky." | Start small, adjust as needed, pause anytime. Low cost, high value |

### Your control levers

- **Tone:** Decide how it sounds—friendly, professional, casual, or formal
- **Purpose:** Define the role and priorities (e.g., "always capture name and phone before ending")
- **Knowledge:** Give it info from your business profile, website, or FAQs so answers are accurate

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">📞</span>Chat vs Voice</div>

There are two AI Employees: **AI Chat Receptionist** and **AI Voice Receptionist**. Both make it easy for customers to connect, but each shines in different situations.

**Chat Receptionist:** Works in website chat, via SMS, or on social channels. Quick and convenient—ideal for customers who want answers without picking up the phone. They can check hours, ask about services, or request a booking while scrolling online.

**Voice Receptionist:** Answers incoming phone calls. For many service businesses, the phone is still the first place customers turn for urgent help. The Voice Receptionist captures details, books appointments, and filters unqualified leads.

**Tip:** You don't have to choose one. Many businesses start with both, connecting with customers across their entire first-touch ecosystem.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🔄</span>How It Works in Action</div>

**On your website:** A customer clicks the chat widget and is greeted right away. The AI answers questions like "Are you open on weekends?" or "Do you repair water heaters?" If the customer is ready, it captures name, phone, and email, offers to book an appointment, and notifies you instantly.

**On the phone:** The AI Receptionist answers in your chosen tone. For simple requests, it provides the answer. For service inquiries, it clarifies needs, captures details, and books a time if your calendar is connected. If a call needs your attention, it routes to you. Every call is logged with a transcript.

**Where results appear:** All conversations—chat or voice—are stored in your Business App Inbox. Leads are automatically added to your contact list with transcripts or message history.

---

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">🎓</span>Train It Like a Teammate</div>

Think of your first day with a new hire. You wouldn't say "Good luck" and leave. You'd show them how you want things done, answer questions, and check in over time. Same with your AI Receptionist.

**Start with direction.** Decide how it should sound and what its main focus should be—your brand voice and job description.

**Feed it knowledge.** It needs to know your hours, services, and the questions customers ask most often.

**Keep training over time.** Once it's running, open transcripts and ask: Does this sound like us? Did it handle the request the way I would? If not, adjust instructions or add missing information. A few minutes of review each week makes a big difference.

**Rhythm:** Review → Improve → Repeat.

<div className="principle-area"><span className="principle-area__icon">✅</span>Readiness Check & Success Metrics</div>

### Before going live

- Do you know your top 5 FAQs?
- Do you have booking rules and hours handy?
- Who will review the first week of conversations?

### Metrics that matter

| Metric | Before AI (Manual) | With AI Receptionist |
|-------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Lead Capture | Some missed when unanswered | Every lead captured, 24/7 |
| Time to Response | Minutes or hours | Instant |
| Bookings | Manual, during hours | Automated via chat or calls |
| After-Hours | Rare or zero | New leads converted overnight |

### Guardrails & good practices

- **Be transparent.** Let customers know they're interacting with AI
- **Offer human handoff.** Customers should always have the option to reach you directly
- **Keep it factual.** Feed only accurate, up-to-date business details
- **Set escalation rules.** Complex requests (detailed quotes, complaints) should hand off to you
