When a client buys social media management through Vendasta Services, they grant page access by switching to their Facebook business page, opening `Settings`, then `New Page Experiences`, and accepting the Vendasta Services request under `Page Access`. The Vendasta Services team needs this access to publish content on your client's behalf. Share these steps with your client. Your client retains full ownership and control of the page.

## How does your client accept the Facebook access request? {#accept-the-facebook-access-request}

For our team to post on your behalf, we need access to your Facebook page.

Log in to Facebook and switch your profile to your business page.

<img src={require('./img/facebook-switch-to-business-page.png').default} alt="Switching to business page profile in Facebook" style={{width: '80%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

Once switched, you should appear on `Manage Page`.

Click `Settings`.

<img src={require('./img/facebook-settings.png').default} alt="Facebook Settings page" style={{width: '80%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

Click `New Page Experiences`.

<img src={require('./img/facebook-new-page-experiences.png').default} alt="New Page Experiences section in Facebook Settings" style={{width: '80%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

You'll see a section for `Page Access` and inside of it you may see an access request from Vendasta Services or Digital Agency.

Accept the request.

<img src={require('./img/facebook-accept-access-request.png').default} alt="Facebook page access request from Vendasta Services" style={{width: '80%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

## Frequently asked questions

<summary>Why does the Vendasta Services team need access to my client's Facebook page?</summary>

When a client purchases social media management through Vendasta Services, the team needs page access to publish content on the client's behalf.

</details>

<summary>Does my client lose ownership of their Facebook page?</summary>

No. Your client retains full ownership and control of the page.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does my client find the access request?</summary>

After switching to the business page in Facebook, your client clicks `Settings`, then `New Page Experiences`. The request appears in the `Page Access` section, from Vendasta Services or Digital Agency.

</details>
