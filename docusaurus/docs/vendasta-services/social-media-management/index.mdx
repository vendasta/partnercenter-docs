# Social Media

Vendasta Services delivers ongoing social posting for your clients through the AI Social Media Manager and builds new social profiles with the one-time Social Page Build. The Social Media Management Standard and Plus packages are no longer sold.

## Overview

Ongoing posting is delivered by the **[AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md)** — an AI employee our team configures and runs on the client's behalf. New profiles are built by the one-time **[Social Page Build](./social-page-build.md)**.

:::info Managed Social Media Management has been retired

The Social Media Management Standard and Plus monthly posting packages are no longer sold. Ongoing social content is delivered through the [AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md), which builds an autonomous content calendar across the client's connected networks, trained on their brand, with approval workflows they control.

:::

## What's Included

### [AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md) — ongoing posting

Sold as a one-time **Setup** or a monthly **AI Workforce Optimization Plan**. Our team configures the AI employee, trains it on the client's brand, and connects their networks. It then builds and publishes an autonomous social content calendar, with approval workflows so nothing goes out without the client's say-so. Requires Social AI Premium.

See [AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md) for full details, or the [AI Workforce](../ai-workforce/index.mdx) overview for how Setup and Optimization Plans compare.

### [Social Page Build](./social-page-build.md) — new profiles

A one-time, fully managed build of a new social profile on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or another free-to-create channel. Includes accurate business data, a business description, logo and header image, and verification of the page.

### [Gaining Facebook Business Page access](./gaining-facebook-business-page-access.mdx)

How the client grants our team the access needed to manage or post to their Facebook Business Page.

## Get started

1. **Client needs a profile created?** Order a [Social Page Build](./social-page-build.md). Our team gathers details on a short onboarding call, then builds and verifies the page.
2. **Client needs ongoing posting?** Sell an [AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md) Setup or Optimization Plan.
3. **Either way, get access.** Walk the client through [Gaining Facebook Business Page access](./gaining-facebook-business-page-access.mdx) so our team can work on their Facebook page.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>What happened to Social Media Management Standard and Plus?</summary>

Those monthly managed posting packages are no longer sold. Ongoing social content is delivered by the [AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md), available as a one-time Setup or a monthly Optimization Plan.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What platforms can a profile be built on?</summary>

Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn are the most common. Other channels are available as long as the profile is free to create or claim. See [Social Page Build](./social-page-build.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are blog posts still available as a service?</summary>

Not as a standalone writing service. Blog content is produced by the [AI Blogger](../ai-workforce/ai-blogger.md), which builds an SEO-focused blog calendar and publishes to the client's WordPress site.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Did Social Page Build replace Social Media Management?</summary>

No. Social Page Build is a one-time build of a new social profile. Ongoing posting that Social Media Management Standard and Plus used to cover is delivered by the [AI Social Media Manager](../ai-workforce/ai-social-media-manager.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>My client doesn't have an Instagram business account. Can your team set one up?</summary>

Yes. Order a [Social Page Build](./social-page-build.md). Our team gathers details on a short onboarding call, then builds and verifies the profile with accurate business data, a business description, and a logo and header image.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the AI Social Media Manager require Social AI?</summary>

Yes. The AI Social Media Manager requires Social AI Premium.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can my client approve posts before they're published?</summary>

Yes. The AI Social Media Manager uses approval workflows that the client controls, so nothing goes out without the client's say-so.
</details>
