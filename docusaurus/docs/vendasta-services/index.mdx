Vendasta Services is a team of experts that fulfills services for your clients, including websites, social media management, digital advertising, review management, listings, and AI Workforce setup. You order these services in Partner Center from `Marketplace` → `Discover Products`. Vendasta Services handles the fulfillment, so you can deliver these services without expanding your team.

## Why use Vendasta Services?

Vendasta Services fulfills complex deliverables with consistent quality and turnaround times, so you can focus on sales and client relationships while the Vendasta Services team handles the execution.

## How do I order and track a Vendasta Services product? {#get-started}

1. **Review service offerings**
   - Navigate to `Partner Center` → `Marketplace` → `Discover Products`
   - Filter for Vendasta Services products
   - Review service details and deliverables

2. **Order a service**
   - Select the service product for your client
   - Complete the order form with required information
   - Submit for fulfillment

3. **Track fulfillment**
   - Monitor progress in Task Manager or Business App
   - Communicate with the fulfillment team as needed
   - Review completed deliverables

4. **Attend pre-launch calls**
   - Participate in kickoff calls for complex services
   - Provide necessary information and assets
   - Set expectations for delivery timelines

## Common service workflows

**Website Development**: Order website service, provide brand assets and content, participate in design review, and launch completed site.

**Social Media Management**: Order social media service, provide brand guidelines, approve content calendar, and monitor ongoing posting and engagement.

**Digital Advertising**: Order ad campaign service, provide campaign objectives, review ad creative, and monitor campaign performance.
