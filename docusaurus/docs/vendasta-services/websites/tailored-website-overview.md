This guide outlines the process and expectations for the Tailored Website service. This is a collaborative, do-it-with-me solution where our professionals work with you to create a website perfectly suited to your business.

## The website build process

The tailored website build is a comprehensive process that moves from initial consultation to final launch in distinct stages. The entire project, from concept to completion, typically requires 3 to 6 months.

### 1. Discovery and consultation

The process begins with a required discovery call with our web experts. This consultation is essential for our team to understand your vision for the website's design, functionality, and page count.

* **How to Prepare:** Be ready to share your ideas and goals for the website. The more detail you can provide, the more accurate the quote and scope of work will be.
* **Timeline:** The discovery call can be booked in as little as 2 business days, depending on availability.

### 2. Custom quote and timeline

Based on the information from the discovery call, our team will build a custom quote and a proposed scope of work. The quote will also outline the full project timeline.

* **Timeline:** The quote will be shared within 5 business days and is valid for 30 days. The project timeline provided is an estimate and may be adapted based on delays or revision requests.
* **Important:** The quote is based on the agreed-upon services. If the scope of the project increases at any point, an additional quote will be created and require approval to proceed.

### 3. Project Kick-off Call

Once you have agreed to the proposed Statement of Work, our team will schedule a call to discuss the specific details of the site before the build begins.

* **How to Prepare:** For this call, please designate a single point of contact for the project, gather a few examples of websites you like, and list any special technical requests.
* **Timeline:** This call can also be booked in as little as 2 business days, depending on availability.

### 4. Staged build and revisions

Our team builds the site in stages, starting with the homepage to ensure the branding and aesthetic are correct before moving on. Each stage follows a build-review-edit-approve process.

* **Review Process:** You will be encouraged to review each stage carefully. A link will be provided that allows you to make pinned comments directly on the website mock-up to specify changes.
* **Timeline:** The timeline for each stage depends on the stage that precedes it. If there is a delay in the revision stage, we will not proceed to the next stage until you are happy with the current progress. Depending on the volume of edits, revised mock-ups are typically delivered within 5 business days.

### 5. Final review and go-live

Before the website is set live, it is important that you review the entire site to ensure every detail is accurate and meets your needs. Small tweaks and changes can continue to be made after the site is launched.

**Timeline:** Once final revisions are received, the requested adjustments will be made within 5 to 10 days, depending on the size of the site. After you give the final go-ahead, the site will be set live within 5 business days.

> To ensure our team is available to monitor site performance immediately after launch, we do not set websites live late in the day or on Fridays.


## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

<details>

<summary>How long does the entire process take?</summary>

The tailored website build process is lengthy. From the initial concept to the final launch, projects typically require 3 to 6 months to complete. It is important to be comfortable with this timeline before starting.
</details>

<details>

<summary>How are revisions handled?</summary>

Revisions are handled in stages. For each part of the build (like the homepage), we will provide a link for you to provide feedback and request edits. We complete the requested changes, share the mock-up again for approval, and only move to the next stage once you are satisfied.
</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens if I need to add more pages or features during the build?</summary>

If the scope of the project increases at any point, a new quote will be created detailing the additional work and cost. This new quote will require your approval before we adapt the scope of the project and proceed with the new requirements.
</details>

<details>

<summary>What should I prepare for the main project kick-off call?</summary>

To prepare for the kick-off call, you should have three things ready: a designated single point of contact for the project, a few examples of websites that you like for design inspiration, and a list of any special technical requests you might have.
</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the Tailored Website Quote?</summary>

The Tailored Website Quote is a service that provides a custom pricing estimate for website projects that require unique design, complex functionality, or fall outside the scope of standard or templated website packages.
</details>

<details>

<summary>When should I request a Tailored Website instead of using a Templated or Accelerated Templated Website?</summary>

If your client needs a fully custom design, advanced features (like booking systems, integrations, eCommerce, etc.), or a large multi-page site, a Tailored Website is the best option.
</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I request a quote?</summary>

Simply submit a request through your Partner Center or contact your Account Manager. Include as many details as possible about the client’s requirements, branding, functionality, and content needs.
</details>

<details>

<summary>What’s included in the custom quote?</summary>

The quote will include: Scope of work, Estimated timeline, Breakdown of deliverables, Total cost, Any optional add-ons or recommendations
</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens after I submit a request?</summary>

Our team will review the information, possibly request a discovery call, and deliver a detailed quote within 2–3 business days, outlining the timeline, scope, and estimated cost.
</details>

<details>

<summary>How is pricing determined?</summary>

Pricing is based on the complexity of the design, number of pages, integrations required, and overall development effort. Every quote is custom to the project’s scope.
</details>

<details>

<summary>Is there a minimum price or starting range?</summary>

Yes, custom website quotes typically start at $5,000 USD, but pricing can vary significantly depending on the scope and complexity of the project.
</details>

<details>

<summary>How long will the build take once approved?</summary>

The timeline varies depending on the scope, but most tailored websites are completed in 4–8 weeks from the project start date.
</details>

<details>

<summary>Will I be involved in the design and development process?</summary>

Yes! Once the quote is approved, you'll work closely with our project team through key milestones such as mockup approval, content delivery, and final review.
</details>

<details>

<summary>Can revisions be made after the website is delivered?</summary>

Yes, one round of revisions is included in the quoted price. Additional revisions can be requested at an extra cost.
</details>
