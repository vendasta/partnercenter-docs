Review Response Standard and Plus help you build your online reputation by providing professional, timely responses to your customer reviews. Engaging with feedback shows current and future customers that you are attentive and value their business.


## What is the review response service?

This service provides a team of professionals who work on your behalf to respond to customer reviews and request reviews on various online platforms. By handling both positive and negative reviews, the service helps establish credibility and foster trust with your customers.

Responding to reviews is a critical part of managing your online reputation. When potential customers see a business actively engaging with feedback, it signals that the business is attentive and committed to a positive customer experience. How a business manages all reviews, especially negative ones, is a key factor for customers when making decisions.


## The onboarding process

The onboarding process differs slightly between the Standard and Plus services. A specific reputation management software product is required to use this service.

### Standard service onboarding
To begin, you must fill out the fulfillment form with as much detail as possible. Missing information can cause delays in starting the service. The team will review the order and begin the process within 2 business days.

### Plus service onboarding
The Plus service also starts with the fulfillment form, but includes an optional onboarding call to ensure a clear understanding of your brand and goals.

To prepare for the onboarding call, please have the following information ready:
* Login access to any review sites you have already claimed.
* The name and contact information for a person at your business to whom we can direct negative reviewers.
* The name of a contact who can approve drafted responses to negative reviews.
* Your business logo.
* A list of review platforms where you want to increase your number of reviews.
* Any special instructions for the tone and language of the review responses.
* A completed list of customers to whom you want to send review requests.


## Connecting your review sites

The service team can only respond to reviews on sites where they have been granted access. It is essential to provide access for the service to function correctly. The team will attempt to connect to these sites within 1 business day.

### Google
There are two ways to provide access to your Google Business Profile:
* **Preferred Method:** You can log in to your profile and add the service's management account as a co-owner. This method does not require you to share your personal login credentials.
* **Secondary Method:** You can provide the login credentials for the account.

_**Two-Factor Authentication:** If your account uses two-factor authentication, the service team will need to contact you every time the listing needs to be updated or reconnected, which may cause delays._

### Facebook
Your Facebook Business Page will receive a request to add our team as a page Admin. This level of access is required to post review responses.

### Yelp
For Yelp, you must provide a username and password. Additionally, the Yelp profile must be complete and include a user photo of a person in order to post responses.

## What’s included

### Standard service
* **Unlimited review responses:** The team will respond to all reviews received on connected platforms for as long as the service is active.
* **Website review widget:** If your website is on the supported website platform, the team will install a dynamic widget that displays your Google reviews directly on your site. This feature is not offered for websites on other platforms.

### Plus service
This includes all features of the Standard service, plus tools for generating new reviews.
* **Review request generation:** The service helps you get new reviews by sending out requests to your customers.
* **Email and SMS templates:** The team will create a review request template for your approval. If you have SMS credits with the required reputation software, an SMS template can also be created.
* **Customer list uploads:** The team will upload your list of customer contacts and send them review requests within 2 business days. Lists cannot exceed 500 customers per upload.
* **Google Q&A responses:** The team will also respond to questions that come through the Google Q&A feature for your business.

## How we respond to reviews

The approach for responding to reviews differs for positive and negative feedback to ensure each customer is handled appropriately. All responses are drafted or published within 2-4 North American business hours after the review appears in our system.

### Responding to positive reviews
Responses to positive reviews are published directly without requiring your involvement. The team will address the reviewer by name when possible, include the business name in the response, and thank them for their feedback.

### Responding to negative reviews
Due to their sensitive nature, responses to negative reviews are drafted and sent to your designated contact for approval via email.
* The response will be personalized and apologetic, addressing the reviewer by name when possible.
* The business name is avoided in the response to prevent further association with the negative experience.
* The response will invite the reviewer to resolve the issue offline by providing contact information for a person at your business.
* If no feedback on the drafted response is received within one business day, it will be published automatically to ensure a timely reply.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<details>

<summary> Is there a limit to the number of reviews you will respond to?</summary>
No, there is no limit. The service team will respond to as many reviews as you receive on the platforms we have access to.
</details>

<details>

<summary> Will you respond to reviews that were received before the service started?</summary>
No, the service only covers reviews received from the date the service is activated.
</details>

<details>

<summary> Do you respond to reviews in languages other than English?</summary>
No, the service team will only respond to reviews written in English.
</details>

<details>

<summary> What about replies to your responses or reviews that a customer edits?</summary>
The service does not address customer replies to our responses or reviews that are edited after the initial response has been posted. It is recommended that your business responds to these interactions directly.
</details>

<details>

<summary> How often can I submit a new customer list for review requests (Plus Service)?</summary>
You can share a new list of customers whenever you are ready. The team will upload it and send out review requests on your behalf.
</details>
