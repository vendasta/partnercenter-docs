When ordering a website with Vendasta Services, a website framework is the baseline from which we build your website. It can be configured and customized to suit your needs, depending on the product you order. You can find all of our [available frameworks here](https://frameworks.yourdigitalagents.com/).

We do have the capability to offer other options, but additional costs and extended timelines for higher levels of customization will need to be considered. If you are looking for additional customization, we will need to scope the work and determine what the requirements are for us to complete this build. Please submit a [Website Quote Request](https://pre-sale-website-request.marketingservices.dev/) to receive a quote and confirmation of what can be accomplished. Website quotes will be delivered to the email you provided on the form in as little as 3 business days.

**What if I want something that looks closer to a different website?**

Whether you are looking to build a new website or redesign your current website, we can typically mimic most websites in general look and feel. Our team uses the powerful Divi theme builder, which enables us to mimic most websites with ease while maintaining a clean, mobile-responsive site. In these scenarios, we will want to [quote out the request](https://pre-sale-website-request.marketingservices.dev/) and confirm the required products to be activated to accommodate and fulfill this work, as there may be additional work that goes above the standard costs of our [Website: Up to 3](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/products/MP-VM63SLW2BWQPQ2XCP3XK52RHWB4FC8TC)/[5](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/products/MP-626DQ35GTDK77P2GX37G6KQGM3NKF338)/[10 Pages](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/products/MP-NJ4M8RMVQJS635F6LPTKDSDXBNC56NWZ) products.Our frameworks can be personalized to maintain or establish brand consistency.

**Can you redesign my existing site?**

Yes, we can! With any build, we ask for your current site as well as any sites that you like to get a starting point and better understand your design preferences. Upon receiving those, we can suggest a framework that mimics your inspiration and functionality. As a note, if the site has increased functionality above our [templated frameworks](https://frameworks.yourdigitalagents.com/), please [reach out for a quote](mailto:marketingservices@yourdigitalagents.com) prior to ordering so we can review the desired functionality and/or the specific design that you would like to mimic.

**Can I send you a mockup with Figma, Adobe, etc?**

We'd be happy to work with you based on a specific mock. As these cases will require design customization, we will need to quote the custom work and additional development time required, even when leveraging a framework that has some similar functionality and sections.  
Our templated website products are priced based on the work of using those templates. When we start changing the designs and/or using alternative templates/frameworks, these take more time than our standard templated builds. Allow us to review the project scope and provide you with the requirements to fulfill this project so you can be all set to place your website order with us!
