# Digital Advertising

Vendasta offers digital advertising through **Managed Ad Campaign** (do-it-for-me, single-network, same-day launch), **Specialty Ad Campaign** (multi-platform, specialist-led, fulfilled through MatchCraft), and **Vendasta Ads in Partner Center** (self-serve AI ad plans for Google, Bing, and Meta). This section covers how to choose a service, complete order forms, work with creatives and tracking, use the Advertising Intelligence Dashboard, and understand policies and platform options.

## Overview

There are three ways to get a digital ad campaign running, depending on which platforms you need, how hands-on you want to be, and whether you have a dedicated Vendasta Ads Enterprise instance:

- [**Managed Ad Campaign**](./managed-ad-campaign.md) – A do-it-for-me campaign on one network (Google/Bing Search, Google Display, or Meta), built and launched the same business day the order is placed, then optimized continuously by the platform and our specialists. Best for the campaigns most clients need, live fast.
- [**Specialty Ad Campaign**](./specialty-ad-campaign.md) – A do-it-with-me, multi-platform campaign (search, social, display, video) built and optimized by specialists. Best for growth and conversions, and the route to LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Performance Max, Yelp, and Amazon.
- [**Vendasta Ads in Partner Center**](./vendasta-ads-partner-center.md) – A self-serve tool that generates an AI-assisted ad plan for Google, Bing, or Meta, lets you share a proposal with your client, and orders the campaign directly in Partner Center. Best for partners without a dedicated Vendasta Ads Enterprise instance who want a plan built in minutes.

Campaigns are billed monthly: media spend plus a management fee calculated on that spend. Reporting is available in the Advertising Intelligence Dashboard.

## Get started

1. **Choose your service.** [Managed Ad Campaign](./managed-ad-campaign.md) for a single network launched the same day, [Specialty Ad Campaign](./specialty-ad-campaign.md) for a multi-platform, conversion-focused campaign, or [Vendasta Ads in Partner Center](./vendasta-ads-partner-center.md) for a fast, self-serve plan and proposal.
2. **Order and provide details.** For a Managed Ad Campaign, place the order in Partner Center with the network, monthly media spend, and campaign assets. For a Specialty Ad Campaign, complete the fulfillment form and attend the onboarding call. For Vendasta Ads, generate a plan in Partner Center, share the proposal with your client, and order once it's approved.
3. **Launch.** Managed Ad Campaigns go live the same business day the order is placed. Specialty Ad Campaigns include one major round of revisions and your approval before going live.
4. **Track performance** anytime in the [Advertising Intelligence Dashboard](./advertising-intelligence-dashboard-partner-setup-overview.md).

<details>
<summary>How do I request a change to my ad spend?</summary>

Use **Request Spend Change** for *future* months: go to **Partner Center** → **Manage Accounts**, select the account, then the three dots next to the active MatchCraft product. Enter the new monthly budget. Changes apply from the next billing cycle and do not affect the current month's spend. To add funds to the current month, use the One-Time Boost add-on.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the refund policy for ad spend?</summary>

- **Unused ad spend:** Once an ad spend amount is agreed upon, unused spend is **not refunded**. When money is collected, the budget is always exhausted for the campaign and the current month.
- **Cancellations and spend changes:** Require **5 business days' notice**. If a change is submitted at the last minute and the renewal happens, the campaign runs at the renewed amount and the change takes effect the following month. Emergency pauses (for example, seasonality) are held as a credit and used on re-activation.
- **Promotional campaigns:** If a promotion ends before the budget is exhausted, the team creates general ads to run with the remaining spend.
- **Campaign performance:** No refunds for performance discrepancies, but the team will hold a complimentary meeting to re-strategize for the following month.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I see campaign performance?</summary>

Use the [Advertising Intelligence Dashboard](./advertising-intelligence-dashboard-partner-setup-overview.md). Connect accounts in settings, set management fees, choose metrics, and view performance by platform and campaign. Reporting is also shared via a live link from the ads team.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What YouTube ad formats are available?</summary>

YouTube ads can be placed before ("pre"), during ("mid"), or after ("post") a video. The main formats:

| Format | Description | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Skippable video ads** | Viewers can skip after 5 seconds. | Plays in the video player; skip after 5 seconds. |
| **Non-skippable video ads** | Must be watched before the video plays. | 15 or 20 seconds, depending on regional standards. |
| **Bumper ads** | Short, non-skippable ads up to 6 seconds; enabled when skippable or non-skippable ads are on. | Up to 6 seconds. |
| **Overlay ads** | Image or text ads on the lower 20% of a video (computer only). | 468x60 or 728x90 pixels. |

Source: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2467968).
</details>
