# Digital Advertising

Vendasta Services offers managed digital advertising through **MatchCraft**: **Express Ads** (fast, search-only, lower budget) and **Managed Ads Campaign** (multi-platform, conversion-focused, specialist-led). This section covers how to choose a service, complete order forms, work with creatives and tracking, use the Advertising Intelligence Dashboard, and understand policies and platform options.

## Overview

Vendasta Services runs digital ad campaigns on your behalf using MatchCraft-powered services. Depending on your goals and budget, you can use:

- **Express Ads** – AI-powered search campaigns, quick launch, low ongoing effort. Best for small budgets and awareness or promotions.
- **Managed Ads Campaign** – Search, social, display, and video with monthly optimization and conversion focus. Best for growth and scaling.

Campaigns are billed monthly (setup plus first month at order); reporting is available in the Advertising Intelligence Dashboard. Order forms, tracking (GTM, Meta Pixel), and creatives (e.g. display banners) are part of the workflow.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Which MatchCraft service should I choose?</summary>

- **Express Ads** – Lower budget, search-only, hands-off, fast launch (e.g. promotions, local awareness).
- **Managed Ads Campaign** – Moderate to higher budget, multi-platform, conversion-focused, specialist optimization.

See [MatchCraft Ads Services](./matchcraft-services-overview.md) for detailed comparison and FAQs.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I request a change to my ad spend?</summary>

Use **Request Spend Change** for *future* months: go to **Partner Center** → **Manage Accounts**, select the account, then the three dots next to the active MatchCraft product. Enter the new monthly budget. Changes apply from the next billing cycle and do not affect the current month's spend. To add funds to the current month, use the One-Time Boost add-on.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the refund policy for ad spend?</summary>

- **Unused ad spend:** Once an ad spend amount is agreed upon, unused spend is **not refunded**. When money is collected, the budget is always exhausted for the campaign and the current month.
- **Cancellations and spend changes:** Require **5 business days' notice**. If a change is submitted at the last minute and the renewal happens, the campaign runs at the renewed amount and the change takes effect the following month. Emergency pauses (for example, seasonality) are held as a credit and used on re-activation.
- **Promotional campaigns:** If a promotion ends before the budget is exhausted, the team creates general ads to run with the remaining spend.
- **Campaign performance:** No refunds for performance discrepancies, but the team will hold a complimentary meeting to re-strategize for the following month.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I see campaign performance?</summary>

Use the [Advertising Intelligence Dashboard](./advertising-intelligence-dashboard-partner-setup-overview.md). Connect accounts in settings, set management fees, choose metrics, and view performance by platform and campaign. Reporting is also shared via a live link from the ads team.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What platforms can we advertise on?</summary>

Search (Google, Bing), display (Google), Facebook & Instagram (Meta), YouTube, LinkedIn, and Amazon Display (US). Support varies by product: Express Ads is search-only; Managed Ads Campaign supports multiple platforms. See [Advertising platforms](./what-digital-ad-platforms-are-available-to-advertise-on-digital-ads-campaign.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What YouTube ad formats are available?</summary>

YouTube ads can be placed before ("pre"), during ("mid"), or after ("post") a video. The main formats:

| Format | Description | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Skippable video ads** | Viewers can skip after 5 seconds. | Plays in the video player; skip after 5 seconds. |
| **Non-skippable video ads** | Must be watched before the video plays. | 15 or 20 seconds, depending on regional standards. |
| **Bumper ads** | Short, non-skippable ads up to 6 seconds; enabled when skippable or non-skippable ads are on. | Up to 6 seconds. |
| **Overlay ads** | Image or text ads on the lower 20% of a video (computer only). | 468x60 or 728x90 pixels. |

Source: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2467968).
</details>
