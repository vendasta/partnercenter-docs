At Vendasta Services, we prioritize transparency and collaboration with our valued agency partners. One common inquiry we receive is whether we can provide admin access to our Google Ads/Analytics accounts for managed digital ad campaigns.  
  
While we appreciate the interest, there are crucial reasons why we must maintain control over these accounts:

1.  **Security:** Admin access entails control over sensitive campaign settings and data, which could be compromised if granted to unauthorized individuals.  
      
    
2.  **Unauthorized Changes:** External access risks unauthorized modifications that may disrupt campaign performance and budgeting, hindering strategic goals.  
      
    
3.  **Data Security:** Protecting client data from breaches or leaks is paramount, necessitating restricted access to mitigate risks.  
      
    
4.  **Collaboration Control:** Maintaining oversight over user access enhances collaboration efficiency and ensures alignment with clients' objectives.  
      
    
5.  **Account Integrity:** Adherence to Google's strict policies is crucial; retaining admin access allows us to monitor compliance, safeguarding against penalties.

Understanding the desire for greater transparency and control, we believe that maintaining strict control over admin access to our Google Ads/Analytics accounts is essential for safeguarding the security, integrity, and effectiveness of our partners’ and clients' campaigns.
