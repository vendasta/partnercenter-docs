When working on our ads fulfillment form, the accuracy and detail you provide are crucial for us to build the best possible campaign in alignment with your vision. In this guide, we're emphasizing certain fields that need your utmost attention:

* * *

## Select ad platforms

*   If you're unsure which platforms to select, you have the option to go with `expert's choice`.
*   If you opt for multiple platforms like Google and Bing, specify if you want ads to run on just one or both, and if you have any preferences for budget allocation between them.

## Sensitive business categories

*   It's imperative to declare if your business falls under any sensitive categories via the provided dropdown.
*   Neglecting this step can result in delays during our verification and ad setup process. Some platforms may require additional forms or applications for certain categories, and declaring these upfront can prevent potential hiccups later on.

## Define your campaign goal

*   Outline the primary objective of your campaign, whether that's to garner phone calls, leads, sales, etc.
*   Specifying this ensures that we craft your campaign to be most effective in achieving the desired outcome.

## Follow Google's advertising policies

* Google has advertising policies covering restricted ad categories, required business verifications, and other restrictions. Ensure that your requested campaign does not violate these policies.
* Review [Google's advertising policies and required certifications](https://examples.yourdigitalagents.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Google-Ads-Policies-and-Restrictions.pdf) for more detail.

* * *

Lastly, we appreciate the time and effort you invest in filling out the form. The clearer and more detailed you are, the better equipped our team will be to execute your vision.
