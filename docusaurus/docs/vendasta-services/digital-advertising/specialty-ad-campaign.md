# Specialty Ad Campaign

## Overview

Specialty Ad Campaign is a **do-it-with-me** solution where our advertising professionals work with you to create and manage an effective online advertising campaign. Campaigns can run across **search, social, display, and video**, and are conversion-focused, with an onboarding call, one major round of revisions, ongoing optimization, and reporting through Advertising Intelligence. It's the right fit for businesses ready to scale and focus on results.

## What's Included

* **Specialist-built, multi-platform campaign** tailored to your goals and budget.
* **Onboarding call** to align on the campaign type and advertising goals.
* **One major round of revisions** before launch, plus a final approval.
* **Quality assurance** pre- and post-launch: checks approximately 48 hours, 4 business days, and 14 business days post-launch to confirm the campaign is pacing properly.
* **Ongoing monthly optimization:** automated bidding, built-in budget management, and smart optimizations make the most of your ad spend; optimization continues on a monthly cadence until the campaign is terminated.
* **Advertising Intelligence reporting** connected in Business App, available throughout the campaign period.
* **Audience segments:** depending on the ad spend tier, you'll get **4–6 audience segments** (subgroups based on geographic location, gender identity, income, and more).

### Platforms

Your campaign can run across a range of platforms. The platforms available depend on your goals and spend level—see [Advertising platforms](./what-digital-ad-platforms-are-available-to-advertise-on-digital-ads-campaign.md) for full details.

* **Search — Google & Bing:** Text ads shown when people actively search for your services (pay-per-click).
* **Display — Google:** Image banners across websites to build awareness and retarget (pay-per-impression).
* **Performance Max — Google:** Goal-based campaigns that run across Google's full inventory (Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Maps).
* **Meta — Facebook & Instagram:** Social ads for demand creation with precise audience targeting.
* **YouTube:** Video ads before, during, or after videos, for reach and awareness.
* **TikTok:** Short-form vertical (9:16) video in the For You feed and other placements, for reach and awareness.
* **LinkedIn:** Business-focused ads for recruitment, lead generation, and visibility (higher minimum ad spend).

### Add-ons

* **Boost (one-time):** a one-time increase in ad spend to generate more views, clicks, conversions, and calls—ideal for short-term promotions. The additional spend can be added in the current campaign cycle as long as our team has 2–3 business days' notice; any amount not spent within the cycle is applied to the following month.
* **Call Tracking:** one forwarding number to track calls from your campaigns and listen to call recordings, within a 500-minute monthly limit. A great way to prove ROI.
* **Landing Page:** a professionally built landing page to complement your campaign and encourage actions like form fills, calls, or store visits. Requires a high-resolution logo and images (stock photos can be used as needed).
* **Strategy Call (monthly):** a monthly 30-minute call with an ads strategist to discuss your campaign's strategy and performance.

## Prerequisites

Complete the order and fulfillment form with as much detail as possible. **Timeline:** we'll review the order and send a confirmation email within 2 business days.

### Campaign assets

To build a successful campaign, we need four assets:

* **Advertiser's logo:** minimum 250px wide; PNG, EPS, Ai, or PSD files preferred.
* **Images:** 1–3 high-resolution images related to the promotion. Image size 1,200 × 628 pixels; image ratio 1.91:1.
* **Promotion & slogan:** ads perform significantly better with a promotion attached. Provide a short, concise slogan and/or promotional offer, 2 sentences maximum (unless otherwise specified).
* **Website link:** a hyperlink where interested customers can find more information about the business or promotion.

### Platform-specific requirements

* **For Facebook/Instagram advertising:** access to approve Meta Business Manager requests; 1:1 ratio, 1080 × 1080 pixel resolution images; a Meta Pixel.
* **For Google Display or Performance Max advertising**, banner images in these sizes:
  * 728×90 (Leaderboard)
  * 300×250 (Medium banner)
  * 300×600 (Half Page)
  * 160×600 (Wide Skyscraper)
  * 320×50 (Mobile leaderboard)
  * 300×50 (Mobile banner)

<details>
<summary>How to grant Facebook Page access</summary>

If your campaign includes advertising on Facebook, we need access to the Facebook Page to run it. Our team sends a request from our Facebook Business Manager (Online Marketing Solutions).

**Method 1: Desktop (directly through Facebook.com)**

1. Log in to Facebook and navigate to your Page.
2. On the left-hand sidebar, click **Professional Dashboard**.
3. Scroll down the left menu until you see **Page Access**.
4. Look for a section titled **Pending Partner Requests** or **Invitations**.
5. Click **Respond to Request** and follow the prompts to **Give Access** to Online Marketing Solutions.

**Method 2: Meta Business Suite**

1. Go to business.facebook.com.
2. Ensure your specific Page is selected in the top-left dropdown.
3. Click **Settings** (the gear icon, usually at the bottom of the left sidebar).
4. Click **Business Settings** (this may open a new tab).
5. Under the **Requests** tab in the left-hand menu, click **Received**.
6. You should see the Business Manager's name, Online Marketing Solutions. Click **Approve**.

**A few things to keep in mind:**

* **Admin rights:** You must be an Admin of the Facebook Page (or the Business Suite) to see and approve these requests. If you're only an Editor, the request won't appear for you.
* **Email notifications:** Facebook usually sends an automated email with a "Respond Now" button. If you can't find it, check your Social or Spam folders.
* Once sent, the request may take up to a day to be transmitted.
</details>

## Getting Started

1. **Fulfillment form** — Complete the order and fulfillment form with as much detail as possible. We review the order and send a confirmation email within **2 business days**.
2. **Onboarding call** — The confirmation email includes a booking link. The call is your opportunity to share information about the campaign; prepare by confirming your desired campaign and advertising goals. It can be booked in as little as **1 business day**, depending on availability.
3. **Campaign build** — Our team builds the campaign from the fulfillment form and onboarding call. We create the campaign for your review and approval within **5 business days** of the onboarding call.
4. **Revisions** — We request that you complete **one major round of revisions** so we can get the campaign right while keeping launch on track.
5. **Final approval and launch** — After we implement changes and complete our quality-assurance process, the campaign is shared once more for final approval. Once approved, we set it live within **5 days** (campaigns are never set live without your approval). Whenever possible, we launch within **30 days**.
6. **Ongoing support** — The campaign is connected to Advertising Intelligence in Business App for reporting. We run quality-assurance checks ~48 hours, 4 business days, and 14 business days post-launch, then optimize on a monthly cadence until the campaign is terminated. When a campaign performs well, increasing the budget can drive more awareness or conversions.

### Ending the campaign

The campaign continues to run with the same monthly budget and ongoing optimizations until you cancel. Terminating a campaign means cancelling the associated products in Partner Center. Please cancel at least **5 business days** before the renewal date. Our team turns the campaign off at the end of the current month's term and exhausts all remaining unused ad spend. **We do not offer refunds for unused ad spend.**

### Service limitations

* **Results guarantees:** We can't guarantee specific results. We implement best-practice strategies, but platform algorithms are unpredictable, so it would be unethical to promise results that can't be guaranteed.
* **Fluctuating timelines:** We launch as quickly as possible, but we can't launch without ironing out every important detail first. Completing the fulfillment form fully helps your campaign launch far more seamlessly.
* **Hold off on post-launch changes:** Making changes right after launch is not ideal. Unless a change is crucial, we recommend waiting at least **5 weeks** so the campaign has time to run and gather data. Once a change is made, our team needs several weeks to re-optimize the campaign, so frequent edits can reset that learning period and work against performance.

## FAQ

### Billing

<details>
<summary>How will I be charged for this product?</summary>

This service charges a one-time setup fee, a monthly management fee (calculated as a percentage of the spend), and the selected ad spend amount. On initial activation, you're charged the first month's spend, the management fee, and the setup fee. The following month, you're charged only the ad spend amount and the management fee.

The **setup fee** is the one-time cost of creating and launching the campaign. It includes the creation and quality assurance of the ad, the setup and verification of the tracking code, the audience research, and the campaign audit and launch.

The **management fee** is a percentage charged on top of the monthly ad spend. It ensures our team can optimize, analyze, and report on the campaign each month.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I want to add budget to, pause, or cancel my campaign?</summary>

The agreed ad spend budget is always exhausted before the campaign is put on hold or canceled, and we don't offer refunds for unused ad spend. We request a **5-day notice period** for all spend change requests and cancellations so we can pause or cancel the account correctly. If a change request is submitted at the last minute and the campaign renews, we run the campaign for the renewed amount, and the spend change, pause, or cancellation takes effect the following month.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the cancellation or refund policy?</summary>

All cancellations must be made **5 days prior to the renewal date**. If a cancellation is requested after this cutoff, the renewal occurs and we run the campaign for the renewed amount until the end of the month. When the cancellation request is made, our team spends the remainder of the ad dollars for that cycle and does not run the campaign the following month.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How does the management fee work when I increase ad spend or add a Boost?</summary>

The management fee is a percentage based on the wholesale ad spend, added on top of the spend. If the ad spend increases, you'll see the exact dollar amount you'll be charged for the management fees, whether the change comes from a Boost or additional spend.
</details>
