Vendasta Services hosts online training sessions exclusively for partners to provide insider knowledge and insights into our fulfillment offerings and processes. These training sessions are crucial to strengthening alignment and ensuring a smooth fulfillment process, from pre-sale to delivery to ongoing support.

With a wide range of session times available, our training webinars make it easy to learn at a time that works best for you. **Session links to join are accessible via the [Webinar calendar](http://www.vendasta.com/webinar).**

## Topics

### Getting started with Vendasta Services

This session introduces you to Vendasta Services, whether you're new to fulfillment or an experienced partner. It covers pre-sale materials, quote generation, proposal requests, and the activation process, along with real-world fulfillment examples and guidance on setting client expectations.

### Fulfilling websites with Vendasta Services

This session covers website project scoping and fulfillment with Vendasta Services. It explains how to scope website projects so they align with client goals and expectations, and how to work through the fulfillment process.

### Fulfilling digital ads with Vendasta Services

This session covers recommended campaign types for different business objectives and compares advertising platforms to help you choose the best fit for your clients. It also covers Vendasta Services' digital ads offerings.

### Fulfilling social and long-form content with Vendasta Services

This session covers how to set client expectations for social and long-form content, and how social posting differs from digital ads. It also covers how to build a social media content strategy.

### Fulfilling listings and reputation AI with Vendasta Services

This session covers managing online listings and reputation with Vendasta Services, including claiming and verifying listings and Google Business Profile guidance. It also covers listing management differences between physical-location and service-area businesses.
