Vendasta Services hosts online training sessions exclusively for partners to provide insider knowledge and insights into our fulfillment offerings and processes. These training sessions are crucial to strengthening alignment and ensuring a smooth fulfillment process—from pre-sale to delivery to ongoing support!

These sessions, hosted by Vendasta Services’ Partner Training Specialist Kendall Johns, are the key to success! 

With a wide range of session times available, our training webinars make it easy to learn at a time that works best for you. **Session links to join are accessible via the [Webinar calendar](http://www.vendasta.com/webinar).**

## **Topics**

**_Getting Started_ with Vendasta Services  
**This session serves as your initiation into the world of Vendasta Services, whether you're a seasoned professional or new to the game. Delve into our comprehensive pre-sale materials, gain insights into quote generation and proposal requests, and master the activation process seamlessly. Elevate your understanding with real-world fulfillment examples and learn to set client expectations effectively, ensuring a proactive approach for peak efficiency.

**Fulfilling _Websites_ with Vendasta Services  
**Unravel the intricacies of website project scoping and fulfillment processes with insider knowledge from Vendasta Services. Discover the art of effective scoping for website projects, ensuring alignment with client goals and expectations. Navigate our fulfillment processes with ease, gaining insights that transcend beyond Vendasta Services offerings, empowering you to craft actionable plans for website success.

**Fulfilling _Digital Ads_ with Vendasta Services  
**Explore recommended campaign types tailored for various business objectives and dive deep into platform comparisons to identify the best fit for your clients. Gain a comprehensive understanding of Vendasta Services' digital ads offerings, equipping you with the knowledge to craft robust advertising campaigns.

**Fulfilling _Social & Long-form Content_ with Vendasta Services  
**Elevate your brand's online presence with insights from Vendasta Services in this multifaceted session. Learn to set achievable client expectations and distinguish between social posting and digital ads for maximum impact. Gain insider knowledge of how to build a successful social media content strategy. Learn tips to navigate the ever-evolving landscape of social and long-form content, ensuring your brand stands out in the digital sphere.

**Fulfilling _Listings & Reputation Management_ with Vendasta Services  
**In this session, delve into the critical aspects of managing online listings and reputation effectively with Vendasta Services. Gain invaluable insights into claiming and verifying online listings, including detailed guidance on Google Business Profile listings. Understand the nuances between different business types and their corresponding listing management strategies, whether for a physical location or a service area business. This session equips you with the knowledge and tools necessary to navigate the intricacies of online listings and reputation management, empowering you to bolster your brand's online presence and reputation effectively.
