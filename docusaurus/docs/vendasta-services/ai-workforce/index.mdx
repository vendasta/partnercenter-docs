# AI Workforce

Vendasta Services configures and runs **AI employees** for you. For each AI employee, you choose one of two options: a one-time **Setup**, or the monthly **AI Workforce Optimization Plan**.

## Setup vs. AI Workforce Optimization Plan

| | **Setup** | **AI Workforce Optimization Plan** |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | One-time, done-for-you configuration and launch | Ongoing monthly subscription |
| **Billing** | One-time fee | Monthly fee |
| **Setup included?** | Yes | Yes — one setup, delivered in Month 1 |
| **Ongoing management?** | No | Yes |
| **Best for** | Getting an AI employee live | Keeping it performing over time |

:::info Each activation covers one AI employee
Each Setup and each Optimization Plan covers a **single AI employee**—not multiple. To add another AI employee, activate a separate Setup or Optimization Plan for each one.
:::

:::warning Setup is only offered for the AI employees listed below

Setups and Optimization Plans are available **only for the AI employees listed in [AI employees](#ai-employees)**. Our team does not build, configure, or support custom AI employees, custom personas, or roles outside that list, no matter how close they are to something on it.

If a client's use case isn't covered by one of the AI employees below, position the closest listed AI employee or confirm with the services team before selling.

:::

## Setup

A one-time, done-for-you service that configures and launches one AI employee. Each setup includes:

- Technical configuration and customization
- Branding and customized instructions
- Follow-up calls to refine knowledge and workflows

## AI Workforce Optimization Plan

An ongoing monthly subscription that keeps your AI employee performing after launch. It includes:

- **One AI employee setup**, delivered in Month 1 (same scope as a standalone Setup)
- A proactive **monthly check-in**
- **Unlimited change requests** (same-day acknowledgment, 2-business-day resolution target)
- **Email access** to the expert team
- A **live training session** for your team

## AI employees

Choose the AI employee to set up or manage. **This is the complete list** — setup is not offered for any AI employee, persona, or role outside it.

- **[AI Receptionist](./ai-receptionist.md)** – Web chat and lead capture, CRM pipeline, booking and service-area connections, and automations.
- **[AI Reputation Specialist](./ai-reputation-specialist.md)** – Review responses and requests, reputation workflows, and external-platform support. Requires Reputation AI Premium.
- **[AI Inside Sales Representative](./ai-inside-sales-representative.md)** – A sales persona that qualifies and captures leads and books appointments. Requires Conversations AI.
- **[AI Support Agent](./ai-support-agent.md)** – Customer support across channels, escalations, and lead capture. Requires Conversations AI.
- **[AI Data Analyst](./ai-data-analyst.md)** – Conversational insights from CRM, review, and social data. Requires Conversations AI plus at least one data source.
- **[AI Human Resources Coordinator](./ai-human-resources-coordinator.md)** – Internal HR support and meeting scheduling. Requires Conversations AI.
- **[AI Social Media Manager](./ai-social-media-manager.md)** – Autonomous social content calendar across your connected networks, brand training, and approval workflows. Requires Social AI Premium.
- **[AI Blogger](./ai-blogger.md)** – Autonomous, SEO-focused blog calendar published to WordPress, with brand training and approval workflows. Requires Social AI Premium and a WordPress site.
