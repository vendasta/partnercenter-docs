# AI Workforce Setup

Vendasta Services offers done-for-you AI workforce setup so you can get AI employees configured quickly. The team handles technical configuration and customization for **AI Receptionist** (web chat, lead capture, booking, automations), **AI Reputation Specialist** (review responses, review requests, reputation workflows), and **AI Data Analyst** (structured insights across CRM, reviews, and social data). This section covers what’s included, the setup process, and how to get the most out of each service.

## What is AI Workforce Setup?

AI Workforce Setup is a set of done-for-you services that configure and launch AI employees:

- **AI Receptionist Setup** – Web chat and lead capture on your website, CRM pipeline and smart list, booking and service-area connections, and 3–5 automations. Process: initial training call, configuration (1 business day, or 3 for custom integrations), onboarding and walkthrough call, 30-day check-in. U.S. businesses need A2P registration for SMS; website backend access is required for chatbot and lead form installation.
- **AI Reputation Specialist Setup** – **Requires Reputation AI Premium.** One-time setup: onboarding and training call, configure and train the AI, automated review requests (SMS + email), AI-powered review responses, review display widgets, brand voice and templates. Monthly service: prompt and automation adjustments, responses on platforms outside Google & Facebook (up to 5 external platforms), negative review approval workflow. Process: fulfillment form, onboarding call, setup (1 business day after call), optional 30-day check-in, ongoing monthly support.
- **AI Data Analyst Setup** – **Requires Conversations AI (any edition) plus at least one of CRM AI, Reputation AI, Social Marketing, or Local SEO.** Do-it-with-me setup of a conversational AI Employee that delivers structured insights in the AIR (Analyze, Interpret, Recommend) framework across connected data sources: CRM (contacts, companies, activities), Reviews/NPS, and Social engagement. Process: fulfillment form, onboarding call, configuration (1 business day, or 3 for multiple data sources), training call, 30-day check-in. No website installation required.

All services include branding, customized instructions, and follow-up calls to refine knowledge and workflows.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What’s the difference between AI Receptionist and AI Reputation Specialist?</summary>

**AI Receptionist** focuses on web chat and lead capture: capturing leads from your website, CRM pipeline, booking and service-area lookups, and automations for notifications and follow-ups. **AI Reputation Specialist** focuses on reviews: automated review responses, review requests (email and SMS), reputation workflows, and optional review widgets. Reputation AI Premium is required for the Reputation Specialist setup. See [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md) and [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does AI Receptionist setup take?</summary>

After the initial training call, configuration is complete in 1 business day (or 3 business days for workflows with custom integrations). The onboarding and walkthrough call can be booked as soon as one business day after setup; the 30-day check-in is 3–4 weeks after completion. U.S. businesses should allow up to two weeks for A2P registration if using SMS. See [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need Reputation AI for the AI Reputation Specialist?</summary>

Yes. Reputation AI Premium is required to receive the AI Reputation Specialist Setup service. See [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What’s included in AI Reputation Specialist monthly support?</summary>

Monthly support includes adjustments to prompts, automations, and templates; responses to reviews on platforms outside Google and Facebook (up to 5 external platforms); and an optional negative review approval workflow. See [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I get just the web chat code installed without full AI Receptionist setup?</summary>

If you have [Website Support](../websites/vendasta-services-website-support.md) active, the Website Support team can install the web chat code. That is basic code installation only, not full end-to-end AI Receptionist configuration. See [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the AI Data Analyst actually do?</summary>

The AI Data Analyst is a conversational AI Employee inside Business App that answers business questions using your connected data (CRM, Reviews/NPS, Social). Every response follows the AIR framework: **Analyze** a pattern in the data, **Interpret** what it means, and **Recommend** a next step. It's an internal tool for owners and managers, with no customer-facing components. See [AI Data Analyst Setup](./ai-data-analyst-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What editions do I need for AI Data Analyst Setup?</summary>

**Conversations AI** must be active (any edition is supported), plus at least one of **CRM AI**, **Reputation AI**, **Social Marketing**, or **Local SEO** so the analyst has data to work with. No other Premium editions are required. The more data sources are active and populated, the richer the insights. See [AI Data Analyst Setup](./ai-data-analyst-setup.md).
</details>
