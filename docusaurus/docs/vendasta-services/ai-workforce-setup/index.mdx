# AI Workforce Setup & Optimization Plan

Vendasta Services offers two distinct services for AI employees: a **done-for-you setup** that configures and launches your AI employee, and an **ongoing optimization plan** that keeps it performing after launch. These are separate services — setup is a one-time engagement, and the optimization plan is a monthly subscription.

---

## AI Workforce Setup

AI Workforce Setup configures and launches AI employees. Each service covers technical configuration, branding, training calls, and a 30-day check-in to refine knowledge and workflows.

### [AI Data Analyst Setup](./ai-data-analyst-setup.md)

**Requires Conversations AI plus at least one of CRM AI, Reputation AI, Social Marketing, or Local SEO.** Conversational AI that delivers structured insights across your connected data.

- **Includes:** AIR framework analysis (Analyze, Interpret, Recommend) across CRM, Reviews/NPS, and Social data
- **Process:** Fulfillment form → onboarding call → configuration (1 business day, or 3 for multiple data sources) → training call → 30-day check-in
- **Requirements:** No website installation required

### [AI Human Resources Coordinator Setup](./ai-human-resources-coordinator-setup.md)

**Requires Conversations AI.** Internal HR assistant that answers policy questions, routes sensitive inquiries to the right contact, and declines out-of-scope requests.

- **Includes:** AI Employee profile, PolicyScope framework (3-level routing), escalation contact configuration, knowledge base, optional appointment booking and Google Drive sync
- **Process:** Fulfillment form → onboarding call → HR Coordinator setup (1 business day, or 3 for large or complex policy libraries) → training call → 30-day check-in
- **Requirements:** Escalation contact information; HR policy documents and handbooks; no website installation required

### [AI Inside Sales Representative Setup](./ai-inside-sales-representative-setup.md)

**Requires Conversations AI.** Sales-qualified AI Employee that qualifies leads, handles objections, and books appointments.

- **Includes:** AI Employee profile, sales role prompt, lead qualification logic, lead capture sequence (Name → Phone → Email), appointment booking, knowledge base
- **Process:** Fulfillment form → onboarding call → ISR setup (1 business day, or 3 for custom integrations/complex knowledge bases) → training call → 30-day check-in
- **Requirements:** Connected Google, Outlook, or Microsoft calendar for appointment booking; A2P registration for U.S. businesses using SMS

### [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md)

Web chat lead capture, CRM pipeline, booking, service-area connections, and automations.

- **Includes:** Chat widget installation, CRM pipeline and smart list, booking and service-area connections, 3–5 automations
- **Process:** Training call → configuration (1 business day, or 3 for custom integrations) → onboarding and walkthrough call → 30-day check-in
- **Requirements:** Website backend access for chatbot and lead form installation; A2P registration for U.S. businesses using SMS

### [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md)

**Requires Reputation AI Premium.** Automated review responses, review requests, and reputation workflows.

- **One-time setup includes:** Onboarding and training call, review request automation (SMS + email), AI-powered review responses, review display widgets, brand voice and templates
- **Monthly service includes:** Prompt and automation adjustments, responses on platforms outside Google and Facebook (up to 5 external platforms), negative review approval workflow
- **Process:** Fulfillment form → onboarding call → setup (1 business day) → optional 30-day check-in → ongoing monthly support

### [AI Support Agent Setup](./ai-support-agent-setup.md)

**Requires Conversations AI.** Customer-facing support agent that resolves issues and only escalates when it can't help.

- **Includes:** AI Employee profile, HEARD framework configuration, escalation logic, channel setup, chat widget installation, knowledge base
- **Channels:** Web chat, SMS, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct, WhatsApp, email
- **Process:** Fulfillment form → onboarding call → agent setup (1 business day, or 3 for multiple channels/complex knowledge bases) → training call → 30-day check-in
- **Requirements:** Website backend access for chat widget installation; A2P registration for U.S. businesses using SMS

## AI Workforce Optimization Plan

The AI Workforce Optimization Plan is a monthly subscription that keeps your AI employee performing after launch. Setting up an AI employee is only the beginning — real value comes from the steady tuning that follows. This plan gives your team a direct line to Vendasta's experts so prompts, automations, and settings can evolve as your business does, without you having to do the technical work yourself.

### What's included

- **1 AI employee setup:** Choose your AI employee at purchase. Setup is delivered in Month 1 and follows our standard setup scope: tasking, prompts, automations, and settings.
- **Monthly AI employee check-in:** A proactive touchpoint each month where our team reaches out to ask for business updates and confirm the accuracy and overall performance of the AI employee.
- **Coverage for AI employees set up by our team:** Ongoing optimization coverage applies to AI employees configured by Vendasta's services team within the standard setup scope: tasking, prompts, automations, and settings.
- **Unlimited changes:** Request as many changes as you need to prompts, automations, and AI employee settings.
- **Email access to our expert team:** Submit requests by email and receive a same-day acknowledgment. Our target is to fully resolve standard requests within 2 business days.
- **Live training session for your team:** A live, guided training session so your team understands how the AI employee works, how to spot issues, and how to make the most of the optimization plan.

### What's not included

The AI Workforce Optimization Plan is focused on tuning and operating AI employees within the Vendasta platform. It is not a custom development engagement. The following are out of scope:

- **Sophisticated automations and integrations beyond the platform:** Work that goes beyond what can be configured within the Vendasta platform in a reasonable amount of time.
- **Custom software development:** Writing, hosting, or maintaining custom code, scripts, or APIs on your behalf.
- **Third-party tool buildouts and administration:** Configuring, administering, or paying for third-party tools and accounts that sit outside the Vendasta platform.
- **Strategic consulting and business process design:** Designing your overall go-to-market strategy, sales process, or operational workflows.
- **Content creation and creative production:** Producing long-form marketing content, brand assets, or creative deliverables.
- **Net-new AI employees beyond the one included:** Additional AI employees can be added through a separate setup engagement.
- **Coverage for AI employees you configured yourself:** Self-configured AI employees can be brought into scope through a setup engagement.
- **Guaranteed business outcomes:** We commit to expert configuration and ongoing optimization, not to specific revenue, lead, or conversion outcomes.

:::tip Need something out of scope?
If a request falls outside the optimization plan, we'll let you know. We can scope your request to see if it's something we can set up and support, and let you know if additional charges apply.
:::

<summary>What to expect: response times, check-ins, and change requests</summary>

**Response and resolution**

- **Same-day acknowledgment.** Every request you email in is acknowledged the same business day so you know it has been received and is queued.
- **2-business-day resolution target.** Most standard requests — prompt edits, small automation tweaks, settings updates — are resolved within two business days. Larger or more complex requests may take longer, or not be included; if so, we'll set expectations up front.
- **Business hours.** Requests are handled during standard business hours, Monday to Friday. Requests received outside of business hours are picked up on the next business day.

**Monthly check-ins**

- Each month, we proactively reach out to ensure your AI employee is performing as expected or to identify any necessary updates.
- If you know of upcoming changes to your business, please notify us during these meetings so we can make proactive adjustments.

**Change requests**

- Submit change requests by emailing marketingservices@yourdigitalagents.com. Please include the AI employee, what you'd like changed, and the desired outcome.
- There is no cap on the number of requests per month — but the more clearly you describe the desired outcome, the faster we can deliver it.
- We will confirm changes once they are live so you can validate them in your environment.

</details>

**Vendasta will**

- Set up your included AI employee within the standard setup scope in Month 1.

## Frequently asked questions

### AI Workforce Setup

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between AI Receptionist and AI Reputation Specialist?</summary>

**AI Receptionist** focuses on web chat and lead capture: capturing leads from your website, CRM pipeline, booking and service-area lookups, and automations for notifications and follow-ups. **AI Reputation Specialist** focuses on reviews: automated review responses, review requests (email and SMS), reputation workflows, and optional review widgets. Reputation AI Premium is required for the Reputation Specialist setup. See [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md) and [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does AI Receptionist setup take?</summary>

After the initial training call, configuration is complete in 1 business day (or 3 business days for workflows with custom integrations). The onboarding and walkthrough call can be booked as soon as one business day after setup; the 30-day check-in is 3–4 weeks after completion. U.S. businesses should allow up to two weeks for A2P registration if using SMS. See [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need Reputation AI for the AI Reputation Specialist?</summary>

Yes. Reputation AI Premium is required to receive the AI Reputation Specialist Setup service. See [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's included in AI Reputation Specialist monthly support?</summary>

Monthly support includes adjustments to prompts, automations, and templates; responses to reviews on platforms outside Google and Facebook (up to 5 external platforms); and an optional negative review approval workflow. See [AI Reputation Specialist Setup](./ai-reputation-specialist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I get just the web chat code installed without full AI Receptionist setup?</summary>

If you have [Website Support](../websites/vendasta-services-website-support.md) active, the Website Support team can install the web chat code. That is basic code installation only, not full end-to-end AI Receptionist configuration. See [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the AI Data Analyst actually do?</summary>

The AI Data Analyst is a conversational AI Employee inside Business App that answers business questions using your connected data (CRM, Reviews/NPS, Social). Every response follows the AIR framework: **Analyze** a pattern in the data, **Interpret** what it means, and **Recommend** a next step. It's an internal tool for owners and managers, with no customer-facing components. See [AI Data Analyst Setup](./ai-data-analyst-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What editions do I need for AI Data Analyst Setup?</summary>

**Conversations AI** must be active (any edition is supported), plus at least one of **CRM AI**, **Reputation AI**, **Social Marketing**, or **Local SEO** so the analyst has data to work with. No other Premium editions are required. The more data sources are active and populated, the richer the insights. See [AI Data Analyst Setup](./ai-data-analyst-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between the AI Support Agent and the AI Receptionist?</summary>

The **AI Receptionist** is built for lead capture: it collects contact information from website visitors and routes inquiries to your sales team. The **AI Support Agent** is built for customer support: it resolves issues, answers questions, handles complaints using the HEARD framework, and only escalates to your team when it cannot help. If your primary goal is support resolution rather than lead capture, the AI Support Agent is the right fit. See [AI Support Agent Setup](./ai-support-agent-setup.md) and [AI Receptionist Setup](./ai-receptionist-setup.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which channels does the AI Support Agent handle?</summary>

The AI Support Agent operates across **web chat**, **SMS**, **Facebook Messenger**, **Instagram Direct**, **WhatsApp**, and **email**. All active channels route to the same agent, so customers get a consistent experience regardless of how they reach out. U.S. businesses using SMS must complete A2P registration. See [AI Support Agent Setup](./ai-support-agent-setup.md).
</details>

### AI Workforce Optimization Plan

The plan includes setup and ongoing optimization for one AI employee. Additional AI employees can be added by activating another instance of the same solution.
</details>

No. Changes to prompts, automations, and AI employee settings are unlimited. Larger or more complex requests may take additional time or charges to deliver — we'll always set expectations up front.

Standard requests include anything that we originally set up for the AI employee — prompt edits, small automation adjustments, and settings changes. Work that requires custom development or integrations beyond the platform is out of scope.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if my AI employee was set up by someone other than Vendasta?</summary>

Optimization coverage applies to AI employees set up by our team within the standard setup scope. If yours was self-configured, talk to your services contact about a setup engagement to bring it into scope.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I submit a request?</summary>

Email marketingservices@yourdigitalagents.com with the AI employee in question, the change you'd like made, and the desired outcome. You'll receive a same-day acknowledgment and an expected resolution timeline.
</details>
