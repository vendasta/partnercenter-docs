# Sales & Marketing Assets

Vendasta Services sales and marketing assets are resources you can use to sell and promote Vendasta Services to your clients: marketing information by product in the Marketplace, a rebrandable sales deck, and unbranded marketing videos. This section points you to each one.

## What are sales & marketing assets?

Sales and marketing assets are resources you can use to sell and advertise Vendasta Services:

- **Marketing information by product** – In the Marketplace, each product page has **Screenshots and Files** with sales decks and other assets. Quick links are available for key products (listings, social, Specialty Ad Campaign, templated website).
- **Rebrandable sales deck** – A full deck showcasing Vendasta Services product categories. You can use it as-is, remove or adjust slides, or pull images and copy into your own materials. It pairs with the Vendasta Go-to-Market Playbook.
- **Marketing videos** – Unbranded videos for Digital Ads, Google Business Profile Optimization, Local Listings Management, Review Responses & Requests, Social Posting, and Website Creation. They cover what customers can expect (e.g. timelines) and best practices; each has a download link.

## Documentation

- [Marketing information by product](./marketing-information-by-product.md) – Marketplace Screenshots and Files and quick links to main product pages
- [Rebrandable sales deck](./rebrandable-sales-deck.md) – Download the deck and link to the Go-to-Market Playbook
- [Marketing videos](./marketing-videos.md) – Unbranded video downloads by service (digital ads, GBP optimization, listings, review responses, social, website creation)

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Where do I find marketing assets for a product?</summary>

Go to `Partner Center` → `Marketplace` → `Discover Products`, open the product page, then click **Screenshots and Files** at the top. You’ll find marketing images and sales decks. See [Marketing information by product](./marketing-information-by-product.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I rebrand the sales deck?</summary>

Yes. The Vendasta Services rebrandable sales deck is designed so you can use it as-is, remove or adjust slides, or take images and copy into your own materials and talk tracks. See [Rebrandable sales deck](./rebrandable-sales-deck.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are the marketing videos unbranded?</summary>

Yes. The marketing videos listed in this section are unbranded and include download links for each service (digital ads, Google Business Profile optimization, listings, review responses, social posting, website creation). See [Marketing videos](./marketing-videos.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I find the rebrandable sales deck?</summary>

Download it from [Rebrandable sales deck](./rebrandable-sales-deck.md), which also links to the Vendasta Go-to-Market Playbook that the deck pairs with.
</details>
