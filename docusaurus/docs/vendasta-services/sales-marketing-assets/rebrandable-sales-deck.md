## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What does the rebrandable sales deck cover?</summary>

The deck covers all of Vendasta's product categories.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I remove slides for products I don't sell?</summary>

Yes. You can remove slides and swap out content so the deck matches the solutions you sell.

</details>

Yes. You can pull images and copy from the deck into your existing sales materials and talk tracks.
