# Listings, Claims, and Optimization

Business listings are how a business appears on directories like **Google Business Profile**, **Apple Business Connect**, and **Bing Places**. Accurate, claimed, and optimized listings improve search visibility and help customers find contact information, hours, and location. Vendasta Services creates and claims listings, guides the business through each platform's verification, and maintains the listings from there: **Listing Claim** (one site of your choice, or a medical package), **Google Business Profile Optimization** (creation, claiming, guided verification, then monthly updates and posting), and **Local Listings Management** (Google, Apple, and Bing—onboarding call, guided verification, monthly monitoring, content updates, and retroactive review responses). This section covers what to expect from each service and how to get the most out of them.

:::warning The client completes the verification

Every listing platform requires the **business itself** to verify — by answering the primary business line, recording a video of the premises, or reading a postcard PIN. No service can do this part on their behalf, and this is the most common source of surprise on listings orders. What Vendasta Services provides is the creation, claiming, and initial setup, hands-on support through whatever the platform asks for, and full ownership transfer once the business is verified. Set this expectation at the point of sale.

:::

## Overview

Listings, claims, and optimization services help you claim, verify, and maintain a business on major directories:

- **Listing Claim** – Manual claiming and setup of **one site of your choice** (Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing, Yellow Pages, TripAdvisor, RateMDs, Doctor.com, and more), **or** a **medical package** (up to three medical-specific sites for one practitioner). Includes guided verification, data accuracy, and rich data.
- **Google Business Profile Optimization** – Fulfillment form → profile creation and claiming → guided verification (can take two weeks or more; Google may use video, phone, postcard, or email verification, completed by the business) → ownership transfer and dashboard connectivity → initial profile optimization (name, address, hours, photos, attributes). Monthly service: profile updates (accuracy, your edits, suspension support) and one service-based post per month. Eligibility: in-person businesses (storefront or service area); not for rentals, lead-gen, or online-only businesses.
- **Local Listings Management** – Across **Google**, **Apple Business Connect**, and **Bing Places**. Process: fulfillment form → required onboarding call (3 business days after order; business details and documents ready) → guided verification per platform (phone, SMS, email, video, or postcard, completed by the business; timelines vary) → ownership transfer. Monthly: profile monitoring on all three, one GBP post per month (you provide content), unlimited product/service/photo uploads on GBP and Bing, and up to 3 months of retroactive review responses on Google (with a designated approver for negative reviews).

All three services set up the claim, walk the business through the verification methods each platform requires, and work to resolve suspensions where possible.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>What’s the difference between GBP Optimization and Local Listings Management?</summary>

**Google Business Profile Optimization** is Google-only: claim, verify, initial optimization, then monthly profile updates and one post per month. **Local Listings Management** covers Google, Apple Business Connect, and Bing Places: required onboarding call, verification on each platform, monthly monitoring on all three, one GBP post (content from you), unlimited uploads on GBP and Bing, and up to 3 months of retroactive review responses on Google. See [Google Business Profile Optimization](./google-business-profile-optimization.md) and [Local Listings Management](./local-listings-management.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How long does claiming and verification take?</summary>

**GBP Optimization:** Claim and verification can take two weeks or more depending on Google’s requirements; most new claims are under a week, but complex cases can take a month or longer. **Local Listings Management:** Process starts within 2 business days; onboarding call can be booked 3 business days after order; verification varies by platform and method (minutes to weeks). See the respective guides for details.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if my listing is already claimed or gets suspended?</summary>

If a listing is already claimed by someone else, the team can request ownership and work with platform support to gain access (no guarantee). If your listing is suspended, the team will work with the platform to try to have it reinstated; platform decisions are final and transparency from them is limited. See [Google Business Profile Optimization](./google-business-profile-optimization.md) (suspensions, support tickets) and [Local Listings Management](./local-listings-management.md) (already claimed, suspensions).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What verification methods might be required?</summary>

Google may use video verification, phone call/text PIN, postcard to the business address, or email. Local Listings Management may use phone, SMS, email, video, or postcard depending on the platform. Methods are determined by the platform (e.g. business category, region), and more than one method may be required. See [Google Business Profile Optimization](./google-business-profile-optimization.md) and [Local Listings Management](./local-listings-management.md).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can the team verify a listing on the client's behalf?</summary>

No. Platforms require the business itself to complete verification, and it is tied to their phone line, address, premises, or domain. Our team creates and claims the listing, tells the client exactly what the platform is asking for and how to complete it, stays with them through repeat or multi-method verification, and takes over ownership and management once they are verified.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is an onboarding call required?</summary>

**Local Listings Management** requires an onboarding call for all customers (bookable 3 business days after order); have business details, a utility bill or business license, and Apple ID (if using existing) ready. **Google Business Profile Optimization** does not require an onboarding call; the process starts from the fulfillment form. See [Local Listings Management](./local-listings-management.md).
</details>
