# Listing Claim

## Overview

Listing Claim is a fully managed service where our Listing Fulfillment team manually claims and verifies a business listing. There are two types—choose the one that fits:

- **Regular listing claim (of your choice):** claim any one site, such as Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing, Yellow Pages, TripAdvisor, RateMDs, or Doctor.com.
- **Medical listing claim:** claim up to three medical-specific sites for one practitioner (RateMDs, Doctor.com, Vitals.com, and WebMD).

When you place the order, our Listing Fulfillment team receives the order and fulfillment form and attempts verification within 48 hours.

## What's Included

### Regular listing claim (of your choice)

- Fully managed manual claiming of **any one site**, such as Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing, Yellow Pages, TripAdvisor, RateMDs, or Doctor.com. Contact us to claim a site outside this list.
- Once claimed, we ensure your **business name, address, phone number, website, hours of operation, and category** are accurate.
- We encourage adding **rich data**—business description; interior, exterior, or staff photos; products and service offerings—for a fuller listing.
- A final email includes the login credentials to the claimed listing (if applicable).

:::note Google Business Profile specifics
When the site is **Google Business Profile**, after claiming we also add a logo, cover photo, up to 5 videos, up to 5 photos, and Highlights, Amenities, Attributes, and Service Options (where applicable). GBP cannot use PO boxes—a physical address is required. Multi-location claiming (10+ locations) is available; see [Prerequisites](#prerequisites).
:::

### Medical listing claim

- Fully managed claiming of **up to three medical-specific sites for one practitioner**: RateMDs, Doctor.com, Vitals.com, and WebMD.
- Once claimed, we ensure the **practitioner name, facility address, phone number, website, hours of operation, and categories/services** are accurate.
- We encourage adding **rich data**—description, practitioner headshot, education, facilities—for a fuller listing.
- A final email includes the login credentials to the claimed listing (if applicable).

## Prerequisites

- **A business-associated Gmail account:** we need a Gmail inbox to claim and monitor the listing and to log in after it's claimed.
- **Claiming information:** provide the required details in the fulfillment form, and pass us the **postcard PIN** promptly if postcard verification is used.
- **A physical address:** most sites require a real address. For service-area businesses, some sites (Google, Yelp) can suppress the address so it isn't shown publicly. Google Business Profile cannot use PO boxes.
- **For a medical listing claim**, the practitioner's: NPI number, driver's license image, state license number, and medical license number (as required by each site).

<details>
<summary>Google Business Profile multi-location (10+ locations)</summary>

Before requesting a multi-location claim, confirm that:

- Your spreadsheet includes 10 or more locations from the same business.
- It includes all locations for that brand you want on this account.
- The business is not in a restricted vertical.
- The business is not a service-area business.
- A verified account doesn't already exist.

What you provide:

- A branded email address and password under the brand's domain (for example, `locations@businessname.com`). Don't attach a recovery email or phone number, or we'll have difficulty signing in.
- A completed sheet with each location's business name, address, zip, phone number, website, and category. If some listings are already claimed, only the claimable ones are processed after upload.

What our team does: we log in to the new email, create a Google location group, upload the spreadsheet (**Add Business → Import Businesses →** select the file), align the primary category across locations, and submit a Bulk Listing Verification request. Google reviews the request against its quality guidelines, contacts us with any questions, and confirms once the account is bulk verified.
</details>

## Getting Started

1. **Order and complete the fulfillment form.** Our Listing Fulfillment team receives it and attempts verification within **48 hours**.
2. **Verification.** Each listing site has its own method (see below).
3. **If the listing is already claimed** by someone else, we work to unlock it (see below).
4. **Listing claim completed.** We confirm the listing data is accurate and send a final email with login credentials (if applicable).

### Verification methods

**Regular listing claim:**

- **Phone call / text message:** the listing site sends an automated call or text to the business phone number with a PIN to enter for validation. Until verified, changes won't appear publicly.
- **Postcard:** the listing site mails a physical postcard with a PIN to the business address. This can take 2 weeks or more. Don't change the listing after a postcard is requested until the PIN is received and entered; changes won't appear publicly until verified.

**Google Business Profile** also offers an **email** method (Google sends a PIN to the business domain email, for example `...@businesswebsite.com`), in addition to phone/text and postcard. If the listing is suspended or needs additional verification, it may take more time.

**Medical listing claim:** medical sites may require an **NPI number**, a **driver's license** upload, a **state license number**, a **medical license number**, and/or **phone/text** PIN verification.

:::warning Postcard PINs expire
When postcard is the only option, the Listing Fulfillment team follows up by email **7 and 14 business days** after the initial email to get the PIN. **Postcard PINs expire within 30 days**, so pass the PIN to the team as soon as you receive it (by email or call).
:::

### If the listing is already claimed

If a listing is already claimed and you're not the owner, we can work to unlock it so it's available to claim again. This can take **two weeks or more**.

- The listing site usually has a **Request Ownership** form that notifies the current owner.
- If the current owner **denies** the request, we work with the platform's Support to gain access but **cannot guarantee ownership**.
- If the current owner **doesn't respond**, the listing becomes available again (after **7 days** on most sites; **4 days** for Google Business Profile) and can be claimed with the regular verification methods.
- **Yelp:** if a Yelp listing is already claimed, the business owner must contact Yelp Support for access—our team isn't permitted to make this request on their behalf.
- **Medical sites:** if a medical listing is already claimed or unclaimable, the practitioner may need to contact the site's Support—our team isn't permitted to make this request on their behalf.

### Listing claim completed

Once the listing is successfully claimed, the Listing Fulfillment team ensures the listing data is accurate (see [What's Included](#whats-included) for each type) and sends a final email with the login credentials to the claimed listing (if applicable).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>What's required from me to claim a listing?</summary>

Provide the required claiming information in the fulfillment form, the postcard PIN if postcard verification is used, and access to a business-associated Gmail account.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What listings can be claimed through this service?</summary>

A regular listing claim can claim Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing, TripAdvisor, RateMDs, and Doctor.com. A medical listing claim covers up to three of RateMDs, Doctor.com, Vitals.com, and WebMD for one practitioner. Contact us to claim a site outside these lists.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why do I need a Gmail account?</summary>

We need access to a Gmail inbox to claim and monitor the listing, and we use that email to log in to the listing after it's claimed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>When will my listings be correct?</summary>

Claiming and correcting listings can take up to 90 days, though in most cases listings are claimed and corrected within 30 days.
</details>

<details>
<summary>I run two different businesses with the same phone number and address. Can both be claimed?</summary>

Businesses listed at an identical address and phone number are seen as duplicates of each other and removed from many sites.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can you claim a listing for a business that's still under construction?</summary>

No—it's best to wait until the business has a permanent address and phone number.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I list a tracking phone number?</summary>

No, tracking numbers can't be used as the listing's phone number. Provide the local phone number for the business.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I don't have a physical address?</summary>

For service-area businesses, most listing sites still require a real address. Some sites, like Google and Yelp, can suppress the address so it isn't viewable online.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the restrictions for Yelp multi-location claiming?</summary>

If you have more than 10 locations, we're unable to offer Yelp multi-location claiming due to restrictions set by Yelp.
</details>
