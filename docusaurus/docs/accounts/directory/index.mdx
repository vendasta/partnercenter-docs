## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>What if I can’t find a business during search?</summary>

Try alternate spellings, search by address or phone number, or add the business manually during account creation.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How many categories should I choose?</summary>

Choose the most specific primary category and only add secondary categories that reflect significant additional services.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I handle duplicates?</summary>

Identify the primary account, consolidate missing details, and delete the duplicates. See [Duplicate Detection](./duplicate-detection).
</details>
