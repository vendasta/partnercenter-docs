# Files

Share sales decks, onboarding resources, and other collateral directly through Business App so clients always have the latest version.

## Upload files from Partner Center
1. In **Partner Center**, navigate to **Business App > Files**.
2. Click **Upload files** and add PDFs, images, or other supported formats.
3. Organize files into folders to match client workflows.
4. Toggle **Visible in Business App** for any asset you want clients to access.

## Client experience
- Clients open **Business App > Administration > Files** to browse folders and download documents.
- File metadata shows the upload date and file type so teams can confirm freshness.
- Removing the visibility toggle immediately hides the asset in Business App.

## Best practices
- Maintain a "Getting Started" folder with onboarding checklists and service explanations.
- Replace outdated versions instead of renaming files, which keeps shared URLs consistent.
- Pair files with Guides to provide both high-level instructions and supporting documentation.
