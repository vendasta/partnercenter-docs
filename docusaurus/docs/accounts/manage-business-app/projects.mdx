# Projects

Projects give clients a transparent view into ongoing work managed through Task Manager. Publishing a project keeps everyone aligned without needing status emails.

## Share a project to Business App
1. Open the project in **Task Manager**.
2. Click **Share** and choose **Business App** as the destination.
3. Select the client account and the users who should see the project.
4. Save to immediately surface the project card in Business App.

## What clients see
- Clients access shared projects from **Business App > Administration > Projects**.
- Each project lists milestones, tasks, assignees, and due dates in real time.
- Clients can comment on tasks to request updates or provide assets.

## Keep projects current
- Update task statuses routinely so the Business App view reflects actual progress.
- Combine shared projects with [Guides](/business-app/administration/guides) or [Files](/accounts/manage-business-app/files) that explain your methodology.
- Archive completed projects instead of deleting them so clients can reference past work when needed.

For additional setup tips, review [Getting Started with Projects in Task Manager](/getting-started/getting-started-guides/getting-started-with-projects-in-task-manager).
