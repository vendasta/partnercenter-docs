# Manage Business App

Business App is your customers' dashboard. They use it to view reports, use product features, and manage account settings. **Manage Business App** is where you configure how customers access that experience, what they see when they sign up, and what content you share with them (files, projects). You'll find it under `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Manage Business App`, including **Customize Business App** for branding and signup options.

## Why it matters

- **Self-serve access** – Customers can log in and use their data without waiting on you.
- **Less support** – Direct access to reports and tools reduces routine support requests.
- **Consistent experience** – Configure features, branding, and onboarding so every customer gets a clear path.

## What's included

- **[Business App Access](./business-app-access.mdx)** – Find each account's Business App URL, enable access, invite users, and use **View as Customer** for support. Covers multi-user access, permissions, customization, and troubleshooting.
- **[Self-Signup](./self-signup.mdx)** – Let customers create their own Business App accounts via a signup link or **Create Account** on the login page. Includes getting the link, enabling the option in **Customize Business App**, and automations for new self-signup customers.
- **[Featured Packages](./featured-packages.mdx)** – Choose packages to show when a client has no active products, so they see a simple upgrade path in Business App instead of browsing the full Store. Configure per market under `Customize Business App` → `Add Your Clients`.
- **[Files](../manage-accounts/files.mdx)** – Upload PDFs, images, and other assets in Partner Center and make them visible in Business App so clients can open `Administration` → `Files` and download the latest collateral and onboarding materials.
- **[Projects](./projects.mdx)** – Share Task Manager projects to Business App so clients see milestones, tasks, and progress under `Administration` → `Projects` and can comment on tasks.

## Get started

1. **Set up customer access**
   - In the account, find the **Business App URL** and confirm login is enabled. Use [Business App Access](./business-app-access.mdx) to enable access, send credentials, and customize what customers see.

2. **Optional: enable self-signup and onboarding**
   - In `Manage Business App` → `Customize Business App` → `Add Your Clients`, copy the **Sign-Up Link** and optionally enable **Show Create Account** on the login page. Configure [Featured Packages](./featured-packages.mdx) so new signups see a clear upgrade path. Use [Self-Signup](./self-signup.mdx) for automations (e.g. welcome email, assign salesperson).

3. **Share content with clients**
   - Use [Files](../manage-accounts/files.mdx) to publish collateral to `Business App` → `Files` and [Projects](./projects.mdx) to share Task Manager projects so clients can follow progress in Business App.
