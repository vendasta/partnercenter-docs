## What is Business App?

Business App is your customers’ dashboard. They log in to view reports, use product features, and manage their account settings.

## Why it matters

- Give customers self-serve access to their data
- Reduce support by enabling direct access
- Configure features and branding for a consistent experience

## What’s included

- Finding account-specific Business App URLs
- Enabling access and sending welcome emails
- Viewing Business App as a customer for support

## How to get started

1. Find a customer’s Business App URL and confirm access
   - See: [Business App Access](./business-app-access)
2. Create users and set permissions (if needed)
   - See: [Give your customers access to Business App](../accounts-manage-users/give-your-customers-access-to-business-app)
   - See: [Configure User Permissions](../accounts-manage-users/configure-user-permissions)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>Where do I find the Business App URL?</summary>

Open the customer account and locate the Business App section. You can also find a link on the Manage Users page. See [Business App Access](./business-app-access).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I view Business App as the customer?</summary>

Yes. Use the “View as customer” option on the account to troubleshoot and verify access. See [Business App Access](./business-app-access).
</details>
