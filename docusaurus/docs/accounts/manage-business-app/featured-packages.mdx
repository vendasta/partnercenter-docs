## Intro

Featured Packages let you surface specific packages to clients during onboarding in the Business App. This creates a direct path to purchasing without requiring clients to browse the Store.

## What is Featured Packages?

Featured Packages is a configuration option within **Customize Business App** that allows you to select and display specific packages when a client signs up and does not yet have anything active.

You can choose which packages appear and tailor them by market.

## Why is Featured Packages important?

Featured Packages reduce friction during onboarding by:

- Guiding clients to relevant packages immediately
- Avoiding the need to search the Store
- Presenting a focused upgrade path when no active products exist

## What you can do with Featured Packages

With Featured Packages, you can:

- Select packages you have already created
- Assign packages by individual market
- Preview how packages appear in the Business App
- Edit and customize selected packages
- Direct clients to purchase from a single upgrade page

## How to set up Featured Packages

1. In Partner Center, go to **Accounts**.
2. Select **Manage Business App**.
3. Click **Customize Business App**.
4. Scroll to **Add Your Clients**.
5. Choose a market.
6. Scroll to **Featured Packages**.
7. Select the packages you want to feature.
8. Preview the experience to confirm how it will appear in the Business App.

### How market selection works

You can configure Featured Packages per market. This allows different packages to appear depending on the selected market during setup.

## What clients see in Business App

When a client signs up and has no active products:

- The homepage displays a message prompting them to **unlock their full potential**
- An **Upgrade now** option appears
- Selecting the upgrade takes them to a page showing the featured packages
- Clients can review and purchase packages directly from that page

:::tip
Use Featured Packages to guide clients to a small number of relevant options instead of sending them to the full Store.
:::

## FAQs

<details>
<summary>Where do I configure Featured Packages?</summary>

You configure Featured Packages under **Customize Business App** → **Add Your Clients**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use any package I have created?</summary>

Yes. You can select from any packages you have already set up.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do Featured Packages replace the Store?</summary>

No. Featured Packages provide a directed option during onboarding but do not remove access to the Store.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can Featured Packages be different by market?</summary>

Yes. You can select Featured Packages based on individual markets.
</details>
