## What are Multi-Location Groups?

Multi-Location Groups let you organize many business locations under a single group for centralized management and reporting.

## Why use Multi-Location Groups?

- Centralize management across locations
- Consolidated reporting for insights and comparisons
- Streamlined user access and permissions

## What’s included

- Create and manage groups (including adding users)
- Reputation and NPS views across locations
- Analytics and reporting (keyword reporting, executive report)

## How to get started

1. Create a new group and add locations and users
   - See: [Create and Manage Groups](./create-multilocation-groups)
2. Review reputation and NPS across locations
   - See: [Reputation and NPS](./reputation-and-nps)
3. Explore analytics and reporting
   - See: [Analytics and Reporting](./analytics-and-reporting)

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>Can I add users to a group after creating it?</summary>

Yes. Open the group, go to the Users tab, and add users with the required access.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I see roll-up reports for multiple locations?</summary>

Open analytics from the group to view consolidated metrics and executive reporting.
</details>
