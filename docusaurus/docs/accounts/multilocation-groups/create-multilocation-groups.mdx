## What are Multi-Location Groups?

Multi-Location Groups let you organize many business accounts (stores, branches, franchises) under a single group. You create and manage groups in Partner Center, and users with access to a group see all of its accounts together in [Multi-Location Business App](/multi-location-business-app). Groups suit enterprise clients, franchise operations, chains, and any client with multiple locations that need coordinated management and consolidated reporting.

:::info
Multi-Location Groups require a `Premium` subscription or higher.
:::

## Create a group

1. In Partner Center, go to `Accounts` → `Multi-location Groups`
2. Click `Create group`
3. Enter a name for the group and save it

<img src={require('../../multi-location-business-app/img/multi-location/add-accounts/multi-location-groups.jpg').default} alt="Multi-Location Groups list in Partner Center with the Create group button" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

Use the client's corporate or brand name so the group is easy to find in the list, for example "Example Coffee Co." or "Example Medical Group."

## Plan sub-groups before adding accounts

Sub-groups segment a group's accounts, for example by region or location type. They appear in Multi-Location Business App for every assigned user and are especially useful for reporting.

:::note
Each level of a group holds either sub-groups or accounts, not both. Create your sub-groups first, then add accounts to them.
:::

To add a sub-group, open the group and click `+ Add sub-group`. To nest a sub-group inside another, hover over the existing sub-group and click `+ sub-group`. See [Sub-groups](../../multi-location-business-app/setup/multi-location-sub-groups.mdx) for details.

**Example structure:**

```
Example Retail Co.
├── Northeast
│   ├── New York Store #001
│   ├── New York Store #002
│   └── Boston Store #003
├── Southeast
│   ├── Atlanta Store #004
│   └── Miami Store #005
└── West Coast
    ├── Los Angeles Store #006
    └── San Francisco Store #007
```

## Add accounts to a group

1. Go to `Accounts` → `Multi-location Groups`
2. Click the group (or sub-group) you want to add accounts to
3. Click `Add accounts`
4. Click `+` next to each account you want to add
5. Click `Done`

The accounts must already exist in Partner Center. See [Add accounts to group](../../multi-location-business-app/setup/add-accounts-to-a-multi-location-group.mdx) for screenshots of each step.

## Give users access to the group

Open the group and go to the `Users` tab to choose who can access the group in Multi-Location Business App.

## Manage the group

When you open a group, you see these tabs:

- `Accounts`: The group's accounts and sub-groups, plus the `Group market` setting, which sets the branding and listing sources the group and its sub-groups use in Multi-Location Business App. It doesn't change the individual accounts in the group.
- `Users`: Who has access to the group
- `Features`: Features available for the group
- `Sources`: Listing sources for the group
- `Account update history`: A record of changes to the group's accounts

To see the group the way your client's users do, click `Open in Multi-Location Business App`.

## Naming conventions

Consistent names make large groups easier to navigate:

- **Group name:** The client's official corporate or brand name
- **Location accounts:** A consistent format, such as "Brand - Location" ("Example Coffee - Downtown"), "Brand - Store number" ("Example Retail - Store #1234"), or "Brand - Area" ("Example Medical - North Campus")

## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>Can I add existing accounts to a Multi-Location Group?</summary>

Yes. Use `Add accounts` on the group to add accounts that already exist in Partner Center. Each account keeps its own data and remains accessible on its own.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I create sub-groups within a Multi-Location Group?</summary>

Yes. Open the group and click `+ Add sub-group`. You can nest sub-groups inside other sub-groups. Each level holds either sub-groups or accounts, so create sub-groups before adding accounts.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What do I need to create Multi-Location Groups?</summary>

You need administrative permissions in Partner Center and a `Premium` subscription or higher.

</details>
