## Keyword Reporting

View keyword ranking distribution across locations:

1. Go to `Accounts > Multi-Location Groups`
2. Select a group, then open `Keyword Rankings`
3. Use filters for locations, keywords, provider, and geography

## Executive Report (Multi-Location)

Use executive-level rollups to compare performance across locations with configurable time ranges and filters.
