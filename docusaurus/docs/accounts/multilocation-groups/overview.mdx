## What are Multi-Location Groups?

Multi-Location Groups let you organize many business locations under a single group for centralized management and reporting.

## Why use Multi-Location Groups?

- Centralize management across locations
- Consolidated reporting for insights and comparisons
- Streamlined user access and permissions

1. Create a new group and add locations and users

2. Review reputation and NPS across locations
   - See: [Reputation and NPS](./reputation-and-nps)
3. Explore analytics and reporting
