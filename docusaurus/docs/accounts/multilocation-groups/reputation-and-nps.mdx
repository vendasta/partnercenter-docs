## Reputation and NPS overview

Use Multi-Location Business App to view roll-up reputation insights and Net Promoter Score (NPS) across locations.

## Access reputation and NPS

1. Open Multi-Location Business App
2. Go to `Reputation`
3. Open `Requests` for request performance metrics
4. Open `Net Promoter Score` for NPS insights

## Request performance metrics

- Requests clickthrough rate
- Total requests sent (email and SMS)
- Total link clicks
- Per-location metrics with delta indicators

## Net Promoter Score

- Overall NPS and score-over-time
- Per-location NPS with promoters, passives, and detractors
- Feedback volume and recent NPS feedback
