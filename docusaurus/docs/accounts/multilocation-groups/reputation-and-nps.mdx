## Reputation and NPS overview

Use Multi-Location Business App to view roll-up reputation insights and Net Promoter Score (NPS) across locations.

## Access reputation and NPS

1. Open Multi-Location Business App
2. Go to `Reputation`
3. Open `Requests` for request performance metrics
4. Open `Net Promoter Score` for NPS insights

## Request performance metrics

- Requests clickthrough rate
- Total requests sent (email and SMS)
- Total link clicks
- Per-location metrics with delta indicators

## Net Promoter Score

- Overall NPS and score-over-time
- Per-location NPS with promoters, passives, and detractors
- Feedback volume and recent NPS feedback

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How can I tell whether an NPS click was a bot click rather than a real customer?</summary>

Check the email-events data for the `probable_bot` flag and reconcile it with the NPS score and the matching email event. If there is no bot flag and no matching score event, treat the click as a real customer action. You can remove the score record if your client requests it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change the Google review threshold for an NPS review request from 7 to 8?</summary>

Open the default NPS review-request templates, set the redirect threshold to a value from 7 to 9, and save. Update both the SMS and email templates if your clients use both channels. The new threshold applies only to requests sent after you save; links already sent keep the threshold they were created with.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does an NPS rating appear without contact information?</summary>

The contact linked to that NPS score was deleted from the CRM. When the CRM contact is removed, the score remains but no contact details are shown.

</details>
