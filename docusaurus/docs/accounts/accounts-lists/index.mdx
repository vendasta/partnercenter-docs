Account Lists group business accounts for bulk operations on listings, billing, and product management. Create account lists manually or import from CSV.

## Account Lists vs CRM Lists

:::warning Critical Distinction  
**Account Lists** (this section) handle business accounts for administrative operations. **CRM Lists** manage contacts and companies for sales activities. These are completely different systems:

### Use Account Lists for:
- **Business listing management** and directory updates
- **Product activation** and subscription management  
- **Billing administration** and payment processing
- **Service configuration** and account settings
- **Multi-location business operations**
- **Bulk administrative operations**

### Use CRM Lists for:
- **Contact and company relationships** 
- **Sales pipeline and lead nurturing**
- **Email marketing campaigns and automation**
- **Opportunity and deal tracking**
- **Sales process automations**

**For sales, marketing, and customer relationship activities, use [CRM Lists](/crm/lists)** which provide advanced contact and company management capabilities.
:::

## Why use Account Lists?

- **Organize business accounts** by service type, billing tier, or geographic region  
- **Bulk administrative operations**: exports, product activation, billing updates
- **Streamline account management**: bulk profile updates, service configuration, multi-location coordination
- **Administrative efficiency**: group accounts for billing operations, product management, and directory maintenance

## What's included?

- **Create and import account lists** for business administration
- **Add and remove business accounts** from administrative groupings
- **Built-in administrative actions** (export account data, activate products, billing operations, service configuration)
- **Business account operations** focused on listings, products, and billing

## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>Can I rename a list?</summary>

There isn't an edit-name option. Delete the list and recreate it with the desired name. Deleting a list does not delete its accounts.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I merge two lists?</summary>

Yes. Add a common tag to both lists, filter by that tag in Accounts, then add the filtered accounts to a new list. See [List Actions](./list-actions).
</details>
