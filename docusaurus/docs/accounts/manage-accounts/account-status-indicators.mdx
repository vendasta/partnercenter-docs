## What are account status indicators?

Account status indicators are small visual markers that help you quickly scan the `Manage Accounts` list. These markers call out accounts that need attention.

## Why are account status indicators important?

You can use these indicators to spot issues that affect access and billing. Acting on them early helps you keep products active and avoid service interruptions for your clients.

## What’s included

- Black vertical bar to the left of the account name: the account is suspended due to non‑payment. When an account is suspended, paid products on that account are deactivated until payment is resolved.

:::warning
The black bar indicates an account‑level suspension. It does not represent the status of an individual product.
:::

## How to resolve a suspended account

1. Open the account from `Manage Accounts`.
2. Review and update the account’s payment method.
3. After payment is successful, reactivate any products you want running again. For steps, see [Manage products](./product-management.mdx).

## Find accounts that need attention

- Scan for the black bar in the `Manage Accounts` list.
- Use search and filters to narrow your list as needed. For options, see [Search and filter accounts](./search-filter-accounts.mdx).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>What does the black bar beside an account name mean?</summary>
It marks an account that is suspended due to non‑payment. Suspended accounts require billing attention before products can run again.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do products stop running when an account is suspended?</summary>
Yes. Paid products on a suspended account are deactivated until payment is resolved and products are reactivated.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I clear the black bar?</summary>
Update the account’s payment method and ensure payment is successful. Then reactivate products as needed. The indicator disappears once the account is back in good standing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I update payment details?</summary>
Open the account from `Manage Accounts` and update the payment method in the account’s billing settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I export a list of suspended accounts?</summary>
You can export the `Manage Accounts` list after applying filters or sorting. The export will include the accounts you see on screen.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the black bar show product‑level issues?</summary>
No. It shows an account‑level suspension only. To review product status, open the account and check the `Products` tab. See [Manage products](./product-management.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will data be lost when an account is suspended?</summary>
No. Suspension deactivates paid products but does not delete account data. Reactivate products after resolving payment to continue where you left off.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do free products cause a suspension?</summary>
No. Suspensions are tied to non‑payment on paid products.
</details>
