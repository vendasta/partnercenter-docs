# Files

Share sales decks, onboarding resources, and other collateral directly through Business App so clients always have the latest version.

## Upload files from Partner Center
1. In **Partner Center**, navigate to **Accounts > Manage Accounts**.
2. Select the account you want to upload a file for.
3. Under the **Files** section, click **Add file**.
4. Upload your file — once added, it is immediately visible to the client in **Business App > Administration > Files**.

## Show the Files page in Business App

The Files page must be enabled globally before clients can see it in Business App. This is a one-time setup that applies to all accounts.

1. In **Partner Center**, navigate to **Administration > Customize Business App**.
2. Go to **Pages > Files**.
3. Check the **Show this page** box.

Unchecking this box removes the Files section from Business App for all accounts.

<img src={require('./img/files-adminstration.png').default} alt="Files page toggle in Customize Business App" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

:::info
As a partner, you will not see the Files section when browsing Business App yourself. To confirm it is visible, log in to Business App directly as a client user, or use the impersonate user feature.
:::

## Client experience
- Clients open **Business App > Administration > Files** to browse and download documents.
- File metadata shows the upload date and source so teams can confirm freshness.

## Best practices
- Maintain a "Getting Started" folder with onboarding checklists and service explanations.
- Replace outdated versions instead of renaming files, which keeps shared URLs consistent.
- Pair files with Guides to provide both high-level instructions and supporting documentation.

## Task Manager file attachments are different

Files attached to tasks or projects in **Task Manager** are separate from Account Files and are not visible to clients in Business App. Task Manager attachments are internal-only — they can be seen by your team but do not appear anywhere in the client's Business App interface.

To share a file with a client:
- **Use Account Files** (this page) — go to the account in Manage Accounts, click **Add file**, and the file will appear in Business App automatically.
- **Use a cloud storage link** — paste a Google Drive or Dropbox link in a public task note so the client can access it from the task.
