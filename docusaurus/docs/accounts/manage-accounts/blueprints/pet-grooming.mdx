# Pet grooming: Keep every pet on its grooming rhythm

The Pet grooming blueprint configures a client account to answer booking requests even with a dog on the table, track each pet's grooming schedule and special needs, and send vaccine record reminders before they expire. It is built for grooming businesses that want every pet back on schedule.

## Who it's for

Pet grooming businesses that want to keep every pet on a regular grooming schedule and track special needs.

## What it solves

When a pet owner calls to book a groom for a dog that is nervous around dryers, the call gets answered immediately and the special need is noted for the groomer. The system gathers size, vaccinations, and preferred times for the team to confirm. Every pet gets a profile, and owners are nudged to rebook before the pet is overdue.

## What's included

The Pet grooming blueprint includes:

- **AI receptionist**: answers calls and web chats day and night. Notes special needs (like a dog nervous around dryers) for the groomer, then gathers size, vaccinations, and preferred times for the team to confirm. The owner is added to the CRM as a contact.
- **Pipeline**: New Client Onboarding for first-time clients.
- **CRM custom object**: Pet Profiles (each pet with breed, coat type, behavioral notes, and last groomed date).
- **Automations**: Post-groom review request (after checkout and skips owners flagged for an incident), Rebooking nudge, Vaccine records expiry alert, and Membership renewal reminder.
- **Campaigns and email templates**: vaccination record reminders, spring shedding season, holiday grooming, win-backs for lapsed clients, and appointment reminders.
- **Smart Lists**: pets overdue for a rebook, vaccine records expiring soon, lapsed clients, and anxious or flagged pets.

## Required products

Your client account needs these products turned on before the blueprint can be applied:

- Campaigns Pro
- Reputation AI
- CRM AI
- Conversations AI – Embedded, or another Inbox product with AI

## Add and apply the blueprint

To use this Blueprint Template, find **Pet grooming: Keep every pet on its grooming rhythm** in the `Blueprint Templates` tab at `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Blueprints`, then add it to your blueprints and apply it to a client account. Make sure the required products are turned on first.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Create a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#create-a-blueprint) and [Apply a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint).

:::warning
Automations from Blueprint Templates keep the state they had in the source account. For Vendasta templates, that means automations arrive as Draft (off), so nothing goes out to your client's customers until you review and turn them on. Review the automations included with this blueprint, test them if needed, and switch on the ones you want from the account's automations list. Add the business's details to the AI Receptionist before switching on automations.
:::
