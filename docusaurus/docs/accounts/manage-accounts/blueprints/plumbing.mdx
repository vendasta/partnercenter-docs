# Plumbing: Never miss an emergency call

The Plumbing blueprint configures a client account to answer calls and chats day or night so the 2 a.m. burst pipe books with you instead of the next plumber on the list. It tracks equipment like water heaters and follows up on open estimates. It is built for plumbing businesses that respond to emergencies and want every job followed up.

## Who it's for

Plumbing businesses that respond to emergency calls, track customer equipment, and want to follow up on every open estimate.

## What it solves

When a homeowner reports water coming through the ceiling at 2 a.m., the call gets answered immediately. The system treats it as an emergency, explains how to shut off the main valve, and takes the address and a callback number. Every water heater and equipment item gets a record, and open estimates are followed up automatically.

## What's included

The Plumbing blueprint includes:

- **AI receptionist**: answers calls and web chats day and night. Treats burst pipes and flooding as emergencies, explains how to shut off the main valve, and gathers the address and callback number. The caller is added to the CRM as a contact.
- **Pipelines**: Job Pipeline and Commercial Account Pipeline.
- **CRM custom objects**: Job / Service Calls, Maintenance Plan Subscriptions, and Equipment / Assets (each installed water heater or system with install date and warranty).
- **Automations**: Post-job review request (when a job is marked complete), Estimate follow-up (chases open quotes with a text, then an email), Maintenance plan renewal reminder, and Missed call text-back.
- **Campaigns and email templates**: seasonal checkups, water heater reminders, win-backs for customers who have gone quiet, and commercial contract renewals.
- **Smart Lists**: lapsed customers, open estimates, review requests not yet sent, and water heater replacement candidates.

## Required products

Your client account needs these products turned on before the blueprint can be applied:

- Campaigns Pro
- Reputation AI
- CRM AI
- Conversations AI – Embedded, or another Inbox product with AI

## Add and apply the blueprint

To use this Blueprint Template, find **Plumbing: Never miss an emergency call** in the `Blueprint Templates` tab at `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Blueprints`, then add it to your blueprints and apply it to a client account. Make sure the required products are turned on first.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Create a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#create-a-blueprint) and [Apply a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint).

:::warning
Automations from Blueprint Templates keep the state they had in the source account. For Vendasta templates, that means automations arrive as Draft (off), so nothing goes out to your client's customers until you review and turn them on. Review the automations included with this blueprint, test them if needed, and switch on the ones you want from the account's automations list. Add the business's details to the AI Receptionist before switching on automations.
:::
