# Top use cases for Blueprint Templates

Blueprint Templates are ready-to-use account configurations built by Vendasta for common industries and workflows. Each one carries the **Vendasta curated** badge and includes AI employees, automations, CRM custom objects, pipelines, campaigns, and more — everything configured and ready to [apply to a client account](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint).

There are 18 Blueprint Templates, each built and tested by Vendasta for one type of local business. Applying one sets up its AI Receptionist, CRM pipeline, follow-up campaigns, and review requests in one step. They're for agencies onboarding new clients, franchises and multi-location brands rolling one standard out to every location, and ISVs setting up their customers.

## What are Blueprint Templates?

Blueprint Templates live in the `Blueprint Templates` tab at `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Blueprints`. You can filter them by **industry** (Home services, Automotive services, Personal services, Pet services) or by **Outcome** (Fill the calendar, Get found locally, Never miss a lead, Five star reputation, Speed to lead, Win back and repeat revenue).

To use a Blueprint Template, add it to your blueprints from the `Blueprint Templates` tab. It appears in your `My blueprints` tab and can be applied to any client account that has the required products turned on.

:::info
Every Blueprint Template requires the same four products: **Campaigns Pro**, **Reputation AI**, **CRM AI**, and **Conversations AI – Embedded** (or another Inbox product with AI). Turn these on before applying the blueprint.
:::

## How to add and apply a Blueprint Template

1. Go to `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Blueprints` → [`Blueprint templates`](https://partners.vendasta.com/blueprints/public).
2. Use the filters on the left to browse by industry or Outcome tag.
3. Click `View details` on a Blueprint Template to see what's included.
4. Click `Add to my blueprints`. The blueprint appears in your `My blueprints` tab.
5. [Apply the blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint) to an individual account, multiple accounts, or a multi-location group. Make sure the required products are turned on first.
6. Add the business's details to the AI Receptionist and review the automations. Switch on the automations you want from the account's automations list.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Create a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#create-a-blueprint) and [Apply a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint).

:::warning
Automations from Blueprint Templates keep the state they had in the source account. For Vendasta templates, that means automations arrive as Draft (off). Review the automations included with each template, test them if needed, and switch on the ones you want from the account's automations list.
:::

:::warning
Most Blueprint Templates add 3 [CRM custom objects](/crm/custom-objects). Each account can have up to 3 custom objects total. If applying a blueprint would push an account past the limit, the application fails for that account with a clear error. Remove one custom object from the account before retrying.
:::
