The Towing blueprint configures a client account to answer stranded drivers immediately, check that everyone is safe, send a dispatch confirmation text, and track every job through completion. It is built for towing businesses that respond to breakdowns, accidents, and roadside emergencies.

## Who it's for

Towing businesses that respond to breakdowns, accidents, and roadside emergencies around the clock.

## What it solves

When a driver reports a breakdown on the highway shoulder, the call gets answered immediately and the system checks that everyone is safe first. It takes the location, the vehicle, and a callback number. The caller is added to the CRM, and when the job moves to dispatched, the customer gets a text confirmation.

## What's included

The Towing blueprint includes:

- **AI receptionist**: answers calls and web chats day and night. Checks that everyone is safe first, then takes the location, vehicle, and callback number. The caller is added to the CRM.
- **Pipeline**: Dispatch Pipeline.
- **CRM custom objects**: Dispatch Tickets (each job with pickup location, destination, and status), Vehicles, and Lien/Impound Cases.
- **Automations**: Dispatch confirmation text (messages the customer when a job moves to dispatched), Post-completion review request (after a standard job and skips accident tows), and Missed call text-back. Automations arrive as drafts for the client to turn on.
- **Campaigns and email templates**: referral partner check-ins, seasonal messages before breakdown season, and win-backs for customers who have gone quiet.
- **Smart Lists**: active referral partners, completed jobs awaiting a review request, and network jobs approaching SLA breach.

## Required products

Your client account needs these products turned on before the blueprint can be applied:

- Campaigns Pro
- Reputation Management (Premium)
- CRM AI
- Conversations AI – Embedded, or another Inbox product with AI

## Add and apply the blueprint

To use this Blueprint Template, find **Towing: Answer every stranded driver fast** in the `Blueprint Templates` tab at `Partner Center` → `Blueprints`, then add it to your blueprints and apply it to a client account. Make sure the required products are turned on first.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Create a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#create-a-blueprint) and [Apply a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint).

Automations arrive as drafts so the client can turn on the ones they want live. Campaigns are also delivered as drafts and editable templates.
