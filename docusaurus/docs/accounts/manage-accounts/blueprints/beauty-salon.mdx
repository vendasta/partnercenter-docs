# Beauty salon: Keep every chair booked

The Beauty salon blueprint configures a client account to answer booking requests even with hands in foils or after closing, save every client's color formula, and invite first-time clients to book a second visit. It is built for salons that take color appointments, want to track client preferences, and need to keep chairs booked.

## Who it's for

Beauty salons and hair studios that offer color services, track client preferences, and want to keep appointments full.

## What it solves

When a first-time client asks for a cut and color, the call or chat gets answered immediately. The system asks about color history, sensitivities, and preferred times, then says the team will confirm. It never claims a booking on its own. Every client is added to the CRM, every color formula is saved as a record, and first-time clients are nudged to book again.

## What's included

The Beauty salon blueprint includes:

- **AI receptionist**: answers calls and web chats day and night. Gathers color history, sensitivities, and preferred times for the team to confirm. The client is added to the CRM as a contact.
- **Pipelines**: New Client Journey for first-time clients, Bridal Journey, and Win-Back.
- **CRM custom objects**: Color Formulas (each color formula saved as a record with codes and processing notes), Service Records, and Memberships.
- **Automations**: Post-visit rebook prompt, Post-visit review request (timed to the service and skips visits with a complaint), Bridal inquiry follow-up, and Membership renewal reminder.
- **Campaigns and email templates**: welcome series, seasonal promotions, win-backs for clients who have gone quiet, and bridal countdown sequences.
- **Smart Lists**: lapsed clients, color clients due for a rebook, new clients with no second booking, and VIP clients.

## Required products

Your client account needs these products turned on before the blueprint can be applied:

- Campaigns Pro
- Reputation AI
- CRM AI
- Conversations AI – Embedded, or another Inbox product with AI

## Add and apply the blueprint

To use this Blueprint Template, find **Beauty salon: Keep every chair booked** in the `Blueprint Templates` tab at `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Blueprints`, then add it to your blueprints and apply it to a client account. Make sure the required products are turned on first.

For step-by-step instructions, see [Create a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#create-a-blueprint) and [Apply a blueprint](/accounts/manage-accounts/blueprints#apply-a-blueprint).

:::warning
Automations are switched on as soon as you apply this blueprint. To hold any back, untick them in the `Select components` step before you apply, or turn them off from the account's automations list afterward. Add the business's details to the AI Receptionist right after applying, because automations start running straight away.
:::
