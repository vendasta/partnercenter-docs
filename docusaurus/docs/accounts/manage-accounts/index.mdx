# Manage Accounts Overview

Manage Accounts is where you add and maintain business accounts. You can create new accounts individually or in bulk, update account details, search and filter accounts, manage products, and remove accounts that are no longer needed.

## Why is Manage Accounts important?

- Ensure complete and accurate business information for reliable reporting and service delivery
- Quickly find the right accounts using search and filters
- Keep account details current with edits and updates
- Activate, adjust, or cancel products as business needs change
- Import multiple accounts efficiently when onboarding many businesses

## What's included in Manage Accounts?

- Account creation (individual and CSV bulk import)
- Account editing and settings updates
- Search and filtering tools
- Product activation and management
- Account cleanup, including deletion and duplicate management

## How to get started

1. Create accounts: start with a single account or import many at once
   - See: [Create Accounts](./create-accounts)
2. Edit existing accounts to keep information current
   - See: [Edit Accounts](./edit-accounts)
3. Find accounts using search and advanced filters
   - See: [Search and Filter Accounts](./search-filter-accounts)
4. Activate and manage products for each account
   - See: [Product Management](./product-management)
5. Remove accounts you no longer need, or resolve duplicates
   - See: [Delete Accounts](./delete-accounts)

## Additional actions

- Adjust user notifications and default settings during bulk import
- Change an account's market when territories change
- Organize accounts using lists for operations and reporting

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>Can I add multiple accounts at once?</summary>

Yes. You can import accounts from a CSV file using the bulk import option. See [Create Accounts](./create-accounts).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What information is required to create an account?</summary>

Required fields include business name, address, phone number, country/region, and categories. See [Create Accounts](./create-accounts).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I update an account's address or category?</summary>

Open the account, choose edit, update the fields, and save. See [Edit Accounts](./edit-accounts).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I find accounts with specific products?</summary>

Use product-based filters such as has product, missing product, or status. See [Search and Filter Accounts](./search-filter-accounts).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I activate or change products for an account?</summary>

Yes. Open the account, go to products, then activate or change editions as needed. See [Product Management](./product-management).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I resolve duplicate accounts?</summary>

Identify the primary account, consolidate data, and remove duplicates as appropriate. See [Delete Accounts](./delete-accounts).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when I delete an account?</summary>

Deletion is permanent. User access, products, and related data are removed. See [Delete Accounts](./delete-accounts).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I filter accounts by creation date or active products?</summary>

Yes. Use date filters for creation date and product filters for active products. See [Search and Filter Accounts](./search-filter-accounts).
</details>
