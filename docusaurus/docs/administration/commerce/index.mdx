Commerce management provides the tools needed to handle payments, billing automation, and tax configurations for your business. Set up payment processing, automate billing for recurring subscriptions, and manage tax rates to ensure compliant financial operations.

## What you can do with Commerce

- Set up Vendasta Payments with new accounts or connect existing Stripe Standard accounts
- Configure default billing automation that applies to all new customer accounts automatically
- Edit tax rates and manage tax exemptions for accounts and specific products
- Process payments through credit/debit cards, ACH transfers, and Pre-Authorized Debits (PADs)
- Automate invoice generation and payment collection for recurring subscriptions

## How it works

Commerce features work together to streamline your financial operations:

- **Payment Processing**: Vendasta Payments supports multiple countries (USA, Canada, New Zealand, Australia, UK, Czech Republic) with competitive processing fees. You can create new payment accounts or connect existing Stripe accounts with a 0.75% platform fee.
- **Billing Automation**: Default settings apply automatically to all new accounts, choosing between automatic charging or invoice generation. Settings only affect accounts created after configuration—existing accounts retain their current settings.
- **Tax Management**: Tax rates apply automatically based on customer postal codes. Accounts without complete addresses will not display tax rates or have taxes applied to transactions.

## Get started

1. Configure payment processing at `Administration` > `Vendasta Payments` 
   - Create new Vendasta Payments account or connect existing Stripe Standard account
   - Complete business verification and banking setup
2. Set up default billing automation at `Administration` > `Default Billing Settings`
   - Choose collection method (automatic charging vs. invoice generation)
   - Select collection timing and apply to specific markets if needed
3. Configure tax rates at `Administration` > `Tax Rates`
   - Edit existing tax rates using the kebab menu
   - Ensure customer addresses include postal codes for automatic tax calculation

:::tip
Vendasta Payments is not available for Free or Trial subscription tiers due to fraud prevention measures.
:::

## Key Commerce Settings

### Payment Processing Configuration
- **New Account Creation**: Complete business information and identity verification for new Vendasta Payments accounts
- **Stripe Connection**: Connect existing Stripe Standard accounts to take advantage of Vendasta's billing features while keeping your negotiated rates
- **Regional Support**: Available in multiple countries with additional sales assistance in UAE, Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, Poland, Switzerland
- **Payment Methods**: Support for credit/debit cards, ACH transfers, and Pre-Authorized Debits (PADs)

### Default Billing Automation
- **Collection Methods**: Choose between automatic credit card charging and invoice generation for manual payment
- **Market-Specific Settings**: Configure different defaults for different markets if applicable
- **Automatic Application**: Settings apply to billing configurations of all new accounts created after saving
- **Individual Overrides**: Modify billing settings for specific accounts through `Accounts` > `Manage Accounts` > account's Products section

### Tax Rate Management
- **Rate Editing**: Modify existing tax rates through the kebab menu in `Administration` > `Tax Rates`
- **Address Requirements**: Accounts must have complete addresses with postal codes for tax calculation
- **Automatic Application**: Tax rates apply to invoices, invoice line items, shopping cart purchases, and sales orders
- **Exemption Management**: Configure tax exemptions for specific accounts or products through tax exemption settings

## Error handling and troubleshooting

### Payment Processing Issues
- **"Unsupported in Your Area" Error**: Check that billing contact information is complete at `Administration` > `My Billing`, especially the billing address with postal code
- **No Setup Option Available**: Indicates account is on Free/Trial tier, in unsupported region, or has account restrictions
- **Card Declined**: Verify payment method details, available funds, and business verification status

### Billing Automation Problems
- **Settings Not Applying**: Remember that default billing automation only affects new accounts created after saving settings
- **Manual Pricing Required**: You must manually set retail prices for each product to enable automatic billing based on active products
- **Market Configuration**: Ensure correct market is selected when configuring default settings for specific regions

### Tax Calculation Issues
- **No Tax Applied**: Verify that customer account has complete address with postal code
- **Incorrect Rates**: Check that tax rates are properly configured and address matches intended jurisdiction

:::warning
Missing billing address information prevents Vendasta Payments activation even in supported regions. Complete all billing contact details before attempting setup.
:::
