## Order settings overview

The ordering system includes a variety of customizable controls so you can tailor workflows and access levels for your team.

**Location:**  
**Partner Center → Administration → Order Settings**

This page lets you configure how orders behave and what actions users can take.

---

## What you can control in order settings

### Workflow step configuration (salespeople)

Enable or restrict specific behaviors for sales users:

- Configure workflow steps for salespeople  
- Remove Terms and Conditions only when resending an order for approval  
- Allow salespeople to edit order forms until activation  
- Require salespeople to complete all form fields before sending an order  
- Disable salespeople from selling standalone products on opportunities and orders  
- Disable salespeople from adding tags to orders  
- Allow customers to send an order to admins from a store page  

---

## Salesperson submit actions

Control what actions salespeople can perform when submitting orders:

- Allow salespeople to send orders directly to admins  
- Allow salespeople to charge the customer’s saved payment method when submitting an order  
- Allow salespeople to send orders to customers for approval  
- Orders sent for approval by salespeople can accept payment  

---

## Admin submit actions

- Admins can bypass the approval workflow and activate orders instantly  

---

## Default content

You can add internal reference forms to every order.  
> **Note:** These fields are for internal use only and **will not be visible to vendors**.

---

## Customer terms of service configuration

Set the terms of service that appear on an order when it’s sent for customer approval.

---
