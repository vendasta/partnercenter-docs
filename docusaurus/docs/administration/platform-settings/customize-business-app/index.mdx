# Customize Business App

This section covers customization options for Business App to help you tailor the experience for your clients. Configure branding, page visibility, notifications, and customer acquisition settings.

## How to access Customize Business App

Navigate to **Partner Center** > **Accounts** > **Manage Business App** > **Customize Business App**.
