# Customize Business App

Control the layout, navigation, and visual elements of your clients' Business App experience. Show or hide pages, customize branding, and create a tailored interface that matches your business and client needs.

## How to access

Go to **Partner Center** → **Accounts** → **Manage Business App** → **Customize Business App**.

## What you can do

- **Page visibility and navigation** – Show or hide dashboard sections (Grow, Listings, Protect, Connect, Reputation), the Automations page, and the Guides tab; organize the interface to match your workflow
- **Branding and visual identity** – Rename Business App, set logo placement, keep visual consistency across the interface
- **User experience** – Add notification banners, custom content (videos, messaging), and control how clients interact with features

## Page visibility and navigation

### Show or hide dashboard pages

1. Go to **Partner Center** → **Accounts** → **Manage Business App** → **Customize Business App**
2. Use **Page Visibility** (or **Pages**) to choose which sections are visible: **Grow**, **Listings**, **Protect**, **Connect**, **Reputation**, and others
3. Configure per client or globally and save

![Show This Page Settings](./img/customize-business-app/show-this-page-settings.png)

### Automations page

1. In Customize Business App, open **Automations**
2. Toggle **Show this Page** and save (enabled = clients see automations; disabled = page hidden)
3. Changes apply immediately; automations still run when triggers are met even if the page is hidden

### Guides section

1. Go to **Customize Business App** → **Pages** → **Guides**
2. Toggle **Guides** tab visibility and save (Show = clients see guides; Hide = tab removed)
3. With multiple markets, configure per market

:::info
Hiding the Guides section can simplify the interface when clients don't need in-app guides or you train through other channels.
:::

## Branding and visual identity

### Rename Business App

1. Go to **Customize Business App** → **Branding**
2. Select **Rename Business App**, enter your preferred name, and save

![Rename Business App interface](./img/rename-business-app/24508342660759.png)

The name updates across the client interface and in emails.

### Logo placement

1. In **Customize Business App** → **Branding**, upload your logo if needed
2. Use placement options: turn off **Display your logo in footer of Business App navigation** to move the logo from bottom-left to top-left
3. Preview and save

## User experience

### Notification banners

1. In **Customize Business App**, open the **Notifications** tab → **Global notification banner**
2. Set message text, display duration, styling, and target audience
3. Add an expiry date if needed and activate

Use banners for announcements, updates, or promotions.

### Client invitation settings

1. Create a landing page (e.g. with an Acquisition Widget for sign-up or trials)
2. Go to **Customize Business App** → **Add Your Clients**
3. Paste the landing page URL into **Invitation Landing Page URL** and save

An **Invite a business** button then appears in the client profile; clients can share your landing page to invite other businesses.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How quickly do interface changes take effect?</summary>

Most changes (page visibility, branding) take effect immediately. Clients see them on next refresh or login.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize the interface differently for different clients?</summary>

Yes. Many settings can be set per client or group. You can also control at user level: **Partner Center** → **Accounts** → **Manage Users** → three dots next to the user → **Edit Permissions**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I hide a page clients are using?</summary>

Access to that section is removed immediately. Communicate in advance and offer alternatives if needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I preview interface changes before applying them?</summary>

Some settings have previews. Best practice is to test with a staging or limited group before rolling out to everyone.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I know which pages my clients use?</summary>

Use analytics and engagement reports to decide what to show or hide.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I restore the default Business App name after renaming?</summary>

Yes. You can change the name back to "Business App" or another name anytime in branding settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between hiding a page and disabling a feature?</summary>

Hiding a page removes it from navigation; the feature may still be reachable. Disabling a feature removes access and functionality.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How does logo placement affect mobile users?</summary>

Test logo visibility and size on desktop and mobile so branding stays consistent.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I schedule notification banners for specific times?</summary>

Check the banner settings for timing and scheduling options.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What should I consider when customizing for clients?</summary>

Focus on client needs, simplicity, and functionality: remove unused features, keep navigation clear, maintain brand consistency, and use client feedback.
</details>
