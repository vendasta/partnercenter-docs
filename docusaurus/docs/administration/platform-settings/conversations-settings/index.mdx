Configure conversation management and communication tools: chat settings, response templates, conversation routing, and who can see the Conversations tab in Business App. You can turn off the Conversations tab for specific users or for all accounts in a market.

## Turn off the Conversations tab

The Conversations tab in Business App can be hidden from clients, for specific users or for all accounts in a market.

:::warning
Removing user access to Conversations also removes access to Conversations Pro, Lead capture, and SMS messaging. Turning it off is not recommended unless you have a specific reason.
:::

### For specific users

1. Go to `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Manage Users`
2. Select the user(s) to change, then click `Bulk Update x users`

   ![Bulk update users interface](./img/bulk-update-users.jpg)

3. Under `Tab Access`, open the `Conversations` dropdown, select `Turn Off`, then `Apply Changes to Users`

### For all users in a market

1. Go to `Partner Center` → `Accounts` → `Manage Business App` → `Customize Business App` → `Conversations`
2. Uncheck `Show this page` and save to remove Conversations from all clients in that market

   ![Conversations settings page](./img/inbox-settings.jpg)

## What you can configure

- **Chat settings** – Live chat and messaging preferences
- **Response templates** – Quick response templates
- **Auto-responses** – Automated replies for common inquiries
- **Conversation routing** – How conversations are assigned to team members
- **Integrations** – External communication platforms
- **Tab visibility** – Which users or markets see the Conversations tab (see above)

## Who gets notified about a conversation

Conversation notifications are not set on this page, and they are not part of [Partner Center notification settings](/administration/platform-settings/client-notifications) either. They follow the conversation itself: anyone who sends a message in a conversation, or who is assigned as Salesperson on the account, is automatically set to follow it and will be notified about it.

For the full picture — who is notified, why someone unexpected is receiving Inbox email, how to stop notifications for a conversation, and how to be alerted on follow-up messages rather than only new leads — see [Lead capture notifications](/business-app/conversations/conversations-ai/understanding-lead-capture-notifications).
