# Platform Settings

Comprehensive control center for customizing your business operations, team management, client experience, and brand identity within the platform. Configure everything from sales team permissions to Business App appearance and communication preferences.

## What it is

Platform Settings serve as your business operations control center, providing comprehensive customization options for how your business operates within the platform. These settings encompass sales team permissions and market configurations, Business App interface customization, brand identity and visual elements, communication management, and AI-powered features.

## What you can do with it

- **Control Sales Team Operations**: Configure role-based permissions, market access, pricing visibility, standalone product restrictions, and reporting capabilities for sales managers and salespeople
- **Customize Business App Interface**: Show, hide, or rearrange Business App sections, add custom videos and branding elements, control client permissions, and organize navigation to match your business workflow
- **Establish Brand Identity**: Upload logos, set colors, customize login experiences, apply branding to social media tools, and maintain visual consistency across all platform touchpoints
- **Manage Communication Settings**: Configure conversation management, chat settings, inbox preferences, and client communication tools for streamlined interactions
- **Set Up Client Notifications**: Control automated communication preferences and notification settings for how and when clients receive updates and information
- **Configure AI Features**: Organize AI knowledge base content for enhanced customer support and configure AI workforce features for automated task management
- **Customize Market Settings**: Manage multiple business territories, configure early access programs, set business priorities, and access advanced platform features
- **Control Customer Experience**: Customize how prospects and clients interact with your services and configure external data sources and business tools

## How it works

Platform Settings operate through a hierarchical configuration system where sales managers have full market access and administrative capabilities while salespeople have limited access based on assignments and market settings. Role-based access controls protect sensitive information while ensuring team members have necessary tools.

Business App customization allows interface layout control, content personalization, and client permission management. Brand identity elements maintain consistency across all platform touchpoints including logos, colors, login experiences, and social media tools.

1. **Review Platform Settings Overview** to understand how all configuration areas work together and impact business operations

## Key functionality

### [Sales and Operations](./customize/index.mdx)
Comprehensive sales team management including role-based access controls (sales managers vs. salespeople), market configuration for multiple territories, pricing and product controls (wholesale pricing visibility, standalone product restrictions), reporting capabilities (snapshot report limits, email campaign access), customer experience customization, early access program configuration, and business priorities management.

### [Business App Customization](./customize-business-app/index.mdx)
Complete Business App interface customization including layout control (show/hide sections), navigation organization, content personalization (custom videos, messages, branding), client permission management, page visibility settings, notification banners, invitation processes, and user interface personalization for optimal client workflow.

### [Brand Identity and Visual Elements](./partner-branding/index.mdx)
Visual branding and identity management including logo upload and placement, color scheme configuration, favicon setup for professional browser presentation, login experience customization, social media tool branding, and comprehensive white-label customization for consistent brand representation.

### [Communication Settings](./conversations-settings/index.mdx)
Conversation management and chat configuration including inbox settings management, bulk user updates, conversation preferences, and client communication tool optimization for streamlined support and interaction processes.

### [Client Notifications](./client-notifications/index.mdx)
Automated communication and notification management for controlling how and when clients receive updates, information, and system-generated communications based on business preferences.

### [AI Knowledge Base](./ai-knowledge-base/index.mdx)
AI-powered customer support configuration including content organization for enhanced automated assistance and knowledge base management for improved client self-service capabilities.

### [AI Workforce](./ai-workforce/index.mdx)
Automated task management and intelligent workflow configuration for improving operational efficiency through AI-powered business process automation and workforce optimization.

## Error handling or troubleshooting

- **Permission Issues**: Review role assignments, market access settings, and ensure proper sales team permission configuration
- **Business App Display Problems**: Verify layout settings, check content visibility controls, and confirm branding element placement
- **Brand Consistency Issues**: Review logo placement, color scheme application, and ensure favicon and login customization are properly configured
- **Communication Problems**: Check conversation settings, verify notification preferences, and confirm client communication tool configuration
