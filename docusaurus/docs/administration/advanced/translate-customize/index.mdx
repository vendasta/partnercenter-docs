Use Weblate to translate and customize text across the Vendasta platform. Access it from `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Translate and customize` or at [weblate.apigateway.co](https://weblate.apigateway.co/). Sign in with `Single-sign-on` using your Vendasta credentials.

![Weblate header image](./img/administration/translate-customize/weblate-header.jpg)

## What is Weblate?

Weblate is a centralized database for all of Vendasta's text content. As a partner you can contribute translations to the Vendasta ecosystem. Learn more about the software at [weblate.org](https://weblate.org/en/).

## Why use Weblate?

Translation is often manual, slow, and error-prone. Weblate automates syncing so source text and translations stay in sync between apps and Weblate.

## Supported languages

Supported languages include Czech, Dutch, French, German, and Spanish. View status and coverage at [Weblate Languages](https://weblate.apigateway.co/languages/).

## What you can translate

- Business App
- O&O products
- Partner Center
- Task Manager

## Translating text

1. Go to [Partner Center](http://partners.vendasta.com/) → `Administration` → `Translate and customize`, or open [weblate.apigateway.co](https://weblate.apigateway.co/).
2. Sign in with `Single-sign-on` and your Vendasta credentials.
3. In Weblate: `Languages` → `Browse all languages` and click the language you want.
4. Click the **project** you want to translate (e.g. Business App), then the **component** (e.g. Executive Report), then `Translate` on the right.
5. In the translation view you see the original (English) and a text box for your translation. After each entry click `Save` or `Suggest` (depending on your permission); you move to the next unit automatically.

More on workflows: [Weblate Documentation – Translating](https://docs.weblate.org/en/weblate-4.2.2/user/translating.html).

## Customizing text (white-labeled translation)

To customize text for your customers:

1. In Weblate, open the **project** you want to customize (e.g. Business App) and the **component** (e.g. Executive Report).
2. At the bottom of the language list click `Create white-labeled translation`.
3. Select a language from the list and click `Create`.
4. The new translation is created shortly; open it from the component page.

## Permission levels

There are two permission tiers in Weblate:

| Role | What you can do |
|------|----------------|
| **Viewer** | View all Global translations, make suggestions, and create white-labeled translations visible only to your customers |
| **Trusted Translator** | All Viewer privileges, plus the ability to review Viewer suggestions and contribute directly to Global translations |

To become a Trusted Translator, contact your Vendasta representative.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What languages are supported?</summary>

In Weblate click `Browse All Languages` for up-to-date statistics. Supported languages include Czech, Dutch, French, German, and Spanish.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I add a language?</summary>

Contact your Vendasta representative for unmet translation requirements.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What parts of the platform support translations?</summary>

In Weblate click `Browse All Projects` to see available projects.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How can I contribute to a translation?</summary>

Follow the quick-start video tour in Weblate for your first contribution; see Quick Links there for more documentation.
</details>

<details>
<summary>When are translation suggestions reviewed?</summary>

Typically within 30 days.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if only part of the site is translated?</summary>

A language may not have 100% coverage. Untranslated content falls back to English.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How are bad or inappropriate translations prevented?</summary>

Only Trusted Translators can update Global translations (the content every user can see).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I limit my users to only specific languages?</summary>

Not at this time.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is there HTML in the text?</summary>

Some HTML is kept inline so translators see full sentences and structure; it helps with context.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why are there curly braces in the text?</summary>

Curly braces mark where dynamic or parameterized content is substituted. Do not modify the text inside the braces.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I translate emails?</summary>

Translatable email templates are in the Email Templates project in Weblate.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I translate videos?</summary>

Translatable video subtitle files are in the Video Captions project in Weblate.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How does Weblate handle plurals, genders, and declensions?</summary>

Current internationalization tech does not fully support declensions.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Weblate available on all subscription levels?</summary>

Weblate translations and contributions are available only on Premium subscription and above.
</details>
