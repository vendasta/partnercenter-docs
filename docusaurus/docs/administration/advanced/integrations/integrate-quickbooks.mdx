The QuickBooks integration in Partner Center is a one-way sync of contacts from QuickBooks to the Partner Center CRM, plus 3 automation triggers. To set it up, go to `Administration` → `Integrations`, click the QuickBooks card, and click `Add Connection`.

## How do I set up the QuickBooks integration? {#setting-up-the-quickbooks-integration}

You can set up a connection with QuickBooks by navigating to the Integrations page of Partner Center.

- Click `Administration` → `Integrations` to navigate to the Integrations page.
- Click on the `QuickBooks Integration Card`.

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_integration_card.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Integration Card" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

- You'll be taken to the QuickBooks marketing page, which provides information on what QuickBooks is and the possibilities an integration with QuickBooks provides. Click `Add Connection`.

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_marketing_page.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Marketing Page" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

- This provides you with a Pre-Connect Form popup where you can define the data you want to synchronize between QuickBooks and the Partner Center CRM. The syncing options available are:
  - Contact Data
  - Invoice Data
  - Sales Receipt Data
  - Payment Data

Click `Continue` after choosing the options that apply.

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_preconnect_form.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Pre-Connect Form" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

- This leads to a single sign-on page where you can sign in to your QuickBooks account and provide permissions to establish the connection.
- Upon successful connection, you'll be automatically redirected to the `Manage` tab on the Integrations page where you can confirm the connection with QuickBooks.
- The established connection is shown on the `Manage` tab and the marketing page, as shown below:

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_connection_manage.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Connection on Manage Tab" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_connection_marketing.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Connection on Marketing Page" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

- Clicking either card will bring you to the `Connection Settings` page where you may specify what data to sync or choose to disconnect the integration.

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_connection_settings.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Connection Settings" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

:::info
After the initial connection with QuickBooks, all contacts in QuickBooks are synced with the Partner Center CRM. You can opt out of this by deselecting the Sync Customer Data option on the form during the initial connection.
:::

## How do I choose which data syncs? {#manage-synced-data}

You can choose the data that you want to sync between QuickBooks and Partner Center CRM:

1. Contact Data
2. Invoice Data 
3. Sales Receipt Data
4. Payment Data

Ensure that there is an active connection with QuickBooks by clicking on the `Manage` tab on the Integrations page and checking for the `QuickBooks Connection` card.

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_connection_card.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Connection Card" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

- Clicking on the `Connection Card` takes you to the `Connection Settings` page. Here, you can specify what data is to be synced.

<img src={require('./img/integrations/quickbooks/quickbooks_data_settings.jpg').default} alt="QuickBooks Connection Settings Page" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Is the QuickBooks integration a two-way sync?</summary>

No. The integration is a one-way sync of contacts from QuickBooks to the Partner Center CRM.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What data can I sync from QuickBooks?</summary>

You can sync Contact Data, Invoice Data, Sales Receipt Data, and Payment Data. Choose the options on the Pre-Connect Form when you connect, and change them later on the `Connection Settings` page.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens in the CRM when I create an invoice in QuickBooks?</summary>

A note is created on the contact's profile in Partner Center with details from QuickBooks, including the invoice number, contact name, and invoice amount.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I stop all QuickBooks contacts from syncing when I first connect?</summary>

Yes. After the initial connection, all QuickBooks contacts sync to the Partner Center CRM unless you deselect the Sync Customer Data option on the form during the initial connection.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I disconnect QuickBooks?</summary>

Go to the `Manage` tab on the Integrations page and click the QuickBooks connection card to open the `Connection Settings` page. From there, you can choose to disconnect the integration.

</details>
