Overview
- Salesforce CRM sync connects Salesforce with Vendasta CRM to keep contacts, companies, opportunities, emails, and meetings in sync.

Availability
- Business App: Available today. Manage from Business App > CRM settings.
- Partner Center: Availability is rolling out. Once enabled for your account, you can configure and manage the integration from Partner Center > Administration > Advanced > Integrations > Salesforce CRM sync.

Notes
- If you don’t see Salesforce CRM sync in Partner Center yet, your account may not be enabled. Contact your Vendasta representative or Support to request access.
- Functionality is the same regardless of where you access the integration; Partner Center simply provides an additional place for admins to configure and manage it.
