Overview

The Salesforce CRM Sync connects your Vendasta CRM data with Salesforce so that accounts and contacts can stay aligned between systems.

Availability

- Business App: available.
- Partner Center: rolling out. This integration will be available in Partner Center. If you do not see Salesforce listed under Administration > Advanced > Integrations yet, contact Support or your Customer Success Manager to request early access/enablement.

Access locations

- Business App: Settings > Integrations > Salesforce CRM Sync
- Partner Center: Administration > Advanced > Integrations > Salesforce CRM Sync (visible once enabled for your account)

FAQ

Q: Is the Salesforce CRM Sync integration available in Partner Center?
A: Yes—Partner Center support is being released. If it is not visible in your account yet, it will be available soon. In the meantime you can use it from Business App or request enablement.

Notes

- Feature availability may vary by region and account permissions.
