## What are billing reports and data?

Billing reports and data in Partner Center show your billing history, upcoming charges, and account activity. Find monthly billing reports in `Administration` → `Reports`, upcoming renewals in `Administration` → `My billing`, and invoices in `Administration` → `Financial Documents`.

## Why are billing reports important?

Billing reports help you:
- Track your spending and subscription costs
- Plan for upcoming expenses
- Analyze product performance and usage
- Maintain accurate financial records
- Access tax information for compliance

## What you can do with billing reports

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **View Billing Reports** | Access historical billing data and tax information |
| **Check Upcoming Charges** | See what you'll be billed for in the next cycle |
| **Export CSV Data** | Download detailed billing data for analysis |
| **Monitor Active Products** | Track renewal dates and subscription status |

## How do I access billing reports? {#how-to-access-billing-reports}

### Finding your billing reports

You can find your Billing Reports in `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Reports`.

### Exporting billing data as CSV

Partner Center admins who have access to Billing Reports can download billing data from Partner Center.

#### How do I download CSV reports? {#how-to-download-csv-reports}

1. Go to `Administration` → `Reports`
2. Click on ![export icon](./img/export-icon.jpg) beneath the month you want the report for

<img src={require('./img/billing-report-screenshot.jpg').default} alt="Billing report screenshot" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

#### What's included in CSV reports

The resulting spreadsheet will include:
- Business details such as company name, address, and phone number
- Market name  
- Product name
- Activation date
- Expiry date

## How do I view and download invoices and credit notes? {#how-to-view-and-download-invoices-and-credit-notes}

### Financial documents

Your invoices and credit notes are stored separately from the monthly billing reports. To access them:

1. Navigate to `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Financial Documents`
2. Use the `Sales Invoices` tab to view invoices, filter by date range or status, then download individual invoices as PDF
3. Use the `Credit Notes` tab to view credits issued for canceled products or services

### How do I export a customized billing CSV? {#how-to-export-a-customized-billing-csv}

You can filter and download billing data directly from the My billing page for a specific date range:

1. Navigate to `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `My billing`
2. Use the filter controls to set your desired date range
3. Click the `Download` icon to export the filtered data as a CSV

This is useful for reviewing charges over a custom period rather than a full calendar month.

### How do I download my outstanding balance report? {#how-to-download-your-outstanding-balance-report}

To download a report of your current outstanding account balance:

1. Navigate to `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `My billing`
2. Click the `Download balance report` icon near the top of the page

## Understanding your billing data

### Active products overview

The Active Products section shows:
- All currently active applications and services
- Monthly renewal amounts for each product
- Next renewal dates for subscription planning
- Product status and billing frequency

### Historical billing information

Your billing reports contain:
- Monthly invoices and charges
- Product activation and deactivation history
- Tax information and compliance data
- Detailed breakdowns by account and product

## Billing data export options

### CSV export benefits

Exporting billing data as CSV allows you to:
- Import data into accounting software
- Create custom reports and analysis
- Track spending trends over time
- Maintain detailed financial records
- Share billing information with stakeholders

### Export file contents

Each exported CSV file includes billing details organized by:
- Business account information
- Product and service details
- Billing dates and amounts
- Market and territory information

## FAQs

<details>
<summary>Where can I find my billing reports?</summary>

You can find your Billing Reports in `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Reports`. This section also includes sales tax collected reporting.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I see what I'll be charged next month?</summary>

Navigate to `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `My billing` and click on the `Active Products` tab. This will show all active apps and services with their next renewal dates and amounts.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I export my billing data?</summary>

Yes! Partner Center admins with access to Billing Reports can download CSV files by going to `Administration` → `Reports` and clicking the export icon beneath the month you want to download.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What information is included in the CSV export?</summary>

The CSV export includes business details (company name, address, phone), market name, product name, activation date, and expiry date for each billing item.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How often are billing reports updated?</summary>

Billing reports are generated monthly. Current month activity is reflected in the Active Products tab, while historical data is available in the Reports section.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I access tax information in my billing reports?</summary>

Yes, sales tax collected reporting is available in the same location as your billing reports under `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Reports`.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How far back can I access billing reports?</summary>

Historical billing reports are maintained in your Partner Center account. The exact retention period may vary, but you can access reports for previous months through the Reports section.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Who can access billing reports and export data?</summary>

Only Partner Center admins who have access to Billing Reports can view and download billing data. Check with your account administrator if you need access to these features.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I get an official financial report for taxes or a loan application?</summary>

There is no single official financial report generated by the platform for tax or loan purposes. However, you can compile the information you need by:

- Filtering your `Sales Invoices` in `Administration` → `Financial Documents` by date range and downloading paid invoices as PDFs
- Exporting a customized billing CSV from `Administration` → `My billing` using the date-range filter and download icon

These exports represent the most complete billing data available from Partner Center for a given period.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the difference between Billing Reports and Financial Documents?</summary>

**Billing Reports** (`Administration` → `Reports`) are monthly CSV exports showing product-level billing activity: useful for analyzing product usage and spend.

`Financial Documents` (`Administration` → `Financial Documents`) contain your formal invoices and credit notes in PDF format: useful for accounting, record keeping, and sharing with clients or accountants.
</details>
