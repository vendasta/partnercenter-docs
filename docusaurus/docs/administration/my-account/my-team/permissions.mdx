You may need to restrict a Partner Center admin's or salesperson's access to accounts or features—for example, to limit the list of actions, keep client information private, or simplify the experience.

## Accessing the permissions screen

1. Go to **Partner Center** → **Administration** → **My Team** and find the user.
2. Click **⋮** → **Edit member**. To see the full list of permissions, the user must be an admin. If the admin box is checked, click **Show more permissions** to see all editable permissions.

![Edit member permissions screen](./img/administration/my-team/edit-member-permissions.jpg)

## Admin permissions

Admin permissions control what an admin can see and do in Partner Center—from Automations and Marketplace to accounts, billing, and team management.

#### Can view and edit Automations

- **Off** – The Automations tab is hidden in the left-hand navigation.
- **On** – The Automations tab is shown; the admin can view, manage, and edit all automations.

#### Access to Dashboard

When on, the admin has access to **Home** and **Reports** in Partner Center navigation. Further report access is controlled by other admin permissions.

#### Access to company billing reports

- **On** – Access to My Billing, Financial documents, Reports, My Plan, and Billing pricing.
- **Off** – No access to those areas.

#### Able to customize the platform

- **Off** – No access to the **Customize** link under **Partner Center** → **Administration**.
- **On** – Full access to **Customize** and everything under it.

#### Can view and edit company profile

- **Off** – No access to the **Company Profile** link under **Partner Center** → **Administration**.
- **On** – Full access to **Company Profile** and everything under it.

#### Can access Marketplace

- **Off** – Marketplace tab is hidden; wholesale pricing is removed from orders.
- **On** – Marketplace tab is shown; admin can access Discover Products, Products, Packages, Manage Store, and Open Vendor Center. Wholesale pricing is visible on orders.

#### Can manage accounts and users

- **Off** – **Users** and **Accounts** under the Businesses tab are hidden.
- **On** – **Users** and **Accounts** are shown.

#### Can manage Marketing

- **Off** – Marketing tab is hidden.
- **On** – Marketing tab is shown.

#### Can manage Sales

Legacy permission; will be retired.

#### Can manage Task Manager

- **Off** – Tasks tab is hidden.
- **On** – Tasks tab is shown; admin can access Task Manager.

#### Can manage groups

Allows the admin to create and see multi-location groups.

#### Can create and manage admins

- **Off** – No access to the **My Team** link under **Partner Center** → **Administration**.
- **On** – Full access to **My Team** and everything under it.

#### Can manage orders

- **Off** – Admin can view orders and has the same order abilities as a salesperson (create and submit orders) but cannot approve or activate orders, unless system configuration allows it via accept-payment workflows.
- **On** – Admin can approve, decline, and activate orders and fully manage them, including charging orders in markets they can access (including all markets when their user access is configured for all-market coverage).

#### Can manage retail billing

When using Vendasta Payments, this permission allows the admin to:

- Create an invoice from a Sales Order or from **Commerce** → **Invoices**
- Set retail pricing under **Marketplace** → **Products** → **Product info**
- Access Subscriptions, Invoices, Payments, Payouts, credit notes, default billing settings, and tax rates

#### Premium Reports

A dropdown that controls a team member's access to [Premium Reports](/reports/premium-reports/):

- **No access to reports** – Cannot open Premium Reports.
- **View all unrestricted reports** – Can open any report shared with the whole team. Cannot change who has access.
- **Manage permissions for all unrestricted reports** – Can open any report shared with the whole team, and can change its per-report sharing settings.

Reports can also be restricted to specific people on a per-report basis. See [Premium Reports access controls](/reports/premium-reports/access-controls) for the full guide.

## Salesperson permissions

Salesperson permissions control access to Proposals, Inbox, Leaderboard, Pricing, Opportunities, and Orders—so you can limit what a team member sees or simplify their experience.

#### Can access Proposals

- **Off** – Proposals tab is hidden.
- **On** – Proposals tab is shown; the salesperson can view, manage, and edit all proposals.

#### Can access Inbox

- **Off** – Inbox is hidden across the platform (navigation, contact and company profile page).
- **On** – Inbox is available; the salesperson can view and interact with all inbox conversations.

#### Can access Leaderboard

- **Off** – Leaderboard tab is hidden.
- **On** – Leaderboard tab is shown; the salesperson can view the Leaderboard.

#### Can access Pricing

- **Off** – Pricing tab is hidden.
- **On** – Pricing tab is shown; the salesperson can view retail pricing and product details.

#### Can manage Opportunities

- **All opportunities** – Salesperson can see all opportunities.
- **Opportunities assigned to the user** – Salesperson only sees opportunities assigned to them.

#### Can manage Orders

- **All orders** – Salesperson can see all orders.
- **Orders assigned to the user** – Salesperson only sees orders assigned to them.
