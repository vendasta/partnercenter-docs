# My Account

My Account in Partner Center is where you manage your Vendasta subscription plan, team members, billing, financial documents, reports, pricing, and company profile.

## What is My Account? {#what-it-is}

My Account is where you manage your Vendasta platform. It includes subscription plan details, team member administration with role-based permissions, billing operations including payment methods and financial reporting, and business profile configuration.

## What can you do with My Account? {#what-you-can-do-with-it}

- **Manage Subscription Plans**: View current plan details, subscription information, and available features based on your service tier
- **Administer Team Members**: Create and manage Partner Center admins, salespeople, and Digital Agents with granular permission controls
- **Control Billing Operations**: Manage payment methods (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), billing contacts, invoicing preferences, and access billing reports
- **Handle Financial Documents**: Access invoices, receipts, credit notes, and export billing data for accounting purposes
- **Configure Company Profile**: Set up business information, company details, and organizational settings
- **Generate Business Reports**: Access analytical data and performance metrics for business insights
- **Manage Pricing Strategies**: Configure and adjust product pricing approaches for your business model

## How does My Account work? {#how-it-works}

The account management system operates through role-based access controls where Partner Center admins have full administrative privileges, salespeople have mid-level access focused on client management, and Digital Agents access Task Manager/Fulfillment for fulfillment work.

## How do I get started with My Account? {#get-started}

1. **Review Your Plan** at My Plan to understand current subscription features and limitations
2. **Set Up Team Access** at My team by inviting team members with appropriate roles and permissions
3. **Configure Billing** at My billing by adding payment methods, setting billing contacts, and reviewing invoicing preferences
4. **Complete Company Profile** to ensure accurate business information across the platform
5. **Access Financial Documents** to review billing history and export data for accounting

## Key functionality

### [My Plan](./my-plan/index.mdx)
Subscription plan management with detailed view of current plan features, service tiers, and available capabilities. Access subscription plan information to understand platform limitations and available functionality.

### [My team](./my-team/index.mdx)
Team member management including Partner Center admins, salespeople, and Digital Agents. Create team members, assign role-based permissions, manage profiles, reset passwords, change email addresses, and control access levels. View team overview with last login dates and role assignments.

### [My billing](./my-billing/index.mdx)
Billing operations including billing model management (flexible activation/cancellation with automatic renewals), payment method administration (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), billing contact configuration, billing reports, upcoming charge visibility, data export capabilities, management fees handling, credit notes, and vCash virtual credit management.

### [Reports](../../reports/index.mdx)
Business analytics and performance reporting for data-driven decision making and operational insights.

### [Financial Documents](./my-billing/index.mdx)
Centralized access to invoices, receipts, credit notes, and other financial documentation for accounting and record-keeping purposes.

### [Pricing](./pricing/index.md)
Product pricing strategy management and configuration for optimizing business revenue models.

### Company Profile
Business information management including company details, organizational settings, and profile configuration.

## How do I troubleshoot My Account issues? {#error-handling-or-troubleshooting}

- **Team Access Issues**: Reset passwords, verify email addresses, check permission assignments, and ensure proper role configuration
- **Billing Problems**: Review payment method validity, verify billing contact information, check for declined payments, and confirm subscription status
- **Plan Limitations**: Understand feature availability based on subscription tier and contact support for upgrade options
- **Financial Document Access**: Ensure proper permissions for viewing invoices and financial data

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What payment methods can I use to pay my Vendasta bill?</summary>

You can add Visa, Mastercard, or Amex as payment methods in `My billing`.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What access do different team member roles have?</summary>

Partner Center admins have full administrative privileges. Salespeople have mid-level access focused on client management, and Digital Agents access Task Manager/Fulfillment for fulfillment work.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What should I check if a team member can't access Partner Center?</summary>

Reset their password, verify their email address, and check their permission assignments and role in `My team`.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I find my invoices and receipts?</summary>

Invoices, receipts, credit notes, and other financial documents are in `My billing`, where you can also export billing data for accounting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I upgrade my plan?</summary>

Review your current features and limitations in `My Plan`, then contact support for upgrade options.

</details>
