## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Does it cost anything to be an affiliate partner?</summary>

No. Signing up and participating is free, and there are no minimum sales required to earn commission.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I track my referrals?</summary>

Yes. You get access to a central hub where you can view your referrals' status.
</details>
