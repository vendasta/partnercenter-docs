# Administration

The **Administration** section provides comprehensive management tools for your platform configuration, team management, and business operations. This centralized hub gives you control over all aspects of your Vendasta platform setup.

## Core Administration Areas

The Administration section is organized into five main areas, each designed to handle specific aspects of your platform management:

### [My Account](./my-account/index.mdx)
Manage your personal account settings, subscription plan, team members, billing information, and financial documents. Access reports and configure your company profile.

### [Platform Settings](./platform-settings/index.mdx)
Configure platform-wide settings including customization options, partner branding, client notifications, AI features, and conversation management. Control how your platform operates and appears to users.

### [Commerce](./commerce/index.mdx)
Set up payment processing, billing automation, order management, tax configurations, and all commerce-related features. Manage the financial aspects of your business operations.

### [Data Management](./data-management/index.mdx)
Configure CRM objects, custom fields, scoring systems, and sales pipelines. Organize and structure your business data for optimal workflow management.

### [Advanced](./advanced/index.mdx)
Access developer APIs, third-party integrations, service accounts, single sign-on configurations, and platform customization tools for advanced users.

## Getting Started

1. **Start with [My Account](./my-account/index.mdx)** to configure your basic account settings and team structure
2. **Configure [Platform Settings](./platform-settings/index.mdx)** to customize your platform appearance and functionality
3. **Set up [Commerce](./commerce/index.mdx)** features if you process payments or manage billing
4. **Organize [Data Management](./data-management/index.mdx)** to structure your business workflow
5. **Explore [Advanced](./advanced/index.mdx)** features as your platform needs grow

Each section contains detailed guides and step-by-step instructions to help you configure your platform according to your business needs.
