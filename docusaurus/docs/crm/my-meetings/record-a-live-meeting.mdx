## Overview

This article explains how to record a live meeting by manually adding a Notetaker. This is useful when you have a meeting that is not on your calendar or when automatic Notetaker joining is not enabled for your account.

## What is recording a live meeting?

Recording a live meeting allows you to manually send your Notetaker to join an active meeting. This is sometimes called an "ad-hoc" meeting because the Notetaker joins on demand rather than automatically from a calendar event.

## When to use this feature

Use this feature when:

- You have a spontaneous meeting that is not on your calendar
- You want the Notetaker to join a meeting scheduled by someone else
- Automatic Notetaker joining is not enabled for your user account
- You need to quickly record a meeting that is about to start

## Requirements

Before recording a live meeting, ensure the following:

- **Meeting recording must be enabled** for your organization
- **Supported platforms** - The meeting must be on Google Meet or Microsoft Teams
- **Meeting timing** - The meeting must be happening now or about to start (future scheduling is not supported)
- **Valid meeting link** - You need the full meeting URL

## How to record a live meeting

1. Navigate to `CRM` → `My Meetings`.
2. Click the **Record a live meeting** button.
3. Paste the meeting URL into the **Meeting link** field.
4. Click **Record**.

The Notetaker joins the meeting immediately and begins taking notes once the meeting starts.

:::tip
Copy the meeting link directly from your calendar invitation or video conferencing app to ensure you have the correct URL.
:::

## Supported meeting links

The following meeting URL formats are supported:

| Platform | Example URL |
|----------|-------------|
| Google Meet | `https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij` |
| Microsoft Teams | `https://teams.microsoft.com/l/meetup-join/...` |

:::info
Zoom meetings are not supported for this feature.
:::

## What happens after you record a live meeting

Once you start recording a live meeting:

1. The Notetaker joins the meeting within a few seconds
2. When the meeting ends, the recording and transcript are processed
3. You can view the recording and AI insights from the **Recordings** tab
4. The meeting is private by default and visible only to meeting participants

## Difference from automatic Notetaker joining

| Feature | Automatic joining | Record a live meeting |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Source | Calendar events | Meeting URL |
| Requires calendar sync | Yes | No |
| Requires user opt-in | Yes | No |
| Scheduling | Based on calendar | Immediate only |
| Best for | Regular scheduled meetings | Spontaneous meetings |

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can I schedule a recording for a meeting in the future?</summary>

No. When you use Record a live meeting, the Notetaker joins immediately. For meetings scheduled in the future, add the meeting to your calendar and enable automatic Notetaker joining.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can I not see the Record a live meeting button?</summary>

The button may not appear if meeting recording is not enabled for your organization. Contact your administrator to enable this feature.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I record a Zoom meeting?</summary>

No. Record a live meeting currently supports Google Meet and Microsoft Teams only.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if the Notetaker does not join the meeting?</summary>

Verify that:
- The meeting URL is correct and complete
- The meeting is currently active or about to start
- The meeting is on Google Meet or Microsoft Teams

If the issue persists, try copying the meeting link again from your calendar or meeting invitation.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will the Notetaker announce itself when it joins?</summary>

This depends on your Notetaker settings. You can configure whether the Notetaker announces itself and displays a compliance message in your [recording settings](./customizing-meeting-recording-settings.mdx).

</details>
