## Overview

When a recorded meeting finishes being analyzed, authorized users receive a notification that the summary is ready. Notifications are sent both as an in-app notification and by email, so you can review the meeting details as soon as they are available.

:::note
Conversation intelligence features (meeting recording, AI summaries, sales coaching, and conversation scoring) are available with CRM AI Pro. See [CRM AI editions](/ai/ai-workforce/ai-sales-assistant#crm-ai-editions) for details.
:::

## How it works

After a meeting is recorded and the AI analysis completes, each authorized user on the meeting automatically receives:

- An **in-app notification** in the notification center
- An **email notification** to their registered email address

Both notifications include the meeting title and a direct link to the [Meeting Details page](./meeting-details-page.mdx) where you can review the summary, transcript, action items, and sales coaching insights.

## Who receives notifications

Notifications are sent to **authorized users within your organization** who are associated with the meeting. You are an authorized user if you are one of the following:

- The **host** of the meeting
- The **creator** of the meeting record
- An **authorized user** added to the meeting

External participants, such as clients, prospects, or anyone outside your organization, do not receive notifications, even if they attended the meeting.

For example, if a sales rep at your company hosts a call with a prospect, only the sales rep (and any other internal team members added as authorized users) receives the notification. The prospect does not receive one, because they are outside your organization.

## What the notification includes

Each notification contains:

- The **meeting title** so you can identify which meeting was analyzed
- A **direct link** to the Meeting Details page for that meeting

Clicking the link opens the meeting in the CRM, where you can review the full summary, transcript, action items, topics, and sales coaching scores.

## Manage notification preferences

You can control whether you receive meeting summary notifications by app, email, or both.

1. Go to `Notification Settings`.
2. Find `Meeting Summary Ready` in the list.
3. Check or uncheck `App` and `Email` to choose how you want to be notified.
4. Select `Save`.

By default, both `App` and `Email` are enabled. If you uncheck both, you will not receive any notification when a meeting summary is ready.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can I turn off meeting summary notifications?</summary>

Yes. Go to `Notification Settings`, find `Meeting Summary Ready`, and uncheck `App` and `Email`. Select `Save` to apply.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long after recording does the notification arrive?</summary>

Notifications are sent as soon as the AI analysis is complete. This typically takes a few minutes after the meeting recording ends, depending on the length of the meeting.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will I get a notification for every meeting I attend?</summary>

You only receive notifications for meetings where you are an authorized user (host, creator, or added as an authorized user). If you attended a meeting but are not an authorized user, you will not receive a notification.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if the meeting analysis fails?</summary>

If the analysis fails, no notification is sent. You can check the status of your meetings from the `Recordings` tab in `My Meetings`.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I receive both an email and an in-app notification?</summary>

Yes. Both are sent by default when analysis completes. You can choose to receive only one or the other from `Notification Settings` by toggling the `App` and `Email` checkboxes for `Meeting Summary Ready`.

</details>
