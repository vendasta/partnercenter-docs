# Leaderboard

The sales leaderboard is a powerful tool for you and your team to compare performance metrics and encourage healthy competition amongst your team. This comprehensive performance tracking system showcases all of your salespeople and important revenue metrics in an easy-to-understand format. With advanced filtering options and export capabilities, the leaderboard provides valuable insights into team performance and individual achievements. The leaderboard serves as both a motivational tool and a data source for strategic sales management decisions.

## Why use the leaderboard?

Transform your sales team performance with visual metrics that drive competition and accountability. The leaderboard eliminates guesswork in performance evaluation and provides clear, measurable insights that help sales managers coach their teams more effectively while motivating salespeople to achieve better results.

## What's included

- **Performance metrics tracking:** See all your salespeople and their key revenue metrics at a glance.
- **Team-based filtering:** Filter by specific sales teams, markets, or custom date ranges to isolate the data you need.
- **Data export:** Download your leaderboard data as a CSV for use in custom reporting or external analysis.

## Get started

1. **Navigate to the leaderboard**
   - In **Partner Center**, go to **CRM** > **Leaderboard**.
   - Your sales performance dashboard will load automatically.

2. **Review team performance**
   - View a list of all salespeople and their corresponding revenue metrics.
   - Use the data to compare performance across your entire team.

3. **Apply filters**
   - Use the filters at the top of the page to focus on specific sales teams or markets.
   - Adjust the date range to analyze performance over specific periods.

4. **Export data**
   - Click the export button to download the current view as a **CSV file**.
   - Use this data in your own reporting systems for deeper analysis.

## Team comparison capabilities

The leaderboard provides comprehensive visibility into your team's sales performance with the ability to compare metrics across different team members, time periods, and market segments. This powerful tool helps identify top performers, track progress over time, and ensure accountability across your entire sales organization.
