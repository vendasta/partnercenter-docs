# Tasks

Managing tasks is an essential part of sales activities in the Vendasta CRM. This comprehensive task management system helps you create, track, and complete sales activities to ensure nothing falls through the cracks. Tasks can be created from multiple sources including CRM records, opportunities, and manual entry, providing flexibility in how you organize your sales workflow. The Tasks system integrates seamlessly with your CRM data to provide context-aware task management that drives consistent follow-up and improved sales outcomes.

## Why use tasks?

Transform your sales activity management with organized task tracking that ensures consistent follow-up and prevents missed opportunities. Tasks eliminate the risk of forgotten activities while providing clear accountability and progress tracking across your entire sales process.

## What's included

- Multiple task creation methods from CRM records, opportunities, and manual entry
- Task management workflows for consistent follow-up
- Integration with contact and company profiles
- Automated task creation capabilities
- Progress tracking and completion status monitoring

## Get started

1. **Navigate to tasks**
   - Access tasks through various CRM sections
   - Use task creation from contact or company records

2. **Create your first task**
   - Go to `CRM` > `Contacts` or `CRM` > `Companies`
   - Click into a specific record to create associated tasks

3. **Set up task workflows**
   - Create tasks from opportunities and deals
   - Configure automated task creation where appropriate

4. **Manage and complete tasks**
   - Track task progress and completion status
   - Use tasks to maintain consistent sales activities

## Task creation methods

You can create tasks in Vendasta in multiple different ways:

- **From CRM records**: Create tasks directly from contact or company profiles
- **From opportunities**: Generate tasks associated with specific deals
- **Manual creation**: Add standalone tasks for general sales activities

## Creating a sales task

You can create tasks in Vendasta in multiple different ways:

### Create tasks from a CRM record

![Create tasks from a CRM record](./img/crm/tasks/create-tasks-from-crm-record.png)

Navigate to your record:

1. **Contact:** In `Partner Center`, go to `CRM` > `Contacts`
2. **Company:** In `Partner Center`, go to `CRM` > `Companies`

After you are on the record's table:

1. Click the name of the record
2. Click `Tasks` in the activity logger in the middle
3. In the activity logger, enter the details of your task
4. Click `Log activity`

### Create tasks from the task table

![Create tasks from the task table](./img/crm/tasks/create-tasks-from-task-table.png)

Navigate to `Partner Center` > `CRM` > `Tasks`

1. Click `Create task`
2. Fill out the task details
3. Click `Create`

## Task management benefits

Sales tasks help you keep track of your sales activities in the CRM, ensuring that important follow-ups and activities are completed on time. This systematic approach to task management improves sales consistency and helps maintain momentum throughout your sales process.
