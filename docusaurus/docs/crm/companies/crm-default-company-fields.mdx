Company fields store information about a company that you work with, such as their name, or the last time you have reached out to one of their contacts. Default properties are created in each company to store some important information. Some of the properties, such as the last engagement date, are automatically populated.

:::info Field Extensions
Beyond the default fields listed below, your CRM may include additional company fields depending on:

- **Product integrations** - Different Vendasta products may add specialized fields
- **Third-party integrations** - Connected services (Google Ads, Facebook, etc.) can extend available fields
- **Custom field configurations** - Your organization may have added custom company fields
- **Feature enablements** - Certain features unlock additional data fields when activated

The fields shown here represent the core default set available to all accounts.
:::

## Basic Company Information

1. **ID:** Unique identifier assigned to each company
2. **External ID:** An identifier used by external systems or databases to reference the company
3. **Company name:** The official name of the company
4. **Website:** The URL address of the company's website
5. **Phone number:** The contact phone number for the company
6. **Fax number:** The fax number for the company (if applicable)
7. **Number of employees:** The total count of employees working in the company
8. **Legal structure:** The legal structure of the company, such as 'private', 'public', 'non-profit', etc.
9. **Industry:** The industry or sector in which the company operates
10. **Annual revenue:** The total revenue generated by the company annually
11. **Description:** A text field for storing additional information about the company

## Address & Location Information

12. **Street address line 1:** First line of the company's physical street address
13. **Street address line 2:** Second line of the company's physical street address
14. **City/locality:** City or locality of the company's primary location
15. **State/province/region:** State, province, or region of the company's primary location
16. **Zip/postal code:** The postal code or ZIP code of the company's location
17. **Country:** The country where the company is located
18. **Latitude/longitude:** Latitude and Longitude of the company's primary location
19. **Timezone:** The timezone in which the company operates or is located

## Lead Management & Classification

20. **Lifecycle stage:** The stage of the company in the customer lifecycle
21. **Lead score:** The calculated lead score of the company based on engagement and behavior
22. **Lead quality:** The customizable lead quality assessment of the company
23. **Lead status:** The current status of the company in the sales pipeline
24. **Lead source:** The original source that brought the company into your system
25. **Priority:** Priority level assigned to the company for sales and marketing activities
26. **Tags:** User-defined keywords or labels that can be used to categorize companies

## Social Media URLs

27. **Facebook URL:** The URL of the company's Facebook page
28. **Instagram URL:** The URL of the company's Instagram profile
29. **LinkedIn URL:** The URL of the company's LinkedIn page
30. **X URL:** The URL of the company's X profile

## Engagement & Activity Tracking

31. **Last activity date:** The date of the last recorded activity associated with the company
32. **Last contact date:** The date of the last contact made with any of the company's contacts
33. **Last campaign email sent date:** Last campaign email sent to the company
34. **Last campaign email sent name:** Name of last campaign email sent for the company
35. **Last campaign email open date:** The date when the last campaign email was opened by any of the company's contacts
36. **Last campaign email clicked date:** The date when the last campaign email was clicked by any of the company's contacts
37. **Last campaign email bounced date:** The date when the last campaign email bounced for any of the company's contacts
38. **Last sales email sent date:** Date of last sales email sent to the company
39. **Last sales email received date:** Date of last sales email received for the company
40. **Next task ID:** The identifier of the next task associated with the company
41. **Next task due date:** The due date for the next task associated with the company

## Attribution & Source Tracking

42. **Record source:** Name of the app or external system that created the company (e.g., 'Salesforce', 'Hubspot', 'Gmail')
43. **Record source drill down 1:** Record source drill down level 1 of the company
44. **Record source drill down 2:** Record source drill down level 2 of the company
45. **Original source:** Original source of the company
46. **Original source drill down 1:** Original source drill down level 1 of the company
47. **Original source drill down 2:** Original source drill down level 2 of the company

## UTM & Campaign Attribution

48. **UTM campaign:** The name or identifier of the campaign associated with the company
49. **UTM medium:** The medium or channel of the campaign associated with the company
50. **UTM source:** The specific source of the campaign associated with the company
51. **UTM content:** The content or variant of the campaign associated with the company
52. **UTM term:** The specific keyword or term associated with the campaign for the company

## Click Tracking IDs

53. **GCLID:** Google click ID used to create the company
54. **FBCLID:** Facebook click ID used to create the company
55. **MSCLKID:** Microsoft click ID used to create the company

## System Fields

56. **Created date:** The date when the company record was created
57. **Updated date:** The date when the company record was last updated
58. **Owner ID:** The unique identifier for the user who created the company record
59. **Parent company:** The parent company to which the company belongs

## Platform-Specific Information (Partner Center Only)

60. **Platform account group ID:** Identifier for an account group inside of the platform
61. **Salesperson:** The salesperson in the platform
62. **Additional salespeople:** Additional salespeople in the platform

## Snapshot & Analytics Data (When Available)

### Review Performance
63. **Review performance grade:** Grade of the companies overall review performance
64. **Number of reviews:** Total number of reviews across all sources for the company
65. **Number of review sources:** Number of sources where the company has been reviewed
66. **Average review score:** Average score or rating received by the company based on reviews
67. **Review data updated date:** The date when the review data was last updated by the Snapshot Report
68. **Google review score:** The overall review score of the company on Google

### Social Media Performance
69. **Social performance grade:** Grade of the company's overall social media performance
70. **Facebook data last updated date:** Date when Facebook data was last updated
71. **Total number of Facebook likes:** Total number of likes on the company's Facebook page
72. **Average number of Facebook likes per post:** Average number of likes per Facebook post
73. **Average number of Facebook posts per month:** Average number of Facebook posts made each month
74. **Instagram data last updated date:** Date when Instagram data was last updated
75. **Total number of Instagram followers:** Total number of followers on Instagram account
76. **Total number of Instagram posts:** Total number of posts published on Instagram account
77. **X data last updated date:** Date when X (Twitter) data was last updated
78. **Total number of X posts:** Total number of posts made on X
79. **Total number of followers on X:** Total number of accounts following the company on X
80. **Total number of following on X:** Total number of profiles the company follows on X

### SEO & Website Performance
81. **SEO local search grade:** Grade of the company's local search performance
82. **SEO daily organic traffic value:** Estimated value of the company's organic traffic per day
83. **SEO number of organic keywords:** Number of keywords for which the company's website ranks in organic search results
84. **SEO organic clicks per month:** Number of organic clicks per month for the company's website
85. **SEO organic domain ranking:** Ranking of the company's website in organic search results
86. **SEO data updated date:** Date when the SEO data were last updated
87. **Website grade:** Overall rating of the website based on various factors by Snapshot
88. **Website mobile speed:** The speed at which the website loads on mobile devices
89. **Website desktop speed:** The speed at which the website loads on desktop computers
90. **Website is secure:** Indicates whether the website has security measures in place (SSL certificate)
91. **Website is mobile friendly:** Indicates whether the website is optimized for mobile devices
92. **Website has video:** Indicates whether the website contains video content
93. **Website has phone number:** Indicates whether the website displays a phone number
94. **Website has physical address:** Indicates whether the website displays a physical address
95. **Website links to Facebook:** Indicates if the company's website homepage links to its Facebook page
96. **Website links to Instagram:** Indicates if the company's website homepage links to its Instagram page
97. **Website links to X:** Indicates if the company's website homepage links to its X account
98. **Website data last updated date:** Date when website data was last updated

### Technology & Business Intelligence
99. **Technology IDs:** IDs of technologies used by the company
100. **Technology IDs updated date:** Date when the technology IDs were last updated
101. **Active products:** The list of active products/editions for the company
102. **GBP claimed:** Whether the company has claimed their Google Business Profile listing

### Listings Performance
103. **Listings performance grade:** Grade of the company's overall listings performance
104. **Incorrect address count:** The number of listings with incorrect addresses found for the company
105. **Incorrect website count:** The number of listings with incorrect websites found for the company

### Snapshot Reports
106. **Snapshot latest created date:** Creation date of the most recently generated Snapshot report
107. **Snapshot latest expiry date:** Expiration date of the most recently generated Snapshot report
108. **Snapshot latest snapshot ID:** Snapshot ID of the most recently generated Snapshot report

:::tip Viewing All Available Fields
To see the complete list of company fields available in your specific CRM instance, navigate to any company record and click the "View all properties" or "More fields" option. This will show both default and integration-specific fields enabled for your account.
:::
