# CRM default contact fields

Contact properties store information about a contact that you work with, such as their name, or the last time you reached out to them. Default properties are created in each contact to store important information. Some of the properties, such as the last engagement date, are automatically populated.

## Contact Information

1. **ID:** Unique identifier assigned to each contact.
2. **External ID:** An identifier used by external systems or databases to reference the contact.
3. **First name:** The given or first name of the contact.
4. **Last name:** The family or last name of the contact.
5. **Email:** The primary email address of the contact used for communication and identification.
6. **Phone number:** The contact's primary phone number for voice communication.
7. **Additional phone number:** A secondary phone number for the contact, providing alternative contact methods.
8. **Job title:** The position or job title of the contact within their organization.
9. **Role:** The specific role or responsibility of the contact within their company or in relation to your business.
10. **Seniority:** The level of seniority or authority of the contact within their organization.
11. **LinkedIn URL:** The URL of the contact's LinkedIn profile for professional networking.
12. **Primary Company:** The primary company record associated with the contact in the CRM.

## Personal Information

13. **Birthday:** The contact's full birth date including month and day.
14. **Birth Year:** The birth year of the contact (e.g., '1980').
15. **Gender:** The gender of the contact (e.g., 'male', 'female', 'other').
16. **Timezone:** The timezone of the contact represented as a string (e.g., 'America/New_York').
17. **Interests:** Contact's interests and hobbies (e.g., 'Hiking', 'Skiing', 'Cooking', 'Paintball').
18. **Skills:** Contact's professional skills and expertise (e.g., 'Java', 'Python', 'Javascript').
19. **Message:** Message or notes sent from the contact, commonly input by the contact themselves on an intake form.

## Address Information

20. **Street address line 1:** First line of the contact's physical street address.
21. **Street address line 2:** Second line of the contact's physical street address (apartment, suite, etc.).
22. **City/locality:** The city or locality where the contact is located.
23. **State/province/region:** The state, province, or region where the contact is located.
24. **Zip/postal code:** The postal code or ZIP code of the contact's location.
25. **Country:** The country where the contact is located.

## Lead Management & Scoring

26. **Lifecycle stage:** The stage of the contact in the customer lifecycle, this field is customizable.
27. **Lead score:** The calculated lead score of the contact based on engagement and behavior.
28. **Lead quality:** The customizable lead quality assessment of the contact based on the lead score.
29. **Tags:** Descriptive keywords that help you find and segment contacts for organization and targeting.

## Activity & Engagement Tracking

30. **Last activity date:** The date of the last recorded activity associated with the contact.
31. **Last engagement date:** The date of the last engagement from the contact.
32. **Last contact date:** The date of the last direct contact made with the contact.

## Email Campaign Tracking

33. **Last campaign email sent date:** The date when the last marketing campaign email was sent to the contact.
34. **Last campaign email sent name:** The name or identifier of the last marketing campaign email sent to the contact.
35. **Last campaign email open date:** The date when the last marketing campaign email was opened by the contact.
36. **Last campaign email clicked date:** The date when the last marketing campaign email was clicked by the contact.
37. **Last campaign email bounced date:** The date when the last marketing campaign email bounced for the contact.
38. **Last campaign email dropped date:** The date when the last marketing campaign email was dropped (not delivered) for the contact.

## Sales Email Tracking

39. **Last sales email sent date:** The date when the last sales email was sent by any salesperson to the contact.
40. **Last sales email received date:** The date when the last sales email was sent by the contact and received by any salesperson.

## SMS Campaign Tracking

41. **Last campaign SMS sent date:** The date when the last marketing campaign SMS was sent to the contact.
42. **Last campaign SMS sent name:** The name or identifier of the last marketing campaign SMS sent to the contact.
43. **Last campaign SMS delivered date:** The date when the last marketing campaign SMS was successfully delivered to the contact.
44. **Last campaign SMS undelivered date:** The date when the last marketing campaign SMS failed to be delivered to the contact.
45. **Last campaign SMS failed date:** The date when the last marketing campaign SMS failed to send to the contact.

## Consent & Privacy Management

46. **Marketing email consent:** The current status for the contact's marketing email consent ('Subscribed', 'Unsubscribed', or unset).
47. **Marketing email consent timestamp:** The timestamp when the marketing email consent status was last updated.
48. **Marketing email consent source:** The source or method that updated the marketing email consent field.
49. **SMS consent:** The current status for the contact's SMS marketing consent ('Subscribed', 'Unsubscribed', or unset).
50. **SMS consent timestamp:** The timestamp when the SMS consent status was last updated.
51. **SMS consent source:** The source or method that updated the SMS consent field.

## Task Management

52. **Next task ID:** The unique identifier of the next task assigned to the contact.
53. **Next task due date:** The due date for the next task assigned to the contact.

## Attribution & Source Tracking

54. **Record Source:** How the contact record was originally created in the CRM system (e.g., 'CRM UI', 'Bulk Import', 'Web Chat', 'Form').
55. **Record Source Drill Down 1:** Additional detail about the primary record source, providing specific methods or channels within the main source category.
56. **Record Source Drill Down 2:** Further granular detail about the record source, giving insights into specific processes or sub-channels.
57. **Original Source:** The original source where a contact first interacted with your business (e.g., website visit, social media, direct contact). Requires customization based on automation or integration.
58. **Original Source Drill Down 1:** More specific details about the original source, such as the particular platform or medium within the original source.
59. **Original Source Drill Down 2:** Deeper specifics about the original source, detailing the exact activity or interaction that brought the contact to your business.

## UTM & Campaign Attribution

60. **UTM Campaign:** The marketing campaign name from UTM parameters (e.g., 'summer_sale', 'black_friday', 'spring_promotion').
61. **UTM Medium:** The medium or channel from UTM parameters (e.g., 'cpc', 'email', 'social', 'organic', 'referral').
62. **UTM Source:** The traffic source from UTM parameters (e.g., 'google', 'facebook', 'twitter', 'linkedin', 'newsletter').
63. **UTM Content:** The content variant from UTM parameters (e.g., 'banner', 'sidebar', 'footer', 'popup', 'cta_button').
64. **UTM Term:** The keyword or search term from UTM parameters (e.g., 'running+shoes', 'digital+marketing', 'small+business+software').

## Click Tracking IDs

65. **GCLID:** Google Click Identifier used to track the contact's origin from Google Ads campaigns.
66. **FBCLID:** Facebook Click Identifier used to track the contact's origin from Facebook advertising campaigns.
67. **MSCLKID:** Microsoft Click Identifier used to track the contact's origin from Microsoft Advertising campaigns.

## System Fields

68. **Updated date:** The date when the contact record was last updated.
69. **Created date:** The date when the contact record was created.

## Partner Specific Information (Does not exist in Business App)

70. **Platform SSC contact IDs:** Identifier associated with the contact in a specific platform or system.
71. **Platform user ID:** The unique identifier of the user associated with the contact in a specific platform or system.
72. **Salesperson:** The individual responsible for managing sales activities for the contact.
73. **Additional salespeople:** Other individuals involved in sales activities for the contact.
