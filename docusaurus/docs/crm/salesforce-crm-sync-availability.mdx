Correction: The Salesforce CRM sync integration will be available in Partner Center.

Earlier responses and some internal guidance suggested the integration was only available in Business App and not in Partner Center. This page clarifies that Salesforce CRM sync will be supported in Partner Center.

What this means for partners:
- You will be able to access and manage the Salesforce CRM sync from Partner Center once released.
- Setup steps and timelines will be added here when release details are finalized.

If you encounter any content elsewhere in the docs stating that the Salesforce CRM sync is not available in Partner Center, please treat this page as the current source of truth and open a docs issue so we can update it.
