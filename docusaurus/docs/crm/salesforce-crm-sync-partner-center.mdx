Note
Earlier guidance stated that the Salesforce CRM sync integration is only available in Business App and that there is no Salesforce sync in Partner Center. That information is outdated.

Correction
Salesforce CRM sync will be available in Partner Center. We will update this documentation with setup steps and timelines as soon as they are generally available.

Why this matters
- Partners will be able to manage Salesforce syncing directly in Partner Center (not only via Business App).
- The previous statement limited availability to Business App and may cause confusion for sales and support teams.

Status
- Coming soon to Partner Center. Timelines and feature scope are subject to change.

If you have questions about timing or access, please contact your Vendasta representative.
