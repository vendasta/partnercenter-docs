## What is this? 

Inbox Pro centralizes lead management for your clients with AI-assisted web chat lead capture, Google Business Messages integration, and nearly unlimited SMS messaging. Setting up the product, to utilize all these functionalities, is easy, but it can be time-consuming and challenging for business owners with limited resources and digital experience. 

That’s why Vendasta’s Marketing Services team developed an adjoining service, Inbox Pro Enablement, to help business owners enjoy all the features Inbox Pro has to offer. We’ll help our clients claim and verify the business listing so that they can respond to messages coming in through Google Search and Maps right from Inbox in Business App. We’ll also install the web chat embed code on their website so that they can provide prompt responses, collect contact information, and acquire new customers effortlessly. 

And until March 1st, 2024, [this service is absolutely free](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/products/MP-DKT6XHPM6NCCDNK2TPDPVD3PG3V7ZHWP/addon/A-5MR5FCFDD3)!

We’re simply making it easier for you and your clients to enjoy the benefits of Inbox Pro, at no additional cost (until March 1st, that is.)
