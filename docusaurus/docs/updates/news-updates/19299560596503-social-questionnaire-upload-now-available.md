## What is this?

To offer clients a seamless and easy experience, we have added our Social Questionnaire to our [Social Posts: 1x Week](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/products/MP-M368D2V2S7662C8H62QT8SBNDGCVT3NS) order form! The Social Questionnaire is something that has been around for a while and it helps us understand the direction and inspiration of the client for their social posts.

## What’s changing?

Marketing Services wants to understand exactly what you and your clients’ needs are and by including our Social Questionnaire directly in the order form, we can do just that. As of now, this change can be seen in all new orders and projects.

## How does it impact you?

This means now you will be able to access the Social Questionnaire as usual but submit it as a PDF right in the order form!

If you have any questions, concerns, or would like to discuss this change further, please don’t hesitate to reach out! We’d be happy to help.
