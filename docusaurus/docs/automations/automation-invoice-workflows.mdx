# Automation workflows for invoices

Automations can streamline your billing processes by triggering actions based on invoice status changes. While automations are powerful for follow-ups and internal notifications, it is important to understand the differences between system-generated invoice emails and custom automation emails.

## Common use cases

The most common use case for invoice-related automation is the **Invoice status changed to past due** trigger. This allows you to:

*   **Send a custom reminder email**: Automatically email a client when their invoice is overdue.
*   **Create a follow-up task**: Assign a task to a salesperson or fulfillment agent to reach out to the client.
*   **Notify your team**: Send an in-app or email notification to internal staff about the overdue payment.
*   **Update CRM data**: Tag the account or move an opportunity to a specific stage based on payment status.

## Limitations and "Gotchas"

When building invoice automations, keep the following limitations in mind:

### Automation emails vs. System emails
The **Send Email** step in automations allows you to send custom, branded emails. However, unlike the system-generated emails sent directly from the **Commerce > Invoices** section, automation emails:
*   **Cannot automatically attach the invoice PDF.**
*   **Cannot automatically generate a dynamic invoice payment link.**

Official invoices, including the PDF attachment and the unique "Pay this invoice" link, are sent automatically by the platform when an invoice is first issued or when you use the **Resend Invoice** option in Partner Center.

### Dynamic payment links
Currently, there is no built-in automation variable (e.g., `{{invoice_payment_link}}`) that dynamically pulls the unique payment link for a specific invoice into an automation "Send Email" step.

**Workaround**: If you wish to include a payment link in an automated reminder email, you can:
1.  **Use a static link**: Direct clients to the **Invoices** tab in their Business App (e.g., `https://business.app/invoices`), where they can view and pay all outstanding invoices.
2.  **Manual intervention**: Use the automation to create a **Fulfillment Task** for your team. The team member can then copy the unique payment link from the invoice in Partner Center and send a personalized message.

## How to set up an invoice reminder

1.  In Partner Center, go to **Automations** and click **Create**.
2.  **Trigger**: Select **An invoice status is changed**.
3.  **Trigger Options**: Set the status to **Past Due**.
4.  **Step**: Add a **Send Email** step.
5.  **Content**: Write a friendly reminder. Instead of a dynamic invoice link, include a button or link directing the user to their Business App to pay: `https://business.app/invoices`.
6.  **Step (Optional)**: Add a **Create a fulfillment task** step to ensure a team member follows up if the invoice remains unpaid after a certain period.
