# Start a campaign for the company

**Start a campaign for the company** enrolls all contacts associated with a company in a specified email campaign. A campaign can only be sent to each company once. This action is available on company-scoped triggers.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a company in scope
2. The step enrolls all contacts on the company in the selected campaign
3. Each contact receives the campaign emails
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Start a campaign for the company**
3. Select the campaign to start

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
