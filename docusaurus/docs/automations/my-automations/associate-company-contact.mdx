# Associate a company and contact

**Associate a company and contact** links a company record and a contact record in the CRM. Use it when both records exist but aren't yet connected — for example, after creating a contact from a form submission and finding the matching company in a separate step.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the **Associate a company and contact** step with both a company and contact in scope
2. The step creates a link between the two records in the CRM
3. The company and contact are now associated — downstream steps can access both
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Advanced**, select **Associate company with contact**

This action uses the company and contact currently in scope — no additional configuration is needed.

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
