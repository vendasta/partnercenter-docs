`Associate company with contact` links a company record and a contact record in the CRM. Use it when both records exist but aren't yet connected, for example after creating a contact from a form submission and finding the matching company in a separate step.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the `Associate company with contact` step with both a company and contact in scope
2. The step creates a link between the two records in the CRM
3. The company and contact are now associated, so downstream steps can access both
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under `Advanced`, select `Associate company with contact`

This action uses the company and contact currently in scope. No additional configuration is needed.

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
