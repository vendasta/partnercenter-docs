# Copy email template to another company

**Copy email template to another company** copies an email template from the source company to another company. This action is available on company-scoped triggers.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a company in scope
2. The step copies the selected email template to the target company
3. The target company now has its own copy of the template
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Copy email template to another company**
3. Select the email template and target company

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
