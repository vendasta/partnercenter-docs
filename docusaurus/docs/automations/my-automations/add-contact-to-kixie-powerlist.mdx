# Add contact to Kixie powerlist

**Add contact to Kixie powerlist** adds a contact to a Kixie powerlist for automated outreach calling. Use it to automatically queue new or qualified leads for sales call sequences.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the **Add contact to Kixie powerlist** step with a contact in scope
2. The step adds the contact to the specified Kixie powerlist
3. The contact becomes available for automated calling in Kixie
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Integrations**, select **Add contact to Kixie powerlist**
3. Configure the powerlist selection as prompted

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
