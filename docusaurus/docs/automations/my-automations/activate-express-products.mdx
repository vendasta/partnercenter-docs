# Activate express products

**Activate express products** activates a specific express product for the account in scope. Only express products are available — standard products use the regular **Activate products** action. This action is available on account-scoped triggers.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with an account in scope
2. The step activates the selected express product for the account
3. The express product becomes available on the account
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Products**, select **Activate express products**
3. Select the express product to activate

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
