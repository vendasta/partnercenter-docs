# Send a plain text email via Gmail

**Send a plain text email via Gmail** sends a plain-text email from your connected Gmail account to the contact in scope. This action is available on contact-scoped triggers.

:::info
Requires a connected Gmail account. The email is sent from the connected Gmail address, not from the Vendasta platform.
:::

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a contact in scope
2. The step sends a plain-text email from your Gmail account to the contact
3. The email appears in both your Gmail sent folder and the contact's inbox
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Send a plain text email via Gmail**
3. Configure the email subject and body

:::tip
Use **Add dynamic content** to personalize the email with contact or account data from previous steps.
:::

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
