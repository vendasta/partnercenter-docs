# Send a marketing email template to a contact

**Send a marketing email template to a contact** sends a one-off marketing email to the contact in scope. This action is available on contact-scoped triggers.

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a contact in scope
2. The step sends the selected email template to the contact
3. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Send a marketing email template to a contact**
3. Select the email template to send

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
