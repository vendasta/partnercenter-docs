# Send a marketing email template to a contact

**Send a marketing email template to a contact** sends a one-off marketing email to the contact in scope. This action is available on contact-scoped triggers.

This step sends a single email that is not associated with any campaign. To enroll a contact in a multi-email campaign sequence instead, use [Start a campaign for the contact](./start-campaign-contact.mdx).

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a contact in scope
2. The step sends the selected email template to the contact
3. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Send a marketing email template to a contact**
3. Select the email template to send

## View performance

Performance stats for emails sent with this step appear only within the automation itself. To view them, open the automation and click into this step to see opens, clicks, and other email metrics.

Because this step sends a one-off email not associated with any campaign, its stats do not appear in `Campaigns`. To send emails whose performance appears in `Campaigns` alongside your other campaign metrics, use [Start a campaign for the contact](./start-campaign-contact.mdx) instead.

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
