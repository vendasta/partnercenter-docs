# Pause campaign for company

**Pause campaign for company** pauses an ongoing campaign for the company. Acts on all associated contacts (up to 50). This action is available on company-scoped triggers.

:::info
If an associated contact isn't on the campaign, it's treated as an error and the failed contacts appear in the activity log.
:::

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a company in scope
2. The step pauses the selected campaign for all contacts on the company
3. No further campaign emails are sent to those contacts
4. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Pause campaign for company**
3. Select the campaign to pause

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
