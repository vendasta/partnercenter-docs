# Send a marketing email template to a company

**Send a marketing email template to a company** sends a one-off marketing email to the company's contacts. This action is available on company-scoped triggers.

This step sends a single email that is not associated with any campaign. To enroll a company's contacts in a multi-email campaign sequence instead, use [Start a campaign for the company](./start-campaign-company.mdx).

## How it works

1. The automation reaches the step with a company in scope
2. The step sends the selected email template to the company's contacts
3. The automation continues to the next step

## Set up the action

1. Open the automation builder and add a new step
2. Under **Campaigns and emails**, select **Send a marketing email template to a company**
3. Select the email template to send

## View performance

Performance stats for emails sent with this step appear only within the automation itself. To view them, open the automation and click into this step to see opens, clicks, and other email metrics.

Because this step sends a one-off email not associated with any campaign, its stats do not appear in `Campaigns`. To send emails whose performance appears in `Campaigns` alongside your other campaign metrics, use [Start a campaign for the company](./start-campaign-company.mdx) instead.

## Outputs

This action produces no outputs available to downstream steps.
