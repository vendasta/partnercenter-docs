# Automations overview

Automations are powerful tools you can use to automate your sales and marketing processes. Map out your personalized workflows in our simple builder and watch your business grow. Automation workflows are simply sets of instructions that tell the platform "when this specific trigger happens, then perform this action."

## Why use automations?

Transform your business operations with intelligent automation that eliminates manual tasks and ensures consistent customer experiences. Automations help you scale your operations, reduce human error, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks — all while freeing up your team to focus on high-value activities.

## Get started

1. **Navigate to Automations**
   - Go to `Partner Center` > `Automations`
   - Review existing automations or click `Create Automation`

2. **Choose a starting point**
   - Start from a template for common workflows
   - Create a custom automation from scratch
   - Review example automations for inspiration

3. **Create your first automation**
   - Define the trigger (when the automation should start)
   - Add conditions if needed (optional filters)
   - Configure the actions (what should happen)
   - Test and activate your automation

## Common automation use cases

**Welcome new clients**: When an account is created, automatically send an email campaign to welcome them, assign a salesperson, and create a task for follow-up.

**Drive product upgrades**: When clients activate a Standard product, automatically send them an email campaign outlining Pro edition benefits and log activity in Sales & Success Center.

**Track failed payments**: When a customer has a failed payment, receive a notification so you can follow up quickly.

**Nurture leads**: Automatically add leads to lists, send targeted campaigns, and create tasks based on engagement levels.
