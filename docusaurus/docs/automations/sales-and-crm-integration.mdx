# Sales and CRM Integration

This guide covers how to integrate automations with your sales processes and CRM system to streamline lead management, automate salesperson assignments, and improve your overall sales workflow.

## Overview

Sales and CRM integration automations help you:
- **Automatically assign salespeople** to new accounts and leads
- **Manage lead lifecycle** and progression through sales stages
- **Notify team members** about important sales events
- **Create and update CRM records** automatically
- **Track sales activities** and maintain accurate records

## Automatic Salesperson Assignment

### Automatically Assign Salespeople to New Accounts

Want to have a specific salesperson automatically assigned to all of your newly created accounts? Here's how to set it up:

#### Step-by-Step Setup

1. Navigate to **Partner Center > Automations > My Automations > Create automation**
2. Select **Blank Template**

![Blank automation template](./img/automations/my-automations/auto-assign-salesperson-blank-template.jpg)

3. Add a trigger: Under "Start the automation when", select **An account is created** and hit Save (adding conditions if needed, but not necessary)

![Account creation trigger](./img/automations/my-automations/auto-assign-salesperson-trigger.jpg)

4. Select the **"+"** button located underneath the trigger you just created
5. Scroll down to **Sales** and select **Assign a salesperson**

![Assign salesperson action](./img/automations/my-automations/auto-assign-salesperson-action.jpg)

6. Select the desired salesperson and click Save
7. Toggle the automation on and you're all set!

### Advanced Assignment Strategies

**Assign by Sales Team**
- Assigns a random salesperson from a specified sales team
- Useful for distributing leads evenly across team members
- Can optionally replace existing salesperson assignments

**Assign by Criteria**
- Use trigger conditions to assign different salespeople based on:
  - Geographic location
  - Account size or value
  - Product interest
  - Lead source
  - Custom fields or tags

**Round-Robin Assignment**
- Create multiple automations with different conditions
- Use account properties to distribute leads evenly
- Implement fair distribution across your sales team

## CRM Automation Actions

### Company Management

**Create Company**
- Creates a company in the CRM
- **Special Cases**: Duplicates are not checked - this blindly creates the company
- **Requirements**: Company name is required
- **Use Case**: Mirror companies from third-party CRM systems

**Update Company Information**
- Automatically update company records when triggers fire
- Sync data from external sources
- Maintain data consistency across systems

### Contact Management

**Create and Update Contacts**
- Automatically create CRM contacts from form submissions
- Update existing contact records with new information
- Sync contact data across different systems

**Contact Lifecycle Management**
- Move contacts through different stages automatically
- Update contact status based on engagement
- Trigger follow-up actions based on contact changes

### Opportunity Management

**Create CRM Opportunity**
- Automatically create opportunities when specific conditions are met
- **Requirements**: At least one of Company/Contact ID must be provided
- **Features**: Creates associations with both company and contact if both are present
- **Use Case**: Create opportunities when meetings are logged for contacts

**Update CRM Opportunity**
- Update opportunity details automatically
- Move opportunities between pipeline stages
- Update opportunity values and status

**Get Associated Records**
- Get contacts associated with opportunities
- Get opportunities associated with companies or contacts
- **Error Handling**: Returns error if associations don't exist

### Pipeline Stage Management

**Automatic Stage Progression**
- Move opportunities through pipeline stages based on activities
- Update lifecycle stages when specific events occur
- Trigger different actions for different pipeline stages

**Stage-Based Automations**
- Create different workflows for each pipeline stage
- Customize follow-up actions based on opportunity stage
- Automate stage-specific communications

## Notification Systems

### Partner Center Admin Notifications

**Configuration Options**
Currently, there is no option to personalize/customize the email that sends Partner Center Admin notifications as it is a transactional email and part of the "general-automation-notification" workflow.

**Setting Up Admin Notifications**
When configuring automations, you can select a step for notifications to be sent to the Partner Center Admin for important events like:
- High-value opportunity creation
- Product deactivations
- Payment failures
- System errors

### Salesperson Notifications

**Notify Salespeople**
- Send in-app and email notifications to assigned salespeople
- Customize notification content with dynamic data
- **Limitations**: Limited support for dynamic variables in notification content
- **Use Case**: Notify French-fluent salespeople when opportunities are created for French-language clients

**Notification Best Practices**
1. **Be Selective**: Only notify for truly important events
2. **Provide Context**: Include relevant account and opportunity details
3. **Set Expectations**: Clearly indicate what action is needed
4. **Avoid Spam**: Don't over-notify team members

## Best Practices for Sales and CRM Integration

### Data Quality

1. **Standardize Data Entry**: Use consistent formats and validation
2. **Prevent Duplicates**: Implement deduplication logic
3. **Maintain Accuracy**: Regular data cleansing and updates
4. **Audit Trail**: Track all automated changes

### Process Design

1. **Map Current Processes**: Document existing sales workflows
2. **Identify Bottlenecks**: Find areas where automation can help
3. **Start Simple**: Begin with basic automations and add complexity
4. **Test Thoroughly**: Validate all scenarios before going live

### Team Adoption

1. **Train Your Team**: Ensure everyone understands the new processes
2. **Provide Documentation**: Create clear guides for common scenarios
3. **Monitor Usage**: Track adoption and identify training needs
4. **Gather Feedback**: Continuously improve based on user input

### Performance Monitoring

1. **Track Key Metrics**: Monitor conversion rates, response times, and lead quality
2. **Regular Reviews**: Assess automation performance monthly
3. **Optimize Continuously**: Make data-driven improvements
4. **Scale Gradually**: Expand successful automations to new areas

Sales and CRM integration automations can significantly improve your team's efficiency and effectiveness. Start with basic automations like salesperson assignment and gradually build more sophisticated workflows as your team becomes comfortable with the system.
