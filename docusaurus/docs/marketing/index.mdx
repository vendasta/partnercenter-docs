## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Email Campaigns**

<details>
<summary>How do I create and send email campaigns?</summary>

To create and send email campaigns:

1. **Go to Partner Center > Marketing > Campaigns**
2. **Click "Create Campaign"** and choose your template or start from scratch
3. **Use the Email Builder** to design your campaign with drag-and-drop tools
4. **Add recipients** by selecting user lists or individual contacts
5. **Preview your campaign** to test appearance and functionality
6. **Schedule or send** your campaign immediately

Ensure you have proper email authentication set up before sending campaigns to avoid deliverability issues.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why are my emails going to spam folders?</summary>

Common causes and solutions for emails going to spam:

- **Missing authentication**: Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records in your DNS
- **Content triggers**: Avoid ALL CAPS, excessive exclamation points, and spam trigger words
- **Poor sender reputation**: Use authenticated domains and maintain good sending practices
- **Domain issues**: Free email addresses (like Gmail) require domain authentication
- **Technical problems**: Check for blacklisting at mxtoolbox.com/blacklists.aspx

See our [Email Deliverability guide](./email-settings/email-deliverability.mdx) for detailed troubleshooting steps.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What email authentication records do I need to set up?</summary>

For optimal email deliverability, you need three DNS records:

- **SPF (Sender Policy Framework)**: Specifies which servers can send emails from your domain
- **DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)**: Adds digital signatures to verify email authenticity
- **DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication)**: Provides policy instructions for handling failed authentication

Find your specific records in **Partner Center > Marketing > Email Settings**. See our setup guides for [GoDaddy](./email-settings/adding-spf-dkim-dmarc-records-godaddy.mdx) and [cPanel/Namecheap](./email-settings/adding-spf-dkim-dmarc-records-cpanel-namecheap.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize which users receive email campaigns?</summary>

Yes, you have several options for targeting campaigns:

- **User Lists**: Create specific lists of recipients for targeted campaigns
- **Account-based**: Send to specific users on individual accounts
- **CRM Integration**: Target campaigns based on CRM contact data
- **Custom Segments**: Filter recipients by location, engagement, or other criteria

You can also **add or remove recipients** from active campaigns as needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I track email campaign performance?</summary>

Monitor campaign success through the Campaign Activity Dashboard:

- **Delivered**: Number of emails successfully delivered to inboxes
- **Opens**: How many recipients opened your email
- **Clicks**: Number of clicks on links within your email
- **Bounces**: Emails that couldn't be delivered (hard and soft bounces)
- **Recipient Engagement**: Individual interaction data for each recipient

Use this data to optimize future campaigns and improve engagement rates.
</details>

### **Lead Generation**

<details>
<summary>How do acquisition widgets work and how do I set them up?</summary>

Acquisition widgets are lead capture forms that embed on client websites:

1. **Create the widget** in Partner Center > Marketing > Acquisition Widgets
2. **Customize appearance** including colors, fields, and drop shadow effects
3. **Get the embed code** provided by the platform
4. **Add code to website** in the header or before closing body tag
5. **Test functionality** to ensure leads are captured properly

Widgets automatically integrate with automations for immediate lead follow-up. See our guides for specific platforms like [Wix embedding](./acquisition-widgets/how-do-i-embed-the-acquisition-widget-on-a-wix-site.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I test my acquisition widget before going live?</summary>

Test your acquisition widget thoroughly:

- **Preview functionality** in the widget builder
- **Submit test forms** using your own email address
- **Check lead capture** in your CRM or lead management system
- **Test on different devices** to ensure mobile compatibility
- **Verify automation triggers** if connected to follow-up workflows

See our [widget testing guide](./acquisition-widgets/how-can-i-test-my-acquisition-widget-code.mdx) for detailed testing procedures.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can acquisition widgets integrate with automations?</summary>

Yes, acquisition widgets work seamlessly with automations:

- **Immediate follow-up**: Trigger welcome emails when forms are submitted
- **Lead nurturing**: Start automated sequences for new leads
- **CRM integration**: Automatically add leads to your contact database
- **Notification alerts**: Get notified when new leads are captured

This ensures no leads fall through the cracks and provides immediate value to prospects. Learn more about [automation integration](./acquisition-widgets/how-do-automations-work-with-the-acquisition-widget.mdx).
</details>

### **Technical Support**

<details>
<summary>What should I do if I get email deliverability errors?</summary>

Common email deliverability errors and solutions:

- **"Listed on spamrl.com as spam source"**: Check external blacklists and request delisting
- **"Domain IP listed on spamrl.com"**: Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC records and request whitelisting
- **"Virus/phishing source"**: Contact support and review email content for security issues
- **Authentication failures**: Update DNS records and verify domain ownership

For persistent issues, [request delisting](https://spamrl.com/delist/) and check [MX Toolbox](http://mxtoolbox.com/blacklists.aspx) for blacklisting status.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the platform have limits on email size?</summary>

Yes, there are email size restrictions:

- **Content size limits** apply to prevent delivery issues
- **Image optimization** is recommended for faster loading
- **Attachment restrictions** may apply depending on content type

Large emails can trigger spam filters and cause delivery problems. Keep campaigns focused and optimize images for best results.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use the same authentication records for multiple locations?</summary>

Yes, but with considerations:

- **Single domain**: Same SPF, DKIM, DMARC records can be used across all locations
- **Multiple domains**: Each domain needs its own set of authentication records
- **Franchise operations**: Centralized authentication is possible with proper DNS configuration

Ensure all sending domains are properly authenticated to maintain deliverability across all locations.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the requirements for setting up email campaigns?</summary>

Before creating email campaigns, you need:

- **Mailing address**: Required by law for commercial email campaigns
- **Authenticated domain**: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records properly configured
- **Sender information**: Valid reply-to email and sender name
- **Recipient lists**: User lists or contact segments for targeting
- **Content compliance**: Follow email marketing best practices and legal requirements

Missing any of these requirements will prevent campaign creation or delivery.
</details>
