To send marketing email from Partner Center, you need a custom domain email address with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records, a complete physical mailing address for CAN-SPAM compliance, and verified user email addresses. Free providers like Gmail can't be used.

## Technical requirements

### Custom domain email required

Under `Partner Center` → `Marketing` → `Email settings` are DNS authentication settings that help ensure your emails are delivered successfully and avoid spam filters.

**Domain Requirements:**
- **Custom Domain Required** - You must have a custom domain email address (e.g., yourname@yourbusiness.com)
- **DNS Access** - You need administrative access to your domain's DNS settings to add authentication records
- **Domain Ownership** - You must own or control the domain you want to send emails from

**Cannot Use Free Email Providers:**
You cannot use authentication records with free email providers such as:
- Gmail.com addresses
- Yahoo.com addresses  
- Hotmail.com addresses
- Outlook.com addresses
- Other free email services

If you don't own a custom domain, consider purchasing one through a domain registrar such as GoDaddy, Namecheap, or your hosting provider. A custom domain allows you to create professional email addresses and implement proper email authentication.

### DNS authentication records

You must configure these authentication records in your DNS:
- **SPF (Sender Policy Framework)** - Authorizes Vendasta to send emails on behalf of your domain
- **DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)** - Adds digital signatures to verify email authenticity  
- **DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication)** - Specifies how to handle emails that fail authentication

Learn how to set up these records in [Email Authentication](/marketing/email-settings/email-authentication).

## Legal compliance requirements

### CAN-SPAM law compliance

All commercial emails sent through the platform must comply with CAN-SPAM Act requirements:

**Physical Mailing Address (Required)**
- **Valid Business Address** - Include a legitimate physical postal address for your business
- **Complete Address** - Must include street address, city, state, and ZIP code
- **Current Address** - Address must be current and where you can receive postal mail
- **Visible in Emails** - Address is automatically included in email footers when configured

**Additional CAN-SPAM Requirements:**
- **Clear Sender Identification** - Emails must clearly identify who is sending them
- **Truthful Subject Lines** - Subject lines must accurately reflect email content  
- **Unsubscribe Option** - Every email must include a working unsubscribe mechanism
- **Honor Opt-Outs** - Unsubscribe requests must be processed within 10 business days

### Required business information

Configure your business information in `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Customize` → `Partner Defaults`:

- **Business Name** - Legal business name or DBA
- **Complete Mailing Address** - Street address, city, state, ZIP code, country
- **Contact Information** - Business phone number and email address
- **Sender details** - Set separately in `Marketing` → `Email settings`. Use `Sender name` and `Reply Address` in the `Sender and reply settings` card, and `Sender domain` and `Email address` in the `Advanced email settings` card. See [Update email settings](/marketing/email-settings/update-email-settings)

## User verification requirements

All users sending emails must have verified email addresses:
- **Email Verification** - All platform users must verify their email addresses
- **Account Access** - Unverified users may have limited platform access
- **Security** - Verification helps maintain platform security and sender reputation

Learn more about [Email Verification](/administration/my-account/my-team/email-verification) requirements.

## Email content requirements

### Size limitations
- **HTML Character Limit** - Email templates cannot exceed 32,000 characters of HTML source code
- **Template Optimization** - Keep content concise and optimize images for faster loading
- **Error Handling** - Emails exceeding the limit will fail to send with a character count error

### Content best practices
- **Professional Design** - Use clean, professional email templates
- **Mobile Optimization** - Ensure emails display properly on mobile devices
- **Clear Call-to-Actions** - Include clear, compelling calls-to-action
- **Relevant Content** - Send targeted, valuable content to your audience

## Getting started checklist

Before sending your first campaign:

- [ ] **Purchase Custom Domain** (if you don't have one)
- [ ] **Configure DNS Records** - Add SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records
- [ ] **Set Up Business Address** - Add complete mailing address in Partner Center
- [ ] **Verify Email Address** - Ensure your sender email is verified
- [ ] **Test Authentication** - Verify all DNS records are properly configured
- [ ] **Create Email Template** - Design your first marketing email
- [ ] **Send Test Emails** - Always test before sending to your audience

For step-by-step setup instructions, see [Update email settings](/marketing/email-settings/update-email-settings).

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can I send campaigns from a Gmail or other free email address?</summary>

No. You can't use authentication records with free email providers such as Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, or Outlook. You need a custom domain email address on a domain you own or control, with administrative access to its DNS settings.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I add the mailing address that appears in my email footers?</summary>

Go to `Partner Center` → `Administration` → `Customize` → `Partner Defaults` and enter your business name, complete mailing address, and contact information. When configured, the address is automatically included in email footers.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What kind of mailing address do I need?</summary>

You need a legitimate, current physical postal address where you can receive postal mail. It must include the street address, city, state, and ZIP code.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I change the sender name and sender email address?</summary>

Go to `Marketing` → `Email settings`. Set `Sender name` and `Reply Address` in the `Sender and reply settings` card, and `Sender domain` and `Email address` in the `Advanced email settings` card.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why did my email fail to send with a character count error?</summary>

Email templates can't exceed 32,000 characters of HTML source code, and emails over the limit fail to send. Keep content concise and optimize images to stay under the limit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How quickly do I need to process unsubscribe requests?</summary>

Under CAN-SPAM, every email must include a working unsubscribe mechanism, and unsubscribe requests must be processed within 10 business days.

</details>
