To send marketing email campaigns from your client's domain, add a verification record and an SPF record with the domain's DNS host, then choose the verified domain in the `Campaign Settings` tab.

If you use or resell marketing email products like Constant Contact, you need to set up these DNS records so the email campaigns sent on behalf of your client's domain work properly.

## What DNS settings do I need to connect a domain? {#domain-connection}

When using marketing email services, you need to verify domain ownership and ensure proper email delivery through DNS settings. The required DNS settings depend on the hosting provider:

![Domain connections showing DNS settings](./img/marketing-email-settings/domain-connections.jpg)

## Where is my DNS hosted? {#dns-hosting-options}

You have several options for where your DNS is hosted:

### 1. WordPress Hosting (Pro or Premium)

Custom domains are available on WordPress Hosting Pro and Premium plans. WordPress Hosting Standard does not support custom domains, so this option doesn't apply to Standard accounts.

#### Name servers

If your domain is using WordPress Hosting name servers, you'll have these settings:

![Name server settings for WordPress Hosting](./img/marketing-email-settings/name-servers.jpg)

#### DNS records

You can add the required DNS records in the WordPress Hosting interface:

![WordPress Hosting DNS record settings](./img/marketing-email-settings/website-pro-records.jpg)

### 2. Google Workspace

If your domain uses Google Workspace, you'll need to add the MX records in your DNS settings:

![Google Workspace MX records](./img/marketing-email-settings/google-workspace-mx.jpg)

### 3. Other DNS providers

If you're using a different DNS provider, you'll need to add the required records in their interface.

## Which DNS records do I need to add? {#required-dns-records}

### Verification record

You need to add a verification record to prove domain ownership to the email marketing service:

![Verification record example](./img/marketing-email-settings/verification-record.jpg)

### Campaign settings

After domain verification, you'll need to set up your campaign settings. Navigate to the `Campaign Settings` tab and follow these steps:

1. Visit the `Campaign Settings` tab
2. Select the domain used for sending emails
3. Choose the verified domain
4. Configure any additional options as needed

![Campaign settings interface](./img/marketing-email-settings/campaign-settings.jpg)

### SPF record

The SPF (Sender Policy Framework) record helps prevent email spoofing by specifying which mail servers are authorized to send emails on behalf of your domain.

1. Add an SPF record to your domain's DNS settings
2. Follow the format provided by your email marketing service
3. This helps improve email deliverability and prevents emails from being marked as spam

![SPF record setup example](./img/marketing-email-settings/spf-record-setup.jpg)

## What do I do if my domain doesn't verify? {#troubleshooting}

If you encounter issues with your email marketing setup:

- Verify all DNS records are correctly entered
- Check for typos or formatting errors
- Allow sufficient time for DNS propagation (can take up to 24-48 hours)
- Confirm you have the necessary permissions to modify DNS settings
- Contact your DNS provider or email marketing service for additional assistance

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How long does it take for DNS records to verify?</summary>

DNS propagation can take up to 24-48 hours. If records still show as missing after that, check them for typos or formatting errors.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why do I need a verification record?</summary>

The verification record proves to the email marketing service that you own the domain. After the domain is verified, you can select it in the `Campaign Settings` tab.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the SPF record do?</summary>

The SPF (Sender Policy Framework) record specifies which mail servers are authorized to send email on behalf of your domain. It helps prevent email spoofing, improves deliverability, and keeps emails from being marked as spam.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I connect a domain hosted on WordPress Hosting Standard?</summary>

No. Custom domains are available on WordPress Hosting Pro and Premium plans only.
</details>
