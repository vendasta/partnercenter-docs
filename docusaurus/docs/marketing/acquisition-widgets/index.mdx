# Acquisition Widgets overview

:::info Legacy Feature Notice
As of November 17, 2025, the Acquisition Widget is now a legacy Vendasta feature. We recommend using [CRM Forms](/marketing/forms) to automatically capture leads and fully customize nurturing automations (including Snapshot Report workflows). If you prefer a setup similar to the original Acquisition Widget, you can use the "Acquisition Widget" form template, which provides a comparable default configuration.
:::

The Acquisition Widget is a lead generation tool you can embed on your website to acquire new leads. While browsing your website, your visitors can provide their contact information through this widget to see how their business is performing online or to try free products.

Once a visitor provides their details, you can configure the widget to perform the following actions automatically:

- Create a Snapshot Report
- Assign the visitor to a salesperson and send the salesperson a hot lead notification
- Add the visitor to a customer acquisition campaign
- Activate products, including:
  - Reputation AI | Standard
  - Local SEO
  - Social Marketing | Standard
  - Customer Voice | Standard
  - Advertising Intelligence
  - Website | Standard
  - CalendarHero (Basic)

:::note
You'll be subject to the standard Snapshot Report and product fees for each account created with the widget.
:::

The widget comes with a wizard that gives you the flexibility to customize the text, container design, and colors. You can set up a widget for every market you have.

## How does the Acquisition Widget work?

To start using the Acquisition Widget, create a widget in **Partner Center** → **Marketing** → [Acquisition Widgets](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketing/widget). Then, embed this widget on relevant pages throughout your website.

When a website visitor submits their contact information, the widget will allow them to search for their business.

- If the lead finds their business, they can submit their information. The widget will automatically collect the rest of the details about their business.
- If the lead doesn't find their business, they will click **Can't find your business? Click here.** This option will take them to a form to provide their business details manually.

The assigned salesperson will then be notified so they can get in touch with the lead ASAP. Simultaneously, a new Snapshot Report will be created, the lead will be added to the customer acquisition campaign of your choice, and/or products will be activated (depending on your configuration)—allowing you to stay top of mind until they're ready to buy.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What changes are being made to the Acquisition Widget in Partner Center?</summary>

The Acquisition Widget has been sunset. Partners who signed up for a Vendasta subscription after November 17th, 2025, will not have access to the Acquisition Widget and should use the [Forms](/marketing/forms) feature.

The **Acquisition Widget** will no longer be available to new partners whose contracts begin on or after **November 17, 2025**.

Existing partners with contracts starting **before this date** will continue to have full access to create, edit, and deploy Acquisition Widgets within Partner Center.

All partners are encouraged to transition to **Forms**, which provides more modern functionality and full compatibility with current and upcoming AI features.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I enable or disable drop shadow for my Acquisition Widget?</summary>

1. Log in to **Partner Center**.
2. Go to **Marketing** → **Acquisition widgets**.
3. Select a widget and go to **Design** (click **Save and Continue** at the end of the page).
4. Scroll to **Fonts** and turn the toggle next to **Drop shadow** on or off.
5. Click **Save & continue** → **Finish**.

<details>
<summary>How do Automations work with the Acquisition Widget?</summary>

There is no direct link between Automations and the Acquisition Widget. You can create an automation that runs when someone submits through the widget by using a **tag** on the widget and an **An account is created** trigger filtered by that tag.

**Configure the widget with a tag:**

1. Go to **Partner Center** → **Marketing** → **Acquisition Widgets**.
2. Create a new widget or select an existing one.
3. In the **Tag** field, enter a unique tag. When someone submits through this widget, the account will get this tag.
4. Click **Save and continue** and complete the rest of the widget.

**Create the automation:**

1. Go to **Partner Center** → **Automations** → **My Automations**.
2. Create a new automation (use **Blank template**).
3. Set the trigger to **An account is created**. Add a condition: **Account data** → **Tags** → **Include any** (or **Include all**) → select the tag from the widget.
4. Save, build the rest of the workflow, and turn on the automation.

:::note
**Can't find your tag in the list?** Add the tag to an account first; once it exists on an account, it will appear in the list.
:::
</details>
