# Proposal Builder Overview

## What is Proposal Builder?

Proposal Builder is a powerful tool within Commerce that allows you to create professional proposals for your clients. With customizable templates and easy-to-use features, you can efficiently create, edit, and track proposals throughout the sales process. The system provides professional PDF generation, notification tracking, flexible pricing displays, and integration with Snapshot Reports to help you close more deals.

## Why is Proposal Builder Important?

Professional proposals are crucial for winning new business and communicating value to prospects. Proposal Builder streamlines the proposal creation process, ensuring consistency in your presentation while providing insights into client engagement. The integration with Snapshot Reports helps prospects understand their business needs, while tracking features help you follow up at the right time to close deals.

## What You Can Do with Proposal Builder

With Proposal Builder, you can:

- Create professional proposals using customizable templates
- Generate polished PDF documents for client sharing
- Track client engagement with email and in-app notifications
- Display flexible pricing options including retail pricing
- Add Snapshot Reports to highlight prospect pain points
- Filter and manage multiple proposals by salesperson
- Preview templates before committing to a design
- Archive proposals when no longer needed
- Handle multiple currencies for international clients

## How to Set Up

To access the Proposal Builder:

1. Navigate to **Partner Center > Commerce > Proposals**
2. Click **Create New Proposal** to begin creating your first proposal
3. Choose from existing templates or create a new template from scratch

### How to Create Proposal Templates

To create a custom proposal template:

1. Go to **Partner Center > Commerce > Proposals > Templates**
2. Click the **Create New Template** button to start creating your template
3. Customize the template with your branding and content structure
4. Save the template for future use

![Create New Template button in Proposals section](./img/create-template.jpg)

### How to Add Snapshot Reports to Proposals

Adding Snapshot Reports helps highlight prospect pain points and recommended solutions:

1. Generate a Snapshot Report for your prospect (recommended to do this ahead of time)
2. Go to **Partner Center > Commerce > Proposals > Manage Proposals**
3. Choose a template or create a new proposal from scratch
4. Select the recipient account
5. Open the **Insert widget** panel and press **Snapshot report**
6. Select the desired sections of the report

![Snapshot Report insertion in Proposals](./img/snapshot-reports-proposal.png)

Once inserted, the sections become widgets that can be rearranged as needed.

:::tip
Generate the Snapshot Report ahead of time. Snapshot Reports can take up to 24 hours to generate, and they make great prospecting tools for first meetings.
:::

## Managing Proposals

From the Proposals dashboard, you can:

- View all current proposals and their statuses
- Edit existing proposals
- Duplicate proposals to save time
- Archive proposals that are no longer needed

### How to Filter Proposals by Salesperson

Managing multiple opportunities simultaneously is challenging. The filtering capability helps salespeople view their proposals without scrolling through colleagues' proposals:

1. Go to **Partner Center > Commerce > Proposals**
2. Press the filter icon to the left of the **Search** bar to open the filter settings
3. Select a **Salesperson** from the dropdown

![Filtering proposals by salesperson](./img/filter-proposals-salesperson.png)

### How to Preview Proposal Templates

To ensure you select the most suitable template for your client:

1. Go to **Partner Center > Commerce > Proposals > Manage Proposals** or **Partner Center > Commerce > Proposals > Templates**
2. Choose a template
3. Scroll through the preview
4. Repeat steps 2-3 until you find a match
5. Name the proposal and select a recipient account
6. Press **Create**

## Best Practices

- Keep proposals concise and focused on client needs
- Use consistent branding throughout your proposals
- Include clear calls to action
- Follow up promptly after sending proposals
- Review analytics to understand client engagement with your proposals
- Generate Snapshot Reports ahead of time for stronger first impressions
- Use proposal templates to maintain consistency across your team

<details>
<summary>Can I create proposals in multiple currencies?</summary>

Yes, if you sell in multiple currencies and have set up multiple markets in your account. When a salesperson associated with a certain market creates a proposal, they'll have access to all products and packages published on that market's Marketplace with the appropriate currency settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I delete a proposal?</summary>

Once a proposal has been created, it cannot be deleted. However, you can archive it by clicking the **Archive** button on the right side of the Manage Proposals page.

![Archive button on the Manage Proposals page](./img/archive-proposal.jpg)
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why should I add Snapshot Reports to proposals?</summary>

The importance of a business's online presence and digital solutions can be difficult to understand. Snapshot Report integration helps get the conversation started with customized content highlighting pain points specific to the prospect and how you can alleviate them. By presenting areas for improvement alongside your recommended solutions, you help prospects understand the value of your proposal.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is filtering by salesperson important?</summary>

Proposal Builder helps salespeople stay on top of multiple opportunities with status tags that show which proposals have been viewed, commented on, or approved in one view. The filtering capability allows salespeople to view their proposals without having to scroll through colleagues' proposals to pick out theirs.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why should I preview templates before use?</summary>

The format and content of a proposal play an important role in successfully closing a new deal, and the right design can vary according to client and opportunity details. Previewing templates ensures you select the most suitable template for your specific client and opportunity.
</details>
