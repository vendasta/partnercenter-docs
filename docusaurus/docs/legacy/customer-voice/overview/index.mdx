## What is Customer Voice?

Online reviews are critical to the success of a local business. Nearly 90% of consumers say they'll only consider using a business if they have an average rating of 3-5 stars. With Customer Voice, your clients can reach their customers via email or text and collect reviews on the sites that are most important to their business.

Customer Voice allows you to assist your clients in the following key areas:

- **Generate more reviews** – Choose a method, provide a customer list, and click send to build credibility with raving reviews.
- **Deliver a powerful listening tool** – Let clients listen to customer feedback, find ways to improve, and address negative feedback before it goes live.
- **Boost star ratings across multiple review sites** – Clients can choose which sites to ask for reviews on and look great on platforms essential to their business.

## Overview page and performance metrics

Business owners need a way to visualize the effectiveness of review requests sent in Customer Voice. On the **Customer Voice** → **Overview** page, you will see metrics for review request performance: how many have been sent, open rate, and click-through rate. Visualizing performance as a funnel can help pinpoint areas for improvement (e.g. changing the subject line to increase open rate). Metrics include requests sent by email and SMS.

The Overview page also includes **SMS usage stats** and a log of **recent requests** sent. At a glance, business owners can see how many credits they have left and when they expire, plus request to purchase more. The recent requests log shows the latest activity, including bulk and single requests.

Go to **Customer Voice** → **Overview**. You can expect:

- A review request performance funnel on the Overview page
- Metrics reporting on email performance over time
- The ability to filter by a specific date range
- Metrics for sent, opened, and clicked requests
- The number of new reviews found on connected accounts

## SMS performance metrics

The performance funnel on the Overview page includes performance metrics for SMS messages (for accounts with an SMS add-on).

![SMS Performance Metrics](./img/legacy-customer-voice/sms-performance-metrics.jpg)

SMS reporting includes the number of SMS messages sent and what percentage have been clicked on (click-through rate). Open rate is not available for SMS. These metrics help businesses understand the value and effectiveness of sending SMS messages.

Go to **Customer Voice Pro** (account must have an SMS add-on) → **Overview**. You can expect:

- A review request performance funnel that includes SMS performance
- A way to toggle between viewing metrics for Email only, SMS only, or Email and SMS (open rate is for email only)
- The ability to filter by a specific date range
- The number of new reviews found on connected accounts

## A2P 10DLC registration

To prevent spam and SMS abuse, phone carriers in the United States require businesses to register their phone numbers before sending SMS messages to customers (A2P 10DLC). US-based businesses must register to continue using Customer Voice SMS review requesting. As of **August 31, 2023**, telecom carriers in the US block messages from unregistered businesses. Register as soon as possible to reduce downtime.

### What is A2P 10DLC?

A2P 10DLC is a system in the United States that allows businesses to send Application-to-Person (A2P) messaging via standard 10-digit long code (10DLC) phone numbers. Businesses must register their business and campaign information with the carriers. Registration is required for clients using Inbox (through Business App) or Customer Voice to send notifications, marketing/promotional messages, and review requests.

**At the moment, this registration only affects US businesses and is only required for businesses that use Customer Voice or Inbox to send SMS to customers.**

### Why A2P 10DLC?

Registering under A2P 10DLC helps authentic businesses run SMS campaigns with maximum deliverability and higher throughput. It also lets carriers monitor bad actors and creates a better experience for US consumers. Fully registered businesses receive a positive reputation score so their SMS is prioritized over unregistered businesses and not filtered as spam.

### What do partners and clients need to do?

**US-based SMBs must register their business to continue to send SMS messages after Aug 31, 2023.**

- In Customer Voice, go to **Settings** → **SMS Configuration** to submit the business for SMS registration.

Registration can take up to 28 days. For step-by-step registration, see [Register for SMS text messaging](../settings/register-for-sms-text-messaging.mdx) in Settings.
