# Customer Voice (Legacy)

:::warning Deprecated
As of February 21st, 2025, Customer Voice is a legacy Vendasta product. Use [Reputation AI Premium](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/products/RM) to collect reviews and NPS via email and SMS.
:::

Customer Voice was a feedback tool that let you invite customers to leave reviews, boost star ratings, and improve SEO. Clients could reach customers via email or text and collect reviews on the sites that mattered most. Online reviews are critical for local businesses; Customer Voice helped generate more reviews, deliver a listening tool for feedback, and boost ratings across multiple review sites.

## What you could do

- **Generate more reviews** – Choose a method, provide a customer list, and send requests.
- **Listen to feedback** – Let clients see customer feedback and address negative feedback before it went live.
- **Boost star ratings** – Target review sites important to the business (e.g. Google, Facebook).

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What products are needed for Customer Voice?</summary>

Customer Voice works with other products for best results. **Local SEO** is needed for the client to have a My Listing page where reviews are collected. **Reputation AI** connects to Customer Voice for review sources; with Reputation AI Standard, Facebook and Google listing URLs are pulled from Reputation AI into Customer Voice. With Reputation AI Pro you can add more review sources. Listings must exist in Reputation AI for this sync.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I activate SMS add-ons?</summary>

SMS add-ons are only available with Customer Voice Pro. Go to **Partner Center** → **Accounts** → **Manage Accounts**, open the account, select **Order Products**, choose the SMS add-on by location and message volume, then complete the order.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize buttons in Customer Voice?</summary>

Yes. Open a template, right-click the button, and use **Insert/Edit Link** to change the button text and the URL it points to. Update **URL** for the destination and **Text to Display** for the label.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I hide the business address on review request emails?</summary>

No. The business address is required for CAN-SPAM compliance. Unsolicited commercial email must include physical address and contact information; it cannot be removed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can the link in the SMS template be changed to a custom one?</summary>

Yes. In **Customer Voice** → **Templates** → **SMS Template**, set the template type to **custom site** and insert your custom link (e.g. a Google Form). Custom links are shortened for SMS and clicks are not tracked.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is the character limit for SMS messages?</summary>

The total limit is 128 characters for the whole message, including business name, SMS greetings, and SMS message.
</details>

<details>
<summary>When do Customer Voice SMS credits reset?</summary>

SMS credits reset on the **1st of each month**, tied to the third-party SMS system renewal, not the add-on renewal date. For example, 100 credits activated April 16th reset to 0/100 on May 1st.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I edit the review request email template?</summary>

Open the **Email template** tab for a customer, click the three-dot menu on the template preview, and select **Edit template**. You can add a custom message, choose review sources, set layout and colors, profile picture, business address visibility, opt-out, and unsubscribe link. Changes apply to all future emails sent to that customer.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I create a custom email review request template?</summary>

Go to **Customer Voice** → **Templates** → **Add New Template**, name it, then choose **New Preferred Review Sites Template**, **New My Listing template**, or **New Blank Template**. Edit a pre-populated template or use a blank one and add variables, images, and text.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I send emails from a custom domain?</summary>

Customer Voice Pro users can set a custom “send from” domain. In **Partner Center** → **Accounts** → open the Business App → **Settings** → **Email Configuration** → edit **Email 'Send From' Information**. Enter the address and display name, then verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC in your DNS. Invalid records must be updated in your DNS. This helps deliverability; content and subject lines also affect spam filtering.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I send SMS review requests?</summary>

**Single new contact:** On the Customer Voice overview tab click **Send request**, fill the form (include phone number), choose an SMS template, then **Send**. **Existing contacts:** Go to **Overview** → **Customers**, select one or more customers with phone numbers, click **Send request**, choose a template, then **Send to [#] Customers**. SMS add-ons required.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I send review requests to multiple customers at once?</summary>

Available in Customer Voice Pro. Go to **Customer Voice** → **Customers**, select multiple customers, click **Send Request** (bottom right), choose a template, then **Send**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I upload multiple customers at once?</summary>

Go to **Customer Voice** → **Customers** → **Import Customers**. Upload a .csv with the correct column headers (e.g. first name, last name, email) in the first row, attach the file, map fields, then upload. Max 500 contacts per CSV; duplicates are not detected on import. A sample template is available on the Import screen.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does a review go once submitted?</summary>

It depends on the template. **My Preferred Sites:** reviews go to the sites you directed users to; status in **Customer Voice** → **Customers** updates to Bounced, Sent, Clicked, or Opened. **My Listing:** reviews go to the My Listing page (Local SEO); status shows as Review Completed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which sources can I request reviews on?</summary>

Two template types: **My Preferred Sites** (third-party sites like Google and Facebook; up to three from **Customer Voice** → **Settings** → **My Preferred Sites**, driven by **Partner Center** → **Administration** → **Customize** → **Listing Sources**) and **My Listing** (the client’s My Listing page). Create templates under **Customer Voice** → **Templates** → **Add New Template**.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change the three suggested review sites (Preferred Sources)?</summary>

Go to **Customer Voice** → **Settings** → **Preferred Review Sites**. Select or clear the sites you want; you can enter review URLs manually. With Reputation AI, listing URLs can be pulled from the Primary Listings tab. Reputation AI Pro adds more options. You cannot add sources without a review URL; inaccurate business info must be fixed before a site appears on My Listing.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do Inbox (Business App) and Customer Voice Pro use the same phone number?</summary>

If the client sent SMS review requests through Customer Voice Pro before sending their first Inbox message, the number is the same. If they used Business App Inbox first, the numbers differ.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I embed the Review Generation Widget on my website?</summary>

In **Customer Voice** → **Tools** → **Widgets**, customize the widget and select **Copy Widget Code**, then paste the code into your site’s HTML. For WordPress, use the editor in **Text** mode (not Visual), paste the code, and adjust width/height if needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is first name not displayed in review requests via SMS?</summary>

The first name is dropped if the 128-character limit is exceeded. Use a shortened business name and keep the SMS message short so the first name fits. The editor shows character count in real time and indicates when the first name will be dropped.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is my custom template failing?</summary>

**HTML:** Smart (curly) quotes in HTML often cause errors; use straight quotes. Unicode is fine in body text but not inside HTML tags. **CSS:** Move each media query into its own `<style>` tag so they don’t conflict.
</details>
