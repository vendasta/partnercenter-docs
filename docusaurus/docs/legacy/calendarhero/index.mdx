# CalendarHero (Legacy)

:::warning Legacy product
CalendarHero is a legacy scheduling product. This documentation is maintained for historical reference for partners who may still have active CalendarHero subscriptions.
:::

CalendarHero was a scheduling and calendar product that let clients offer online booking, meetings, and reminders through the Business App. It supported a free plan (one-on-one meetings, one meeting type) and a Team plan (group meetings, multiple types, SMS reminders, custom branding). The following articles cover access, activation, team seats, direct sign-in, and support.

## What is CalendarHero?

CalendarHero provided online scheduling and calendar integration for Vendasta clients. Users could access it via **Business App** → **CalendarHero** after the product was activated for the account. It offered:

- **Free plan** – One-on-one meetings, one meeting type, Outlook and Google Calendar integrations, included with the platform subscription.
- **Team plan** – Group meeting types, multiple meeting types, SMS reminders, confirmation emails, and custom branding.

Partners could activate the free version for many accounts at once using account lists and **Activate Product** in Partner Center.

## What you can do (reference)

- Give clients access to CalendarHero through Business App
- Activate the free version of CalendarHero for all accounts or selected lists
- Manage team seats and replace users via the CalendarHero Users page
- Use direct sign-in to CalendarHero (same email as Business App)
- Understand what happens when a subscription is cancelled (accounts are deleted and cannot be recovered)
- Get product support from CalendarHero’s support page

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>How does a Business App user access CalendarHero?</summary>

Create a Business App user in Partner Center with CalendarHero activated for the account. The user signs in to Business App and selects CalendarHero in the left navigation. Their CalendarHero account is created automatically and they can set up their calendar and meeting links. See [Business App user access](./how-does-a-business-app-user-access-calendarhero.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can Vendasta CalendarHero accounts sign in to CalendarHero directly?</summary>

Yes. After the account is created via **Business App** → **CalendarHero**, clients can sign in directly at CalendarHero using the same email (Google or Microsoft) as in Business App.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I activate CalendarHero for all my client accounts?</summary>

Enable the product via Start Selling, create an account list, then use the kebab menu next to the list and choose **Activate Product**. Search for and select CalendarHero, then click **Activate**. See [How to activate CalendarHero for all accounts](./how-to-activate-calendarhero-for-all-accounts.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I replace users on CalendarHero Team seats?</summary>

Team plan administrators manage members from the [CalendarHero Users page](https://app.calendarhero.com/org/manageusers). After a user is deleted, the seat can be assigned to a new user when that user logs in to Business App and accesses CalendarHero. Deleting a user removes them and all their data permanently. See [How to replace team seat users](./how-to-replace-users-of-calendarhero-team-seats.mdx).
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens when a CalendarHero subscription is cancelled?</summary>

When the CalendarHero subscription is cancelled for an account through Vendasta, all users’ accounts are deleted and cannot be recovered.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where can I get support for CalendarHero?</summary>

For product support, use the [CalendarHero support page](https://calendarhero.com/get-support).
</details>
