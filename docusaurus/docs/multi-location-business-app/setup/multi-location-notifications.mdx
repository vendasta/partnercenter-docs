# Multi-Location Notifications

## Understanding Multi-Location Notification Limitations

:::important
Multi-location groups do not have built-in user notifications. Notifications in the Vendasta platform only work at the individual location level. This means:

- Users will receive separate notifications from each individual location in their group
- There are no consolidated multi-location digest emails or executive reports
- Partners need to create custom solutions for multi-location communication
:::

## Create Custom Multi-Location Notifications Using Automations

### Overview

Since there are no built-in multi-location notifications, you can create custom executive report reminders using Vendasta's Automations platform. This approach allows you to:

- Send personalized, branded emails to multi-location clients
- Consolidate multiple location notifications into one monthly reminder
- Direct clients to their multi-location executive reports and dashboards
- Maintain regular engagement while reducing email noise

### Implementation Steps

#### Step 1: Create a Custom Email Template

1. Navigate to **Automations** in your Partner Center
2. Create a new email template with:
   - **Hard-coded content** specific to your client/brand
   - **Brand name** and personalized messaging  
   - **Direct link** to their multi-location business app report
   - **Clear call-to-action** to review the dashboard

![Custom Multi-Location Email Template](../img/multi-location/multi-location-notification-email-for-specific-client.png)

*Example of a custom multi-location executive report email template with personalized branding and direct link to the dashboard.*

#### Step 2: Set Up the Automation

1. Create a new automation, with trigger "Manually started"
2. Use the "Delay until certain day of month" automation step, and set to send at the beginning of each month (e.g., 2nd of the month)
3. Add a step to send your custom notification email campaign


#### Step 3: Configure Account Settings

1. **Start the automation** from the account designated as "headquarters" for the multi-location group
2. **Remove clients** from individual location executive report notifications to reduce noise
3. **Test the automation** with a small group first

### Real-World Case Study

**Client:** 22-location podiatry clinic  
**Approach:** Custom monthly multi-location executive report reminder

**Results:**
- Reduced email noise from 22 messages to 1 consolidated notification
- Increased engagement with multi-location reporting
- Demonstrated ongoing partner value through personalized communication
- Improved client satisfaction with streamlined reporting

### Best Practices

#### Email Content Guidelines
- **Personalize** with client's brand name and specific messaging
- **Include direct links** to their multi-location dashboard
- **Highlight key value points** you're delivering
- **Use clear call-to-action** language
- **Keep content concise** but informative

#### Automation Management
- Start automation from the designated "headquarters" account
- Use monthly scheduling rather than rolling 30-day cycles
- Monitor delivery rates and engagement
- Regularly review and update email content

#### Client Communication
- Inform clients about the new notification system
- Explain how it replaces individual location emails
- Provide clear instructions for accessing reports
- Set expectations for timing and frequency

## Scaling Your Notification Strategy

As you implement this solution across multiple clients, consider these approaches:

1. **Create template variations** for different client types and industries
2. **Develop standard messaging frameworks** that can be quickly customized
3. **Build automation libraries** for rapid deployment to new clients
4. **Train team members** on setup and customization processes
5. **Document client preferences** to streamline personalization

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Automation Not Starting**
- Verify you're starting from the correct headquarters account
- Check that the account has proper multi-location group assignment
- Ensure email template is properly configured

**Emails Not Delivering**
- Confirm recipient email addresses are current
- Check spam filters and email deliverability
- Verify automation scheduling settings

**Multiple Multi-Location Groups**
- Choose the primary group for notifications
- Consider separate automations for different business segments
- Document which group each automation serves
