# Multi-Location Business App Overview

The Multi-Location Business App is a powerful portal that enables partners to help their business clients manage and monitor multiple store or service locations from a single unified interface. This comprehensive platform streamlines multi-location management with consolidated dashboards, group organization, and cross-location insights.

![Multi-Location Portal](./img/multi-location/overview-1.jpg)

## What is Multi-Location Business App?

The Multi-Location Business App provides a centralized command center for businesses with multiple locations. Instead of managing each location separately, users can:

- **View aggregated performance data** across all locations
- **Create and manage business groups** for logical organization
- **Monitor key metrics** like leads, reviews, listings, and social media performance
- **Generate executive reports** with consolidated insights
- **Manage communications** across all locations from one inbox
- **Track advertising performance** and ROI across multiple sites

## Key Benefits

### **Centralized Management**
- Single dashboard for all locations
- Unified reporting and analytics
- Streamlined communication workflows
- Cross-location performance comparisons

### **Business Groups Organization**
- Group locations by geography, business type, or custom criteria
- Sub-group functionality for hierarchical organization
- Bulk actions across groups
- Scalable structure that grows with your business

### **Comprehensive Insights**
- Aggregated metrics across all locations
- Individual location performance tracking
- Trend analysis and historical data
- Customizable reporting periods

## Portal Navigation

The Multi-Location Business App includes these main sections:

### **Home Dashboard**
Central overview showing key metrics across all locations including leads, reviews, listings performance, and recent activity.

### **Conversations**
Unified inbox for managing communications across all locations with filtering and routing capabilities.

### **CRM** 
Consolidated contact management and lead tracking across multiple locations.

### **Executive Report**
Comprehensive reporting with location-specific and aggregated insights, customizable for different stakeholder needs.

### **Reputation Management**
Review monitoring, response management, and reputation tracking across all locations.

### **Listings Management**
Google Business Profile, Bing Places, and directory listing management for all locations.

### **Social Media**
Social media posting, engagement tracking, and performance analytics across locations.

### **Advertising**
Campaign management and performance tracking for location-based advertising.

### **Data Exporter**
Advanced data export capabilities for custom reporting and external integrations.

## Getting Started

### **Prerequisites**
- Active Business App accounts for each location
- Partner or admin access to the multi-location portal
- Proper user permissions configured

### **Setup Process**
1. **Create Business Groups**: Organize locations into logical groups
2. **Add Locations**: Assign existing Business App accounts to groups
3. **Configure Permissions**: Set user access levels for each group
4. **Customize Dashboard**: Configure metrics and reporting preferences
5. **Train Users**: Ensure team members understand the multi-location interface

## Best Practices

### **Group Organization**
- Use consistent naming conventions for groups
- Organize by logical business criteria (geography, type, size)
- Create sub-groups for hierarchical management when needed
- Regular review and adjustment of group structures

### **User Management**
- Assign appropriate permission levels for each user
- Create location-specific access where needed
- Regular audit of user permissions and access
- Proper onboarding for new multi-location users

### **Reporting Strategy**
- Establish regular reporting schedules
- Customize reports for different stakeholder needs
- Use filters effectively to focus on relevant data
- Archive historical data for trend analysis

The Multi-Location Business App transforms complex multi-location management into a streamlined, efficient process, enabling businesses to scale operations while maintaining visibility and control across all locations.
