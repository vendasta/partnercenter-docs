# Multi-Location Business App Overview

The Multi-Location Business App is a powerful portal that enables partners to help their business clients manage and monitor multiple store or service locations from a single unified interface. This comprehensive platform streamlines multi-location management with consolidated dashboards, group organization, and cross-location insights.

![Multi-Location Portal](./img/multi-location/overview-1.jpg)

## What is Multi-Location Business App?

The Multi-Location Business App provides a centralized command center for businesses with multiple locations. Instead of managing each location separately, users can:

- **View aggregated performance data** across all locations
- **Create and manage business groups** for logical organization
- **Monitor key metrics** like leads, reviews, listings, and social media performance
- **Generate executive reports** with consolidated insights
- **Manage communications** across all locations from one inbox
- **Track advertising performance** and ROI across multiple sites

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Setup & Configuration

<details>
<summary><strong>If a user is added to a multi-location group, will they receive daily digest emails for each account in the group?</strong></summary>

No, multi-location groups are designed for viewing aggregated statistics, not for notifications. Daily digest emails still come from individual accounts and their associated users. Users must be added to the individual accounts separately to receive daily notifications from those accounts.
</details>

### Conversations

<details>
<summary><strong>How long does it take for conversations to load after enabling "Multi-location Conversations"?</strong></summary>

The loading time varies depending on the number of accounts and conversations being aggregated. It can take anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes. There is no completion notification, so we recommend checking back after a few minutes to see if all conversations have loaded.
</details>

### Keyword Management

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I add more than 15 keywords at once using bulk add?</strong></summary>

No, the limit is 15 keywords per bulk addition. However, you can repeat the process multiple times to add more keywords as needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What happens if a location has reached its keyword limit?</strong></summary>

Keywords will be skipped for locations that have reached their limit, but they will still be applied to other locations that have available quota. You'll receive a summary showing which keywords were not applied and why.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>When will I see bulk-added keywords appear in the system?</strong></summary>

Changes are reflected in the Multi-Location Business App within 24 hours after bulk addition.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Will keywords be automatically favorited when bulk added?</strong></summary>

Yes, but only if the admin settings for favoriting and syncing are enabled in your account configuration.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does the order of keywords matter during bulk addition?</strong></summary>

Yes, keywords are applied in the exact order you enter them until each location's keyword limit is reached. Plan your keyword priority accordingly.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I remove keywords in bulk like I can add them?</strong></summary>

No, the bulk feature is only available for adding keywords. Keywords must be removed individually from each location.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Will I get a report of which keywords were successfully applied?</strong></summary>

Yes, the system tracks and reports which keywords were successfully added to each location, including any failures with explanations.
</details>

### Google Q&A Management

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I respond to Google Q&A from multiple locations in one place?</strong></summary>

Yes, the Multi-Location Business App allows you to monitor and respond to Google Q&A across all your locations from a single dashboard. Simply navigate to Google Business Profile > Google Q&A to see a consolidated feed of questions and post answers directly to Google.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does Google Q&A help my multi-location business?</strong></summary>

Google Q&A in the Multi-Location Business App helps you create meaningful customer communication, avoid misinformation about your brand, capture customer insights, and potentially boost search rankings - all while saving time by managing all locations from one interface.
</details>
