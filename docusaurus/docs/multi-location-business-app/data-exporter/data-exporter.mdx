# Data Exporter

Users of the Multi-Location Business App can export review, listing, or Google Business Profile data to a CSV, allowing flexibility in workflows, auditing, and internal reporting across many locations.

## Exporting data

1. In the Multi-Location Business App, navigate to **Data Exporter** in the left-hand navigation menu.
2. Click the **data source** drop-down and choose from:
   - All reviews
   - Review summary by source
   - Google Business Profile summary
   - Listing data
3. Use the filter options to customize the data you want to export.
4. Click **Export data to CSV**.

## Configuration

The Data Exporter can be enabled or disabled on a group-by-group basis by navigating to **Partner Center → Accounts → Multi-location Groups → choose a group → Features**, then toggling the feature on or off.
