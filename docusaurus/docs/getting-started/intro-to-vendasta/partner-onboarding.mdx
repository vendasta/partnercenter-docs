# Partner onboarding

Every partner with a paid Vendasta subscription is provided onboarding support to help you get set up for success on the platform.

## Free Onboarding Session

Every Partner paying a subscription with Vendasta is entitled to a free session with an Onboarding Specialist. These calls are designed to help with early set up in the platform.

### What is included?

- **Team Setup**: Support with configuring internal Team Roles, Permissions, and Salesperson Profiles
- **Payment Configuration**: Assistance with setup to accept customer payments ("Vendasta Payments")
- **Branding & Customization**: Configure platform branding, email settings & custom domains (Custom Domains available on Professional subscription & above)
- **Client Management**: Walkthrough of how to import client/company lists, and finding leads within the platform
- **Meeting Setup**: Set up your Meeting Link
- **AI Configuration**: AI Chat Receptionist configuration and installation support

## Additional Onboarding Sessions

Our Onboarding Specialists can assist with a variety of training topics in addition to the first free onboarding session. These sessions are available for purchase.

:::info
Pricing details for Onboarding packages can be viewed at [vendasta.com/pricing](https://www.vendasta.com/pricing).
:::

### Example topics covered in additional training sessions:

- **CRM walkthrough** (pipeline, lists, etc)
- **Snapshot Report** (how to run, edit, send, add competitor data)
- **Customer Billing, Subscriptions, Sales Orders**
- **Email Marketing** (creating, editing, sending campaigns)
- **Marketplace and Package Building**
- **Custom Products in Vendor Center**
- **Business App & Executive Report**
- **Conversations AI setup overview**
- **Dashboard Walkthroughs**
- **Reputation Management**
- **Local SEO**
- **Social Marketing**
- **Advertising Intelligence**
- **Campaigns Pro**
- **Automations**

To learn more about onboarding packages, contact onboarding@vendasta.com.
