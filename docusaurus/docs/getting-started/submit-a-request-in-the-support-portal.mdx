Open support requests in the Support Portal at [partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets](https://partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets). Select `Create ticket`, choose the account and product, and describe the issue. Every ticket you open and its status are in one place, so you can check progress at any time.

## Before you start

You need a Partner Center login.

Have these ready:

- The account and product the request is about
- A short description of the issue
- Optional: your replication steps, the affected user, and screenshots or a video of the error

## Open the Support Portal

You can reach your tickets two ways:

- Go to [partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets](https://partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets).
- In Partner Center, select the help icon in the top navigation, then select `My tickets`.

## Create a ticket

<img src={require('./img/support-portal/tickets-page-create-ticket.png').default} alt="The Tickets page with the Create ticket button outlined in the top right" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

1. On the `Tickets` page, select `Create ticket`.
2. Enter a `Subject`.
3. Choose the `Product` the request is about.
4. Search for and choose the `Account` the request is about.
5. Describe the issue in the `Description` field. Keep it to one issue per ticket.
6. Select the paperclip icon to attach files, such as screenshots or a video of the error.
7. Select `Submit ticket`.

<img src={require('./img/support-portal/create-ticket-form.png').default} alt="The New support ticket form with Subject, Product, Account, Description, and Also notify fields and a Submit ticket button" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

The portal asks for the product and account up front, so our team starts with the details they need and you get less back-and-forth.

:::tip
Add anyone who should follow the ticket to `Also notify`. Include your replication steps and the affected user, if there is one, in the description.
:::

## Track your tickets

The `Tickets` page lists every ticket with its status, ticket number, and created and updated dates. Use the tabs above the list to narrow it:

- `All tickets`
- `My tickets`
- `Open`
- `Pending your reply`
- `Closed`
- `Pending development team`
- `Pending third party`

Use `Search` to find a ticket, or `Filters` to narrow the list. Select a ticket's name to open it.

<img src={require('./img/support-portal/ticket-list.png').default} alt="The Tickets page listing sample tickets with their status, ticket number, and created and updated dates" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

## Add information to an open ticket

To add information to an open request, reply on that ticket instead of opening a new one, so the history stays together.

1. Open the ticket from the `Tickets` page.
2. Select `Add a response...` and write your reply.
3. Send it. Your reply reaches your agent.

When the ticket says `Did this resolve your issue?`, select `Close as resolved` to close it.
