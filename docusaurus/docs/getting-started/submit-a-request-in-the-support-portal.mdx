Submit support requests at [partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets](https://partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets). Select `Get help`, choose the product and account, and describe the issue. Every support request you open and its status are in one place, so you can check progress at any time.

## Before you start

You need a Partner Center login.

Have these ready so our team can start right away:

- The account and product the request is about
- The client's account group ID, which starts with `AG-`. Copy it from the URL of the client's Business App and paste it in the `Description`.
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead
- The steps to reproduce the issue, the affected user, and when it happened
- Optional: screenshots or a video of the error

## Open the Support requests page

You can reach your support requests two ways:

- Go to [partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets](https://partners.vendasta.com/crm/tickets).
- In Partner Center, select the help icon in the top navigation, then select `My support requests`.

## Create a support request

On the `Support requests` page, select `Get help`.

<img src={require('./img/support-portal/tickets-page-create-ticket.png').default} alt="The Support requests page with the Get help button outlined in the top right" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

The `New support request` form opens. Fill it in:

<img src={require('./img/support-portal/create-ticket-form.png').default} alt="The New support request form with Subject, Product, Account, Description, and Also notify fields and a Send request button" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

1. Enter a `Subject`.
2. Choose the `Product` the request is about.
3. Search for and choose the `Account` the request is about.
4. Describe the issue in the `Description` field. Keep it to one issue per request.
5. Select the paperclip icon to attach files, such as screenshots or a video of the error.
6. Optional: to copy a teammate or your customer success contact, enter their email address in `Also notify`. They get notified about the request and can follow its progress.
7. Select `Send request`.

The portal asks for the product and account up front, so our team starts with the details they need and you get less back-and-forth.

## Track your support requests

When our team replies to a request, you get an email with a button that opens the request in the portal.

The `Support requests` page lists every request with its `Subject`, `Status`, `Requested by`, `Request #`, `Submitted` date, and `Last update`. Use the tabs above the list to narrow it:

- `All requests`: every request visible to your organization
- `My requests`: the requests you submitted
- `Needs your reply`
- `Open`
- `Fix in progress`
- `Waiting on a provider`
- `Resolved`

Use `Edit views` to change which tabs show. Search by subject, email, or request number, or use `Filters` to narrow the list. Select a request's subject to open it.

## Add information to an open request

To add information to an open request, reply on that request instead of opening a new one, so the history stays together.

1. Open the request from the `Support requests` page.
2. Select `Add a response...` and write your reply.
3. Send it. Your reply reaches your agent.

When the request says `Did this resolve your issue?`, select `Close as resolved` to close it.

## Need help right away?

Select the help icon in the top navigation, then `Chat with Vendasta AI` for instant answers. To reach a person, type "Speak with support" in the chat. Phone support is available on certain subscription tiers.
