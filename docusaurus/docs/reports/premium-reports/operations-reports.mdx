# Operations reports

Monitor your operational systems and account health with detailed insights into social marketing connections, automation performance, and system status. These reports help maintain service quality and identify issues before they impact performance.

## Account connection reports

### Social marketing usage

Monitor account connections across all product services, categorized by status and connection expiration dates.

![Social Marketing Usage Report](./img/social-marketing-usage.jpg)

View connections categorized by:
- **Connected**: Active, functioning connections
- **Broken Connections**: Connections requiring attention
- **Available Connections**: Potential new connections
- **Expiration dates**: When connections will expire (if applicable)

### Connection status details

Get detailed connection information per account to maintain service quality.

![Connections Report](./img/connections-report.jpg)

**Connection insights include:**
- **Connection Status**: Active, expired, or broken connection identification
- **Connection Expiration Date**: Timeline for connection renewal (when applicable)

This report helps proactively manage account health and prevent service disruptions.

### Broken connection management

View all broken connections in one centralized location for efficient troubleshooting and resolution.

Take direct action through the report using provided vendor links to quickly resolve connection issues and restore service functionality.

## Automation performance reports

Monitor automation execution, success rates, and error tracking to ensure optimal automation performance.

![Automations Dashboard](./img/automations-dashboard.jpg)

**Dashboard includes the following insights for your selected time range:**

1. **Automation execution counts** by completion date showing activity trends
2. **Top performing accounts** with highest automation completion counts  
3. **Top 10 automations** across all accounts with execution totals
4. **Top 10 actions** completed across all automations with execution counts
5. **Activity error counts** by completion date for trend analysis
6. **Error details table** with actionable error information

**Error management:**
- Click **See Errors** under Actions to view detailed error information
- Access error details directly in **Manage Automations** in Partner Center
- Track error trends to identify systemic issues

Use the **Completed Date** filter at the top to adjust the time range for all visualizations according to your analysis needs.

## Download options

Export operational data for further analysis or reporting:

1. Click the three-dot icon in the top-right corner of any report
2. Select **Download** to access available formats (CSV or PDF)

![Download Option](./img/download-option.jpg)

All operational reports support data export for integration with external analysis tools or executive reporting.

## Data freshness

Operational reports update daily at 12:00 AM UTC. Data freshness indicators at the bottom of each dashboard show:

1. **Refresh interval** (bold text): Update frequency for the lowest-refresh data source
2. **Last refresh timestamp** (bottom text): Earliest update time across all data sources

![Data Freshness](./img/data-freshness.jpg)

:::note
Operations reports provide critical insights into system health and account status. Regular monitoring helps maintain service quality, prevent disruptions, and ensure optimal automation performance across your organization.
:::
