The Vendasta reporting platform provides comprehensive analytics and insights across all aspects of your business. From SaaS metrics and productivity tracking to premium operational reports, these tools help you understand performance, identify opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

## Why use Reports?

Gain visibility into your business performance with analytics that track key metrics across products, teams, and operations. Reports help you identify trends, optimize workflows, and demonstrate value to stakeholders, all while making informed decisions based on real data.

## What's included

- **[SaaS Metrics](./metrics/index.mdx)**: Track key SaaS metrics including MRR, churn, customer lifetime value, and growth trends
- **[Premium Reports](./premium-reports/index.mdx)**: Access advanced operational reports for sales, billing, connections, and data freshness

## Get started

1. **Navigate to Reports**
   - Go to `Partner Center` → `Reports`
   - Explore available report categories
   - Review default metrics and dashboards

2. **Review SaaS Metrics**
   - Access the SaaS Metrics Dashboard
   - Understand key performance indicators
   - Track growth trends over time

3. **Access Premium Reports**
   - Review available premium report types
   - Generate reports for specific time periods
   - Export data for further analysis

4. **Configure report settings**
   - Set up report schedules if available
   - Customize report parameters
   - Share reports with team members

## Common report types

**SaaS Metrics**: Track monthly recurring revenue, customer churn, lifetime value, and growth metrics to understand business health.

**Operational Reports**: Monitor sales activities, billing status, product connections, and data freshness to ensure smooth operations.

**Productivity Reports**: Measure team performance, task completion rates, and project delivery metrics for fulfillment operations.
