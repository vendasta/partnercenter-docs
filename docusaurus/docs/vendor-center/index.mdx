# Vendor Center overview

Vendor Center is your platform for creating and managing custom products and services that you can sell through the Vendasta Marketplace. Built to give you complete control over product creation, pricing, integration, and fulfillment, Vendor Center helps you build products that integrate seamlessly with the Vendasta ecosystem.

## Why use Vendor Center?

Create custom products tailored to your specific market needs with complete control over every aspect of your offering. Vendor Center eliminates the need for separate product management systems, provides built-in integration capabilities, and connects directly to the Vendasta Marketplace — helping you scale your product portfolio efficiently.

## Get started

1. **Access Vendor Center**
   - Navigate to [vendors.vendasta.com](https://vendors.vendasta.com/) or access through Partner Center navigation
   - Ensure you have Partner Center Admin access

2. **Review onboarding guide**
   - Read the [Vendor Onboarding Guide](./vendor-onboarding-guide/index.mdx) for requirements
   - Understand product structure, pricing, and operational requirements
   - Review technical and marketing requirements

3. **Create your first product**
   - Click `Add Product` in Vendor Center or `Create Product` in Partner Center
   - Configure basic information, integration settings, and editions
   - Add marketing content and set visibility

4. **Set up integrations**
   - Configure SSO and fulfillment settings
   - Integrate with Task Manager if providing services
   - Set up order forms and reseller notifications

## Common workflows

**Product Creation**: Create a product with editions and add-ons, configure pricing and billing terms, add marketing materials, set up integrations, and publish to the Marketplace.

**Product Management**: Update product information, modify pricing and editions, manage order forms, track orders and projects, and communicate with resellers.

**Vendor Operations**: Set up team members with appropriate roles, create projects for order fulfillment, integrate with Task Manager, and manage reseller relationships.
