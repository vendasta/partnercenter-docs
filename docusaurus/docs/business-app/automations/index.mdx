# Business App Automations overview

Business App Automations let you build workflows that run when something happens in the app—for example when a contact or company is created or updated, or when a campaign email is opened. You define a **trigger** and then add **steps** such as sending an email or SMS, updating a company, or running an automation from an external system like Zapier.

## Why use Business App Automations?

- **Save time**: Follow up with contacts after campaign activity, or create companies from external data, without manual steps.
- **Stay consistent**: Use templates so you and your clients reuse the same workflows.
- **Connect tools**: Trigger automations from Zapier so events in QuickBooks, CRMs, or other apps drive actions in the Business App.

## Get started

1. **Open Automations**
   - Go to **Business App** → **My Business** → **Automations** (or **Administration** → **Automations** depending on context).

2. **Create an automation**
   - Click to create a new automation, choose a trigger (e.g. when a contact or company is created or modified), add conditions if needed, then add steps (send email, send SMS, update company, etc.).

3. **Use templates**
   - Under **Partner Center** → **Automations** → **Templates** → **Manage Client Templates**, create or use templates so you and your clients can start from pre-built workflows.
