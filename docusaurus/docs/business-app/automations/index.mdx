# Business App Automations

Automations in Business App let you run workflows automatically when something happens—such as a new contact, a company update, or a campaign email being opened. You set a **trigger** and add **steps** (for example send an email or SMS, update a company, or run an automation from Zapier). That way you can follow up with leads, keep data in sync, and connect Business App to other tools without doing each step by hand.

## What you can do

- **Trigger on events** — Run automations when contacts or companies are created or updated, or when contacts open campaign emails.
- **Send messages** — Use steps to send plain text emails or SMS through Conversations (with Conversations AI Pro).
- **Use templates** — Create and publish reusable automation templates for your clients, or use platform templates (e.g. create companies from Visual Visitor).
- **Connect external apps** — Use Zapier to trigger Business App automations from QuickBooks, CRMs, and other systems.

## Where to manage automations

In Business App, go to **My Business** → **Automations** or **Administration** → **Automations** to create and edit automations. In Partner Center, use **Automations** → **Templates** → **Manage Client Templates** to build and publish templates for clients.
