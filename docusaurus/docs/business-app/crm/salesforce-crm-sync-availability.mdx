## What is Salesforce CRM sync?

Salesforce CRM sync connects your account to Salesforce so you can keep CRM data aligned across systems.

## Why is Salesforce CRM sync important?

- Reduce manual data entry
- Keep records consistent for your team
- Help your CRM stay up to date

## What’s included

- You can connect and manage the Salesforce CRM sync from either portal you use.
- The same connection is available from your Business App and from Partner Center.

:::warning
Availability depends on your plan and user permissions. If you do not see the option to connect Salesforce, contact an account admin.
:::

## Access the Salesforce CRM sync

1. Sign in to Business App or Partner Center.
2. Open the CRM area.
3. Go to integrations and select `Salesforce CRM sync`.
4. Follow the on-screen steps to connect or manage the sync.

:::info
Connection steps and labels may vary based on your permissions. If you are unsure where to find the setting, use search within your portal to look for “Salesforce” or “CRM sync”.
:::

## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)

<details>
<summary>Is Salesforce CRM sync available in Business App?</summary>
Yes. You can access and manage the connection from Business App.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is Salesforce CRM sync available in Partner Center?</summary>
Yes. You can access and manage the connection from Partner Center.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need to set up the connection in both places?</summary>
No. Set up the connection once. You can manage the same connection from either portal.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Who can connect Salesforce CRM sync?</summary>
Users with the required permissions. If you cannot see the option, ask an account admin to grant access or complete the connection.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I check the connection status?</summary>
Open the Salesforce integration page in your portal to view and manage status.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I disconnect the sync?</summary>
Yes. Use the Salesforce integration page to disconnect when needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I do not see Salesforce in integrations?</summary>
You may not have the required permissions, or your plan may not include the integration. Contact your admin for help.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the sync change my existing records right away?</summary>
Changes depend on your connection settings. Review settings carefully before enabling or updating the sync.
</details>
