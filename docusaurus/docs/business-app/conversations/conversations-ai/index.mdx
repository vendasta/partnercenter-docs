# Conversations AI

Conversations AI is a paid product upgrade that adds AI receptionist functionality to the standard Conversations platform. The AI employee can handle incoming communications across multiple channels when human staff are unavailable.

## What Conversations AI Unlocks

Conversations AI gives your clients access to three powerful AI components:

- **AI Receptionist** - Automated assistant that handles calls, messages, and web chat across all channels. See [AI Workforce Overview](../../../ai/ai-workforce/) for setup and capabilities.

- **AI Capabilities** - Custom behaviors and actions the AI can perform, like booking appointments or checking inventory. Learn more in [AI Capabilities](../../../ai/ai-capabilities/).

- **Knowledge Base** - Business information and content the AI references to provide accurate answers. Configure sources in [Knowledge Base](../../../ai/knowledge-base/).

Together, these components create an intelligent receptionist that knows your client's business and can take meaningful action on their behalf.

## AI Receptionist Capabilities

The AI employee functions as an automated receptionist with these capabilities:

### **Voice Call Handling** (Premium only)
- Answers incoming phone calls automatically
- Collects caller information (name, phone number, reason for call)
- Books appointments using connected calendar systems
- Provides business information (hours, location, services)
- Transfers calls to human staff when requested

### **Message Response Automation**
- Responds to SMS messages with relevant business information
- Handles WhatsApp conversations in multiple languages
- Replies to Facebook and Instagram direct messages
- Processes web chat interactions on business websites
- Manages email inquiries through unified Conversations interface

### **Lead Information Collection**
- Captures contact details from all communication channels
- Records inquiry context and lead source
- Creates CRM entries automatically
- Tags conversations for follow-up priority
- Logs interaction history for team reference

### **Custom Business Actions**
- Connect to external APIs through Custom Capabilities
- Check appointment availability in booking systems
- Verify inventory or service availability
- Process simple requests without human intervention
- Execute custom workflows based on inquiry type

### **Knowledge Base Integration**
- Access business profile information (hours, location, services)
- Reference uploaded documents and FAQ content
- Provide accurate answers based on business-specific training
- Direct complex inquiries to appropriate human staff
- Maintain conversation context across interactions

## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Product Selection**

**Q: Which Conversations AI edition should I choose?**
- Choose Standard if WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and web chat are your main channels
- Go with Pro if you need SMS support for US/Canada businesses
- Upgrade to Premium if you handle voice calls and want full AI receptionist functionality

**Q: What countries can use SMS messaging?**
Currently, businesses located in United States and Canada can use SMS messaging. International businesses can use web chat, email, Facebook, or Instagram chat.

### **Setup & Configuration**

**Q: How is a business assigned an SMS number?**
When activating Conversations AI Pro, businesses automatically receive an SMS number. The system first attempts to assign a local number based on the business profile address. If unavailable, a neighboring area number is assigned. View the assigned number at **Business App > Settings > Conversations Settings > Your SMS number**.

**Q: Can I get an 800-number instead?**
If no local numbers are available, contact support for assistance with 800-number assignment.

**Q: How much does the AI employee know about the business?**
The AI employee accesses business profile information (location, hours, services) and can be trained with additional knowledge including FAQs, pricing details, service descriptions, and appointment booking instructions for accurate, personalized responses.

### **Usage & Notifications**

**Q: How will users know if they received a message?**
New messages appear live in Business App without page refreshing. Users can receive instant email notifications for new messages or daily digest emails.

**Q: What websites support web chat?**
Web chat installs on any website where custom code can be added to the `<head>` element. A WordPress plugin is provided for easy WordPress installation.

**Q: Can AI respond to social media post comments?**
No, AI only responds to direct messages between customers and business pages, not public post comments.

**Q: Can businesses connect personal social accounts?**
No, businesses need Instagram Business accounts or Facebook Business Pages for AI integration.

### **Platform Communication**

**Q: Are there charges for platform messaging between partners and clients?**
Platform messaging is free with no limitations on messages sent between partners, vendors, and accounts through Conversations.

**Q: How do I enable Conversations in my client's Business App?**
Go to **Partner Center > Administration > Customize Business App > Conversations Messages** and check "show this page" to enable cross-platform messaging.

**Q: What about spam messages?**
Currently, there's no spam blocking feature. If clients experience unusually high spam, contact support for number reassignment.

## Setup Process

1. **Product Activation**: Enable Conversations AI edition through marketplace
2. **Business Profile**: Complete all business information fields
3. **AI Training**: Upload knowledge documents and configure responses
4. **Channel Setup**: Connect required communication channels
5. **Integration Testing**: Verify AI responses and human handoff
6. **Team Training**: Ensure staff understand AI handoff procedures
7. **Monitoring**: Review conversation logs and adjust AI responses
