# Conversations Overview

Conversations is a powerful lead capture and customer communication tool that centralizes all your client interactions in one place. It enables businesses to connect with customers across multiple channels while providing AI-powered assistance and team collaboration features.

![Conversations Overview](./img/inbox/inbox-overview-1.jpg)

## What is Conversations?

Conversations centralizes customer messaging with integrations to email, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and more. It eliminates the need for juggling multiple communication tools by bringing everything into one shared team interface.

## Core Benefits

- **Centralized Communication**: All customer messages in one place across channels
- **Team Collaboration**: Shared conversations that any team member can handle
- **Lead Capture**: Capture leads from website forms and follow up immediately
- **24/7 Availability**: AI-powered chat widget handles customers outside business hours
- **Multi-Location Support**: Manage conversations across multiple business locations

## Standard Features (Included)

Every Business App account includes these messaging capabilities:

### **Email Integration**
- Send and receive emails from a shared business email address
- Team members can collaborate on email conversations
- Automatic contact creation from email interactions

### **Social Media Integration**
- **Facebook Pages**: Connect and respond to Facebook messages
- **Instagram Business**: Manage Instagram direct messages
- Available worldwide for seamless social customer service

### **Website Lead Capture**
- Install forms on business websites to capture contact details
- Automatically create conversations from form submissions
- Follow up with leads via email or SMS

### **Team Features**
- Shared team interface - no messages tied to individual team members
- Everyone can view and participate in customer conversations
- Collaborative customer experience delivery

### **Inbox activity events**
- **NPS Feedback** events appear as inbound timeline events with their corresponding event icon and the title **Contact has submitted NPS survey.**
- **Review Matched** events appear as inbound timeline events with their corresponding event icon and the title **Review Matched.**
- Multiline NPS feedback is displayed with preserved line breaks in the inbox timeline.

## Pro Features (Upgrade)

Conversations AI Pro unlocks advanced features:

### **AI-Assisted Web Chat Widget**
- 24/7 AI assistant for website visitors
- Responds in 40+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and more
- Collects visitor contact information for follow-up
- Smooth handover from AI to human agents
- Available worldwide for any website

### **SMS Messaging** (US & Canada)
- Unlimited one-on-one SMS messaging with customers
- Automatic local phone number assignment
- A2P 10DLC registration included for US businesses
- Integration with CRM for automatic contact creation

### **WhatsApp Integration** (International)
- Connect WhatsApp Business accounts
- Essential for businesses outside US/Canada where WhatsApp is preferred
- Manage WhatsApp conversations alongside other channels

## Getting Started

1. **Enable Conversations**: Turn on the feature in Partner Center
2. **Connect Channels**: Set up email, social media, and other integrations
3. **Configure AI**: Set up web chat widget and train AI knowledge base
4. **Team Training**: Get your team familiar with the shared Conversations interface

Conversations transforms how businesses handle customer communication, providing the tools needed for professional, responsive customer service at scale.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Can Conversations in Business App integrate with other CRMs or Messaging systems?</strong></summary>

Businesses can centralize their customer and contact data in the Business App CRM using available integrations with Zapier, Quickbooks, and more. Additionally, automations can be created that send messages when certain events happen.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What automations are available for Conversations in Business App?</strong></summary>

Automations in Business App includes triggers when a message is received, and actions to send an SMS or email. You can build powerful automated workflows for your business with the Automations system, along with all the integrations available in Business App.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Is there a per-use cost for the AI-assisted web chat?</strong></summary>

There's no per use cost for AI in Conversations AI Pro. Unlimited AI responses from the AI-assisted web chat lead capture is included.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What languages does the AI support?</strong></summary>

Your AI assistant can respond to leads in over 40 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Turkish, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian, Japanese, Chinese and many more.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>If we install the Conversations AI Pro chat on our website, will it be only AI agent answering customers or will real agents talk to customers?</strong></summary>

The web chat is 100% AI-managed lead capture. Small businesses are busy, and can't always reply instantly – that's why the AI assistant will get a website visitor's name and number, and then notify the business they have a new lead – the business can reply via SMS as soon as possible, and move the conversation off the website and onto the customer's phone.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How much does the chatbot know about the business? Can it answer questions?</strong></summary>

The AI assistant can access "knowledge" from the business profile, so it can answer questions about services, location and contact info, and hours. It can also be trained on the text from a business website. You can also add additional text Q&A 'knowledge' to the AI assistant so it can answer frequently asked questions from any supplied text.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I install the web chat widget?</strong></summary>

The web chat widget can be installed on any website. To install, copy and paste the widget install code into the `<head>` element of your website, typically just before the closing `</head>` tag.
</details>
