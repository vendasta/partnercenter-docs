# Facebook Messenger Integration

Connect your Facebook Business Pages to Conversations to manage Facebook messages alongside all other customer communications in one centralized interface.

## Features

- **Unified Messaging**: Handle Facebook messages in the same interface as email, SMS, and other channels
- **Team Collaboration**: Multiple team members can respond to Facebook messages
- **Worldwide Availability**: Available globally for all Facebook Business Pages
- **Auto-Contact Creation**: New Facebook conversations automatically create contacts in CRM

## Setup Requirements

- Facebook Business Page (not personal profile)
- Admin access to the Facebook Page
- Business App with Conversations enabled

## Getting Started

1. **Enable Facebook Integration**: In Partner Center, navigate to Business App configuration
2. **Connect Facebook Page**: Sign in with Facebook and select your business page
3. **Grant Permissions**: Allow access to messaging and page management
4. **Test Connection**: Send a test message to verify integration

## Best Practices

- Respond to Facebook messages promptly to maintain good customer relationships
- Use consistent branding and tone across all messaging channels
- Monitor message performance and response times
- Train team members on Facebook messaging etiquette

Facebook Messenger integration is included with all Business App accounts at no additional cost.
