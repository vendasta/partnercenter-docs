# Instagram Messenger Integration

Connect your Instagram Business account to Conversations to manage Instagram direct messages alongside all other customer communications in one unified interface.

## Features

- **Unified Messaging**: Handle Instagram DMs in the same interface as email, SMS, and other channels
- **Team Collaboration**: Multiple team members can respond to Instagram messages
- **Worldwide Availability**: Available globally for Instagram Business accounts
- **Auto-Contact Creation**: New Instagram conversations automatically create contacts in CRM

## Setup Requirements

- Instagram Business Account (not personal account)
- Connected Facebook Business Page (Instagram Business accounts must be linked to Facebook)
- Admin access to both Instagram and Facebook accounts
- Business App with Conversations enabled

## Connection Steps

Instagram integration requires a few additional steps due to Facebook's connection requirements:

1. **Sign in with Instagram**: From Conversations settings, click "Sign in with Instagram"
2. **Facebook Authentication**: Sign in to Facebook or click "Reconnect" if already signed in
3. **Grant Full Access**: Make sure to give access to "All Pages" when connecting Facebook
4. **Select Instagram Account**: Choose the desired Instagram Business account from the list
5. **Enable Messaging**: Back in Conversations settings, click "Turn on Messaging"

## Best Practices

- Maintain professional and engaging responses on Instagram
- Use Instagram's visual nature to enhance customer interactions
- Monitor response times to maintain good customer experience
- Ensure team members understand Instagram messaging best practices

Instagram Messenger integration is included with all Business App accounts at no additional cost.
