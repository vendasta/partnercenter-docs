# Score

Use Score settings to define how Business App evaluates client performance and engagement.

## Configure scoring inputs
- [Customize the Executive Report scorecard](/business-app/executive-report/customize-executive-report)
- [Tune listing and review scoring thresholds](/business-app/executive-report/reputation)

## Apply scoring to CRM workflows
- Align score categories with pipeline stages so sales teams can prioritise leads.
- Combine scores with automations to trigger nurture campaigns or follow-up tasks.

## Tips
- Review score performance quarterly to ensure benchmarks still match client goals.
- Communicate score definitions to clients so they understand what affects their grade.
