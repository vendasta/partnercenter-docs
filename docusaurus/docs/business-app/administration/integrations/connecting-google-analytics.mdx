# Connecting Google Analytics in Business App

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Properties can be connected to Website Pro through Business App.

To connect your Google Analytics account to the Business App:

1. Open Business App and navigate to **Administration > Connections > Browse**.
2. Click the **Example (GA4)** connection card.
3. On the GA4 marketing page, click **Add Connection**.
4. Follow the prompts to sign in to your Google account and authorize the connection.
