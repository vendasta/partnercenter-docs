# AI knowledge base

The AI knowledge base powers the responses that AI features deliver in Business App. Keep it updated so the AI workforce can answer accurately.

## Build and organise content
- [Create and manage knowledge base collections](/ai/knowledge-base/)
- [Add your own custom guides](/business-app/administration/add-your-own-custom-guides)
- Use tags or categories to match content with specific AI assistants.

## Maintain quality
- Review analytics to spot missing or unclear entries.
- Schedule periodic audits when products or pricing change.

## Share with clients
- Encourage administrators to upload FAQs, policies, and troubleshooting steps.
- Align knowledge entries with notification templates so messaging stays consistent.
