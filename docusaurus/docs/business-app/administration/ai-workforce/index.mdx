# AI workforce

Set up AI assistants to answer calls, reply to texts, and support customers across channels.

## Configure AI roles
- [Overview of the AI workforce](/ai/ai-workforce/)
- Align AI assistant personas with your brand voice before enabling them for clients.

## Voice receptionist
- [Deploy the AI Voice Receptionist](/business-app/administration/ai-voice-receptionist)
- Share call recordings and transcripts with staff so they can fine-tune responses.

## SMS receptionist
- [Enable AI-powered SMS responses](/business-app/administration/ai-powered-sms-responses)
- Combine SMS responses with notification rules to deliver consistent follow-up.

### Tips
- Review AI analytics regularly to spot unanswered questions.
- Pair AI assistants with the knowledge base categories that fit their responsibilities.
- Train staff on how to pause or adjust AI responses when handling sensitive scenarios.
