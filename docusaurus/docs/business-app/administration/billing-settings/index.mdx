# Billing settings

The billing settings section in Business App lets users keep their payment information current. 

![Adding Payment Method](../img/business-app/credit-card/add-payment-method-2.jpg)

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary>Where can a client add their credit card details?</summary>

Your clients can add their credit card details in the **Business App > Administration > Billing Settings > Add Payment Method.**

</details>

<details>
<summary>What payment options can my clients use?</summary>

Your clients can use the following payment methods:

* Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
* ACH transfers
* Canadian Pre-Authorized Debits (PADs)

</details>
