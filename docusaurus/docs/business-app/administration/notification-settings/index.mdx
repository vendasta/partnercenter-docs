The notification settings in **Business App** let you control how clients and users receive alerts across products — including what events trigger an email, whether they arrive instantly or as part of a digest, and the default behavior for each notification type.

Business App notifications are organized by product. Each section lets you review **Instant Email Notifications** and **Daily Digest Emails** for that product.  
Admins can manage default behaviors in **Partner Center**, while clients can personalize their settings directly within **Business App → Settings → Notification Settings**.

---

## Global settings

At the top of the Notification Settings page, clients can configure global defaults that apply to all products unless individually overridden.

- **Instant email and In-App (when available)** — Controls whether real-time notifications are sent via email and appear in-app.  
- **Daily Digest** — Controls whether daily summary emails are sent.  

Each dropdown offers:
- **Default (Enabled)** — Uses the system default (usually enabled).  
- **Enabled** — Always sends this notification.  
- **Disabled** — Turns off this notification type completely.  

## Types of notifications

### Instant email notifications
Instant emails are sent in real time when specific events occur — like new leads, reviews, or post approvals. These notifications help clients stay on top of key activity without waiting for daily summaries.

### Daily digest emails
Digest emails combine multiple events into a single summary message sent once per day. They're ideal for clients who prefer fewer emails while still staying informed about business activity.

## Manage notification settings

To view or adjust notifications:

1. In **Business App**, go to **Settings → Notification Settings**.  
2. Expand each product (e.g., Business App, Reputation Management, Social Marketing, WordPress Hosting).  
3. Use the dropdown beside each item to toggle between:
   - **Enabled (Default)** — the notification is turned on by default.
   - **Disabled (Default)** — the notification is turned off by default.
4. Click **Save** to apply changes.
