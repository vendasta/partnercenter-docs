# Email history

Email history keeps a running log of all messages Business App sends to and receives from your clients' contacts. Use it to prove delivery, confirm automations fired, or diagnose why a customer did not see a notification.

## Access email history
1. Sign in to **Business App**.
2. Go to **Settings > Conversations > Email history**.
3. Use the search, date, and status filters to narrow the list to the messages you need.

## Review message details
- **Delivery status** shows whether the email was sent, bounced, deferred, or marked as spam.
- **Channel source** identifies which workflow, product, or user triggered the message.
- **Preview** opens the original content so you can confirm dynamic fields and branding rendered correctly.

## Export records
- Select individual messages or choose **Select all** to export the full result set.
- Click **Export** to download a CSV that includes timestamps, recipients, and delivery metadata for audits.

## Troubleshooting tips
- Filter for `Bounced` or `Deferred` statuses to surface problem recipients quickly.
- Compare the timeline against [Email configuration](/business-app/administration/email-configuration) to verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are still valid.
- If a workflow did not trigger the expected email, cross-check the related automation in [Notification settings](/business-app/administration/notification-settings) or your CRM setup.
