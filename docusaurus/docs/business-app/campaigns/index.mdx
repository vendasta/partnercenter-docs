# Campaigns overview

Campaigns in Business App (Campaigns Pro) let your clients run email and SMS marketing from one place. They can create campaigns from scratch or from templates, add steps (email, SMS), use AI to generate content, and send to filtered contact lists. The base plan includes email sending; SMS requires an add-on.

## Why use Campaigns?

- **Email and SMS in one place**: Build multi-step campaigns that mix email and SMS (SMS add-on required for text messaging).
- **Templates and AI**: Start from ready-made templates or generate copy with AI.
- **Targeted sends**: Choose time zones, send days, and filter contacts so the right message reaches the right audience.
- **Integrated with Business App**: Campaigns live alongside contacts, Conversations, and automations.

## Get started

1. **Open Campaigns**
   - Go to **Business App** → **Campaigns** → **Create Campaign** (or start from **Templates**).

2. **Create a campaign**
   - Name the campaign, set time zone and send days, then click **Add Step** to add email (from scratch, template, or AI) or SMS.

3. **Send**
   - Use the **Contacts** tab to choose recipients (with filters if needed), then publish and send.

:::info
The base Campaigns Pro plan includes email (e.g. 10,000 emails/month). To send SMS campaigns, clients need an **SMS add-on** (available in the United States and Canada).
:::
