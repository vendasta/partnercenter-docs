# Campaigns Overview

Campaigns in Business App let your clients run email and SMS marketing from one place. The base plan includes email sending (e.g. 10,000 emails per month); SMS requires an add-on. Clients can create campaigns from scratch or from templates, add email or SMS steps, use AI to generate content, and send to filtered contact lists.

## Why use Campaigns?

- **Email and SMS in one place**: Build multi-step campaigns that mix email and SMS (SMS add-on required for text messaging).
- **Templates and AI**: Start from ready-made templates or generate copy with AI.
- **Targeted sends**: Choose time zone, send days, and filter contacts so the right message reaches the right audience.
- **Integrated with Business App**: Campaigns live alongside contacts, Conversations, and automations.

## Tracking your sending limits

<img src={require('./img/campaigns-pro/emails-sent-counter.png').default} alt="Marketing emails and SMS sent this billing period counters" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

At the top of the Campaigns page you'll see two counters showing how much of your monthly allotment has been used for the current billing period:

- **Marketing emails sent this billing period** — tracks email usage against your monthly limit (e.g. 0 of 10,000)
- **Marketing SMS sent this billing period** — tracks SMS usage if you have an SMS add-on enabled (e.g. 0 of 50)

Both counters reset at the start of each billing period.

## How to use Campaigns

**Step 1** — Go to **Business App** → **Campaigns** → **Create Campaign**. Give your campaign a name and select **Create**.

**Or:** In the **Templates** section, choose a template to start from.

![Alternative method to create campaign](./img/campaigns-pro/campaigns-step1-alt.jpg)

**Step 2** — Configure your campaign. Set the time zone and choose which days emails are sent.

![Configure campaign settings](./img/campaigns-pro/campaigns-step2.jpg)

**Step 3** — Click **Add Step** to add an email (write your own, use a template, or use AI) or add SMS.

![Add campaign step](./img/campaigns-pro/campaigns-step3.jpg)

**Step 4** — Create your email. You can use AI to generate email content.

![Create email with AI](./img/campaigns-pro/campaigns-step4.jpg)

**Step 5** — When you're ready, click **Publish** to start your campaign.

![Publish campaign](./img/campaigns-pro/campaigns-step5.jpg)

To send your campaign, go to the **Contacts** tab. You can add filters and send only to contacts that match your criteria.

![Campaign contacts](./img/campaigns-pro/campaigns-contacts.jpg)

## Get started

1. **Open Campaigns** — **Business App** → **Campaigns** → **Create Campaign** (or start from **Templates**).
2. **Create a campaign** — Name it, set time zone and send days, then **Add Step** for email or SMS.
3. **Send** — Use the **Contacts** tab to choose recipients (with filters if needed), then publish and send.

:::info
The base Email & SMS Marketing plan includes email (e.g. 10,000 emails/month). To send SMS campaigns, clients need an **SMS add-on** (available in the United States and Canada).
:::

## Troubleshooting campaigns

### "Some contacts cannot be added to this campaign"

This error occurs when a campaign uses **dynamic fields** (e.g., a personalization variable like `{{first_name}}`) and one or more contacts are missing a value for that field. Contacts with missing values are excluded.

**How to fix it:**
- Remove or make optional the dynamic field from the email step, or
- Update the affected contacts in the CRM to fill in the missing field values before adding them to the campaign.

### Campaign shows "Ongoing" but no emails are being delivered

1. Confirm that recipients were actually added — open the campaign and check the **Contacts** tab to verify the contact list is populated.
2. Check that contact email addresses are valid (no obvious formatting errors).
3. Confirm the campaign has at least one published email step.

### Email delivery delays (deferred emails)

Emails may be **deferred** rather than sent immediately if the receiving mail server temporarily rejects them. This is a normal part of email delivery — the sending system retries automatically. Deferred emails are not failures; they typically deliver within a few hours. No action is needed unless an email remains undelivered for more than 24 hours.

### Featured templates not loading

If the templates gallery appears blank or fails to load, check whether the account has complete **business profile information**, particularly the business name, address, and phone number (NAP). Missing NAP data can prevent template rendering.

**How to fix:** Go to **Business App** → **Administration** → **Business Profile** and ensure all required fields are filled in.

### Image upload errors

Supported image formats for campaigns are **JPEG** and **PNG**. If an upload fails:
- Confirm the file is a `.jpg` or `.png` (not `.webp`, `.gif`, `.heic`, etc.)
- Keep file size under 5 MB
- Try re-saving the image in an image editor and re-uploading

### Restarting a published campaign with the same contact list

Published campaigns cannot be re-sent to the same list directly. To send again:

1. Open the campaign and click **Duplicate** (or use the three-dot menu → **Copy campaign**).
2. Add your contacts to the new copy.
3. Publish the copy.

## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can you export campaign activity from Email & SMS Marketing in Business App?</summary>

No. Campaign activity can only be viewed in Business App. Open a campaign and click the number under **Recipients** to open the campaign activity table.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How many emails can a business send per month?</summary>

You can send up to 10,000 emails per month by default. You can purchase an additional email-sending limit in increments of 1,000 or 10,000 as an add-on.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need an SMS add-on to use SMS in Email & SMS Marketing?</summary>

Yes. To send SMS messages in Email & SMS Marketing, an **SMS add-on is required**. SMS add-ons are only available in the United States and Canada.
</details>
