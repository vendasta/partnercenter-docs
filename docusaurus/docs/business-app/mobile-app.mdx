# BusinessApp Mobile App

BusinessApp.io is the official mobile app that allows your clients to access their Business App communications and key features on the go. The app provides push notifications for new leads and messages, ensuring clients never miss important customer interactions.

## App Store Links

- **iOS**: [BusinessApp.io on Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/businessapp-io/id6747438513)
- **Android**: [BusinessApp.io on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.businessapp)

## System Requirements

- **iOS**: Requires iOS 13.0 or later
- **Android**: Requires Android 5.1 or later
- **App Size**: Approximately 219.5 MB
- **Publisher**: Vendasta Technologies Inc.

## Key Benefits

- **Push Notifications**: Instant alerts for new leads, messages, and important updates
- **Mobile Access**: Access conversations, CRM, and key features anywhere
- **Quick Response**: Respond to customer inquiries immediately without waiting to reach a computer
- **Lead Management**: Never miss a lead with real-time mobile notifications

## Login Process

### Step 1: Download and Install
1. Download BusinessApp.io from your device's app store
2. Install the app on your mobile device

### Step 2: Login with OTP
1. Open the app and enter your Business App login credentials (same as web version)
2. Check your email for a one-time password (OTP)
3. Enter the OTP code in the app to complete authentication

### Step 3: Choose Partner (if applicable)
- If your email is associated with multiple partners, select the appropriate partner account
- If you only have access to one partner, you'll be logged in automatically
- To switch partners, you must log out and repeat the login process

## Troubleshooting

### **Login Issues**

**Q: I'm not receiving the OTP email**
- Check spam/junk folders
- Verify email address is correct
- Wait 2-3 minutes for delivery
- Try resending the code

**Q: I see multiple partner accounts when logging in**
- This is normal if your email is associated with multiple partners
- Select the correct partner account for your business
- Partners may see a Vendasta account (used for partner communications)

### **Notification Issues**

**Q: I'm not getting push notifications**
- Ensure notifications are enabled in your device settings
- Check that the app has notification permissions
- Note: Notifications don't work for Partner or Super Admin accounts

**Q: How do I enable notifications?**
1. Go to device Settings > Notifications
2. Find BusinessApp.io in the app list
3. Enable "Allow Notifications"
4. Configure alert styles as preferred

### **Feature Access**

**Q: Some features are missing compared to the web app**
- Not all web features are available on mobile yet
- Core communication and CRM features are prioritized
- Additional features will be added in future updates

**Q: Why do some pages open in the browser?**
- Some complex features haven't been adapted for mobile yet
- These will open in your mobile browser automatically
- Full mobile versions are planned for future releases

### **Account Access**

**Q: How can I see all accounts associated with my email?**
- The app login process shows all partner accounts your email can access
- This is useful for troubleshooting account access issues
- Previously, users couldn't see this complete list

**Q: Can I stay logged in to multiple partner accounts?**
- No, you can only be logged in to one partner account at a time
- To switch accounts, log out and log back in with different partner selection

The BusinessApp mobile app ensures your clients can stay connected to their business communications and respond to leads instantly, regardless of location.
