# AI Overview

AI tools provide intelligent automation for customer interactions and sales processes. Deploy AI employees that handle inquiries 24/7, configure custom capabilities for specific business needs, and maintain a knowledge base that ensures accurate responses.

## Understanding AI Employee Components

Understanding the distinction between these three concepts is essential for building effective AI employees:

### Knowledge Base

The knowledge base contains factual content your AI can reference when answering questions. The AI searches this information and includes relevant pieces in its responses.

**Example scenario: Booking appointments**
- Your knowledge base contains the URL to your booking page (from your website)
- When a customer asks "How do I book a meeting?", the AI searches for related information
- The AI responds with: "Here's the booking link you can use: [link]"

**What it's for:**
- Business hours, location, contact information
- Pricing, product specifications, service details
- FAQs, policies, procedures
- Any factual information customers might ask about

[Learn more about Knowledge Base ›](./knowledge-base/)

### Capabilities

Capabilities tell your AI employee **how to act** and **when** to take specific actions in conversations. Think of these as process instructions you'd give to a human employee.

**Example scenario: Booking appointments**
- Your capability says: "Whenever someone asks a question, ask if they'd like to book an appointment"
- This makes your AI proactive, not just reactive
- The AI now consistently offers to book appointments, even when not explicitly asked

**What it's for:**
- Conversation flows and processes (when to capture leads, how to qualify customers)
- Tone and communication style
- Conditional logic (if customer asks X, do Y)
- Business rules and priorities

[Learn more about AI Capabilities ›](./ai-capabilities/)

### Tools
Actions the AI can take in other systems:

Tools connect your AI employee to external systems via APIs, allowing them to actually *do things* instead of just talking about them.

**Example scenario: Booking appointments**
- Your tool connects to an external calendar system via API
- Instead of sharing a booking link, the AI can directly create an appointment
- The customer gets "I've booked your appointment for Tuesday at 2pm" instead of a link to click

**What it's for:**
- Creating appointments in calendar systems
- Checking live inventory or product availability
- Looking up order status in real-time
- Creating records in your CRM
- Any action that requires connecting to another system

[Learn more about creating custom tools ›](./ai-capabilities/creating-custom-capabilities)

:::tip Quick Reference
- **Knowledge** = Information to reference (reactive, fact-based)
- **Capability** = Instructions on how to behave (proactive, process-based)
- **Tool** = Ability to take action in external systems (integration-based)
:::

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between AI Workforce and AI Capabilities?</summary>

**AI Workforce** refers to the specific AI employees (Chat Receptionist, Voice Receptionist, Sales Assistant) that interact with customers. **AI Capabilities** are the specialized skills and behaviors you configure for these employees to handle specific business tasks and situations.

Think of AI employees as the team members, and capabilities as their training and specialized skills.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do AI employees access business information to answer customer questions?</summary>

AI employees use the **Knowledge Base** to provide accurate answers. The knowledge base includes:

- Website content and pages
- Uploaded documents (FAQs, policies, procedures)
- Manual knowledge entries you create
- Basic business information (hours, location, services)

When customers ask questions, AI employees search this knowledge base to provide relevant, accurate responses.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can AI employees handle phone calls or just chat/messaging?</summary>

AI employees can handle both:

- **AI Chat Receptionist**: Website chat, SMS, social media messaging, and email
- **AI Voice Receptionist**: Phone calls with natural conversation and call routing
- **AI Sales Assistant**: Multi-channel support for sales processes

Choose the appropriate AI employee based on your primary customer communication channels.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I ensure my AI employee gives accurate information about my business?</summary>

Maintain an up-to-date **Knowledge Base** with:

1. **Current business information** - hours, services, pricing, policies
2. **Comprehensive FAQs** covering common customer questions  
3. **Regular updates** when business information changes
4. **Clear, specific language** that AI can easily understand
5. **Testing interactions** to identify knowledge gaps

The quality of your knowledge base directly impacts AI response accuracy.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize what my AI employee says and how they behave?</summary>

Yes, through **AI Capabilities** configuration:

- **Standard capabilities** provide common business functions out-of-the-box
- **Custom capabilities** let you define specific behaviors and responses
- **Integration capabilities** connect with external systems and APIs
- **Communication style** can be adjusted for your brand voice

You have full control over how your AI employees interact with customers.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if the AI employee doesn't know how to handle a customer request?</summary>

AI employees are designed with fallback options:

- **Knowledge base search** for relevant information
- **Graceful escalation** to human team members when needed
- **General assistance** for common requests outside specific knowledge
- **Clear communication** when they can't provide specific answers

This ensures customers always receive helpful responses or proper routing to human support.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I integrate AI employees with my existing business systems?</summary>

Integration options include:

- **API capabilities** for connecting with external systems
- **CRM integration** for lead capture and customer data
- **Automation workflows** that trigger based on AI interactions
- **Knowledge base sync** with existing documentation systems

Custom capabilities allow deep integration with your specific business tools and processes.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can multiple AI employees work together or share information?</summary>

Yes, AI employees share access to:

- **Common knowledge base** ensuring consistent information across all channels
- **Integrated capabilities** that work across different AI employees
- **Unified customer context** for seamless handoffs between channels
- **Shared learning** from customer interactions and feedback

This creates a cohesive AI workforce that provides consistent customer experiences.
</details>

AI tools integrate seamlessly with your existing workflows to provide automated customer support while maintaining the quality and accuracy your customers expect.
