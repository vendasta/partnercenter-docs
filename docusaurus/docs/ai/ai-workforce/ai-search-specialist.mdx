## What is the AI Search Specialist?

The **AI Search Specialist** is an AI employee that helps your business show up and get chosen across traditional search and AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

:::info Open beta
The AI Search Specialist is available now as a free, open beta preview for all Local SEO Pro accounts. No additional charge applies during the beta. These capabilities move to **SEO AI Premium** at general availability, targeted for later this year. If you don't upgrade at that time, your Local SEO Pro account continues as it is today.
:::

## What's included in the open beta

| Feature | What it does | How to access |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Search Specialist chat** | Ask questions about keyword performance, listing health, and search visibility in plain language | Open the AI Search Specialist in Business App |
| **Business Profile Optimization** | Automatically reviews and improves your business data across Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Facebook, and more | Runs automatically in the background, once you turn it on |
| **Brand Visibility Report** | Shows how your business appears in AI tools, benchmarked against competitors | Click the chip in the chat interface |
| **AEO Audit** | Evaluates whether your website is discoverable by AI tools, with prioritized recommendations | Click the chip in the chat interface |
| **SEO Audit** | Baseline website SEO performance report with actionable, ranked recommendations | Click the chip in the chat interface |

## How to access the AI Search Specialist

1. Log in to **Business App**.
2. In the left-hand navigation, go to **AI** → **AI Workforce**.
3. Click **Chat** to open the AI Search Specialist.
4. Type a question directly into chat, or click one of the three report chips: **Brand Visibility**, **AEO Audit**, or **SEO Audit**.

## How to run a report

**Brand Visibility**, **AEO Audit**, and **SEO Audit** reports are user-initiated. They run only when you click a chip in the chat interface.

1. Open the AI Search Specialist chat.
2. Click the chip for the report you want (for example, "What are customers asking AI?" for Brand Visibility).
3. The report runs in the background. This takes approximately 10 minutes.

<img src={require('./img/ai-search-specialist/ai-search-specialist-chat.png').default} alt="AI Search Specialist chat with report suggestion chips for Brand Visibility, SEO issues, and AI readiness" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

A few things to know:

- Each report type is available once per month per account.
- If a teammate already ran a report this month, you'll see their existing report instead of triggering a new one. This is by design.
- If a report doesn't generate after 10 minutes, refresh the chat.

## How Business Profile Optimization works

Business Profile Optimization runs automatically once you turn it on with a single click. It:

- Reviews your business profile data
- Fills in missing services, attributes, descriptions, payment methods, and service areas
- Syncs this data to your connected Google, Facebook, Bing, and Apple listings

You'll see optimization reflected in your listings over time, and you'll be notified any time your profile is updated. An activity log showing exactly what changed is planned for a future release.

:::note
Business Profile Optimization is turned off by default. Turn it on in the AI Search Specialist's configuration to let it run automatically in the background.
:::

## FAQs

<details>
<summary>Is there any cost to me during the beta?</summary>

No. The AI Search Specialist open beta is included at no additional charge for all Local SEO Pro accounts. Your subscription price doesn't change.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens at the end of the beta?</summary>

These features move to the SEO AI Premium edition at general availability, targeted for later this year. You'll get advance notice and an option to upgrade to keep them. If you don't upgrade, your Local SEO Pro account continues as it is today.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I turn off Business Profile Optimization?</summary>

Business Profile Optimization is turned off by default. You can turn it on in the AI Search Specialist's configuration, and it will run automatically in the background.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My report hasn't generated after 10 minutes. What do I do?</summary>

Try refreshing the chat. If the report still hasn't generated after 15 minutes, contact support through the chat icon in Business App.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The AI Search Specialist gave me inaccurate information. How do I flag it?</summary>

Use the thumbs down icon on any response in the chat. You can also reach out to your Vendasta partner or contact support directly.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will reports run automatically every month?</summary>

Not during the beta. Reports are user-initiated only and run when you click a chip. Automated monthly reporting is planned for SEO AI Premium at general availability.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I have multiple users on my account. Do we each get our own reports?</summary>

No. Reports are shared at the account level. If one user runs a report, everyone on that account can access the same result for that month.

</details>
