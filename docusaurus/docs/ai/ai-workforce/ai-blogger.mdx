## What is the AI Blogger?

The **AI Blogger** is an AI employee that runs a client's blogging program for them. On a schedule you set, it generates a blog calendar, writes long-form SEO-optimized posts, sources or generates featured images, and drafts, schedules, or publishes directly to a connected WordPress site. You can also work with it conversationally inside Business App: ask it to draft a post, refine a draft, reschedule, or delete content.

The AI Blogger is the blogging counterpart to the [Social Media Manager](./social-media-manager.mdx), and both unlock on the same edition.

:::info Requirements
The AI Blogger requires **Social AI Premium** to be active on the account, and a single-location business profile. Blog publishing requires a connected **WordPress** site.
:::

## Why it matters for your clients

Publishing consistent, search-optimized content takes editorial time most small businesses do not have. Without a system to run it, clients see long gaps between posts, topics researched and abandoned, content that is not structured for Google Snippets or AI Overviews, and drafts that never reach the schedule.

The AI Blogger covers the full content lifecycle — topic research, writing, image sourcing, and WordPress publishing — on a schedule you control, while leaving every post open to review.

## What's included

The AI Blogger ships with seven default capabilities:

| Capability | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate Blog Calendar** | Controls what gets published — goal, total posts, blog length, image source, networks, cadence, posting time, and save behavior |
| **Customize Blog Post Instructions** | Controls how each post is written — structure, SEO optimization, brand guardrails, localization, and a hard stop on invented promotions |
| **AI Image Generation** | Generates WordPress-optimized featured images |
| **Search Web** | Surfaces trending customer questions, industry data, and current news so posts stay grounded and timely |
| **Find Royalty-Free Images** | Sources imagery from Pexels and Pixabay when AI image generation is not selected or has reached its limit |
| **List Integration Connections** | Fetches active blog platform connections so posts publish only to connected WordPress sites |
| **Manage Blog Posts** | Publishes posts, and lists, edits, reschedules, and deletes them conversationally |

Each capability is fully editable, and you can add your own with `+ Add Capability`.

## How to configure it

1. Open the client's Business App and go to `AI` → `Workforce`.
2. Click `Configure` on the AI Blogger.
3. *(Optional)* Rename the AI employee and upload an identifying image.
4. Open `Blog Calendar Generation` to set the trigger frequency, time, time zone, and start and end dates.
5. Open the `Generate Blog Calendar` capability to set the goal, total posts, blog length, image source, networks, cadence, posting time, and save behavior.
6. Add knowledge sources covering the client's products, services, audience, and brand voice.
7. Save. The AI Blogger then runs automatically on the trigger schedule.

### Trigger defaults

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency | Weekly, Mondays |
| Time | 9:00 AM |
| Time zone | The business time zone configured in Business App |

### Calendar defaults

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Total posts per run | 1 |
| Blog length | Long, 1,450–1,550 words |
| Images | AI-generated |
| Networks | The first active WordPress connection found |
| Cadence | Next week |
| Posting time | Best time |
| Save as | Schedule |

:::tip
Start with `Save as: Draft` while your client gets comfortable with the output. Switch to `Schedule` once they are confident in the results. `Save as: Published` publishes immediately on every run and is not recommended for a forward-looking calendar.
:::

## FAQs

<details>
<summary>Which edition includes the AI Blogger?</summary>

Social AI Premium. The Social Media Manager unlocks on the same edition.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which blog platforms are supported?</summary>

WordPress. Connect the client's WordPress site under integrations before running the AI Blogger.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if a client has several WordPress connections?</summary>

The AI Blogger uses the first active WordPress connection it finds. Posts are not duplicated across multiple sites.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How many posts does each run generate?</summary>

One by default, set by **Total posts** in the Generate Blog Calendar capability. Combined with the trigger frequency, those two settings determine total publishing volume.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will it invent promotions or pricing the client has not shared?</summary>

No. The Customize Blog Post Instructions capability includes a built-in hard stop preventing the AI from fabricating discounts, offers, or pricing the business has not communicated.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does it repeat topics?</summary>

No. The AI Blogger checks for content duplication against previous months before generating.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can it run outside the schedule?</summary>

Yes. Chat with the AI Blogger at any time to generate a post or calendar on demand, independent of the trigger.

</details>
