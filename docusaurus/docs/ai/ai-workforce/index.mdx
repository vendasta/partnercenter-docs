# AI Workforce

Vendasta's AI Workforce provides intelligent AI Employees designed to streamline your sales processes, improve productivity, and enhance customer interactions. These AI-powered employees work 24/7 alongside your team, handling routine customer interactions so your human staff can focus on complex, high-value activities.

## What is the Vendasta AI Workforce?

AI Workforce is a collection of intelligent employees that work alongside your team to:   

- **Automate routine tasks** - Handle repetitive customer inquiries, lead capture, and appointment booking automatically
- **Extend business hours** - Provide 24/7 customer service without additional staffing costs
- **Improve customer interactions** - Deliver instant, personalized responses using your business knowledge
- **Integrate seamlessly** - Work within your existing tools, CRM, calendar, and automation workflows

## How AI Employees Work

### The Foundation: Knowledge + Capabilities

Every AI Employee is built on two core foundations:

#### **Knowledge Base** - What They Know
Your AI Employees access a centralized knowledge repository containing:
- **Business Profile** - Hours, location, services, contact information
- **Website Content** - Product details, policies, FAQs automatically synced from your site
- **Custom Documents** - Price lists, manuals, specialized information you upload
- **Manual Knowledge** - Specific facts and answers you add as text

This knowledge is continuously accessible, ensuring AI Employees always have accurate, up-to-date information when answering customer questions.

:::tip Learn More
See [Knowledge Base Overview](../knowledge-base/) for detailed information on managing your AI's knowledge sources.
:::

#### **Capabilities** - What They Can Do
Capabilities are specialized skills that define your AI Employee's actions and behaviors:
- **Lead Capture** - Automatically collect and qualify contact information
- **Appointment Booking** - Schedule meetings using your connected calendars
- **Call Transfer** - Route calls to appropriate team members based on context
- **Custom Actions** - Connect to your business systems via API integrations

Each AI Employee can have multiple capabilities enabled, and they intelligently decide which to use based on customer requests.

:::tip Learn More
See [AI Capabilities Overview](../ai-capabilities/) for detailed information on configuring and creating capabilities.
:::

### Multi-Channel Communication

AI Employees interact through multiple channels, each optimized for its medium:
- **Website Chat** - Real-time conversations through your website chat widget
- **Voice Calls** - Natural phone interactions with voice-to-text processing
- **SMS & Email** - Automated responses and follow-ups via messaging
- **API Integration** - Programmatic access for custom workflows

### Intelligent Processing

Behind every interaction, AI Employees use sophisticated natural language processing to:
- **Understand Intent** - Recognize what customers want, even with varied phrasing
- **Maintain Context** - Remember conversation history and reference previous messages
- **Detect Language** - Automatically identify and respond in 40+ languages
- **Analyze Sentiment** - Adjust tone and approach based on customer emotions
- **Extract Information** - Identify key details like names, dates, and preferences

### Continuous Learning

AI Employees improve over time through:
- **Conversation Analysis** - Identifying patterns and optimization opportunities
- **Performance Monitoring** - Tracking success rates for different interactions
- **Knowledge Updates** - Incorporating new information as you add it
- **Feedback Integration** - Learning from human oversight and corrections

This continuous improvement ensures your AI Employees become more effective at serving your customers as they handle more interactions.

## AI Employees + Automations: Intelligent Workflows

Combine AI Employees with Vendasta's automation engine to create sophisticated, intelligent workflows that scale your business operations.

### How AI + Automations Work Together

AI Employees can be embedded directly into automation workflows through the **"Send request to an AI Employee"** step. This allows you to leverage your AI's intelligence within automated business processes.

**The Integration Flow:**
1. **Automation Triggers** - Form submission, time-based schedule, CRM event, or custom trigger
2. **Context Sent to AI** - Automation provides relevant data (form fields, customer info, etc.)
3. **AI Processes Intelligently** - Uses knowledge base and capabilities to generate appropriate response
4. **Response Used in Workflow** - AI output drives next steps (send message, create task, update CRM, etc.)

### Real-World Use Cases

#### Instant Form Response
**Scenario:** Customer submits a contact form requesting a quote for plumbing services.

**What Happens:**
- Form submission triggers automation
- AI Chat Receptionist receives form data (name, service needed, property details)
- AI crafts personalized response using business knowledge (pricing, availability, process)
- Instant SMS and email sent to customer with helpful information
- Lead automatically created in CRM with qualification notes

**Business Impact:** Every form submission gets immediate, intelligent response—even at 3 AM.

#### Appointment Follow-Up
**Scenario:** Customer books an appointment through AI Voice Receptionist.

**What Happens:**
- Appointment booking triggers automation
- AI generates confirmation message with relevant details
- SMS reminder sent 24 hours before appointment
- If customer responds with questions, AI can answer using knowledge base
- Cancellation requests automatically handled and calendar updated

**Business Impact:** Reduces no-shows and eliminates manual reminder work.

### Getting Started with AI + Automations

**Quick Setup in Partner Center:**
1. Navigate to **Automations** in your dashboard
2. Click **"Create automation"**
3. Browse pre-built templates featuring AI Employee integrations
4. Select your preferred AI Employee and customize the prompt
5. Configure follow-up actions (send messages, create tasks, update CRM)
6. Enable and test your automation

**Popular Templates:**
- **Form submission response** - AI responds instantly to form inquiries
- **Appointment reminders** - AI sends personalized pre-appointment communications
- **Lead qualification** - AI analyzes and scores incoming leads
- **Customer onboarding** - AI guides new customers through setup process

:::tip Pro Tip
Start with a pre-built template and customize it for your specific needs. You can create multiple variations for different form types, customer segments, or business scenarios.
:::

This modular system lets you build AI Employees tailored to your exact business needs.
