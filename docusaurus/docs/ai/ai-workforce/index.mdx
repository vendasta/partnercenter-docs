- **Streamline workflows** - Integrate AI capabilities directly into your existing business processes

## AI Employees + Automations Integration

Take your AI Employees to the next level by integrating them with Automations. This powerful combination allows you to create intelligent, automated workflows that leverage your AI's knowledge and capabilities.

### Use AI Employees within Automations

The "Send request to an AI Employee" step in Automations allows you to send a prompt to any of your AI Employees and use their response within your automation workflow. This opens up countless possibilities for intelligent automation.

**How it works:**
1. **Trigger an automation** - Any automation trigger (form submission, time-based, etc.)
2. **Send a request to your AI Employee** - The automation prompts your AI with context and instructions
3. **AI processes the request** - Your AI Employee uses their full knowledge base and capabilities to generate a response
4. **Use the response** - The AI's response can be used in subsequent automation steps (send emails, SMS, create tasks, etc.)

### Featured Use Case: Instant Form Response

**The Challenge:** When potential customers submit forms on your website, they want immediate acknowledgment and answers to their questions. Manual responses are slow and inconsistent.

**The Solution:** Use the "Form submission – AI Chat Receptionist automatically replies with a custom message" automation template.

**What happens:**
1. **Customer submits a form** on your website
2. **Automation triggers** and sends the form data to your AI Chat Receptionist
3. **AI crafts a personalized response** using the form information and your business knowledge
4. **Instant reply sent** via SMS and/or email to the customer
5. **Follow-up actions** can be triggered based on the inquiry type

### Setting Up AI + Automations

**In Partner Center:**
1. Go to **Automations** in your dashboard
2. Click **"Create automation"**
3. Choose **"Form submission – AI Chat Receptionist automatically replies with a custom message"** template
4. **Customize the automation:**
   - Select which AI Employee to use
   - Adjust the prompt/instructions for your AI
   - Choose whether to send SMS, email, or both (SMS available in Partner Center)
   - Set up any additional follow-up actions
5. **Enable the automation** and set it to run multiple times

**Benefits of this integration:**
- **Instant customer engagement** - No delay between inquiry and response
- **Personalized responses** - AI uses form data and business knowledge for relevant replies
- **24/7 availability** - Works even when your team is offline
- **Consistent quality** - AI maintains professional tone and accurate information
- **Scalable solution** - Handles any volume of form submissions

:::tip Pro Tip
You can customize the AI's instructions to handle different types of forms differently. For example, service inquiries might get different responses than contact forms or quote requests.
:::

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary>What is AI Workforce and how does it work?</summary>

AI Workforce is a collection of intelligent AI Employees that work alongside your team to automate routine tasks, improve customer interactions, and streamline business processes. These AI Employees can handle customer inquiries, capture leads, book appointments, and integrate with your existing workflows through Automations.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between AI Chat Receptionist and AI Voice Receptionist?</summary>

The **AI Chat Receptionist** handles text-based conversations through your website chat widget, capturing leads and answering questions 24/7. The **AI Voice Receptionist** answers phone calls, can book appointments using your calendar, and handles voice interactions with callers. Both use the same knowledge base but are optimized for their respective communication channels.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do AI Employees work 24/7?</summary>

Yes! AI Employees are available 24/7, 365 days a year. They never take breaks, sick days, or vacations. This means your customers can get instant responses to their questions and inquiries even when your human team is offline, helping you capture leads and provide support around the clock.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do AI Employees learn about my business?</summary>

AI Employees learn through your **Knowledge Base**, which includes website content, custom documents (like FAQs and price lists), and business information you provide. You can continuously add and update knowledge sources to improve their responses. The more relevant information you provide, the more helpful and accurate your AI Employees become.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize what my AI Employees say and do?</summary>

Absolutely! You can customize AI Employee behavior through **Capabilities** and **Additional Instructions**. You can enable or disable specific skills (like appointment booking or lead capture), write custom prompts to guide their responses, and even create custom capabilities that integrate with your business tools and processes.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if an AI Employee doesn't know the answer to a question?</summary>

When an AI Employee encounters a question they can't answer from their knowledge base, they'll acknowledge that they don't have that specific information and offer to take a message or connect the customer with a human team member. This ensures customers never feel abandoned and all inquiries are properly handled.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can AI Employees integrate with my existing business tools?</summary>

Yes! AI Employees can integrate with various business systems through **Custom Capabilities**. We provide detailed guides for popular integrations like Shopify and ServiceTitan, and you can create custom integrations with any system that has an API. Plus, the **Automations integration** allows AI Employees to work within automated workflows.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Will AI Employees replace my human staff?</summary>

No, AI Employees are designed to **augment** your human team, not replace them. They handle routine inquiries and repetitive tasks, freeing up your human staff to focus on more complex, high-value activities that require human judgment, creativity, and relationship building. Think of them as your most reliable team members who handle the basics so humans can focus on what they do best.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How accurate are AI Employee responses?</summary>

AI Employee accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of your knowledge base. When provided with comprehensive, up-to-date information, AI Employees are highly accurate and consistent. They don't have "off days" and always follow the instructions and knowledge you've provided. You can monitor conversations and continuously improve their knowledge base for even better performance.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can AI Employees handle multiple languages?</summary>

Yes! AI Employees can detect and respond in over 40 languages, including English, French, Spanish, and many others. This makes them valuable for businesses serving diverse customer bases or operating in multiple markets. The language capabilities vary by voice selection for the AI Voice Receptionist.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I monitor and improve my AI Employees' performance?</summary>

You can review all AI Employee interactions through the **Conversations** tab in your Business App. This includes call recordings, chat transcripts, and conversation summaries. Use these insights to identify areas for improvement, update your knowledge base, and refine your AI Employee instructions for better performance over time.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How secure is AI Workforce and my customer data?</summary>

AI Workforce follows enterprise-level security practices to protect your data and customer information. All conversations are processed securely, and the system only accesses the knowledge you explicitly provide. Customer data is handled according to privacy regulations, and you maintain full control over what information your AI Employees can access and share.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can AI Employees book appointments and manage my calendar?</summary>

Yes! The AI Voice Receptionist and AI Chat Receptionist can both book appointments when you connect your calendar. They can check real-time availability, collect booking details, and automatically create calendar events. You can configure which types of appointments they can book and set specific instructions for the booking process.
</details>

Basic AI Employee setup can be completed in under 30 minutes. This includes configuring their name, selecting capabilities like lead capture and appointment booking, and adding initial knowledge sources. For more advanced configurations like custom capabilities or complex integrations, setup may take longer but comprehensive documentation guides you through each step.
</details>

<summary>What types of businesses benefit most from AI Workforce?</summary>

AI Workforce is particularly valuable for service-based businesses, e-commerce stores, healthcare practices, real estate agencies, home services companies, and any business that receives frequent customer inquiries. Companies that want to improve lead capture, provide 24/7 customer support, or automate appointment booking see immediate benefits from AI Employees.
</details>
