# AI Workforce

Vendasta's AI Workforce provides intelligent AI Employees designed to streamline your sales processes, improve productivity, and enhance customer interactions. These AI-powered employees work 24/7 alongside your team, handling routine customer interactions so your human staff can focus on complex, high-value activities.

## What is the Vendasta AI Workforce?

AI Workforce is a collection of intelligent employees that work alongside your team to:   

- **Automate routine tasks** - Handle repetitive customer inquiries, lead capture, and appointment booking automatically
- **Extend business hours** - Provide 24/7 customer service without additional staffing costs
- **Improve customer interactions** - Deliver instant, personalized responses using your business knowledge
- **Integrate seamlessly** - Work within your existing tools, CRM, calendar, and automation workflows

## How AI Employees Work

While the jobs that AI Employees might complete are varied, all AI Employees are built from the same integrated components working together to deliver sophisticated customer interactions. Understanding the foundation of these AI Employees will help you configure them to best fit your business needs.

### The Foundation: Knowledge + Capabilities

Every AI Employee is built on two core foundations:

#### **Knowledge Base** - What They Know
Your AI Employees access a centralized knowledge repository which might include:
- **Business Profile** - Hours, location, services, contact information
- **Website Content** - Product details, policies, FAQs automatically synced from your site
- **Custom Documents** - Price lists, manuals, specialized information you upload
- **Manual Knowledge** - Specific facts and answers you add as text

This knowledge is continuously accessible, ensuring AI Employees always have accurate, up-to-date information when answering customer questions.

:::tip Learn More
See [Knowledge Base Overview](../knowledge-base/) for detailed information on managing your AI's knowledge sources.
:::

#### **Capabilities** - What They Can Do
Capabilities are specialized skills that define your AI Employee's actions and behaviors which might include:
- **Lead Capture** - Automatically collect and qualify contact information
- **Appointment Booking** - Schedule meetings using your connected calendars
- **Call Transfer** - Route calls to appropriate team members based on context
- **Custom Actions** - Connect to your business systems via API integrations

Each AI Employee can have multiple capabilities enabled, and they intelligently decide which to use based on customer requests.

:::tip Learn More
See [AI Capabilities Overview](../ai-capabilities/) for detailed information on configuring and creating capabilities.
:::

## Getting Started with AI Employees

Setting up your first AI Employee is quick and straightforward:

### Basic Setup (Under 30 Minutes)
1. **Connect knowledge sources** - Start with Business Profile and website sync
2. **Enable basic capabilities** - Turn on lead capture and/or appointment booking
3. **Set communication preferences** - Configure greetings and basic instructions
4. **Test and launch** - Try a few conversations and go live

### Advanced Configuration (As Needed)
- **Add custom knowledge** - Upload specialized documents and FAQs
- **Create custom capabilities** - Build integrations with your business systems
- **Set up automations** - Create intelligent workflows combining AI with automation
- **Monitor and optimize** - Review conversations and refine knowledge and instructions

:::info Quick Tip
Start simple and expand over time. Your AI Employee can be live and helping customers with basic knowledge in under 30 minutes. Add complexity as you identify opportunities for enhancement.
:::

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary>What is AI Workforce and how does it work?</summary>

AI Workforce is a collection of intelligent AI Employees built on two foundations: **Knowledge** (what they know about your business) and **Capabilities** (what actions they can perform). They use natural language processing to understand customer requests, access your knowledge base for accurate information, and execute capabilities like lead capture or appointment booking. <details>
<summary>What's the difference between an AI Employee and a simple chatbot?</summary>

Simple chatbots follow rigid scripts with limited responses. AI Employees use advanced natural language processing to understand intent and context, maintain conversation history across multiple messages, access a comprehensive knowledge base for accurate answers, and execute real business actions through capabilities (booking appointments, capturing leads, updating CRM). They also continuously learn from interactions and improve over time, whereas basic chatbots remain static.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between AI Chat Receptionist and AI Voice Receptionist?</summary>

The **AI Chat Receptionist** handles text-based conversations through your website chat widget, capturing leads and answering questions 24/7. The **AI Voice Receptionist** answers phone calls, can book appointments using your calendar, and handles voice interactions with callers. Both use the same knowledge base but are optimized for their respective communication channels.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do AI Employees work 24/7?</summary>

Yes! AI Employees are available 24/7, 365 days a year. They never take breaks, sick days, or vacations. This means your customers can get instant responses to their questions and inquiries even when your human team is offline, helping you capture leads and provide support around the clock.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do AI Employees learn about my business?</summary>

AI Employees access a centralized **Knowledge Base** that pulls from multiple sources: your Business Profile (automatically synced), website content (crawled and indexed), uploaded documents (PDFs, manuals, FAQs), and manual knowledge you add. This knowledge is continuously accessible during conversations, so AI Employees always reference current, accurate information. You can add or update sources anytime, and the improvements are immediately reflected in responses.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What are capabilities and how do they work?</summary>

**Capabilities** are specialized skills that define what actions your AI Employee can perform. Think of them as job responsibilities—lead capture, appointment booking, call transfer, or custom integrations. Each capability includes instructions that tell the AI when and how to use it, and optionally connects to external systems via APIs. You can enable multiple capabilities per AI Employee, and they intelligently activate the right one based on customer requests. This modular system lets you build AI Employees tailored to your exact business needs.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I customize what my AI Employees say and do?</summary>

Yes! Customization happens at three levels: **Knowledge Base** (control what information they can access), **Capabilities** (enable/disable specific actions and configure parameters), and **Additional Instructions** (write custom prompts for tone, greetings, escalation rules, and special scenarios). You can even create fully custom capabilities that integrate with your proprietary business systems. This flexibility ensures your AI Employee represents your brand and follows your exact business processes.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if an AI Employee doesn't know the answer to a question?</summary>

When an AI Employee encounters a question they can't answer from their knowledge base, they'll acknowledge that they don't have that specific information and offer to take a message or connect the customer with a human team member. This ensures customers never feel abandoned and all inquiries are properly handled.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can AI Employees integrate with my existing business tools?</summary>

Absolutely! AI Employees integrate in three ways: **Built-in integrations** (CRM, calendars like Google and Outlook), **Pre-built custom capabilities** (detailed guides for Shopify, ServiceTitan, and other popular platforms), and **Custom capabilities you build** (connect to any system with an API using webhooks and REST endpoints). Additionally, the **Automations integration** lets AI Employees participate in complex multi-step workflows alongside your other business systems.
</details>

<summary>How do AI Employees handle multiple languages?</summary>

AI Employees automatically detect the language a customer is using and respond in that same language. They support 40+ languages including English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, and many others. This happens seamlessly without any configuration—the natural language processing system identifies the language and generates appropriate responses using your knowledge base content. For AI Voice Receptionist, specific voice selections support different language sets.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do Knowledge Base and Capabilities work together?</summary>

Think of it this way: **Knowledge Base** is what your AI Employee knows (business hours, pricing, policies), and **Capabilities** are what they can do with that knowledge (capture leads, book appointments, update CRM). When a customer asks a question, the AI references the Knowledge Base for facts and uses Capabilities to take actions. For example, answering "What are your hours?" uses Knowledge, while "I'd like to book an appointment" activates the appointment booking Capability which then uses Knowledge to check availability and business policies.
</details>
