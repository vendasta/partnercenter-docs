# AI Workforce

Vendasta's AI Workforce provides intelligent tools and assistants designed to streamline your sales processes, improve productivity, and enhance customer interactions. These AI-powered solutions integrate seamlessly with your existing workflows to provide automated support and insights.

## What is AI Workforce?

AI Workforce represents a collection of intelligent tools that work alongside your team to:

- **Automate routine tasks** - Let AI handle repetitive work so your team can focus on high-value activities
- **Enhance decision making** - Get AI-powered insights and recommendations based on data analysis
- **Improve customer interactions** - Leverage AI to provide better, more personalized customer experiences
- **Streamline workflows** - Integrate AI capabilities directly into your existing business processes

## AI Employees + Automations Integration

Take your AI Employees to the next level by integrating them with Automations. This powerful combination allows you to create intelligent, automated workflows that leverage your AI's knowledge and capabilities.

### Use AI Employees within Automations

The "Send request to an AI Employee" step in Automations allows you to send a prompt to any of your AI Employees and use their response within your automation workflow. This opens up countless possibilities for intelligent automation.

**How it works:**
1. **Trigger an automation** - Any automation trigger (form submission, time-based, etc.)
2. **Send a request to your AI Employee** - The automation prompts your AI with context and instructions
3. **AI processes the request** - Your AI Employee uses their full knowledge base and capabilities to generate a response
4. **Use the response** - The AI's response can be used in subsequent automation steps (send emails, SMS, create tasks, etc.)

### Featured Use Case: Instant Form Response

**The Challenge:** When potential customers submit forms on your website, they want immediate acknowledgment and answers to their questions. Manual responses are slow and inconsistent.

**The Solution:** Use the "Form submission – AI Chat Receptionist automatically replies with a custom message" automation template.

**What happens:**
1. **Customer submits a form** on your website
2. **Automation triggers** and sends the form data to your AI Chat Receptionist
3. **AI crafts a personalized response** using the form information and your business knowledge
4. **Instant reply sent** via SMS and/or email to the customer
5. **Follow-up actions** can be triggered based on the inquiry type

### Setting Up AI + Automations

**In Partner Center:**
1. Go to **Automations** in your dashboard
2. Click **"Create automation"**
3. Choose **"Form submission – AI Chat Receptionist automatically replies with a custom message"** template
4. **Customize the automation:**
   - Select which AI Employee to use
   - Adjust the prompt/instructions for your AI
   - Choose whether to send SMS, email, or both (SMS available in Partner Center)
   - Set up any additional follow-up actions
5. **Enable the automation** and set it to run multiple times

**Benefits of this integration:**
- **Instant customer engagement** - No delay between inquiry and response
- **Personalized responses** - AI uses form data and business knowledge for relevant replies
- **24/7 availability** - Works even when your team is offline
- **Consistent quality** - AI maintains professional tone and accurate information
- **Scalable solution** - Handles any volume of form submissions

:::tip Pro Tip
You can customize the AI's instructions to handle different types of forms differently. For example, service inquiries might get different responses than contact forms or quote requests.
:::
