The AI Search Specialist automatically keeps clients' business descriptions optimized for local search. It runs on a monthly schedule and refreshes a description only when new services or tracked keywords should be reflected—no client action required.

## How it works

Each month, the AI evaluates the client's existing description against:

- Their website and business profile content (services, specialties, voice)
- Tracked keywords and their search performance data
- Business category

A description is updated only when there is a meaningful signal to act on—new services, a niche specialty not yet in the description, or tracked keywords missing from it. Descriptions updated within the last 90 days are left untouched, and descriptions that already reflect current services and keywords are skipped until the next cycle.

## What clients experience

When the AI Search Specialist updates a description:

1. The client receives an **AI Search Specialist: Profile Description Updated** notification in Business App.
2. The notification links to Business Profile so they can review the change.
3. The updated description syncs to Google, Bing, Apple, and Facebook.

Clients can review and edit their description in Business Profile at any time.

## Requirements

- Local SEO Premium subscription
- Keywords tracked in the client's account

## Common client questions

**"Why did my description change automatically?"**
The AI detected new services or tracked keywords not yet reflected in the description. Direct clients to their Business Profile notification to review the update.

**"Can they undo the change?"**
Yes. Clients can edit or replace their description in Business Profile at any time.

**"How often will it change their description?"**
The AI checks once a month but only updates when there is something meaningful to add. Clients with stable services and keyword coverage may see infrequent or no changes.
