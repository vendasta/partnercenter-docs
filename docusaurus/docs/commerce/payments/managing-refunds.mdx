This guide explains how to issue refunds for a client in Vendasta, including refund limits, steps for initiating a refund, required details for high-value refunds, processing timelines, and Stripe fees.

To refund a payment within the $1000 limit:

1. Log in to `Partner Center`.
2. Navigate to `Commerce` → `Payments`.
3. Click the three-dot kebab icon to access the `Refund` option for the invoice or payment you want to refund.
4. Enter the refund amount (partial refunds are supported).
5. Confirm and `submit` the refund.

## Troubleshooting refund errors

You may encounter error messages such as:

- `Unable to refund due to balance`
- `Invalid merchant health`

These may occur due to balance limitations, merchant health issues, or restrictions on refund amounts.  
If this happens, contact Vendasta Support for assistance.

## Refunds over $1000

To refund more than $1000, contact Vendasta Support. Support routes your request to the Vendasta Finance team.

Include the following details in your request:

- Client account name
- Refund amount
- Reason for the refund
- Invoice number

The Finance team processes the refund on your behalf.

## Refund processing time

Refunds typically take **5–10 business days** from the date they are issued.  
Your clients receive the refunded funds within this window.

Once processed, a **refund receipt or notification** is automatically emailed to your client.

## Refund fees

Vendasta does **not charge a fee** for refund processing.

However, Stripe does **not return the original processing fee** when you issue a refund:

- **2.99% + $0.30** per transaction

This fee is deducted from your Stripe account.
