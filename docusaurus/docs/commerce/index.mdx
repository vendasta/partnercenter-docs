# Commerce overview

The Vendasta Commerce platform provides comprehensive tools for managing all aspects of your business transactions and financial operations. From creating sales orders and invoices to processing payments and managing subscriptions, Commerce streamlines your entire financial workflow in one integrated platform.

## Why use Commerce?

Simplify your financial operations with automated billing, streamlined order processing, and comprehensive payment management. Commerce eliminates manual invoicing tasks, reduces billing errors, and provides complete visibility into your revenue streams — helping you get paid faster and manage cash flow more effectively.

## What's included

- **[Orders](./orders/index.mdx)**: Create, manage, approve, and track sales orders with simplified workflows and bulk ordering
- **[Invoices](./invoices/index.mdx)**: Create and send invoices, apply tax rates, manage discounts, and track invoice status
- **[Subscriptions](./subscriptions/index.mdx)**: Manage automated recurring billing for products and services
- **[Payments](./payments/index.mdx)**: Process payments, handle payment collection, and manage payment methods
- **[Payouts](./payouts/index.mdx)**: Track and manage payouts to your bank account
- **[Credit Notes](./credit-notes/index.mdx)**: Issue credit notes for refunds and adjustments

## Get started

1. **Navigate to Commerce**
   - Go to `Partner Center` > `Commerce`
   - Explore the different sections: Orders, Invoices, Subscriptions, Payments

2. **Set up billing settings**
   - Configure default billing terms and payment methods
   - Set up tax rates if applicable
   - Configure subscription billing preferences

3. **Create your first order**
   - Navigate to `Commerce` > `Orders` > `Create Sales Order`
   - Add products and configure billing terms
   - Submit for approval or activate directly

4. **Manage subscriptions**
   - Review active subscriptions in `Commerce` > `Subscriptions`
   - Configure billing settings per account
   - Monitor upcoming invoices and renewals

## Common workflows

**Order to Invoice**: Create a sales order, collect customer approval, activate products, and automatically generate invoices for recurring billing.

**Subscription Management**: Set up recurring subscriptions, customize billing schedules, and manage subscription changes or cancellations.

**Payment Processing**: Collect payments directly from orders, process recurring payments, and handle payment failures with automated retry logic.

**Financial Reporting**: Track revenue, monitor outstanding invoices, and generate financial reports for accounting purposes.
