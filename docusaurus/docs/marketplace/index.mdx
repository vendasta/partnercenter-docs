# Marketplace overview

The Vendasta Marketplace is your central hub for discovering, enabling, and managing products and services to sell to your customers. From browsing available products to creating custom packages and configuring your store, the Marketplace provides everything you need to build a comprehensive product portfolio.

## Why use the Marketplace?

Build a complete product offering without the complexity of managing multiple vendors. The Marketplace provides access to hundreds of proven products, flexible packaging options, and powerful store customization tools — helping you maximize revenue while minimizing operational overhead.

## What's included

- **[Discover Products](./products/index.mdx)**: Browse and enable products from the Vendasta Marketplace
- **[Packages](./packages/packages-overview-creation.mdx)**: Create bundled product packages for maximum customer value
- **[Manage Store](./manage-store/index.mdx)**: Configure and customize your Marketplace store appearance and settings

## Get started

1. **Discover products**
   - Navigate to `Partner Center` > `Marketplace` > `Discover Products`
   - Browse categories and review product details
   - Click `Start Selling` on products that fit your market

2. **Configure product settings**
   - Go to `Marketplace` > `Products` to adjust retail pricing
   - Set up white-label branding and auto-activation
   - Configure upgrade paths and billing frequencies

3. **Create packages**
   - Navigate to `Marketplace` > `Packages`
   - Use recommended packages or create custom bundles
   - Set pricing and marketing materials

4. **Set up your store**
   - Customize store theme and appearance
   - Organize products and packages by category
   - Configure currency and payment settings

## Common workflows

**Product Enablement**: Browse marketplace products, enable those relevant to your market, configure pricing and branding, and make them available in your store.

**Package Creation**: Select products for a package, set bundle pricing, create marketing materials, and publish to your store for customer purchase.

**Store Customization**: Configure store theme, organize products into categories, set up payment methods, and customize the customer shopping experience.
