# Store sharing & embedding

Your Marketplace store is a customer‑facing storefront where prospects can discover solutions, compare options, and purchase. Share it via website embed, direct links, or within marketing campaigns.

## Store sharing overview

Use the **Public Store** for a standalone, embeddable store that integrates with your customer management. Share it via website embed, direct links, or within marketing campaigns.

## Embed your store on your website

1. Navigate to **Partner Center** → **Marketplace** → **Manage Store** → **Public Store**
2. Choose an embedding version:
   - **Store with Sign-In** – Includes customer authentication options
   - **Store without Sign-In** – Streamlined experience without authentication UI
   - **Store without Sign-In and Top Bar** – Minimal interface for seamless website integration
3. Copy the embed code snippet
4. Paste it into your website's HTML editor

**Benefits**
- New products and packages automatically appear on your website
- Store content appears as part of your website
- Theme settings ensure brand consistency
- Choose the best integration method for your site design

### Remove the "Sign In" button when embedding

If you want to hide the Sign In button on the embedded Public Store:

1. Go to **Partner Center** → **Marketplace** → **Manage Store** → **Public Store**
2. Under Embed Code, select **Store without sign-in** (or **Store without sign-in and top bar** for a minimal header)
3. Copy the updated code and replace any previously embedded version on your website

## Share a direct link

1. Navigate to **Partner Center** → **Marketplace** → **Manage Store** → **Public Store**
2. Copy the **Share URL** — a direct link to your standalone store
3. Distribute via email signatures, social media, marketing campaigns, or direct customer communication

Find your standalone store URL at **Partner Center** → **Marketplace** → **Manage Store** → **Public Store** → **Copy Share URL**.

## Multi‑market store management

For partners using Markets to serve different customer segments, choose which market's store appears on your website using the **Market Selector** in **Marketplace** → **Manage Store**.
