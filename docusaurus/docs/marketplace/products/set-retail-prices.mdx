To set a custom retail price for a product upgrade in Partner Center, go to `Marketplace` → `Products`, open the product, click the `Product Info` tab, and click `Edit` next to the edition under `Retail price`. Until you set a price, Business App shows the Pro edition MSRP.

When a customer clicks on a Pro feature in Business App, they are prompted to upgrade from the Standard edition to the Pro edition. Business App displays the Pro edition MSRP by default. This article explains how to set a custom retail price and how to reset it back to the default.

## How do I set a retail price? {#set-retail-prices}

The steps are the same for all products. The only difference is which product to search for in step 2.

1. Go to `Marketplace` → [`Products`](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/manage-products).
2. Search for and click the product you want to price:
   - **Reputation AI, Customer Voice, Website, Social AI**: search by product name
   - **Advertising Intelligence**: search for *Advanced Reporting*
   - **Local SEO (Citation Builder)**: search for *Citation Builder*
   - **Local SEO (Listing Sync)**: search for *Listing Sync*
3. Click the `Product Info` tab.
4. If applicable, select a `Market`.
5. Under `Retail price`, expand the edition you want to price (for example, `Pro`) and click `Edit`.
6. Enter the `Setup fee` and `Retail price`, and select a `Billing period`.
7. Click `Save`.

The currency comes from your Public Store Settings. To change it, click the `Public Store Settings` link at the top of the `Retail price` section.

The new price will appear in Business App when customers click an upgrade button.

## How do I reset a retail price to the default MSRP? {#reset-retail-prices}

To revert a custom price back to the default MSRP:

1. Go to `Marketplace` → [`Products`](https://partners.vendasta.com/marketplace/manage-products).
2. Search for and click the product.
3. Click the `Product Info` tab.
4. If applicable, select a `Market`.
5. Under `Retail price`, expand the edition and click `Edit`.
6. Click `Reset to MSRP`. The price resets right away, so you don't need to click `Save`.


## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Why does Business App show the MSRP as the upgrade price?</summary>

Business App displays the Pro edition MSRP by default. Set a custom retail price in `Marketplace` → `Products` to show your own price instead.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I find Local SEO or Advertising Intelligence products to price?</summary>

In `Marketplace` → `Products`, search for *Citation Builder* or *Listing Sync* for Local SEO, and *Advanced Reporting* for Advertising Intelligence.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change my retail price back to the default?</summary>

Open the product in `Marketplace` → `Products`, click the `Product Info` tab, expand the edition under `Retail price`, and click `Edit`. Click `Reset to MSRP`. The price resets right away.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change the currency for a retail price?</summary>

The retail price currency is set in your Public Store Settings. Click the `Public Store Settings` link at the top of the `Retail price` section to change it.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does my custom retail price appear?</summary>

The price appears in Business App when customers click an upgrade button for a Pro feature.
</details>
