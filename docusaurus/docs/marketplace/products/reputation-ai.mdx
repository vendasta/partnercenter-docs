# Reputation AI Premium

Reputation AI Premium (formerly Reputation Management Premium) allows your clients to monitor reviews, mentions, and social media, while actively requesting feedback from their customers.

## SMS Review Requests

While Reputation AI Premium includes email review requests in the base price, sending review requests via SMS requires an additional configuration.

### SMS Add-on
SMS messaging is not included in the base Reputation AI Premium price. To enable this feature for your clients:
1. Navigate to **Partner Center** > **Marketplace** > **Products**.
2. Enable the **SMS add-on** for the relevant account.
3. Configure the retail pricing for the add-on.

### Included Credits
For accounts in the US and Canada, the first **50 SMS credits** are typically included at no extra cost once the SMS add-on is activated. These credits allow your clients to start sending review requests immediately after registration is complete.

After the initial 50 credits are used, additional SMS credit bundles must be purchased to continue sending SMS requests.

### A2P 10DLC Registration
For US-based businesses, carrier compliance (A2P 10DLC) is required before SMS messages can be sent. 
- You or your client must complete the registration form in **Business App** > **Settings** > **SMS Settings**.
- Registration typically takes 1-4 weeks for carrier approval.
- Ensure the client's website has a clear privacy policy and SMS opt-in disclosure to avoid rejection.

## Client Experience
Once the add-on is active and registration is approved, clients can:
- Send review requests via **Email and SMS**, **Just Email**, or **Just SMS**.
- Track their SMS credit usage directly within **Business App**.
- Manage review request templates and schedules.

For detailed end-user workflows, refer your clients to the [SMS Review Requesting guide](https://docs.businessapp.io/reputation/reviews/sms-review-requesting).
