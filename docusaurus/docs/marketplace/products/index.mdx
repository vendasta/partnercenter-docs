# Products overview

Products is your centralized command center for understanding, managing, and optimizing all enabled products. From marketing info to metrics and configuration, this is where you control growth trends, settings, and revenue.

## Why use Products?

Products helps you optimize your portfolio and grow revenue by identifying what drives growth, understanding store sales and adoption patterns, configuring retail pricing and white-label branding, and setting up upgrade paths to improve customer experience.

## Get started

1. **Navigate to Products**
   - Go to `Partner Center` > `Marketplace` > `Products`
   - Review all enabled products and their status

2. **Configure product settings**
   - Adjust retail pricing by clicking the lock icon
   - Set billing frequency and auto-activation
   - Configure white-label branding and upgrade paths

3. **Create custom products**
   - Click `Create Product` to add your own products
   - Configure billing, pricing, and marketing materials
   - Publish to your store

4. **Monitor product performance**
   - Review active accounts and growth metrics
   - Track activations and deactivations
   - Analyze store sales and adoption patterns

## Common workflows

**Product Configuration**: Enable marketplace products, adjust retail pricing, set up white-label branding, configure auto-activation, and set upgrade paths.

**Custom Product Creation**: Create custom products, configure billing terms, set retail pricing, add marketing materials, and publish to store.

**Product Optimization**: Review product metrics, identify growth trends, optimize pricing strategies, and improve customer experience through upgrade paths.
