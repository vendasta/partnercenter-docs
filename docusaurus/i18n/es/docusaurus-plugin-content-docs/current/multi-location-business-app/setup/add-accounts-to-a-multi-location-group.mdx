Agregar cuentas a un grupo multiubicación permitirá que se vean en [Multi-Location Business App](/multi-location-business-app).

1. Ve a [`Partner Center`](https://partners.vendasta.com/dashboard?marketId=default)
2. Ve a `Accounts` → [`Multi-location Groups`](https://partners.vendasta.com/brands/manage)

   <img src={require('../img/multi-location/add-accounts/multi-location-groups.jpg').default} alt="Multi-location Groups menu in Partner Center" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

3. Haz clic en el grupo al que quieres agregar cuentas
4. Haz clic en `Add accounts`

   <img src={require('../img/multi-location/add-accounts/add-accounts-button.png').default} alt="Add accounts button" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />

5. Haz clic en `+` junto a cada cuenta que quieras agregar
6. Haz clic en `Done`

   <img src={require('../img/multi-location/add-accounts/done-button.png').default} alt="Done button" style={{width: '100%', border: '1px solid #e0e0e0', borderRadius: '8px', boxShadow: '0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)'}} />
